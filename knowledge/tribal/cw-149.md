# ShopFloor Setup Sheet Generation — Automated Documentation

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

CAMWorks ShopFloor generates setup sheets automatically from the CAM project, including: tool list with holder assemblies, fixture references with 3D images, work coordinate origins overlaid on the model, operation sequence with estimated cycle times, and special instructions per operation. Customize the template to match your shop's standard format. This eliminates the manual creation of setup sheets that typically takes 30-60 minutes per job and is often skipped under time pressure.
