# Trochoidal Turning — Extend Tool Life in Deep Grooves

**Category:** `tooling` · **Subcategory:** `tool_selection` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

SolidCAM's trochoidal turning toolpath reduces tool wear and extends tool life by maintaining controlled chip load in deep grooves and challenging profiles. It uses circular interpolation moves instead of straight plunges, spreading wear across the insert edge. Use trochoidal turning for deep grooves (> 3x insert width) and hard materials above 45 HRC.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `H`
- Operation types: `turning`, `grooving`
