# PEEK Medical Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.82` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

1-2 flute uncoated, high rake, 200-500 m/min, 0.1-0.2mm fz. Air only for biocompatibility. Diamond-coated prevents metal contamination. Down-cut prevents lifting. FDA traceability: link SprutCAM version to every implant. Validate with CMM per 21 CFR 820.

- Material groups: `N`
- Operation types: `finishing`
