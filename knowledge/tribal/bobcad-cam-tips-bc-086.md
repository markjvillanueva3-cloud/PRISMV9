# Material Removal Simulation for Visual Verification

BobCAD material removal simulation renders the stock being progressively cut in real-time. Rotate the view during simulation to inspect all surfaces. Use the section view to check internal features (bores, internal pockets). The simulation speed control allows slow-motion review of critical areas and fast-forward through simple passes. Compare the simulated result against the solid model to verify complete material removal. Remaining material appears as distinct color against the finished surface.
