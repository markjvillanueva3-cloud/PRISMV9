# FMEA for NC Program Risk Management

Per SprutCAM operation: failure modes, effects, RPN=S×O×D. High-RPN: add probing, reduce tool limits, add redundant passes. For Swiss-type turning with 20+ operations, FMEA prioritizes which operations need extra controls. Focus mitigation on top 5 RPN operations for maximum risk reduction.
