# Steep and Shallow Continuous Spiral Eliminates Step Marks

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `spiral` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:fusion360-docs`

## Tip

Enable the Continuous option in Steep and Shallow to replace closed contour loops with a continuous spiral-style toolpath. This eliminates step marks between consecutive Z-level passes, minimizes tool lifts, and reduces cycle time. The result is noticeably better surface finish on mold and die surfaces where witness lines are unacceptable.
