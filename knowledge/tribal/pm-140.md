# Uncertainty Budget for Aerospace Tolerances

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.8` · **Source:** `web:powermill-forum`

## Tip

Total uncertainty RSS: machine (±0.005mm gantry), tool deflection (±0.010mm long-reach at 150N), thermal (±0.008mm over 6hr), material spring-back (±0.003mm residual stress), fixture (±0.004mm). RSS total: ±0.015mm. For ±0.025mm spec: Cpk≈1.11 — marginal. Identify and improve dominant contributor. PowerMill error analysis informs fixture and tooling decisions.
