# 2D Contour Linking with Lead-In Arc for Clean Entry

In 2D Contour, configure the Linking tab to use a tangential arc lead-in with a radius of 50-100% of the tool diameter. A straight-line lead-in creates a witness mark at the entry point because the tool transitions from rapid to cutting feed abruptly. An arc lead-in smoothly engages the material at cutting speed, producing an invisible entry point. Set the lead-in sweep angle to 60-90 degrees for best results, and position the entry at an internal corner or non-critical surface.
