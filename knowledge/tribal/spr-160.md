# Sensitivity Ranking for Focus

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.79` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

Vary ±10%: step-over (35% of Ra variation), feed (25%), speed (20%), DOC (15%), runout (5%). Top 2 = 60%. For SprutCAM: step-over is #1 for finish quality, feed is #2. Speed and DOC are secondary. Focus optimization effort on the parameters that actually matter.
