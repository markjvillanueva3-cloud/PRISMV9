# Conformal Cooling Channel Programming

SprutCAM programs conformal channels via 5-axis contour or additive. 20-40% faster cooling, 30-50% less warpage vs straight. Verify flow connectivity at intersections. Clean after drilling. For hot spots near deep ribs and thick sections.
