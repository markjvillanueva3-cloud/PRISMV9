# Full CAD-CAM Associativity — Automatic Toolpath Update on Design Change

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

SolidCAM runs inside SolidWorks/Solid Edge/Inventor with full associativity — all 2D and 3D geometries used for machining are linked to the design model. When you modify the CAD model, all CAM operations automatically update their toolpaths. Always use model references (faces, edges) rather than sketched geometry for CAM boundaries to maintain this link.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `all`
