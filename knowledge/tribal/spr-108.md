# Archard Wear for Speed-Life Trade-Off

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.78` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

V = K×F×d/H. Double speed → quadruple wear. Roughing: -20% speed = 2.5× life. Finishing: accept shorter life at higher speed. Decision depends on tool cost vs machine rate. SprutCAM can encode two speed profiles (economic/max) per tool-material combination in the technology database.
