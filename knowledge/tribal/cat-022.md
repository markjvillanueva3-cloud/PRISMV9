# Raster Angle Optimization for Surface Finish Direction

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:catia-docs`

## Tip

In CATIA Sweeping operations, the raster angle determines the direction of parallel passes relative to the X-axis. Choose the raster angle to align with the longest dimension of the machining area to minimize the number of retract-reposition moves. For mold cavities, orient passes perpendicular to the parting line direction so polish marks align with the draw direction. Experiment with 2-3 angles and use CATIA's cycle time estimation to compare.

- Operation types: `sweeping`
