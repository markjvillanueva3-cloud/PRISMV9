# Post-Machining Verification for Quality Documentation

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:worknc-postverify`

## Tip

WorkNC generates post-machining probing cycles that measure final dimensions and compare against nominal values. Reports can be output as text files on the controller, transmitted via DNC, or integrated with quality management systems. This provides traceability for aerospace (AS9102 FAI) and medical (ISO 13485) requirements without removing the part from the machine.

- Operation types: `probing`
