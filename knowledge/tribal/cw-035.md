# Flat Area Detection — Automatic Identification of Horizontal Surfaces

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

Enable flat area detection to automatically identify and machine horizontal surfaces with a face mill or flat end mill instead of a ball end mill. Ball end mills leave excessive cusp height on flat areas, requiring extensive bench polishing. CAMWorks can detect flat zones within a tolerance angle (typically 5°) and apply a separate operation with appropriate toolpath. This produces superior flatness and saves hours of manual finishing.
