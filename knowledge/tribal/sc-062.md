# HSM Radial Finishing — Ideal for Circular Pocket Floors

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

Use HSM Radial finishing for circular or near-circular pocket floors and boss tops. The radial passes converge toward the center, providing uniform coverage without the overcutting that occurs when linear passes cross a circular boundary. Set the angular increment to achieve your target scallop height — for a 10mm ball nose at 0.005mm scallop, use approximately 2-3 degree increments. Add a final concentric cleanup pass at the outer boundary to remove the radial entry/exit marks.
