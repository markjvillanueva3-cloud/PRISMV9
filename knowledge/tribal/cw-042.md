# Spiral Finishing — Continuous Single-Path Motion Eliminates Step Marks

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

Spiral finishing creates a single continuous toolpath from the center outward (or outside inward), eliminating the step-over marks visible in raster finishing. The spiral maintains constant engagement and avoids retract-reposition moves. Best for shallow, bowl-shaped features and lens surfaces. Ensure the spiral pitch matches the target scallop height — too tight wastes time, too loose leaves visible cusps. Spiral also works well with high-feed face mills on large flat-bottom cavities.
