# Port Machining with Deep Cavity 5-Axis Access

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-port`

## Tip

For port geometries (intake/exhaust manifolds, hydraulic blocks), TopSolid's 5-axis port machining tilts the tool to follow the internal channel geometry while avoiding collisions with the port walls. Use conical ball-nose or lollipop cutters for maximum reach. Set the tool axis to follow the port centerline with a lead angle of 5-10° for chip evacuation. Enable flood coolant or through-tool coolant for deep port cavities exceeding 3x tool diameter.
