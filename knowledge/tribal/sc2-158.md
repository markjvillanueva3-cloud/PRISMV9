# SURFCAM Swiss-Type Thread Whirling Operations

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:surfcam-docs`

## Tip

SURFCAM supports thread whirling for Swiss-type machines, where a ring of cutters surrounds the workpiece and rotates at high speed while the part feeds through. Program thread whirling by defining the thread pitch, whirling ring diameter, number of inserts, and the synchronization ratio between whirling spindle and part feed. Thread whirling produces bone screws, worm gears, and lead screws 5-10x faster than single-point threading. Set whirling RPM to 2000-4000 for titanium bone screws with 0.05-0.1mm depth per pass.

- Material groups: `S`
- Operation types: `turning`, `threading`
