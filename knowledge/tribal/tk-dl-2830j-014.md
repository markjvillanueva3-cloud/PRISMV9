# Spatial process variation requires spatial models, not just SPC

**Category:** `quality` · **Subcategory:** `spc` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `document:mit2830j@lecture21`

## Tip

For processes with spatial variation (injection molding cavity-to-cavity, wafer uniformity, multi-cavity dies), standard SPC on individual measurements misses the pattern. Use spatial models: (1) decompose into radial + angular components, (2) fit polynomial surfaces, (3) track spatial signature changes cycle-to-cycle. A process can be 'in control' on average but have worsening spatial uniformity.
