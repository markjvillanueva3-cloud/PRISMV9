# Geodesic Finishing for Complex Freeform Surfaces

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-tutorials`

## Tip

Geodesic finishing in Cimatron follows the natural curvature of surfaces, producing uniform scallop height on complex freeform geometry. Use this strategy for organic shapes where raster or Z-level would produce uneven surface quality. Set 'Path Type' to 'Geodesic' and 'Step-Over' based on target Ra — typically 0.1mm for Ra 0.8μm on hardened steel.

- Material groups: `P`, `H`
- Operation types: `finishing`
