# Z-Level Finishing with Automatic Steep/Shallow Detection

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `contour` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:siemens-docs`

## Tip

NX Z-level finishing produces smooth, continuous cutting patterns that deliver consistent surface finish in both steep and shallow areas. The system automatically detects surface slope and adjusts step-down accordingly. Combine Z-level for steep regions with contour-area finishing for shallow regions to eliminate witness lines at the transition.
