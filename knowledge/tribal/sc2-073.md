# Machine-Specific Post Processors for Major Brands

**Category:** `post_processor` · **Subcategory:** `controller_quirk` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:surfcam-post-library`

## Tip

SURFCAM includes 200+ pre-built M-POST post processors for major CNC manufacturers: Fanuc, Siemens, Heidenhain, Mazak, Haas, Okuma, DMG Mori, Makino, and others. Always start with the closest matching pre-built post rather than building from scratch. Verify the post output against the machine's operator manual for: coordinate format (decimal point vs. implied decimal), feed rate mode (per-minute vs. per-revolution), and coolant codes (M08/M07/M50-M59).

- Operation types: `posting`
