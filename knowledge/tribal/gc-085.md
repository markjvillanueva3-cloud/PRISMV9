# Stock comparison overlay quantifies material deviation from design intent

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:gibbscam-docs`

## Tip

GibbsCAM's stock comparison overlays the in-process workpiece on the design solid and quantifies the deviation. The system reports maximum gouge depth, maximum remaining stock thickness, and the locations of both. Use this to verify that finishing stock allowances are consistent and that no areas have excessive residual material from the roughing operations. Set the comparison tolerance to your finishing requirement (e.g., 0.02mm for precision molds). Export the comparison report as a color-mapped image for documentation or quality records.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
