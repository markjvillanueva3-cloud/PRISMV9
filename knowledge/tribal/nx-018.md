# Machining Knowledge Editor for Shop Standards

**Category:** `automation` · **Subcategory:** `lights_out` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:siemens-docs`

## Tip

Use NX's Machining Knowledge Editor to customize the rules that FBM uses to assign operations, tools, and parameters to recognized features. Map your shop's preferred tool sizes, speeds/feeds, and operation sequences to specific feature types. Once configured, every programmer in the shop gets consistent programs without memorizing tribal knowledge.
