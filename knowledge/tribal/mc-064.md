# Swarf cutting uses the tool's side to machine ruled surfaces in one pass

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:mastercam-docs`

## Tip

Mastercam Swarf (flank milling) toolpath drives the side of an endmill along a ruled surface, cutting the entire wall height in a single pass. This eliminates multiple Z-level passes and produces excellent surface finish on straight or slightly twisted walls. Swarf only works on truly ruled surfaces — verify by checking that the surface can be generated by sweeping a straight line. Typical applications include turbine blade flanks, mold draft walls, and cylindrical port walls.
