# Grooving with Peck Cycle for Deep Narrow Slots

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:fusion360-docs`

## Tip

For grooves deeper than 3x the insert width, enable the Peck option in Turning Groove to break the cut into incremental plunges. Set each peck depth to 1-2x the insert width and include a 0.1-0.2mm retract between pecks to break chips. Without pecking, deep grooves trap chips between the insert flanks and groove walls, causing insert fracture and poor surface finish. Use flood coolant directed at the groove entry to flush chips from the narrow slot.
