# Vericut Integration for Verification

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

Export SprutCAM toolpaths to Vericut for force-based verification. Catches excessive forces geometric simulation misses. Cycle time differences >10% = optimization opportunity. Required sign-off for complex 5-axis programs. Also validates robot programs where collision risk is highest.
