# Facing for Datum Preparation on Forgings

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:siemens-nx-docs`

## Tip

65-75% step-over, one-way, wiper inserts. Datum flatness ±0.01mm across full face. NX handles irregular forging stock shapes. CMM-verify datum before feature machining. Critical first operation — all subsequent accuracy depends on datum quality.

- Operation types: `roughing`
