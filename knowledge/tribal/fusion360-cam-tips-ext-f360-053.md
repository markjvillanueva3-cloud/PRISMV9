# Flow Finishing with Contact Point Optimization

In Multi-Axis Flow finishing, the contact point between the tool and the surface is maintained at a consistent position on the cutter as it traverses the surface. Use the Lead/Lean angles (typically 3-10 degrees of lead) to position the effective cutting point away from the tool tip center, where surface speed is zero on a ball nose. This eliminates the dead zone at the ball tip and dramatically improves surface finish on shallow regions.
