# Radial Finishing for Dome and Circular Features

WorkNC's radial finishing generates passes radiating from a central point, ideal for dome-shaped and rotationally symmetric features. Set angular increments of 1-3 degrees depending on surface quality requirements. The passes fan out like spokes with stepover measured as angular spacing. Add a final spiral cleanup pass at the hub area where radial passes converge to eliminate the central star pattern.
