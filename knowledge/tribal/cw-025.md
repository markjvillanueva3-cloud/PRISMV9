# VoluMill Multi-Level Roughing — Full-Depth Helical Entry for Maximum Efficiency

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

VoluMill supports full axial depth of cut (up to 4x diameter for carbide end mills) with helical entry to full depth before beginning lateral cutting. This 'deep and narrow' strategy removes more material per pass than conventional 'shallow and wide' roughing. Set axial depth to 2-3x tool diameter for steel and 3-4x for aluminum. The helical entry diameter should be 15-25% of tool diameter to maintain manageable entry forces.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `N`
- Operation types: `roughing`
