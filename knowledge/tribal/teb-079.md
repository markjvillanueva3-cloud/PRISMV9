# Composite Trimming with 5-Axis Routing

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `83` · **Source:** `web:tebis-tutorials`

## Tip

For CFRP/GFRP composite trimming in Tebis: PCD or diamond-coated compression routers, 200-400 m/min, 0.02-0.05mm feed/tooth. Program 5-axis tool normal to the surface edge for clean trim lines. Use zig cutting only (no zigzag) for consistent fiber cutting direction. Vacuum fixturing mandatory — composites delaminate under mechanical clamp forces. Dust extraction required for safety.
