# Multi-Channel Timeline Optimization

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

SprutCAM timeline shows all turrets/spindles simultaneously. Overlap compatible ops (OD turret 1 + drilling turret 2). Wait codes at sync points (part transfer, steady rest). Target >60% parallel utilization. Optimize by maximizing overlap — this is the #1 cycle time lever on mill-turn.

- Operation types: `turning`
