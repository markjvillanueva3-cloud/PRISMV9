# Cast Iron Dry Machining

Gray/ductile cast iron: dry or air blast. Short brittle chips. Uncoated or TiN carbide, 150-250 m/min, 1.5-2×D DOC. Standard offset roughing works. Coolant reduces life via thermal shock. hyperMILL's standard roughing strategies are sufficient — no need for constant engagement in cast iron.
