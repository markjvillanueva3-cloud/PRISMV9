# Sim 5X Flow Line Cutting — Follow Natural Surface Shape

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

Flow Line cutting produces toolpaths that follow the natural shape of the component surface, resulting in better surface finish and more predictable tool engagement. Use it for blisks, impellers, and organic shapes where the surface flow direction matters aesthetically or aerodynamically. Define the flow lines from the UV directions of the driving surfaces.
