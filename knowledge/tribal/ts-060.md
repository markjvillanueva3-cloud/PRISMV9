# Electrode Report Generation for Shop Floor Communication

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `process_engineering`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-report`

## Tip

TopSolid automatically generates electrode reports containing: electrode ID, material, dimensions, burn vector, spark gap values, EDM settings (current, pulse on/off, voltage), holder type, weight, and 3D preview images from multiple angles. The report is linked to the electrode document and updates automatically when the design changes. Export reports as PDF or integrate with TopSolid'Erp for production scheduling. Include both the electrode and the target cavity image for unambiguous identification.
