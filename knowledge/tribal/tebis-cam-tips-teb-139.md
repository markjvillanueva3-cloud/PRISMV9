# Facing Operations with Large Diameter Tools

Tebis facing operation machines the top surface using face mills or large flat end mills. Set step-over to 65-75% of cutter diameter for full coverage. Enable one-way cutting to avoid conventional milling on return. For interrupted cuts (bolt holes, keyways), reduce feed 20% at entry to prevent insert chipping. Use wiper inserts for single-pass mirror finish.
