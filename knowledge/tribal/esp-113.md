# Inconel and Nickel Superalloy Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:esprit-materials`

## Tip

Inconel 718 and similar superalloys generate extreme heat in ESPRIT programming. Use ceramic inserts for roughing (300-600 m/min, aggressive DOC 2-3mm) or coated carbide for finishing (20-40 m/min, light DOC 0.2-0.5mm). ProfitMilling engagement control is critical — Inconel's work-hardening rate is 3x that of stainless. Program round inserts (RCMT/RCHT) that distribute heat across a larger contact area. High-pressure coolant (100+ bar) is mandatory for carbide tools.
