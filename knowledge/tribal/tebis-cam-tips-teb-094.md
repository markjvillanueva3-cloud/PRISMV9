# Tebis CAD Repair for Imported Surfaces

Imported STEP/IGES models often have surface gaps, overlaps, and tangency breaks. Tebis CAD repair tools: close gaps (up to 0.1mm), extend short surfaces, rebuild degenerate faces, and smooth tangency transitions. Run surface analysis first to identify problems, then repair systematically. Quality of machined surfaces directly depends on quality of the input geometry — garbage in, garbage out.
