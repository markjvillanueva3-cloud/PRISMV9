# TopSolid Digital Twin — Process Optimization Loop

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-docs`

## Tip

Establish a continuous improvement loop between the digital twin and production: (1) TopSolid'Cam generates the program with predicted performance, (2) ShopFloor monitors actual performance during production, (3) compare actual vs predicted (cycle time, tool life, quality), (4) identify discrepancies and root causes, (5) update TopSolid templates and knowledge base with learned corrections. This loop converges actual performance toward the digital model's predictions over successive production runs. After 5-10 iterations, predictions typically achieve 95%+ accuracy for cycle time and tool life.
