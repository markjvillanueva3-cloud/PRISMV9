# FMEA and Sensitivity for Risk-Based Optimization

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `79` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-forum`

## Tip

Per hyperMILL operation: failure modes, effects, RPN=S×O×D. MAXX barrel cutters have different failure modes than ball-end: tilt error → gouge (high severity), barrel contact loss → air cut (medium). Combine FMEA with sensitivity analysis: high-RPN × high-Sobol parameters get the most optimization attention. For MAXX: tilt angle is both high-RPN and high-sensitivity.
