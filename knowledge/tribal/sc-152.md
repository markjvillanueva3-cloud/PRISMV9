# Uncertainty Budget for iMachining vs Conventional

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `80` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-forum`

## Tip

iMachining reduces force-related uncertainty sources by 50%: lower deflection (±0.004mm vs ±0.008mm), less thermal generation, more consistent chip load. RSS total for iMachining: ±0.009mm vs ±0.013mm conventional. This allows tighter tolerances from the same machine. Quantify the improvement to justify iMachining licensing costs.
