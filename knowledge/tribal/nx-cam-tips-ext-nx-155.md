# Robust Material Hardness Tolerance in Parameters

Aerospace alloys have batch-to-batch hardness variation: Ti-6Al-4V ±3 HRC, Inconel 718 ±2 HRC, Al 7075-T6 ±5 BHN. Design NX cutting parameters that perform acceptably across the full range. Moderate speeds (70-80% of theoretical optimum) with consistent chip load produce the most stable results. Extreme high-speed parameters that work at nominal hardness fail unpredictably at the hard end of the range.
