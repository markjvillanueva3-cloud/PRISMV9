# Turning with iMachining Technology

SolidCAM iMachining for turning maintains constant chip area. Reduces forces and vibration on lathes. Material level slider works the same as milling. Particularly effective for hard turning (>45 HRC) where conventional approaches produce chatter. iMachining turning is unique to SolidCAM.
