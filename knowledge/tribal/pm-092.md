# Taylor Tool Life for Economic Cutting Speed

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.81` · **Source:** `web:powermill-forum`

## Tip

Taylor: VT^n = C. Economic speed V_econ = C/((1/n-1)×(Ct/Cm+tc))^n. V_econ is typically 70-80% of max productivity speed. For expensive aerospace parts, use V_econ to maximize tool life and minimize in-cut failure risk. For production parts where machine rate dominates, use max productivity speed. PowerMill's feed optimization can apply these concepts per toolpath segment.
