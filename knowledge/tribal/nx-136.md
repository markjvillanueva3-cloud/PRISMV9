# Composite Machining for CFRP/GFRP Parts

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.83` · **Source:** `web:siemens-community`

## Tip

For composite machining in NX: use PCD or diamond-coated compression routers at 200-400 m/min, feed 0.02-0.05mm/tooth. Program 5-axis tool normal to the surface edge for clean trim lines. Use 'Zig' cutting only (no zigzag) to maintain consistent fiber cutting direction. Vacuum fixturing is mandatory — composites delaminate under mechanical clamp forces. Set step-down to 0.5-1× material thickness.
