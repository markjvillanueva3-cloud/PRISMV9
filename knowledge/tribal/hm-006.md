# hyperMILL Optimised Roughing maintains constant tool engagement for extended tool life

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `93` · **Source:** `document:hypermill-manual`

## Tip

hyperMILL Optimised Roughing (3D Machining module) maintains constant tool engagement angle throughout the toolpath, similar to Mastercam Dynamic. Parameters: stepover percentage (typically 10-25%), radial engagement limit, entry method (helical, ramp, pre-drilled). The strategy avoids full-width cuts that overload tools. Best for hardened materials, high-speed machining, and deep cavities where consistent chip load matters.

- Operation types: `roughing`, `3d_roughing`
