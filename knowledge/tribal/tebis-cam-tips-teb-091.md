# Tebis ProFile for Surface Quality Analysis

Tebis ProFile analyzes surface quality by computing: curvature distribution, zebra stripes, reflection lines, and deviation maps. Use ProFile before machining to identify surface quality issues in the CAD model (faceting, tangency breaks, micro-waviness). Fix surface defects in the CAD before generating toolpaths — machining amplifies any existing surface quality problems.
