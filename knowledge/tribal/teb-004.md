# Active Surface Technology Extends Surfaces for Clean Tool Exit

**Category:** `mold_die` · **Subcategory:** `parting_line` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:tebis-tutorials`

## Tip

Tebis Active Surface extends machining surfaces beyond part boundaries so the tool enters and exits on extended geometry rather than abruptly stopping at edges. Extend by at least 1.5x tool diameter. This prevents dwell marks at parting lines and edges. For mold cavities, extend all parting-line surfaces upward to the blank top. The extended surfaces are non-associative copies, so original design surfaces remain untouched.
