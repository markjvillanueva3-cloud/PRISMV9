# Swarf Cutting — Side-of-Tool Machining for Ruled Surfaces

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

Swarf cutting uses the side (flute) of the tool to machine ruled surfaces (surfaces generated by a straight line moving through space). This produces excellent surface finish on impeller blade walls, turbine vanes, and draft walls. The tool axis aligns tangent to the surface at each point. Ensure the tool is long enough to span the full surface height in one pass. Use solid carbide end mills with variable helix to suppress chatter during full-flute-length engagement in swarf cutting.
