# Disc Cutter for Slot and Groove Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.82` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Define disc cutter geometry in PowerMill: diameter, thickness, bore, tooth count. 3+2 positioning per slot orientation. Speed based on outer diameter. Feed per tooth accounts for high tooth count (20-60). Flood coolant for chip evacuation. Disc cutters produce more consistent slot widths than end mills and are preferred for mold runner slots.
