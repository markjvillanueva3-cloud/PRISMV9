# Trimming Operations for Composite and Sheet Parts

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:surfcam-5axis-trimming`

## Tip

SURFCAM 5-axis trimming machines the edge profile of composite layups, thermoformed parts, and sheet metal. The tool axis is controlled to maintain a constant angle relative to the edge surface normal (typically 0° for flush trim, 45° for chamfer trim). Use a compression router bit for composites to prevent delamination. Set the feed rate based on the material stack thickness and cutter engagement, not just the surface speed.
