# Titanium chip color indicator

**Category:** `tooling` · **Subcategory:** `tool_selection` · **Domain:** `shop_floor`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `operator:aerospace_lead`

## Tip

Watch chip color when cutting Ti-6Al-4V: silver/light gold = good parameters. Dark blue/purple = too hot — reduce speed immediately. If chips are dark brown/black, you're burning the tool and workpiece. Through-spindle coolant is mandatory above 45 m/min.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `S`
- Operation types: `face`, `pocket`, `profile`
