# Thickness Allowance for Progressive Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

Roughing 0.5mm, semi 0.15mm, finish 0.0mm. iMachining handles roughing with consistent stock. HSM finishing removes final layer. Progressive approach extends tool life 40-60%. iMachining's accurate IPW makes each subsequent operation more efficient.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
