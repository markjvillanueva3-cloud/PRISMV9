# Port Machining — 5-Axis Roughing and Finishing of Curved Channels

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

Port machining automates the programming of curved internal channels (intake ports, exhaust manifolds, hydraulic passages). Define the port centerline curve and cross-section profiles — CAMWorks generates roughing and finishing toolpaths that follow the port geometry. Use ball end mills sized to 60-70% of the minimum port cross-section for roughing, and 40-50% for finishing to allow proper tool orientation around tight bends. Enable gouge checking against the port walls.
