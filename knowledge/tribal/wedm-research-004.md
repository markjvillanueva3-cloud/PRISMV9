## Tip

Relative Surface Roughness CNN-LSTM (R-CNN-LSTM) model predicts surface roughness in real-time using 3D force time-series signals during machining. The hybrid architecture: CNN extracts spatial features from force patterns, LSTM captures temporal dependencies. Validated on 6061 aluminum alloy with superior accuracy vs standalone CNN or LSTM models.
