# Multi-Axis Drilling with Rotary Axis Control

Multi-axis drilling (added late 2025) lets you define a rotary axis for drilling operations, enabling drilling at compound angles on 4- and 5-axis machines without manual WCS rotation. This eliminates the need for separate setups or 3+2 indexing just to drill angled holes — program them directly in the main setup with automatic axis positioning.
