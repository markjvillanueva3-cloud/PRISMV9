# AMSAA for Program Maturity Assessment

Cumulative failures vs production hours. Power law decrease = maturing. Stalls: tool vendor change, material batch, maintenance. Mature SprutCAM programs: <0.5% scrap after 50+ cycles. Track turning and milling failures separately. Robot programs need 100+ cycles to stabilize due to additional variability sources.
