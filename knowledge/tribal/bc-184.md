# BobART Texture Mapping for Surface Finishing Effects

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:bobcad-docs`

## Tip

BobART applies texture patterns (cross-hatch, leather, diamond, custom) to surfaces for decorative or functional finishing. Define the texture parameters: pattern type, depth (0.05-0.5mm), spacing (0.5-5mm), and orientation angle. Apply textures to specific surface regions using boundary curves. For grip surfaces on handles, use diamond or knurl patterns at 0.1-0.3mm depth. For decorative panels, use custom textures imported as grayscale images. Machine textures with ball-nose tools sized to 50-70% of the texture feature spacing for adequate detail. Two-pass machining (roughing + finish) produces cleaner textures.
