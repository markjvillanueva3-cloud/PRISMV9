# Uncertainty Budget for Turned Parts

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.8` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

RSS: chuck concentricity (±0.005mm), bar straightness (±0.010mm), tool wear (±0.005mm), thermal (±0.003mm), machine (±0.003mm). Total: ±0.013mm. For ±0.020mm tolerance: Cpk≈1.03 — marginal. Bar quality is often the dominant error source on Swiss-type machines. Use precision ground bar stock.
