# Flowline Finishing Follows Surface Natural Direction

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:worknc-flowline`

## Tip

WorkNC's flowline finishing generates toolpaths that follow the natural parametric direction of the surface. This produces passes aligned with the geometry flow, ideal for organic shapes where parallel passes create abrupt direction changes. Select the dominant flow direction and set the cross-step perpendicular to it. Flowline produces the most aesthetically pleasing finish on consumer products and automotive Class-A surfaces.
