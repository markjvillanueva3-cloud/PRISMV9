# TrueMill Constant Engagement Eliminates Corner Load Spikes

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `94` · **Source:** `web:surfcam-hexagon-truemill`

## Tip

TrueMill is engagement-angle driven, not geometry driven — the shape of the part geometry does not dictate toolpath flow. Each cut is computed so the next cut maintains the desired engagement angle. This eliminates the sudden load spikes that occur in conventional roughing when the tool plows into internal corners. Set the engagement angle to 40-60° for carbide end mills in steel to achieve 2-5x MRR increases while keeping spindle load meters well below redline.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`
- Operation types: `roughing`, `pocketing`
