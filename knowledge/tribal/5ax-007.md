# DO NOT output G17 plane call in 5-axis program safety line on Hurco

**Category:** `cnc_programming` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `document:hurco-5axis-post`

## Tip

On Hurco 5-axis, avoid G17/G18/G19 plane designation in the program safety line when using Transform Planes or 5-axis simultaneous. Plane codes can conflict with G68.2 Transform Planes and cause unexpected behavior. The traditional G0 G20 G40 G80 G54 G90 safety line is sufficient. Re-establish plane only when returning to 3-axis operations after cancelling transform planes.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `5axis`, `multiaxis`, `3plus2`
