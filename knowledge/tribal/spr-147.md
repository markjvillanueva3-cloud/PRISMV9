# Volumetric Accuracy Compensation

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.81` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

Import machine 21-error map, SprutCAM adjusts coordinates to pre-compensate. Valuable for large parts (1m+) where errors compound. Improves from ±0.03mm to ±0.01mm. Requires periodic calibration (laser or ball-bar). For robot machining, volumetric compensation is even more critical due to lower baseline accuracy.
