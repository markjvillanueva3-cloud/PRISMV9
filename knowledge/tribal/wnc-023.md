# Z-Level Finishing for Steep Wall Surface Quality

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `93` · **Source:** `web:worknc-zfinish`

## Tip

WorkNC's Z-level finishing generates constant-Z contour passes ideal for steep surfaces exceeding 45 degrees from horizontal. Set the Z-step by target scallop height: 0.005-0.02 mm for final finish, 0.05-0.15 mm for semi-finish. Enable variable Z-step to slightly vary the increment and avoid synchronization marks. Use ball-nose cutters with the largest practical diameter to minimize cusp height on walls.
