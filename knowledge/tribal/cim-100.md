# Inconel Roughing with Ceramic Inserts

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

For Inconel 718 in Cimatron: ceramic inserts at 200-400 m/min, 0.1-0.15mm feed/tooth, 1-2mm DOC. No coolant — ceramics thermal shock crack. Air blast for chip clearing only. Tool life short (10-20 min) but MRR 3-5× higher than carbide. Constant engagement essential — interrupted cuts destroy ceramic. Reserve ceramic for roughing, finish with carbide tools.
