# Helical Entry for Deep Pocket and Hole Features

SURFCAM helical entry spirals the tool down into the material, combining a circular motion with a gradual Z-descent. This is the preferred entry method for deep pockets and large holes. Set the helix diameter to 2-3x the tool diameter (must be smaller than the pocket opening) and the helix pitch to 0.5-2mm per revolution. The helix centerpoint should be positioned in the largest open area of the pocket. For small pockets where the helix won't fit, fall back to ramp entry.
