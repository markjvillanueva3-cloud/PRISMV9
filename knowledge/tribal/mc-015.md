# Multiaxis safe zone provides 5-axis collision buffer

**Category:** `safety` · **Subcategory:** `coolant_safety` · **Domain:** `safety`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:mastercam-docs`

## Tip

Mastercam Multiaxis provides a configurable safe zone around the machined part as a collision buffer. This safety envelope prevents the tool or holder from approaching closer than the specified distance to any part surface. Combine safe zone with advanced gouge-checking to catch both holder collisions and tip gouges. Set the safe zone to at least the holder's maximum radius plus 2mm clearance.

- Operation types: `multiaxis`, `5_axis`
