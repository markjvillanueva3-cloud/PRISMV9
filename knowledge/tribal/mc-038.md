# Use entry feed and dwell to protect tools at cut start

**Category:** `speeds_feeds` · **Subcategory:** `cutting_parameters` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `83` · **Source:** `web:community`

## Tip

Set separate Entry Feed rates in Mastercam toolpath parameters — typically 50% of the cutting feed — to reduce load when the tool first engages material. Add a short dwell (0.1-0.5 seconds) after entry to let the spindle reach programmed RPM before material removal begins. This is especially important for large-diameter tools and interrupted cuts where the spindle may lag during initial engagement.

- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
