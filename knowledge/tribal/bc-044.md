# Finish Turning with Insert Angle Gouge Protection

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:bobcad-turning-finish`

## Tip

BobCAD finish turning validates the front and back angles of the cutting insert against the part profile. Warnings appear when the insert geometry cannot reach required angles. Use 35° diamond inserts (VCMT/VBMT) for profiles up to 93° from axis, or 55° (DNMG) for general finishing. Set nose radius compensation to match the actual insert corner radius. BobCAD calculates G41/G42 with TNR compensation automatically for accurate profile generation.
