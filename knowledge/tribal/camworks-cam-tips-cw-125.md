# Composite Machining — Diamond Tooling with Dust Extraction

CFRP and GFRP composites require PCD or diamond-coated tools to resist the abrasive carbon/glass fibers. Use compression routers (up-down helix) to prevent delamination on both top and bottom surfaces. Vc 150-400 m/min, fz 0.02-0.06mm/tooth. No coolant — water damages some resin systems. Instead, use vacuum dust extraction at the cutting zone (carbon fiber dust is a health hazard and conductive — it damages machine electronics). Program climb milling with shallow passes (ap ≤ 1x diameter) to minimize delamination forces.
