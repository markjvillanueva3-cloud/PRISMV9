# Robot Force-Controlled Grinding

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.81` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

PowerMill Robot programs grinding with force/torque feedback. Constant material removal rate regardless of surface curvature. Define target force (10-50N), abrasive RPM, and path pattern. Robot compliance handles surface variations. For turbine blade root finishing and weld blending.
