# Hybrid Additive + Subtractive Workflow — Near-Net Shape to Finish

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

CAMWorks supports hybrid manufacturing workflows: additive (3D print, DED, or powder bed) creates a near-net shape, then subtractive (CNC machining) finishes critical surfaces. Import the as-printed stock model into CAMWorks and program finishing operations only on surfaces requiring tight tolerances or surface finish. Key advantages: 70-90% material savings on aerospace parts (buy-to-fly ratio), complex internal channels impossible with pure subtractive, and reduced roughing time. Challenge: as-printed surfaces have 0.5-2mm dimensional variation, so finishing stock must accommodate this.

- Operation types: `milling`, `finishing`
