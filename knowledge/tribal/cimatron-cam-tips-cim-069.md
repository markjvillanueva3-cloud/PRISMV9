# Core/Cavity Parting Surface Generation

Cimatron generates core/cavity splits with automatic parting surface creation. Define the parting line, and Cimatron creates the parting surface extending to the mold base. These surfaces become machining boundaries — separate toolpaths for core and cavity sides. The parting surface quality directly affects mold shut-off quality — ensure G2 continuity for clean parting.
