# Swiss-Type Thread Whirling — High-Speed Medical Screw Threading

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

Thread whirling is a Swiss-type specialty for medical bone screws and implants. A ring of cutters rotates around the stationary workpiece, generating the thread in a single pass. In CAMWorks, program thread whirling as a custom cycle with parameters: thread pitch, major/minor diameters, whirl ring RPM, and feed rate (= pitch per revolution of the ring). The process is 5-10x faster than single-point threading for long threaded sections. Post processor must output the thread whirling G-code specific to your Swiss machine brand (Star, Citizen, Tornos).
