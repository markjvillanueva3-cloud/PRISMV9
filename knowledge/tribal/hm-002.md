# hyperMILL turning clearance radius/plane defines safe rapid traverse zone

**Category:** `cnc_programming` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `92` · **Source:** `document:hypermill-manual`

## Tip

In hyperMILL turning cycles, Clearance radius (X) and Clearance plane (Z) define the safe zone for G0 rapid movements. All approach and retract rapids stay within this envelope. Clearance distance adds a feedrate buffer before rapids begin. Set clearance values outside workpiece + allowance + expected thermal growth. For internal operations (boring), clearance radius must be smaller than bore diameter.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `turning`, `boring`
