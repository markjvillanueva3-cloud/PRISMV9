# HSM Trochoidal Roughing for Hard Materials

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `adaptive` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-forum`

## Tip

For roughing hardened tool steel (>48 HRC), use Cimatron's trochoidal (VoluMill-style) roughing with constant chip load. Set radial engagement to 8-12% of tool diameter, full axial depth (1×D), and feed rate 3-5× higher than conventional. This maintains constant tool engagement angle below 60°, dramatically extending carbide tool life.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `H`
- Operation types: `roughing`
