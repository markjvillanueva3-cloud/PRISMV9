# Pencil Tracing for Internal Corner Cleanup

**Category:** `finishing` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:tebis-docs`

## Tip

After main finishing passes, pencil tracing cleans internal corners and fillets left by larger tools. Tebis detects concave regions automatically. Use a ball-end mill 50-70% of the smallest fillet radius. Enable 'Both Ways' for symmetric corners to halve cycle time. Pencil tracing targets the exact transition zone that Z-level and raster strategies miss — run as the final finishing operation.
