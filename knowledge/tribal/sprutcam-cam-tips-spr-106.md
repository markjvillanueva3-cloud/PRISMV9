# Hertzian Contact for Ball-End Selection

σ_max ∝ (F/R²)^(1/3). Larger ball = lower stress = longer life. Min diameter = 2×step-over/sin(max_slope). For hardened steel at 52 HRC: 6mm ball min for step-over ≤0.3mm. Prevents high tip stress causing premature CBN failure. SprutCAM tool selection should always verify Hertzian limits.
