# Plunge Roughing for Extreme Conditions

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Plunge roughing drills vertically, repositions, repeats. Step-over 60-70% of diameter. Effective for: narrow deep slots (L/D>4), hard materials (>45 HRC), weak spindles. Forces primarily axial (strongest direction). Slower MRR but dramatically safer. PowerMill's plunge roughing generates efficient patterns with minimized rapid repositioning distance.

- Material groups: `H`
- Operation types: `roughing`
