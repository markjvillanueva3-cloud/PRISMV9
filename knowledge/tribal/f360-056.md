# Radial Finishing for Circular and Dome Surfaces

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `83` · **Source:** `web:fusion360-docs`

## Tip

Radial finishing generates toolpaths that fan outward from a center point, making it ideal for dome-shaped surfaces, lens molds, and circular features. Set the angular increment to 1-3 degrees for fine surface finish on optical-quality surfaces. The stepover increases toward the outer edge, so combine Radial with a Scallop pass on the periphery to maintain consistent cusp height across the entire surface.
