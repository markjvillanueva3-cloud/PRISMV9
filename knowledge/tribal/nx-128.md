# Additive Manufacturing in NX

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `contour` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.82` · **Source:** `web:siemens-nx-docs`

## Tip

NX supports powder bed and DED additive manufacturing. Define build orientation, support structures, scan patterns (stripe, island, contour+fill), and process parameters (laser power, scan speed, hatch spacing). For hybrid manufacturing, alternate additive and subtractive operations. NX's simulation predicts thermal distortion and residual stress. Use compensated geometry to counteract predicted distortion.
