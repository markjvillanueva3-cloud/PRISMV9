# Uncertainty Budget for Mold Cavity Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.81` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-forum`

## Tip

Total uncertainty: machine positioning (±0.003mm), tool diameter (±0.005mm H6), tool deflection (±0.008mm for 6mm ball at 100N), thermal growth (±0.005mm over 4hr), measurement (±0.002mm CMM). RSS total: ±0.012mm. This determines achievable tolerance class. For ±0.01mm: improve largest contributor (deflection — larger diameter or shorter tool).
