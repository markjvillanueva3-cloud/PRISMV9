# Add center points to 2-level factorials to detect curvature

**Category:** `quality` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `document:mit2830j@lecture14`

## Tip

A 2-level factorial design can only fit linear models. Add 3-5 center points (all factors at midpoint) to test for curvature without increasing the number of factorial runs. If the center point average differs significantly from the factorial average, there is curvature and you need a response surface design (CCD or Box-Behnken).
