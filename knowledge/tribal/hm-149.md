# Taguchi Robust Design for Stable Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

## Tip

L9: speed, feed, step-over (3 levels). Noise: hardness (±2 HRC), wear state. S/N for Ra. Taguchi-optimal maximizes signal-to-noise. For hyperMILL MAXX Finishing: apply Taguchi to barrel tilt angle and step-over — these are the most sensitive parameters unique to barrel cutter strategies.
