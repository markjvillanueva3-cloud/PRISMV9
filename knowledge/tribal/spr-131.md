# Thickness Allowance Progressive Strategy

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

Roughing 0.5mm, semi 0.15mm, finish 0.0mm. Hardened: 0.3→0.15→0.05→0.0mm. Each removes only its layer. Extends tool life 40-60% vs 2-pass. SprutCAM stock model tracks remaining material accurately for each operation in the progressive sequence.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
