# NC Manufacturing Review for Shop-Floor G-Code Validation

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:catia-docs`

## Tip

CATIA's NC Manufacturing Review workbench allows shop-floor operators to visualize and verify machining programs without a full CAM license. Operators load the .CATProcess or 3DEXPERIENCE manufacturing item and play back tool paths with material removal simulation. They can measure: (1) remaining stock at any point, (2) minimum wall thickness after machining, (3) tool-to-fixture clearance. Enable 'Compare with Part' to color-map the machined stock against the design model — red indicates overcut (gouge), blue indicates remaining stock (undercut). This validation catches 70-80% of programming errors before first article.
