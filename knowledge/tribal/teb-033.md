# Geodesic Finishing Follows Natural Surface Flow Lines

**Category:** `finishing` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:tebis-docs`

## Tip

Tebis geodesic finishing generates toolpaths that follow the shortest paths on the surface (geodesic curves). The toolpath flows naturally with the surface shape, producing superior finish on organic forms. Particularly effective for automotive Class A surfaces, turbine blades, and freeform sculptures. Step-over is measured along the surface, not in XY projection, ensuring truly uniform scallop height. Computation time is higher than Z-constant but the surface quality justifies it for visible parts.
