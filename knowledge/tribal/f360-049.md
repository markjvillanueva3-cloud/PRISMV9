# Morphed Spiral Inner vs Outer Boundary Control

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:fusion360-docs`

## Tip

Morphed Spiral quality depends heavily on your inner and outer boundary selections. Select the outer boundary as the outermost contour of the machining area and the inner boundary as the smallest feature or center point. When boundaries differ drastically in shape, the spiral can compress unevenly — add intermediate guide curves or split the surface into regions with similar aspect ratios for cleaner spiral distribution.
