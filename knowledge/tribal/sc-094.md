# Stock Comparison — Real-Time Remaining Material Visualization

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

Enable Stock & Target Comparison in SolidCAM Simulator to see a real-time color map of remaining material versus the target part during playback. Green indicates within tolerance, red shows excess material, and blue shows gouging. Set the comparison tolerance to match your drawing tolerance (typically 0.01-0.05mm for finishing). Use the comparison result to identify where rest machining operations are needed — areas remaining red after all operations indicate tool access problems requiring additional setups or smaller tools.
