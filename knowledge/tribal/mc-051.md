# Pocket Roughing with Island Facing reduces secondary operations

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `pocket` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `83` · **Source:** `web:community`

## Tip

Enable Island Facing within Pocket Roughing to machine the top of islands (bosses) as part of the roughing cycle rather than programming a separate facing operation. Island Facing adds a pass at each stepdown level across the top of detected islands. This eliminates a standalone Face Mill or Area Rough pass and keeps the stock model accurate for subsequent operations. Saves 1-3 minutes per setup on parts with multiple boss features.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `pocketing`
