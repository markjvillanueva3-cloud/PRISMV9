# Impeller and Blade Module

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Cimatron blade module: define hub, shroud, blade, splitter. Plunge roughing between blades, flowline finishing on surfaces. Barrel cutters for 3-5× wider step-over. Check singularities at leading/trailing edges. 5-axis link moves navigate between blades. For pump impellers and turbocharger compressor wheels.
