# Used wire disposal: metal recycling, not trash

Used brass wire is a recyclable commodity. Collect it in the waste wire bucket (never let it overflow). Average wire consumption is 8-15 kg per 40-hour week depending on cutting volume. Current scrap brass price: $2-4/kg. More importantly: used wire in the trash can cut maintenance staff. Keep the wire collection area clean and empty the bucket daily. Some shops compact used wire into bricks for higher scrap value.
