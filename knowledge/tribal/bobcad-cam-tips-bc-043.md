# OD Roughing with Automatic Stock Recognition

BobCAD Turning roughing automatically detects raw stock boundary (round, hex, or custom profile) and generates minimum passes to clear to the finished profile plus stock allowance. Set depth of cut to 2-4mm for carbide inserts in steel. Use 'Constant depth' mode for consistent chip load or 'Variable depth' to avoid thin final passes. BobCAD's turning wizard guides setup including stock definition, chuck/collet specification, and tailstock support.
