# Tool holder positional orientation follows specific conventions per spindle type

**Category:** `setup` · **Subcategory:** `alignment` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `document:hypermill-tool-builder`

## Tip

hyperMILL TOOL Builder requires correct positional orientation: Z-axis coaxial with main flange pointing toward spindle. BT: X-axis toward center of one slot. SK/CAT: X-axis toward center of flattest slot. HSK: X-axis toward flattest slot on notch side, coupling at flange upper side. CAPTO: Y-axis toward reference notch. KM: Y-axis through hole axis toward recess. Incorrect orientation causes simulation misalignment and false collision reports.
