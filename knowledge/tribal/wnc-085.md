# Reaming with Controlled Feed and Speed

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `drilling` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:worknc-reaming`

## Tip

WorkNC's reaming operation uses precise feed and speed control. Set cutting speed to 50-70% of drilling speed and feed rate to 2-3x drilling feed for best finish. Reaming allowance: 0.1-0.2 mm per side for hand reamers, 0.15-0.3 mm for machine reamers. Enable flood coolant and set retract feed equal to cutting feed to prevent marking. Use expansion reamers for tight tolerances.
