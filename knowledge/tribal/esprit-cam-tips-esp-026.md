# ProfitTurning Entry Strategy Protects Insert Nose

Configure ProfitTurning's entry strategy to use a rolling or tangential entry rather than plunging directly into the stock face. A 15-30 degree rolling entry gradually loads the insert edge, preventing the thermal shock and edge chipping that occurs with direct plunge entries — especially critical for coated carbide and ceramic inserts. For interrupted cuts (castings, forgings with flash), reduce entry feed to 50% of cutting feed.
