# Latin Hypercube Sampling for Efficient DOE

**Category:** `optimization` · **Subcategory:** `feed_optimization` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `77` · **Source:** `web:tebis-forum`

## Tip

LHS generates space-filling sample plans more efficiently than full factorial. For 5 parameters at 3 levels: full factorial = 243 runs, LHS = 30-50 runs with comparable coverage. Use LHS for initial screening of Tebis parameter space before targeted RSM optimization. LHS ensures each parameter level is sampled equally while exploring the full multi-dimensional space.
