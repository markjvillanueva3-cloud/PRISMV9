# Use MBase Manufacturing Templates for Repeatable Mold Processes

Tebis MBase (Manufacturing Base) stores proven process templates that encode tooling, strategies, and parameters for specific mold features. Create MBase templates for common features like ribs, bosses, pockets, and parting surfaces. When a new mold arrives, AutoMill matches geometry to MBase templates and applies the stored process. This reduces programming time by 60-80% on repeat geometries and ensures consistent quality across all programmers.
