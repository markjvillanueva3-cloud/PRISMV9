# Steep and Shallow Automatic Boundary Detection

**Category:** `finishing` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:tebis-docs`

## Tip

Tebis automatically detects steep and shallow surface regions and applies appropriate finishing strategies: Z-level for steep (>65°), raster or 3D-offset for shallow (<65°). Set the threshold angle. Enable boundary overlap at 1-2mm to eliminate the transition witness line. Use the same tool for both passes to avoid tool-change marks at the boundary.
