# Cpk vs Cp: always use Cpk for real processes

**Category:** `quality` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `95` · **Source:** `document:mit2830j@lecture3`

## Tip

Cp measures potential capability assuming process is centered. Cpk measures ACTUAL capability including mean offset. Cpk = min((USL-μ)/(3σ), (μ-LSL)/(3σ)). A process with Cp=2.0 but off-center can have Cpk=0.5 and produce 10% scrap. Always report Cpk, not just Cp. Target Cpk ≥ 1.33 (4σ) for production, Cpk ≥ 1.67 (5σ) for safety-critical.
