# Stainless Steel Requires Continuous Chip Formation

For austenitic stainless (304, 316) in WorkNC, prevent work hardening by maintaining positive chip load at all times. Never allow the tool to rub (keep feed above 0.05 mm/tooth). Use waveform roughing at 10-15% engagement with 1.5-2x diameter axial depth. Speed: 80-120 m/min for carbide. Always use flood coolant. Enable constant chip load compensation in corners to prevent rubbing.
