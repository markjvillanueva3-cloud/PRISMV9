# Composite Drilling — Preventing Entry and Exit Delamination

Drilling composites requires special strategies to prevent delamination at entry and exit surfaces. WorkNC programs: (1) reduced feed at entry (50% of steady-state feed for first 2mm), (2) peck drilling with short retracts to clear dust, (3) dramatically reduced feed at exit (30% of nominal for last 1mm to prevent push-through delamination), (4) back-up support plate on exit side when possible. Use brad-point or dagger drills designed for composites — standard twist drills cause severe delamination. Maximum hole diameter without pilot: 6mm for CFRP, 10mm for GFRP.
