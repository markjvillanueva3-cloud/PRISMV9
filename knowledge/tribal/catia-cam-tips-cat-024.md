# Helical Surface Machining for Bore and Cylinder Finishing

For cylindrical bore finishing in CATIA, use the Helical machining strategy which generates a continuous spiral path from top to bottom. This avoids the Z-level step marks inherent in ZLevel machining on cylindrical surfaces. Set the helical pitch equal to your desired stepover (typically 0.1-0.3mm for finishing). The continuous helical path also reduces acceleration/deceleration events, producing smoother machine motion and better surface finish on bore walls.
