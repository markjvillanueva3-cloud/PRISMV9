# EKL Scripts Automate Repetitive CAM Parameter Adjustments

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:catia-docs`

## Tip

Use CATIA EKL (Enterprise Knowledge Language) scripts to automate repetitive CAM tasks like applying standard feeds/speeds tables, adjusting stock allowances based on material type, or batch-modifying operation parameters. EKL can access and modify machining operation attributes programmatically. Example: a script that reads the material property from the part design and automatically sets the appropriate cutting speed, feed per tooth, and depth of cut across all operations in the Manufacturing Program.
