# Mill-Turn Stock Tracking — Avoid Air Cuts Automatically

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

SolidCAM automatically tracks material removal during each turning and milling operation, updating the in-process stock model. This enables smarter toolpaths that avoid air cuts and reduce redundant motion in subsequent operations. Always sequence your operations in the CAM tree in the actual machining order so stock tracking reflects reality.

- Operation types: `mill_turn`, `turning`
