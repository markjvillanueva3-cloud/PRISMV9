# Vise jaw alignment check

**Category:** `fixturing` · **Subcategory:** `vise` · **Domain:** `shop_floor`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `operator:quality_lead`

## Tip

Every Monday morning: run a dial indicator across the fixed jaw. If TIR exceeds 0.0005" (0.013mm), re-seat the jaw with a soft hammer and re-indicate. 90% of 'mystery' taper errors trace back to jaw alignment drift from weekend thermal cycling.
