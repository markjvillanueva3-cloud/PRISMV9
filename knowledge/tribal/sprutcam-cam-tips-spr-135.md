# Trochoidal Milling for Hard Materials

8-15% radial engagement, full depth, 3-5× feed vs conventional. Constant engagement prevents impact loading. SprutCAM adaptive roughing handles this. Slot width independent of tool diameter. Work-free chips improve tool life. For >45 HRC: trochoidal is the preferred roughing strategy.
