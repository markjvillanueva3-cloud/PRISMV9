# PCE for Rapid Uncertainty Quantification

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.76` · **Source:** `web:powermill-forum`

## Tip

Polynomial Chaos Expansion approximates distributions via Hermite polynomials. Converges in 100 samples vs 10,000 for Monte Carlo on smooth responses. Build surrogate from initial DOE, then explore cheaply. Use PCE to quickly estimate P(Ra > spec) for different PowerMill parameter combinations without thousands of physical trials.
