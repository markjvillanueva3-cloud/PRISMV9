# Aluminum Machining — High Speed with Large Chip Load

Aluminum (6061, 7075) allows aggressive machining: Vc 300-1000 m/min, fz 0.1-0.3mm/tooth, ap up to 2x diameter. Use 2-3 flute uncoated carbide or polished DLC-coated tools to prevent built-up edge. Climb milling is essential — conventional milling welds aluminum to the cutting edge. Set minimum chip thickness > 0.02mm to avoid rubbing. For VoluMill in aluminum, axial depth = 3-4x diameter with 10-15% radial engagement yields maximum MRR. Use flood coolant or MQL (not dry) to prevent chip welding.
