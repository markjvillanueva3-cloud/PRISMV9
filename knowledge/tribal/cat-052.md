# Material Removal Simulation Video Mode vs Photo Mode

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:catia-docs`

## Tip

CATIA offers two material removal simulation modes: Video mode (real-time animated) and Photo mode (static comparison). Video mode shows the tool cutting through stock in real time and is best for verifying approach/retract moves and finding rapid-into-material errors. Photo mode overlays the final machined shape against the design part, highlighting remaining stock (positive deviation) and gouges (negative deviation) with color maps. Run Video first to catch crash-level errors, then Photo for dimensional verification.
