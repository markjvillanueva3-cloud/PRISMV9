# SURFCAM Wire EDM Slug Retention with Micro-Joint Tabs

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `process_engineering`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:surfcam-docs`

## Tip

SURFCAM's wire EDM slug retention feature inserts micro-joint tabs that prevent the cut slug from falling into the tank and potentially shorting the wire. Place tabs at the top of vertical cuts or at 120° spacing for circular cutouts. Tab width should be 0.2-0.5mm — wide enough to hold the slug but narrow enough to remove easily. For heavy slugs (>0.5kg), use 3-4 tabs. After the main cut, return to each tab location and cut through at reduced power. Program a 0.1mm overlap at each tab re-entry point.
