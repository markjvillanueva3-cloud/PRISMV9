# Surface Extension for Forming Dies

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:siemens-nx-docs`

## Tip

Extend 2-5mm for clean exit. Prevents deceleration marks and corner deviation. For aerospace forming dies: edge quality affects formed part. Extension mandatory on Class-A tooling. NX creates extensions automatically.
