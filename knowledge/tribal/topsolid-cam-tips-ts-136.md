# TopSolid'Design Sheet Metal — Flat Pattern to CNC Laser/Punch

TopSolid'Design's sheet metal module creates bend-aware models with automatic flat pattern generation. The flat pattern includes bend allowance calculations (K-factor, bend deduction, or bend table) and exports directly to CNC laser/punch programming. Bend relief cuts, corner treatments, and formed features are handled parametrically. For combined sheet metal and machined parts (brackets with precision bores), design in TopSolid'Design and program both the laser cutting and post-bend machining in TopSolid'Cam.
