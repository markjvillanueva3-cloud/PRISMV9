# SURFCAM Port Machining for Complex Internal Channels

SURFCAM's multi-axis port machining strategy is designed for machining internal ports and channels in valve bodies, manifolds, and engine components. The tool follows the port centerline while the 5-axis motion maintains optimal cutting geometry throughout the channel. Define the port as a sweep along a guide curve with a cross-section profile. For intersecting ports, use rest machining to clean up the intersection blend. Limit tool extension to 4xD to minimize deflection inside deep ports.
