# Progressive Thickness for Ti-6Al-4V

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:siemens-nx-docs`

## Tip

0.3→0.15→0.05→0.0mm. Each removes its layer. 40-60% longer life. IPW tracks remaining stock. Spring-back on thin walls may require offset on final pass. NX can apply spring-back compensation based on predicted residual stress.

- Material groups: `S`
