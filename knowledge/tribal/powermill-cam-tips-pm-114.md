# AMSAA Reliability Growth Tracking

Plot cumulative failures vs production hours. Power law decrease indicates program maturing. Stalls/reversals: investigate tool vendor change, material batch, machine maintenance. Mature PowerMill programs achieve <0.5% scrap after 50+ cycles. Track roughing and finishing failures separately — different root causes and improvement trajectories.
