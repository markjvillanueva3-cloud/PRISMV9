# Automatic Strategy Generation from Part Analysis

**Category:** `automation` · **Subcategory:** `part_catcher` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `82` · **Source:** `web:fusion360-docs`

## Tip

The Manufacturing Extension's whole-part strategy generation analyzes your model and creates a complete roughing-to-finishing operation sequence automatically. It identifies steep walls, shallow floors, pockets, holes, and freeform surfaces, then assigns appropriate strategies (Adaptive, Contour, Parallel, Pencil, Drill). Review the generated sequence carefully — it may over-segment simple parts into too many operations or miss custom fixturing constraints. Use it as a 70% starting point and refine the remaining 30% manually.
