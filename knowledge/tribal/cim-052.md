# Swarf Cutting for Draft Walls in Mold Cavities

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Cimatron swarf cutting uses the tool's flute length to machine ruled surfaces in a single pass. Define the drive surface (wall) and check surface (floor). Swarf cutting is 5-10× faster than Z-level for draft walls. Verify the surface is truly developable — swarf on doubly-curved surfaces causes gouging. Set tool tilt limits to ±3° from surface normal for smooth finish quality.
