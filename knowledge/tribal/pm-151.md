# Impeller Plunge Roughing Between Blades

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `contour` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Plunge roughing between impeller blades removes bulk material safely. Axial forces push into the hub (strongest direction). Step-over 50-60% of tool diameter. After plunge roughing, switch to 5-axis contour roughing for passage finishing. PowerMill's blade module manages the transition between plunge and contour operations automatically.
