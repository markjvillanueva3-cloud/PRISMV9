# Parallel Lace Cutting for Large Flat Regions

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:esprit-3d-machining`

## Tip

For large shallow or flat areas, ESPRIT's parallel lace cutting generates back-and-forth toolpaths with minimal retracts. Use a flat-bottom or bull-nose cutter instead of a ball-nose for better surface finish and higher material removal. Set stepover to 60-75% of the effective cutting width for roughing or calculate from cusp height for finishing. Enable 'lead-in/lead-out arcs' at path boundaries to prevent witness marks.
