# Hardened Steel (>45 HRC) with Light Passes and Dry Cutting

**Category:** `material_specific` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:bobcad-hard-milling`

## Tip

For steels above 45 HRC, use light axial depths (0.1-0.3mm) at high surface speed (150-300 m/min CBN, 200-500 m/min ceramic). Adaptive Roughing maintains the consistent light engagement needed — any spike risks catastrophic tool failure. Feed: 0.02-0.05mm/tooth for ball-nose finishing. Avoid coolant — thermal shock causes CBN/ceramic micro-fractures. Use air blast only. BobCAD's feed optimization is essential for hard milling to prevent overload at transitions.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `H`
- Operation types: `finishing`, `roughing`
