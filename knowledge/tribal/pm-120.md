# Cast Iron Dry Machining Strategy

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Gray/ductile cast iron: dry or air blast. Chips are short, brittle. Uncoated or TiN carbide, 150-250 m/min. DOC 1.5-2×D. Standard offset roughing works — no constant-engagement needed. Coolant reduces tool life via thermal shock. PowerMill programs for cast iron are simpler than steel — focus on efficient chip evacuation and rapid move optimization.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `K`
- Operation types: `roughing`
