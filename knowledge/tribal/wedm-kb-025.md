# Workpiece leveling: tram to <0.01mm across full length

**Category:** `setup` · **Domain:** `general`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `handbook:sodick_operation_manual`

## Tip

Before wire cutting, tram the workpiece surface to within 0.01mm across its full length using a dial indicator on the machine's edge finder. An un-level workpiece means the wire path is not perpendicular to the surface — the cut will have an unintended taper. For parts longer than 200mm, check at 3+ points. Magnetic chucks can warp thin parts — use parallels underneath to support the span.
