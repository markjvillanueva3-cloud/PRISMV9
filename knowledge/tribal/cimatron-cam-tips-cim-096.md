# Vericut Integration for Independent Verification

Export Cimatron toolpaths to Vericut for independent force-based verification. Vericut catches excessive cutting forces that geometric simulation misses. Compare cycle time estimates — differences >10% indicate feed optimization opportunities. Use Vericut for final sign-off on complex 5-axis programs before first article. Required by many aerospace and automotive OEMs.
