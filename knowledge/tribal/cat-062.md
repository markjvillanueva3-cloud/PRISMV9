# Process Templates Capture Best-Practice Operation Sequences

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:catia-docs`

## Tip

CATIA Process Templates capture complete machining sequences (operations, tools, feeds/speeds, strategies) as reusable templates. Create templates for common part families (e.g., 'pocket with boss', 'flanged bore'). When applied to a new part, the template automatically maps operations to the corresponding geometric features. Store templates in a shared catalog with descriptive names and revision numbers. Templates reduce programming time by 50-70% for repeat part families.
