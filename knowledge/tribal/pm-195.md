# Probing for Stock Verification

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Probe rough stock before semi-finishing to prevent overload. Verify WCS at each setup. Check dims between operations. Output for Renishaw/Heidenhain/Blum. Store for SPC. PowerMill macros automate probe-measure-correct-continue loops at timed intervals.
