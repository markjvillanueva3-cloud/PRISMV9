# ANN+GA optimization achieves 39.37% Ra improvement on AISI 1020

**Category:** `ai_ml` · **Domain:** `process_engineering`

## Tip

Hybrid Artificial Neural Network with Genetic Algorithm (ANN+GA) optimization of wire-cut EDM parameters on AISI 1020 mild steel achieves 39.37% improvement in surface roughness (Ra). Key optimized parameters: ON time, OFF time, peak current, and wire feed rate. The ANN model predicts outcomes while GA searches the parameter space for optimal combinations.
