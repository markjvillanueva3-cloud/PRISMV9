# Dynamic Mill Rest Mill strategy targets material left by previous operations

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `93` · **Source:** `document:mastercam-dynamic-milling`

## Tip

Dynamic Mill 'Rest Mill' strategy specifically targets material left from previous roughing operations. Uses dynamic motion to rapidly remove corner material and narrow areas the larger roughing tool could not reach. Specify the previous tool diameter or reference previous operation — Mastercam calculates remaining stock automatically. Always run after main roughing, before finishing passes.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `rest_machining`
