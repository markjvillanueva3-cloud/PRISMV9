# Taguchi Robust Design for Stable Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.78` · **Source:** `web:powermill-forum`

## Tip

Apply Taguchi L9 array: factors = speed (3 levels), feed (3 levels), step-over (3 levels). Noise factors = material hardness variation (±2 HRC), tool wear state (new/mid/end). Measure S/N ratio for surface roughness. The Taguchi-optimal parameters maximize the signal-to-noise ratio, meaning the surface finish is least sensitive to uncontrollable noise factors. These robust parameters outperform 'optimized' parameters that only work under ideal conditions.
