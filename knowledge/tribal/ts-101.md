# Inconel Machining with Ceramic and Carbide Strategies

**Category:** `material` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-inconel`

## Tip

For Inconel alloys (718, 625, X-750) in TopSolid, use two distinct approaches: ceramic roughing at 200-400 m/min with round inserts and 0.5-1 mm depth of cut, or carbide roughing at 15-30 m/min with 6-8% engagement adaptive paths. Ceramic cutting generates extreme heat that softens the workpiece ahead of the cut—this is desirable and should not be suppressed with coolant (use air blast). Carbide cutting uses flood coolant at maximum pressure. Never stop the tool in-cut with either approach.

- Material groups: `S`
- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
