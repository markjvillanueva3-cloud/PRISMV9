# OptiRough does the work of six roughing toolpaths in one

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `contour` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:mastercam-docs`

## Tip

Mastercam OptiRough is the only 3D toolpath using Dynamic Motion technology. A single OptiRough toolpath cuts both on stepdowns (-Z) and stepups (+Z), removing maximum material with minimum passes. It replaces multiple Area Rough, Pocket, and Contour roughing operations. One customer reported going from 1 part per endmill to 45 parts per endmill after switching to OptiRough.
