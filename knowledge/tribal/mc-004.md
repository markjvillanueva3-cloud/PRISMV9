# Radial Chip Thinning Factor compensates feed for light stepovers

**Category:** `speeds_feeds` · **Subcategory:** `chip_load` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:community`

## Tip

When using Dynamic toolpaths with low radial engagement (5-10% Dc), enable Radial Chip Thinning in Mastercam to automatically increase the programmed feedrate. At 10% stepover the actual chip thickness is much less than the programmed feed-per-tooth, so RCTF compensates by boosting IPM to maintain the target chip load. Without RCTF, you are running far below optimal feed and wasting cycle time.

- Operation types: `roughing`
