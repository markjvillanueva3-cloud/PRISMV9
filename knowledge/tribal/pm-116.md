# Predictive Maintenance from Cycle Trends

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.79` · **Source:** `web:powermill-forum`

## Tip

Track cycle time across runs. 5-10% increase over 50 parts: bearing wear, backlash growth, or guideway wear. PowerMill estimated vs actual gap is the indicator. Schedule maintenance before accuracy degrades. Cross-reference with machine vibration data for root cause. Proactive bearing replacement at 80% rated life prevents catastrophic spindle failure.
