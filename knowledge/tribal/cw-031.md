# VoluMill for Titanium — Aggressive Parameters with Tool Protection

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) benefits enormously from VoluMill's constant engagement. Use axial depth 1.5-2x diameter, radial engagement 8-12% of diameter, and surface speed 50-80 m/min with coated carbide. The key is consistent chip load — VoluMill's algorithm prevents the engagement spikes that cause work hardening and premature tool failure in titanium. Enable through-tool coolant and set minimum chipload to 0.06mm/tooth to avoid rubbing, which accelerates flank wear in Ti alloys.
