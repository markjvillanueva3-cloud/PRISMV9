# Grooving and Cut-Off Chip Control

Grooving: 50-70% of turning feed, pecking for widths >3mm. Cut-off: synchronize bar feed with sub-spindle catch. 0.5s dwell at final diameter for clean separation. SprutCAM programs G-code peck and dwell sequences. Chip length control prevents jam in chip conveyor.
