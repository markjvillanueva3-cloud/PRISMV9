# Manufacturing Instruction Templates for Standardization

**Category:** `automation` · **Subcategory:** `part_catcher` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:edgecam-docs`

## Tip

Create Manufacturing Instruction (MI) templates in Edgecam Strategy Manager to standardize machining processes. An MI template captures the complete sequence: feature recognition rules, operation order, tools, cutting parameters, and strategies. Apply the MI to new parts by dragging onto the feature tree — Edgecam maps template operations to recognized features. Store MI templates on a shared server location for team access and version control.
