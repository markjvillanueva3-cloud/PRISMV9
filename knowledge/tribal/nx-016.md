# Multi-Axis Deburring Operation

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `78` · **Source:** `web:siemens-community`

## Tip

NX provides a dedicated multi-axis deburring operation that follows sharp edges on the IPW with a chamfer or ball-nose tool in 5-axis simultaneous mode. Define the deburring edges on the IPW directly rather than on the part model. This catches real-world burr locations that only exist after previous machining operations.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `deburring`, `5-axis`
