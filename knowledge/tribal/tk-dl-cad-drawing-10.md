# Always include projection symbol and general tolerance block

**Category:** `quality` · **Subcategory:** `first_article` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `document:cad_drawing_standards@section6`

## Tip

Two items that MUST appear on every technical drawing: (1) Projection symbol — third-angle (ASME/North America) or first-angle (ISO/Europe) — to prevent view misinterpretation. (2) General tolerance block (e.g., ISO 2768-mK) covering all undimensioned features. Without these, the shop floor has to guess orientation and tolerance class, leading to scrap.
