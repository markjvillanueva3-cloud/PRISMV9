# Additive DED for Mold Repair

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `81` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-docs`

## Tip

hyperMILL Additive: DED contour+fill, 30-50% overlap, 60-80% layer. Interleave machining every 3-5 layers. For worn mold surfaces, damaged edges, conformal cooling additions. Reduces lead time vs new mold from 8 to 2 weeks.
