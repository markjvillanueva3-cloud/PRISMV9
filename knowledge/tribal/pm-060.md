# Taper Ball Nose for Draft Wall Finishing

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Taper ball nose cutters combine a ball tip with a tapered shank for improved rigidity on deep cavity walls. In PowerMill, define the ball radius and taper half-angle precisely. Use for Z-level finishing on draft walls — the taper provides a larger effective cutting zone while the ball tip handles floor transitions. Verify that the taper angle matches the wall draft angle to prevent interference.
