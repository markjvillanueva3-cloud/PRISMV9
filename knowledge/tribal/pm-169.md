# Gutowski Energy Benchmarking

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.76` · **Source:** `web:powermill-forum`

## Tip

P = P₀ + k×MRR. e = P₀/MRR + k. At low MRR, idle power dominates. Maximize MRR within limits for efficiency. Benchmark PowerMill programs by comparing specific energy across strategies. Vortex at high MRR always more energy-efficient per unit removed.
