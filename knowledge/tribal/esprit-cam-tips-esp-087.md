# Knowledge-Based Machining (KBM) Automates Decisions

ESPRIT's Knowledge-Based Machining (KBM) system captures expert programming decisions as rules: 'IF feature = through-hole AND diameter < 20mm AND material = steel THEN use carbide drill + peck cycle with 1x diameter peck depth'. Build KBM rules progressively from your best programmers' decisions. Once mature, KBM can automatically program 60-80% of standard features, freeing expert programmers for complex multi-axis work.
