# Titanium Machining — Controlled Engagement with Through-Tool Coolant

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) requires: Vc 40-80 m/min (carbide), fz 0.06-0.12mm/tooth, ap 1-2x diameter. Use 4-5 flute variable-helix carbide with AlTiN coating. Through-tool coolant at 40+ bar is essential — titanium's low thermal conductivity means heat concentrates at the cutting edge. Never let the tool dwell in the cut (causes work hardening). VoluMill's constant-engagement is ideal for titanium — engagement spikes that are tolerable in steel cause immediate edge failure in Ti. Avoid recuts at all costs.

- Material groups: `P`, `S`
