# iMachining Cast Iron — Level 5-6 with Dry Cutting and Dust Extraction

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

Gray cast iron (FC250, GG25) and ductile iron (FCD600, GGG60) respond well to iMachining Levels 5-6 with cutting speeds of 150-250 m/min using TiCN or Al2O3-coated inserts. Cast iron produces discontinuous chips that break easily, allowing aggressive parameters. Critical: machine dry — coolant on cast iron creates abrasive slurry that accelerates flank wear. Install dust extraction at the machine enclosure since graphite dust from gray iron is a health and machine maintenance concern. The Technology Wizard optimizes engagement for cast iron's brittle fracture behavior, preventing edge chipping on interrupted cuts through porosity and hard spots.
