# SPC Integration for Mold Shop Quality

Integrate CMM measurement results with SPC charts for critical mold dimensions. Track: cavity depth, pin hole positions, parting line flatness, and guide pin bore diameter. Set control limits at ±2σ from process mean. When a trend is detected (7 consecutive points on one side of centerline), re-qualify the machining process — likely causes: tool wear, fixture shift, or thermal drift.
