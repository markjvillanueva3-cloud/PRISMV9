# hyperMILL Z-Level Finishing creates horizontal slice passes for steep walls

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `92` · **Source:** `document:hypermill-manual`

## Tip

hyperMILL Z Level Finishing creates constant-Z passes down steep walls and vertical surfaces. Parameters: Z-step (typically 0.1-0.5mm for finishing), limit angle (cuts only surfaces steeper than angle), smoothing (blend between Z passes). Combine with Complete Finishing for shallow areas. For large parts, split Z-level into multiple depth ranges for better tool control and easier restart after tool change.
