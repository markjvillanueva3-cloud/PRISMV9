# Pencil Milling for Corner Cleanup

After Z-level or raster finishing, use Pencil Milling to clean up internal corners and fillets that larger tools can't reach. Set 'Corner Detection' to 'Automatic' and use a ball-end mill 50-70% the radius of the smallest fillet. Enable 'Both Ways' cutting for shorter cycle times on symmetric corners.
