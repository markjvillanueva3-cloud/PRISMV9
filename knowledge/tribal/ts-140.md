# TopSolid'Design Bill of Materials — CAD-Driven Manufacturing Planning

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-docs`

## Tip

TopSolid'Design generates structured BOMs with material specifications, raw stock requirements, and purchased component lists. The BOM links to TopSolid'Cam for automatic material assignment — the CAM system inherits the material grade and heat treatment state from the design BOM. For production planning, export the BOM to ERP systems (SAP, Oracle) with material requirements and estimated cycle times from TopSolid'Cam. This closed-loop integration ensures material procurement matches the actual machining requirements.
