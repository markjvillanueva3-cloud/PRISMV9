# Stainless Steel Work-Hardening Prevention

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

304/316 stainless: coated carbide, 80-120 m/min, 0.08-0.12mm fz, 0.5-1×D DOC. Never dwell or feed below 0.04mm/tooth. Climb milling exclusively. PowerMill constant chip load roughing prevents intermittent engagement that causes work-hardening. Flood coolant mandatory. Adjust tool life down 30% vs standard steel. Use high-helix geometry for better chip evacuation.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `M`
- Operation types: `roughing`
