# hyperMILL approach/retract macros add tangential or circular entry for smooth cutting

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `document:hypermill-manual`

## Tip

hyperMILL approach/retract macros define tool movement from clearance zone to cutting zone. Options: Radial (straight line), Tangential (smooth tangent entry), Circular (arc entry with defined radius/angle), Ramp (angled descent). Tangential and circular entries reduce tool shock at cut start. Ramp angle references turning axis accounting for infeed direction. Always verify macro geometry doesn't collide with fixtures.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `turning`, `milling`
