# Standard Workflow Macros for Consistent Shop Practices

**Category:** `automation` · **Subcategory:** `tool_presetter` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:surfcam-workflow`

## Tip

Create SURFCAM workflow macros that enforce your shop's standard practices: tool naming conventions, mandatory operations (spot drill before drill, deburr after pocket), stock allowance standards, and tolerance requirements. Macros can prompt the programmer for key inputs (material, tolerance class, batch size) and auto-configure the remaining parameters. This ensures consistency across programmers and reduces errors from manual parameter entry.
