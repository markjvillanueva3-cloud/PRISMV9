# Cycle-to-cycle (CtC) control: check stability with K < 1

**Category:** `quality` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `93` · **Source:** `document:mit2830j@lecture20`

## Tip

In cycle-to-cycle manufacturing control, the loop gain K = Kc × Kp must satisfy |K| < 1 for stability. K_p is process gain (often ≈1), K_c is controller gain. Start with Kc = 0.3-0.5 and tune up. Going above K=1 causes oscillation that INCREASES variance instead of reducing it. The optimal gain minimizes σ²_output = σ²_d × (1-K)² / (1-(1-K)²).
