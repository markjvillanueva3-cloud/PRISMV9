# FMEA Integration with SolidCAM Operations

Per operation: failure modes (tool break, drift, defects), effects (scrap, rework), RPN=S×O×D. iMachining reduces occurrence ratings for force-related failures (tool breakage, deflection errors) by 50-70%. Re-score FMEA after switching to iMachining — many previously high-RPN operations drop to acceptable levels.
