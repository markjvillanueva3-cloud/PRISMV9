# Adaptive Machining for Cast/Forged Blanks

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `adaptive` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:siemens-nx-docs`

## Tip

NX scans actual blank via probing/CMM, adjusts toolpaths to real stock. Eliminates air cutting on near-net-shape. 30-50% roughing cycle reduction on investment castings. Essential for titanium forgings where buy-to-fly ratio makes every cut expensive.
