# Hertzian Contact Stress for Ball-End Tool Design

**Category:** `optimization` · **Subcategory:** `tool_path` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `78` · **Source:** `web:tebis-forum`

## Tip

Ball-end mill contact follows Hertzian theory: σ_max ∝ (F/R²)^(1/3) where F = normal force, R = ball radius. Larger ball radius reduces contact stress and wear rate. For hardened steel finishing, minimum ball diameter = 2× step-over / sin(max_slope_angle). This prevents the high contact stress at the tool tip that causes premature CBN insert failure on hard mold steel.
