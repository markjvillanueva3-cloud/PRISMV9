# Turning Drill Operations — Center, Peck, and Deep Hole on Lathe

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `drilling` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

CAMWorks turning supports center drilling, peck drilling, deep hole drilling, reaming, and tapping on the lathe spindle axis. Always start with a center drill or spot drill to establish a pilot for subsequent drills. For deep holes (L/D > 5), use peck drilling with peck depth starting at 1x diameter and reducing to 0.5x diameter as depth increases. For L/D > 10, switch to gun drilling with through-tool coolant pressure > 40 bar for effective chip evacuation.
