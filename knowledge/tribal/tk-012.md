# Safety: never reach into running machine

**Category:** `safety` · **Subcategory:** `chip_handling` · **Domain:** `safety`

**Confidence:** `100` · **Source:** `safety:incident_review`

## Tip

NEVER reach into the work zone while spindle is rotating, even at low RPM. Use the chip hook tool to clear chips. Two machinists in this shop have lost fingertips from 'just brushing away chips.' The machine does not care about your deadline.
