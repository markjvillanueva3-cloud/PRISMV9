# Aluminum Mold Roughing for Prototype Tools

For aluminum prototype molds (6061/7075), use aggressive roughing: 3-flute uncoated carbide, 50% radial engagement, 1.5×D axial depth, 12,000+ RPM. Cimatron's adaptive roughing maintains constant MRR. Chip evacuation is critical — use flood coolant or air blast with chip auger. Surface speeds can reach 300-500 m/min. Stock allowance 0.15mm for finishing pass.
