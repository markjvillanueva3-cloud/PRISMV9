# Predictive Maintenance from Cycle Time Trend Data

**Category:** `optimization` · **Subcategory:** `cycle_time` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `79` · **Source:** `web:tebis-forum`

## Tip

Track cycle time trend across production runs. Gradual increase (5-10% over 50 parts) indicates: spindle bearing wear (increased settling), ball screw backlash growth (more compensation moves), or guideway wear (slower acceleration). Cross-reference with machine maintenance logs. Tebis estimated vs actual cycle time gap is the key indicator for scheduling preventive maintenance.
