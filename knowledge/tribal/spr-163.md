# Copula for Dependent Failure Modes

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.75` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

Flank/crater/chipping correlated. Gaussian copula models joint distribution. Ignoring dependence underestimates 15-25%. For SprutCAM turning: flank wear correlates with notch wear at depth-of-cut line. Joint modeling prevents over-optimistic tool change intervals.
