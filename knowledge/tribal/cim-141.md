# Morris Screening for Many-Factor Problems

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.76` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-forum`

## Tip

Morris elementary effects efficiently screens 10+ factors. Compute μ* (mean absolute effect) and σ for each. High μ* = important. High σ = interactions/nonlinearity. Screen with Morris, then do detailed DOE on top 3-4 factors. For Cimatron with many adjustable parameters (speed, feed, DOC, step-over, tolerance, lead/lean, etc.), Morris identifies which actually matter.
