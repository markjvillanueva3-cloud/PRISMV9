# Taper barrel cutters combine wall finishing and floor blending in a single tool

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `pencil` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:mastercam-docs`

## Tip

Taper barrel cutters have a tapered shank section with a barrel-profile cutting edge, designed to finish steep walls at an angle. In Mastercam's Accelerated Finishing, the taper barrel tool type engages steep surfaces (60–90°) with the barrel profile while the taper provides clearance. This eliminates the need for separate wall-finishing and floor-blending operations. The tilt angle at the side controls how much of the barrel profile contacts the wall — adjust this between 3–8° based on wall curvature. Taper barrel cutters are particularly effective on mold core side walls where a single pass replaces the traditional sequence of parallel finishing followed by pencil cleanup. Cycle time reduction of 50–70% is typical on tall-wall mold components.
