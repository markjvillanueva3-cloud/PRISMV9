# Steep and Shallow Threshold Angle Fine-Tuning

The default 45-degree threshold angle in Steep and Shallow is a starting point, not an answer. For ball nose cutters, lower the threshold to 30-35 degrees so more area is machined with contour passes, which produce better finish on walls. For bull nose tools with larger corner radii, raise it to 50-55 degrees because the flat bottom handles shallow areas more effectively. Test on a sample part — the transition zone between strategies is where surface quality issues appear.
