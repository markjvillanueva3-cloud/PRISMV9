# Equidistant Finishing for best HSM surface quality

**Category:** `surface_finish` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `document:hypermill-manual-en-4@p915`

## Tip

hyperMILL Equidistant Finishing provides constant infeed on the surface, making it particularly suitable for high-speed milling. It machines equidistantly within a closed guide curve or flowing between two guide curves. The constant surface step ensures uniform scallop height across freeform surfaces.
