# Inconel Machining — Low Speed, High Pressure, Short Engagements

Inconel (718, 625) is among the most difficult materials to machine. Use Vc 20-40 m/min (carbide), fz 0.04-0.08mm/tooth, ap 0.5-1.5x diameter. Ceramic inserts enable Vc 200+ m/min but require extreme rigidity and absolutely no interrupted cuts. Through-tool coolant at 70+ bar is critical. VoluMill is the preferred roughing strategy — constant engagement prevents the catastrophic work hardening that occurs when engagement varies. Replace tools at 50% of normal wear life — Inconel causes sudden catastrophic failure with little warning.
