# Composite Machining with Compression Routers and Dust Control

CFRP and GFRP composites require diamond-coated or PCD tools with compression geometry (up-cut on bottom, down-cut on top) to prevent delamination. Set cutting speed to 200-500 m/min with 0.05-0.1mm/tooth feed. Avoid conventional end mills — they delaminate the top or bottom ply depending on helix direction. Use SURFCAM's 'Stock allowance = 0' for net-shape cutting (no finish pass needed). Require dust extraction (not coolant) — water-soluble coolants damage some resin systems.
