# Thermal Compensation for Long Operations

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `81` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-forum`

## Tip

Roughing >3h: Z-drift 0.01-0.03mm. Probe every 90min. Schedule finishing during stable thermal windows. hyperMILL VIRTUAL Machining can insert probe macro calls at operation boundaries. MAXX Finishing's shorter cycle times reduce total thermal exposure — fewer compensation cycles needed.
