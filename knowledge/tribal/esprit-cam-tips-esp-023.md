# ProfitTurning Dynamic Roughing Maintains Constant Chip Load

ESPRIT ProfitTurning applies the same constant-engagement philosophy as ProfitMilling to lathe roughing. Instead of conventional box-cut passes that cause engagement spikes in corners and shoulders, ProfitTurning dynamically adjusts the depth of cut to maintain consistent chip load throughout the pass. This enables 30-50% higher feed rates and 2-3x deeper cuts compared to conventional turning, while extending insert life by up to 300%.
