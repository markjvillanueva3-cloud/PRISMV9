# Resolution III designs alias main effects with 2-factor interactions

**Category:** `quality` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `document:mit2830j@lecture14`

## Tip

In fractional factorial designs, Resolution III (like 2^{3-1}) aliases each main effect with a 2-factor interaction. This means if A is aliased with BC, you can't tell if the effect is from A alone or from the BC interaction. Resolution IV aliases 2-factor interactions with each other (main effects are clear). Always use Resolution IV or higher for important screening experiments.
