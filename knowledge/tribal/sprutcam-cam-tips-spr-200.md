# Sensitivity Ranking for Turning Optimization

Vary ±10%: nose radius (30% of Ra), feed (25%), speed (20%), DOC (15%), bar runout (10%). For turning, nose radius is #1 for finish — different from milling where step-over dominates. Focus turning optimization on TNR selection and feed rate. SprutCAM stores optimal combos per material.
