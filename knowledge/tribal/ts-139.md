# TopSolid'Design Surface Modeling — Complex Shapes for Mold and Die

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `process_engineering`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-docs`

## Tip

TopSolid'Design includes advanced surface modeling (loft, sweep, blend, N-sided patch, filling surface) for mold and die applications. Surfaces created in TopSolid'Design transfer to TopSolid'Cam with full G2/G3 continuity preserved — no faceting or degradation. For mold parting surfaces, use the 'parting line' tool to automatically generate shut-off surfaces between core and cavity. These surfaces define the machining boundaries in TopSolid'Cam for electrode and mold insert programming.
