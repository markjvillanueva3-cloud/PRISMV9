# Vericut Integration for Independent Verification

**Category:** `setup` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:tebis-docs`

## Tip

Export Tebis toolpaths to Vericut for independent verification. Vericut force-based simulation catches excessive cutting forces that geometric simulation misses. Compare Vericut estimated cycle time against Tebis — differences >10% indicate feed rate optimization opportunities. Use Vericut for final sign-off on complex 5-axis mold programs before first-article production.
