# Facing with Wiper Inserts

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

65-75% step-over. One-way. Wiper for mirror finish. Interrupted faces: -20% feed. Target ±0.01mm flatness. SolidCAM facing operation. iMachining doesn't handle facing — use dedicated operation for top surface preparation before iMachining pockets.

- Operation types: `roughing`
