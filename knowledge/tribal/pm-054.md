# Toolpath Templates for Standardized Operations

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.88` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Create toolpath templates that capture: strategy selection, cutting parameters, leads/links, tool definition, and boundary setup. Apply templates to similar features across different parts — PowerMill remaps geometry references automatically. Templates enforce shop standards and reduce programming time by 50-70% for repeat geometry types. Version-control templates to track process improvements.
