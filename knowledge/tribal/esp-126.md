# ESPRIT Edge Machine Learning Toolpath Suggestions

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `pocket` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.76` · **Source:** `web:esprit-docs`

## Tip

ESPRIT Edge's AI Assistant analyzes your programming patterns across hundreds of completed jobs and suggests operation parameters for new features. When you create a new pocket operation, Edge compares the feature geometry (depth, width, corner radii, material) against historical jobs and proposes tool selection, cutting parameters, and strategy (ProfitMilling vs. conventional). Acceptance rate improves over time as the model trains on your shop's specific machines and tooling inventory.
