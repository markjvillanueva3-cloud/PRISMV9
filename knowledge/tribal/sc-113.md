# Solid Probe Surface Inspection — Mid-Process Quality Gate

**Category:** `quality` · **Subcategory:** `inspection` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

Insert Solid Probe surface inspection cycles between roughing and finishing operations to verify stock allowance before committing to the finishing pass. Probe 5-10 points on critical surfaces and compare to the nominal model. If measured stock exceeds the expected value by more than 0.1mm (indicating tool deflection or wear during roughing), the probe cycle can trigger an alarm or automatic tool offset update. This prevents the finishing tool from encountering unexpected excess material that causes tool breakage or poor surface finish.

- Operation types: `probing`, `inspection`
