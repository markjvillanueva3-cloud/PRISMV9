# Shut-Off Surface Machining for Interlocking Mold Features

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-shutoff`

## Tip

Shut-off surfaces are interlocking faces where core and cavity meet to form through-holes or windows in the molded part. TopSolid identifies shut-off regions and generates precision machining operations with tight tolerances (0.01-0.02 mm) to ensure positive steel-to-steel contact. Use Z-level finishing with very fine stepdown (0.02-0.05 mm) for shut-off surfaces. The shut-off angle should be a minimum of 3-5° for reliable mold opening; shallower angles cause galling.
