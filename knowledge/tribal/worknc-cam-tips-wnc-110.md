# Knowledge Capture Preserves Expert Decisions

WorkNC's process templates capture experienced programmer decision rules: material/feature combinations mapped to strategies, tools, and parameters. For example: 'For hardened steel pockets deeper than 3xD, use waveform roughing at 8% engagement with CBN-coated carbide.' Sharing these templates across the team preserves tribal knowledge and raises the baseline skill level.
