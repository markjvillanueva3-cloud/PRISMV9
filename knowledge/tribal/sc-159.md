# AMSAA Reliability Growth for Program Maturity

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `77` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-forum`

## Tip

Cumulative failures vs production hours. Power law decrease = maturing. iMachining programs mature faster (fewer tool-related failures from the start). Mature SolidCAM programs: <0.5% scrap after 50+ cycles. Compare iMachining maturity curve vs conventional — iMachining typically reaches maturity in 20-30 cycles vs 50+ for conventional.
