# Additive DED Layer Strategy Optimization

Optimize DED build: contour-first then fill (better dimensional accuracy) vs fill-first then contour (faster). Layer height 60-80% of bead height. Overlap 30-50%. Interleave machining every 3-5 layers. PowerMill Additive simulates thermal accumulation to predict distortion.
