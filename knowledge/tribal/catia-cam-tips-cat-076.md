# DELMIA Machining Integration for Shop Floor Connectivity

DELMIA on the 3DEXPERIENCE platform extends CATIA machining with shop-floor connectivity, including DNC (Direct Numerical Control) transfer, machine monitoring, and production scheduling. The CAM programmer creates the Manufacturing Program in CATIA/DELMIA, and the production planner assigns it to physical machines with scheduling constraints. NC programs are version-controlled on the platform, ensuring the machine always receives the latest approved revision. Enable change notifications so operators are alerted when programs are updated.
