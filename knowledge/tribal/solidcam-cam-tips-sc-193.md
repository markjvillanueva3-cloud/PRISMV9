# Chamfering Operations

Chamfer mills (45/60/90°), ball-end, spot drills. SolidCAM inherits edge information from SolidWorks model. 3D chamfers: 5-axis normal. 0.3-0.5mm standard on all edges. Verify width. iMachining doesn't handle chamfers — use dedicated chamfer operation.
