# Turbo HSM Constant-Z — Steep Wall Finishing to 0.4 µm

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

Turbo HSM Constant-Z strategy produces exceptional surface quality on steep walls, achieving Ra 0.4 µm with proper parameters. It smooths both cutting moves and retracts to maintain continuous machine motion — critical for keeping high feed rates and eliminating dwell marks. Use Constant-Z for walls steeper than 45° and switch to Linear or Constant Stepover for shallow areas.
