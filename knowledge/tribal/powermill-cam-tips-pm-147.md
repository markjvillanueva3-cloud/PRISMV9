# Geodesic Finishing for Complex Freeform

Geodesic paths follow shortest distance along the surface, producing uniform coverage regardless of parameterization. Better than raster on doubly-curved surfaces. Set step-over based on scallop height. Computation-intensive — plan extra time for complex surfaces. PowerMill geodesic is ideal for optical surfaces where uniform finish quality is critical.
