# Hotelling T² for Multivariate SPC

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.77` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

Correlated dims need multivariate SPC. T²=(x-μ)ᵀS⁻¹(x-μ). MYT decomposition finds offending dim. Prevents false alarm explosion from multiple univariate charts. For turned parts with 10+ critical dims, Hotelling T² is far more reliable than running 10 separate X-bar charts.
