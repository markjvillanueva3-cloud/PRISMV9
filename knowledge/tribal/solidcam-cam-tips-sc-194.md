# Spiral Finishing for Flat Surfaces

Continuous spiral eliminates witness marks. Step-over per scallop. Climb. Auto center-start. For shutoff faces and flat pockets. Combine with iMachining roughing and HSM rest-finishing for complete workflow. Best for visible flat surfaces.
