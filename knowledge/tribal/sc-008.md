# iMachining 3D Step-Up Rest Roughing — Clean Slopes Efficiently

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

After deep roughing, iMachining 3D switches to step-up mode to remove rest material on sloped surfaces. The axial depth gets smaller as it machines higher steps, and the wizard automatically increases feed rate and cutting angle to maintain constant tool load. Use the Scallop parameter to control how much rest material triggers an additional pass on slopes.

- Operation types: `3d_roughing`, `rest_machining`
