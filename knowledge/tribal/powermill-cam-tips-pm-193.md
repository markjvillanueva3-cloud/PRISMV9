# Facing for Parting Surface Flatness

65-75% step-over with face mill. One-way. Wiper inserts for mirror finish. Interrupted: -20% feed at entry. Target ±0.01mm across full plate. PowerMill facing handles irregular stock and avoids clamping automatically. Critical for mold shut-off quality.
