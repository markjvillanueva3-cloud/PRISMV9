# Cleanup Finishing Blends Strategy Transitions

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:worknc-cleanup`

## Tip

After combining steep/shallow finishing, WorkNC's cleanup finishing removes residual cusps at strategy transition boundaries. The operation detects boundary regions where two strategies meet and generates additional tight-stepover passes (50% of normal) to blend the surfaces. Set detection width to 2-3x the finishing stepover for complete coverage of transition zones.
