# Deep Hole Drilling with Gun Drill Strategies

WorkNC supports gun drilling strategies for holes with L/D ratios exceeding 10:1. Program pilot holes first (2-3x diameter depth with twist drill), then switch to gun drill with through-tool coolant at 50-70 bar. Set the feed rate to 0.005-0.02 mm/rev and use constant RPM mode. Monitor coolant pressure and chip formation during the first article to verify proper chip evacuation.
