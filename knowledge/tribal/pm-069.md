# Electrode Machining Workflow in PowerMill

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `process_engineering`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Machine graphite electrodes with: uncoated carbide, 2-flute, 15,000-24,000 RPM, 0.05-0.08mm feed/tooth. No coolant — use vacuum dust extraction. Roughing: offset area clear with 0.1mm stock allowance. Finishing: 3D offset or raster with 0.02mm step-over. Apply different undersizes for roughing electrodes (0.3mm/side) vs finishing electrodes (0.05mm/side). Program datum pads for CMM qualification.
