# Fine-Tune Optimal Load by Material Hardness

For hardened steels (>45 HRC), reduce the Optimal Load to 8-15% of tool diameter to keep radial engagement forces manageable on carbide end mills. For aluminum alloys, push Optimal Load to 40-50% of tool diameter since the lower cutting forces allow aggressive engagement. Matching Optimal Load to material hardness directly controls chip thickness and prevents premature edge chipping on harder workpieces.
