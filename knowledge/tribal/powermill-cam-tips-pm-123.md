# Plastics and Engineering Polymers

Delrin/nylon/acetal: 1-2 flute uncoated, high rake, 200-500 m/min, 0.1-0.2mm fz. Compressed air cooling — plastics melt when hot. Down-cut prevents lifting thin features. For glass-filled polymers (PA66-GF30), use diamond or DLC-coated tools — glass fibers are highly abrasive. PowerMill can apply material-specific feed overrides per region.
