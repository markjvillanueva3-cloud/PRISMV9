# In-Process Stock Visualization — Remaining Material Analysis

CAMWorks simulation shows the in-process stock shape after each operation with color-coded remaining material. Blue indicates material within finishing stock, green shows areas at final size, red highlights missed material (excess stock). Use this visualization to verify: (1) no un-machined areas remain before finishing, (2) finishing stock is uniform (no thick/thin spots that cause tool deflection variation), (3) step-over marks from roughing are within acceptable limits. Export stock comparison reports showing the distribution of remaining material thickness.
