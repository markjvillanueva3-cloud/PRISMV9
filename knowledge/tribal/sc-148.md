# Stochastic Chatter Probability with Stability Lobes

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `79` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-forum`

## Tip

Chatter onset stochastic: damping ±15%, hardness ±5%, overhang ±0.5mm. Generate P(chatter) contours via Monte Carlo of stability lobes. Select RPM/DOC with P(chatter)<5%. iMachining's constant engagement inherently avoids many chatter conditions, but RPM selection remains critical. Use SolidCAM's spindle speed recommendations as starting point.
