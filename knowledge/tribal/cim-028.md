# Hard Milling Strategy for >50 HRC Materials

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `adaptive` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.88` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-forum`

## Tip

For hardened tool steel (50-65 HRC): use CBN or ceramic-coated carbide tools, constant chip load at 0.03-0.06mm/tooth, radial DOC ≤5% of diameter for trochoidal, axial DOC = 1×D. Cimatron's HSM mode maintains constant engagement angle. No coolant — use air blast only to prevent thermal shock cracking. Target surface finish Ra 0.4-0.8μm directly from machining to minimize polishing.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `H`
- Operation types: `finishing`, `roughing`
