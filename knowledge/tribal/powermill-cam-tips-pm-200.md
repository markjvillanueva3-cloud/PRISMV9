# Surface Extension Best Practices

Extend 2-5mm beyond edges for clean exit without deceleration marks. PowerMill creates extensions automatically or manually. Tool completes stroke on extension before retract. Also prevents corner radius deviation at edges. Critical for visible mold surfaces and Class-A automotive dies.
