# Digital Twin Feedback for Continuous Improvement

SolidCAM toolpath → execute → collect data → compare → update. After 10 iterations: ±3% force, ±5% finish accuracy. iMachining's predictable forces make digital twin calibration faster — fewer variables to calibrate. Focus twin modeling on finishing operations and thermal effects where iMachining doesn't directly optimize.
