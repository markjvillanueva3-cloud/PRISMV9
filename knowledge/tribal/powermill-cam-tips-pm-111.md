# PCA for Multi-Response Optimization

Optimizing Ra, accuracy, tool life, cycle time simultaneously: PCA extracts principal components capturing 85-90% of variance. Optimize first 2-3 PCs for balanced compromise. Avoids conflicting optima across individual responses. For PowerMill aerospace work with 6+ quality attributes, PCA captures essential variation in 2-3 components.
