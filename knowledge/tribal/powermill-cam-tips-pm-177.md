# Impeller Plunge and Contour Sequence

Plunge roughing between blades removes bulk (axial forces). Then 5-axis contour roughing for passages. Then flowline finishing hub-to-shroud. PowerMill blade module manages transitions. Barrel cutters on blade surfaces for 3-5× wider step-over. Check singularities at blade edges.
