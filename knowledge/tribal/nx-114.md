# Manufacturing Wizard for Guided Programming Workflows

**Category:** `automation` · **Subcategory:** `lights_out` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `82` · **Source:** `web:siemens-nx-docs`

## Tip

NX Manufacturing Wizard creates step-by-step guided workflows that enforce your shop's programming sequence. Define wizard steps for: geometry selection, tool assignment, parameter entry, and verification checkpoints. Each step can have validation rules that prevent advancing until requirements are met (e.g., cannot proceed past roughing without IPW verification). Junior programmers follow the wizard to produce programs that meet senior-level standards. Wizards are stored as XML configurations that can be shared across NX seats.
