# WCS Probing to Establish Part Zero Automatically

Use the Probe WCS operation to automatically find part zero by probing reference surfaces. Program a 3-point plane probe for the Z-datum, a 2-point line probe for X alignment, and a single-point probe for Y position. This eliminates manual edge-finding with indicators and establishes WCS in under 60 seconds. The probe stores the result in the specified G54-G59 offset automatically. Always probe at slow feed — 300-500mm/min for Renishaw OMP series probes — to avoid overtravel.
