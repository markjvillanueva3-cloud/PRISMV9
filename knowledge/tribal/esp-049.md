# Live Tooling RPM and Feed for Swiss Machines

**Category:** `speeds_feeds` · **Subcategory:** `surface_speed` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:esprit-swiss`

## Tip

Swiss machine live tooling spindles typically have lower power (0.5-3 kW) and speed (6,000-12,000 RPM) than dedicated machining centers. In ESPRIT, set conservative speeds and feeds: reduce by 30-40% from machining center recommendations. Use the smallest effective tool diameter to maximize RPM within the spindle's range. For cross-drilling, pecking is essential — Swiss live tool holders have limited rigidity, so peck depths of 0.5-1x drill diameter prevent tool breakage.

- Operation types: `swiss_milling`, `swiss_drilling`
