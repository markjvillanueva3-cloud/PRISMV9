# Auto 3+2 Axis in Turbo HSR/HSM — Access Undercuts Automatically

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `82` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

The Auto 3+2 module automatically identifies undercut areas that cannot be reached from a single direction and generates indexed multi-axis toolpaths. This minimizes the number of separate operations with different machining directions. Enable Auto 3+2 in Turbo HSR to efficiently rough undercut regions without manually creating multiple coordinate systems.

- Operation types: `roughing`, `3plus2`
