# Inconel Roughing with Ceramic Inserts

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-docs`

## Tip

Inconel 718: ceramic 200-400 m/min, 0.1-0.15mm fz, 1-2mm DOC. No coolant (thermal shock). Air blast only. Tool life 10-20 min but MRR 3-5× carbide. hyperMILL constant engagement essential. Reserve for roughing only, finish with carbide. Program fixed-time tool changes.

- Material groups: `S`
