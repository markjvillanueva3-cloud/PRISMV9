# Crown Machining with 5-Axis Undercut Access

WorkNC Dental machines crowns using 5-axis strategies that access undercut regions without collision. The software automatically calculates tool orientations to reach all surfaces of the crown geometry including the intaglio (internal) surface. Use 1-2 mm ball-nose cutters at 40,000-60,000 RPM for zirconia. Typical crown machining time: 8-15 minutes with 0.01 mm tolerance.
