# Material-Specific Strategies for Titanium

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `adaptive` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) in SprutCAM: use adaptive roughing with 8% radial engagement, full depth (1×D axial), cutting speed 40-60 m/min with coated carbide. Critical: enable flood coolant with 70+ bar pressure for chip evacuation. Avoid recutting chips — titanium chips work-harden and destroy the cutting edge. Use climb milling only. Set dwell time 0 at corners to prevent heat buildup.
