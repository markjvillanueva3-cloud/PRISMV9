# CUSUM is optimal for detecting sustained shifts of known size

**Category:** `quality` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `document:mit2830j@lecture9`

## Tip

If you know the approximate shift size δ you want to detect, CUSUM with k=δ/2 is the optimal detector (lowest ARL₁). Standard params: k=0.5σ (half-sigma slack), h=5σ (decision interval) gives ARL₀≈465 and detects 1σ shift in ~10 samples. CUSUM accumulates evidence over time — it will ALWAYS eventually detect a sustained shift, no matter how small.
