# Trochoidal Milling for Slotting Operations

WorkNC's trochoidal milling generates circular arcing motions for slot cutting that limit radial engagement to 8-12% of tool diameter. This allows full-depth cutting (up to 2x cutter diameter) at high feed rates without the heat and wear of conventional full-width slotting. Set the trochoidal width based on slot width and tool diameter. Particularly effective in stainless steel and hardened steel.
