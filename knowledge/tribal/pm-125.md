# Helical Milling for Precision Holes

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `drilling` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Helical interpolation: circular + Z-feed. One tool for multiple hole sizes. Lower forces than drilling (no chisel edge). No breakthrough burr. In PowerMill: helical diameter = hole size, pitch 0.3-0.5mm, flat-end mill 60-70% of diameter. Superior to drilling in hardened steel and titanium where drill wandering causes position errors.

- Material groups: `P`, `S`, `H`
- Operation types: `drilling`
