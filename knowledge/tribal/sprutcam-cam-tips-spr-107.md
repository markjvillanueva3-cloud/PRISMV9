# Coffin-Manson for Insert Thermal Fatigue

Nf = C×(Δε)^(-β), Δε=α×ΔT, β≈2.0 carbide. Large temp swings accelerate cracking. For SprutCAM interrupted operations: commit to dry or continuous flood. Intermittent coolant halves life. #1 cause of premature insert failure in interrupted roughing. Program coolant M-codes consistently.
