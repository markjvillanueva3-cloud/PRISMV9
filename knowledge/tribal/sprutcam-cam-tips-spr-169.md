# Gutowski Energy Model for Turning

P = P₀ + k×MRR. Turning MRR = π×D×f×ap/1000. At low MRR (finishing small diameters), idle power dominates. Maximize MRR within limits. SprutCAM programs should rough at high MRR for energy efficiency, not just productivity.
