# Barrel Cutter Strategies for NX Finishing

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:siemens-community`

## Tip

NX supports barrel (segment, tangent, lens) cutters with large effective cutting radius. Define the barrel geometry precisely: barrel radius, tip fillet, taper angle. Use 'Surface Area' or 'Flow Cut' finishing with automatic tilt to maintain the barrel contact zone on the surface. Barrel cutters produce 3-5× wider step-over for the same scallop height as ball-end mills, reducing finishing cycle time by 60-80%.
