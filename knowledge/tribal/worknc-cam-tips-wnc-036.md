# Electrode Machining with Graphite-Specific Strategies

WorkNC provides graphite-specific machining strategies for EDM electrodes. Graphite machines differently from metal: no coolant (dust extraction required), high spindle speeds (15,000-30,000 RPM), moderate feeds, and sharp uncoated or diamond-coated tools. Set the finishing tolerance tighter (0.002-0.005 mm) as graphite machines very cleanly. Use climb milling exclusively to prevent chipping at edges.
