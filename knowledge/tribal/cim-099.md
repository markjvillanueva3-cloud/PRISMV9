# Cast Iron Dry Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-tutorials`

## Tip

Gray and ductile cast iron machines best dry or with air blast. Chips are short and brittle. Use uncoated or TiN-coated carbide at 150-250 m/min. DOC can be aggressive (1.5-2×D). Standard offset roughing works well for cast iron — constant engagement strategies aren't needed. Coolant actually reduces tool life via thermal shock cycling in interrupted cuts.
