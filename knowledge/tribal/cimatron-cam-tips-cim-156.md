# PEEK Medical Device Machining

PEEK implants in Cimatron: single/2-flute uncoated with high positive rake, 200-500 m/min, 0.1-0.2mm feed/tooth. Compressed air cooling — no liquid coolant for biocompatibility. Diamond-coated tools prevent metal contamination. Down-cut direction prevents lifting thin features. FDA requires complete traceability: link Cimatron program version to every produced implant.
