# Spot Drilling — Establish Accurate Hole Location Before Full Drill

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `drilling` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

Always start holes with a spot drill or center drill to create a guide cone for the twist drill. The spot drill point angle should be ≥ the twist drill point angle (typically 90° spot for 118° drill, or 120° spot for 140° drill). Spot depth: drill to the point where the spot diameter equals or slightly exceeds the twist drill diameter. For CNC machines with rigid setups and short carbide drills, spot drilling can be eliminated for holes ≤ 10mm with tolerance > ±0.05mm.
