# Trochoidal Milling for Hard Material Slots

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `adaptive` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

8-15% radial, full depth, 3-5× feed vs conventional. Constant engagement prevents impact loading. Slot width independent of tool diameter. For >45 HRC: trochoidal is preferred. Cimatron adaptive roughing handles trochoidal paths with engagement angle control.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `H`
- Operation types: `roughing`
