# Rapid Height Optimization per Operation

Incremental (10mm above stock) vs absolute. Safe-area rapids when crossing obstacles only. 5-15% savings. Configure per-operation based on geometry. Verify in simulation. Critical for multi-pocket mold cavities with varying depths. Don't use single global safe height.
