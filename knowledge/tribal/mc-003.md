# Dynamic Mill Minimize Burial prevents over-engagement in corners

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:mastercam-docs`

## Tip

In Mastercam 2025+, the Minimize Burial option in Dynamic Mill and Area Mill prevents the tool from over-engaging in tight corners and narrow slots. When enabled, the toolpath automatically adjusts the approach angle and stepover near corners to keep radial engagement below the set maximum. Enable this for parts with thin walls or deep narrow features to prevent tool deflection and chatter.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `2d_pocket`
