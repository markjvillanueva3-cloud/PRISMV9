# Rapid Move Height Optimization

**Category:** `optimization` · **Subcategory:** `cycle_time` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:tebis-docs`

## Tip

Incremental safe heights (10mm above stock) vs absolute fixed Z. Safe-area rapids only when crossing obstacles. 5-15% cycle time savings on complex parts. Configure per-operation. Verify in simulation that rapids clear fixturing. Critical for multi-pocket mold cavities with varying pocket depths.
