# Additive/Hybrid Manufacturing

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.8` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

SprutCAM supports DED path planning. Contour+fill patterns, 30-50% bead overlap, 60-80% layer height. Interleave subtractive every 3-5 layers for accuracy. For repair, feature addition, near-net-shape. Reduces machining on expensive materials by 60-80%.
