# Polishing-Ready Surface from Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

Ra 0.4-0.8μm directly: (1) scallop formula for step-over, (2) constant scallop mode, (3) sharp tools <50% life, (4) lead/lean for ball-end, (5) air blast for hardened. Reduces polishing 50-70%, saving 20-40 hours per mold. SprutCAM 3D offset with constant scallop is the preferred strategy.
