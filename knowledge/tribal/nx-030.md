# Toolpath Analysis for Cut Validation

**Category:** `quality` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `82` · **Source:** `web:siemens-docs`

## Tip

NX integrated toolpath analysis shows feed rate, spindle speed, and tool engagement along the toolpath in a color-coded display. Use this to identify areas of excessive engagement (potential tool breakage) or low feed (wasted cycle time) before running ISV. Fix these issues in the CAM operation rather than patching them in the G-code.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`, `3-axis`, `5-axis`
