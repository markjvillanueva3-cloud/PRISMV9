# Hurco WinMax conversational is production-ready

**Category:** `programming` · **Subcategory:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `controller_specific`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `controller:hurco_winmax_features`

## Tip

Unlike most conversational systems (designed for prototypes), Hurco WinMax conversational is genuinely production-capable. It supports: canned pocket/contour/drill patterns, transform (rotate/mirror/scale), part import from DXF/STEP/IGES directly on the control, merged conversational + NC in same program. Key advantage: modify programs directly on the machine without CAM. Ideal for job shops with high mix/low volume.

- Operation types: `pocketing`, `profiling`, `drilling`
