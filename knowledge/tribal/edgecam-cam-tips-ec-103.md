# Aluminum HSM Strategy in Edgecam

For aluminum high-speed machining: use Waveform with 8-12% radial engagement, full flute depth, and 3-5x normal feed rate. Select 3-flute polished carbide end mills. Surface speed: 300-1000 m/min depending on alloy (6061 higher, 7075 lower). Use compressed air blast instead of flood coolant to prevent built-up edge. Set tolerance to 0.005mm and enable arc fitting for smooth motion. Aluminum is forgiving — push speeds aggressively.
