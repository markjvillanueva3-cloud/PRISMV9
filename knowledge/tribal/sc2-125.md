# Ramp Entry for Closed Pocket Initial Engagement

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `pocket` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:surfcam-ramp-entry`

## Tip

SURFCAM ramp entry gradually descends the tool into the material at a controlled angle (1-5°) rather than plunging. This distributes the cutting load across the bottom and side of the tool, preventing center-of-tool overload. For closed pockets, combine ramp entry with a zigzag pattern to clear a starting groove. Set the ramp length to achieve the full depth within 2-3x the pocket width. For hard materials (>40 HRC), reduce the ramp angle to 1-2° and use a bull-nose end mill.

- Material groups: `H`
- Operation types: `pocketing`, `roughing`
