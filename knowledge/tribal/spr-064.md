# Cast Iron Machining without Coolant

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-tutorials`

## Tip

Gray and ductile cast iron machines best dry or with air blast. Cast iron chips are short and brittle — no stringy chip problems. Use uncoated or TiN-coated carbide at 150-250 m/min. DOC can be aggressive (1.5-2×D). SprutCAM's standard offset roughing works well — cast iron doesn't require constant engagement strategies. Coolant causes thermal shock and actually reduces tool life in cast iron.
