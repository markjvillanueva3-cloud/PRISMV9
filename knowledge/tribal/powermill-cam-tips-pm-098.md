# Archard Wear Model for Flank Wear Prediction

V = K×F×d/H. Doubling speed quadruples wear (distance + K both double). Use to predict tool life at different PowerMill speed settings. Roughing: reduce speed 20% for 2.5× longer life. Finishing: accept shorter life at higher speed. Trade-off depends on tool cost vs machine rate ratio for the specific job.
