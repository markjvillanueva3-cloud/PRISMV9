# Spiral Finishing Eliminates Entry/Exit Witness Marks

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `spiral` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:worknc-spiral`

## Tip

WorkNC's spiral finishing generates a continuous helical toolpath that covers the surface without retracts, eliminating witness marks from tool entry/exit. The spiral starts from center and works outward with automatic stepover adjustment. Best for shallow bowl-shaped geometries and flat-bottomed cavities. Set spiral direction to climb milling and overlap to 5-10% for seamless surface coverage.
