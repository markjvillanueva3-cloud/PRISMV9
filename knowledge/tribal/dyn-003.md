# Dynamic Mill From Outside strategy cuts standing cores from the boundary inward

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `92` · **Source:** `document:mastercam-dynamic-milling`

## Tip

Dynamic Mill 'From Outside' strategy machines open pocket shapes or standing cores by treating the outermost chain as stock boundary. Tool moves freely outside this area, starting from outside and working toward inner boundaries. Use for: boss machining, standing features, open-sided pockets. Not suitable for fully enclosed cavities — use Stay Inside strategy instead.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `pocketing`
