# 5-Axis Automatic Collision Avoidance — Tool and Holder Check

**Category:** `safety` · **Subcategory:** `coolant_safety` · **Domain:** `safety`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

SolidCAM's Sim 5X module includes automatic collision avoidance that checks both tool and holder against the part, fixture, and machine components. When a collision is detected, the system automatically tilts the tool away while maintaining surface contact. Always define your actual holder geometry in the tool library — generic holders lead to unnecessary avoidance moves or missed collisions.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `5axis_finishing`, `5axis_roughing`
