# Operation Templates for Standardized Programming

**Category:** `workflow` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:bobcad-templates`

## Tip

BobCAD operation templates save complete machining setups (tool, parameters, linking, tolerances) for reuse. Create templates for common operations: 'Roughing Steel 20mm', 'Finishing Mold 6mm BN', etc. Apply templates to new geometry for instant parameter population. V36+ allows copying entire machine setups — paste to recreate identical setups instantly. Store templates on shared drives for team consistency. Export templates as backup before major parameter changes.

- Material groups: `P`
- Operation types: `setup`
