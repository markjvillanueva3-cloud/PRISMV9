# Pencil Tracing for Corner Cleanup

After finishing, pencil trace cleans internal corners/fillets. hyperMILL auto-detects concave regions. Ball-end 50-70% of smallest fillet radius. Both-ways for symmetric corners halves cycle time. Essential transition zone between Z-level and raster strategies. Run as final finishing operation.
