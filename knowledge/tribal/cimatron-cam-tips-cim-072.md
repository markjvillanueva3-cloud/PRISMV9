# Constant Scallop Height Finishing

Cimatron constant scallop varies step-over based on local surface curvature to maintain uniform scallop height. Set target scallop (e.g., 0.005mm for polishing-ready). Produces 20-30% shorter cycle times than fixed step-over while ensuring uniform quality. Essential for mold surfaces where consistent polish quality across varying curvature is critical for part appearance.
