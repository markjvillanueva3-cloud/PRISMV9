# CAD Surface Repair for Imported Models

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-docs`

## Tip

Close gaps to 0.1mm, extend surfaces, rebuild degenerate. Fix before programming. UV continuity affects toolpath. Parting surfaces need G2 minimum. hyperMILL surface analysis identifies issues. Global Fitting improves surface quality post-import.
