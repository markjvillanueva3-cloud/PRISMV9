# Levels Manager in 2026 uses tree structure with groups and search

**Category:** `setup` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `83` · **Source:** `web:mastercam-docs`

## Tip

Mastercam 2026 introduces a tree-structured Levels Manager with nested groups, subgroups, drag-and-drop organization, and search/filter tools. Organize levels by function: raw stock, finished part, fixtures, toolpath boundaries, construction geometry. Use naming conventions (e.g., L10-Stock, L20-Part, L30-Fixtures) for quick filtering. This replaces the flat level list and dramatically improves management of complex multi-setup parts.
