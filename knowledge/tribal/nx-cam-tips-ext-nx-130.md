# Trochoidal Milling for Slot and Pocket Roughing

NX's trochoidal milling maintains constant radial engagement by using circular tool motions. Set 'Engagement Percentage' to 8-15% of diameter for steel, up to 25% for aluminum. Trochoidal paths allow full axial depth (1×D or more) with feeds 3-5× higher than conventional slotting. Best for: narrow slots where conventional approaches cause excessive tool engagement, and HSM roughing of hard materials (>45 HRC).
