# NCJob Templates for Standardized Programming

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-docs`

## Tip

hyperMILL NCJob templates capture complete strategies: tools, parameters, leads/links, boundaries, sequences. Apply to similar parts — hyperMILL remaps geometry automatically. Enforce shop standards. Reduce programming 50-70% on repeat geometry. Version-control templates for process improvement tracking.
