# Composite Trimming for Automotive Parts

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.83` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-tutorials`

## Tip

CFRP/GFRP trimming: PCD or diamond-coated compression routers, 200-400 m/min, 0.02-0.05mm feed/tooth. 5-axis tool normal for clean edges. Vacuum fixturing only. Zig cutting for consistent fiber direction. Dust extraction mandatory (hazardous). Cimatron programs along trim curves with automatic approach/retract moves. Delamination-free cuts require sharp tools and controlled feed rates.
