# ProfitTurning Constant Chip Load in Contour Changes

**Category:** `speeds_feeds` · **Subcategory:** `chip_load` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:esprit-profitturning`

## Tip

When turning profiles with diameter changes, shoulders, and fillets, ProfitTurning automatically adjusts feed rate to maintain constant chip load. At convex shoulders the effective depth of cut increases — ProfitTurning reduces feed to compensate. At concave fillets the engagement drops — feed increases. This eliminates the chatter and insert chipping that occur with constant-feed turning in complex profiles.

- Operation types: `turning_roughing`, `turning_finishing`
