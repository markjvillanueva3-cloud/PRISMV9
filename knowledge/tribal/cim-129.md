# Predictive Maintenance from Cycle Time Trends

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.79` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-forum`

## Tip

Track cycle time across runs. Gradual increase (5-10% over 50 parts): spindle bearing wear, ball screw backlash growth, or guideway wear. Cross-reference with maintenance logs. Cimatron estimated vs actual gap is the key indicator. Schedule preventive maintenance before accuracy degrades. Proactive replacement of bearings at 80% rated life prevents catastrophic in-cut failures.
