# Raster Finishing Direction Optimization

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-forum`

## Tip

Choose raster finishing direction based on surface flow and cutter marks visibility. For mold cavities visible in the final product, align raster direction with the draw direction (perpendicular to parting line). For surfaces requiring polishing, use 45° raster angle to create a cross-hatch pattern that aids manual polishing. Set 'One Way' cutting for best finish, 'Zig-Zag' for faster cycle.
