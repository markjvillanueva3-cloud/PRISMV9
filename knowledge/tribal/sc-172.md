# HSR/HSM 3D Finishing Strategies

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

SolidCAM HSR (High Speed Roughing) and HSM (High Speed Machining) for 3D finishing. HSR: constant Z, rest machining. HSM: raster, spiral, constant scallop, pencil. Use constant scallop for uniform quality on freeform. Pencil for corner cleanup. Combine strategies per surface region.
