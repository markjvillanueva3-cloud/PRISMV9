# Bar Stock Quality Impact on Accuracy

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-tutorials`

## Tip

Bar straightness (±0.010mm), concentricity (±0.005mm), hardness variation (±2 HRC) are often the dominant error sources on Swiss-type machines. Use precision ground bar. SprutCAM parameters can't compensate for bad bar — garbage in, garbage out. Verify bar quality before production.
