# Hertzian Contact Stress for Ball-End Tool Selection

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.78` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-forum`

## Tip

Ball-end contact: σ_max ∝ (F/R²)^(1/3). Larger ball radius reduces contact stress and wear rate. Minimum ball diameter = 2× step-over / sin(max_slope_angle). This prevents high contact stress at tool tip that causes premature CBN failure on hardened mold steel. For H13 at 52 HRC: use 6mm ball minimum for step-over ≤0.3mm.
