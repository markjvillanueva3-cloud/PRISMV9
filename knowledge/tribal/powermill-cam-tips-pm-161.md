# Wiener Process for Stochastic Wear Modeling

dVB = μdt + σdW. Predict RUL distribution. Update μ/σ from measurements. More accurate than deterministic Taylor for variable conditions. For PowerMill multi-operation programs where cutting conditions change between operations.
