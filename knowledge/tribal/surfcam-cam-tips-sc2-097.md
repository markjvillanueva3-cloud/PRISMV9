# Deep Hole Drilling with Gun Drill and BTA Strategies

SURFCAM deep hole drilling (L/D > 10) supports gun drill and BTA (boring and trepanning association) strategies. For gun drilling, program constant feed with through-tool coolant at 70+ bar pressure. Do not peck — gun drills rely on continuous chip flushing. For BTA boring, set the pilot bush approach sequence and the steady-rest engagement points. Monitor spindle load throughout the cycle — a sudden increase indicates chip packing or drill wandering.
