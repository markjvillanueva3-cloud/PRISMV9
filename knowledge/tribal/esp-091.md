# AI-Assisted Toolpath Generation in ESPRIT EDGE

**Category:** `automation` · **Subcategory:** `part_catcher` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:esprit-edge-ai`

## Tip

ESPRIT EDGE incorporates AI-assisted toolpath generation that learns from previous machining operations to suggest optimal strategies for new parts. The system analyzes the part geometry, material, machine capabilities, and available tooling to recommend operations in priority order. Accept suggestions with one click or modify them. The AI improves over time as it learns from your shop's actual machining results and programmer preferences.
