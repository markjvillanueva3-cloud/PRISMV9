# Machine-Specific Posts for Major CNC Brands

BobCAD includes pre-built post processors for major CNC manufacturers: Fanuc, Siemens, Heidenhain, Mazak, Haas, Okuma, DMG Mori, Hurco, and others. Start with the closest matching pre-built post rather than building from scratch. Verify output against the machine's operator manual for: coordinate format, feed rate mode (G94/G95), coolant codes, and work offset format. BobCAD's post support team provides custom post modifications for specific machine configurations.
