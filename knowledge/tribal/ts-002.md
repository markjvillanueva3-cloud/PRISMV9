# Parametric Machining Templates for Part Families

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `93` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-parametric`

## Tip

TopSolid's parametric machining allows you to create master CAM programs on a representative part, then apply them to an entire family of similar parts with different dimensions. The system maps features by topology rather than explicit geometry references, so operations adapt to size/shape variants. Store templates in the TopSolid PDM vault for team-wide reuse. This can reduce programming time by 80-90% for families of prismatic parts.
