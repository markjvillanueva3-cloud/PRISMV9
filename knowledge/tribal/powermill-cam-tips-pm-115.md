# Dimensional Invariant Validation Checks

Validate physics: P = Fc×Vc (must equal spindle power), specific energy u = P/MRR, chip ratio rc = chip/uncut thickness. Deviation >20% from predictions = process problem. Use as go/no-go for new PowerMill programs. These invariants are universal — work for any material, tool, and machine combination. Quick sanity check before committing to production.
