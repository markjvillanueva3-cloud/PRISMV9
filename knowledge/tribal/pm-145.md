# Turbine Blade Hub Finishing

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Hub surfaces between turbine blades: use flowline finishing from blade root to blade tip. Short rigid ball-end mills. Lead angle 10-15° for consistent contact. PowerMill blade module generates hub toolpaths that navigate between adjacent blades without collision. Verify clearance at blade leading/trailing edges where access is tightest.
