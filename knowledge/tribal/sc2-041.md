# Tool Axis Control: Lead, Lag, and Side-Tilt Angles

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:surfcam-5axis-toolaxis`

## Tip

SURFCAM 5-axis provides three tool axis control modes: lead angle (tilt in the feed direction), lag angle (tilt opposite to feed), and side-tilt (tilt perpendicular to feed). For ball-nose finishing, use 10-15° lead angle to cut with a point ahead of the tool center, improving surface finish and preventing the zero-speed center from rubbing. For side milling, use 1-3° side-tilt to prevent the non-cutting side of the tool from contacting the surface.
