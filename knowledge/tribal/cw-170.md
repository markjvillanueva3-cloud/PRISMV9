# Swiss-Type Bar Remnant Management — Material Yield Optimization

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

Swiss machines run from bar stock, and the bar remnant (stub left in the guide bushing) is waste. CAMWorks calculates the number of parts per bar and the remnant length. Optimize by: (1) adjusting part-off width (thinner part-off blade = more parts per bar), (2) nesting different parts on the same bar (short part + long part), (3) minimizing grip/face stock. For expensive materials (titanium, Inconel), even 2mm per part savings across 10,000 parts saves significant material cost.
