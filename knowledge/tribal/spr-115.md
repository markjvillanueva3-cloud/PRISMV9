# PCA for Multi-Response Turning Optimization

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.76` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

Optimizing Ra, roundness, tool life, cycle time: PCA extracts 2-3 PCs capturing 85-90% of variance. Optimize PCs for balanced compromise. Avoids conflicting optima. For SprutCAM turning with 6+ quality attributes (Ra, roundness, cylindricity, taper, dimension, concentricity), PCA finds the efficient compromise solution.
