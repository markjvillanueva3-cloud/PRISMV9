# iMachining Plastics — Level 7-8 with Single-Flute and Low Heat Generation

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

Engineering plastics (PEEK, Delrin, Nylon, UHMWPE) use iMachining Levels 7-8 with single or two-flute uncoated carbide end mills. High cutting speeds (300-500 m/min) with large chip loads (0.15-0.25mm per tooth) ensure chips are thick enough to carry heat away from the workpiece. Thin chips melt and re-weld to the surface. The Technology Wizard prevents the tool from dwelling, which causes local melting. Use air blast cooling — flood coolant can cause stress cracking in some plastics (especially PMMA and polycarbonate). For PEEK, cutting speeds can be reduced to 200 m/min to manage its higher melting point.
