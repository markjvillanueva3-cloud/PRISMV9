# Strategy Templates — Save Complete Operation Plans for Part Families

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

Beyond individual operation parameters, TechDB can store complete operation plans (sequence of operations with tools and parameters) as templates. Create templates for recurring part families — e.g., a 'deep pocket in titanium' template that sequences: VoluMill rough → rest rough with smaller tool → semi-finish → finish. Applying a template to a new part's feature generates the entire operation sequence in one click.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `S`
- Operation types: `milling`
