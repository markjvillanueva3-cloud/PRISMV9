# Cusp Analysis for Quality Verification

Edgecam's cusp analysis displays a color map of theoretical surface quality across the machined part. Red zones indicate cusp heights exceeding specification — these need additional passes or smaller stepover. Use cusp analysis after programming to verify Ra/Rz requirements are met before sending to the machine. This pre-verification eliminates trial cuts and reduces first-article rejection rates by identifying surface quality issues in advance.
