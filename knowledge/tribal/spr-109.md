# Fourier Analysis for Chatter Diagnosis

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.79` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

FFT of vibration/audio: peaks between tooth-passing harmonics = regenerative chatter. Shift RPM 10-15% to move stability boundary. SprutCAM can output multiple RPM options. Chatter frequency reveals dominant mode (tool vs workpiece). For turning: chatter manifests at different frequencies than milling — analyze accordingly.
