# HSM Contour-Driven Finishing — Follow Part Features for Best Results

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

HSM Contour-Driven finishing generates toolpaths that follow specific contour curves on the part surface. Select the driving curves from edges, sketches, or projected curves on the model. This strategy excels on parts with strong directional features like turbine blade leading/trailing edges, where toolpath alignment with the feature produces superior surface finish compared to generic raster or constant-Z approaches. Use overlap of 10-15% to eliminate gaps between contour bands.
