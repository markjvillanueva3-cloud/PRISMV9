# Aluminum HSM Strategy in ESPRIT

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:esprit-materials`

## Tip

For aluminum high-speed machining in ESPRIT: use ProfitMilling with 8-12% radial engagement, full flute depth axial engagement, and 3-5x normal feed rate. Select 3-flute polished carbide end mills for chip evacuation. Surface speed: 300-1000 m/min depending on alloy (6061 higher, 7075 lower). Enable compressed air blast instead of flood coolant to prevent built-up edge. Set tolerance to 0.005mm and enable arc fitting for smooth motion at high feed rates.
