# Facing with Wiper Inserts for Plate Flatness

Face mill 65-75% step-over, one-way. Wiper inserts for single-pass mirror finish. Interrupted faces: -20% feed at entry. For parting surface flatness ±0.01mm across plate. Cimatron facing handles irregular stock and avoids clamp regions automatically.
