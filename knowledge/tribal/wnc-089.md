# Cusp Analysis Visualizes Surface Quality Pre-Cut

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:worknc-cusp-analysis`

## Tip

WorkNC's cusp analysis displays a color-coded map of theoretical cusp height across the surface before cutting. Areas exceeding the target are highlighted in red. Use this to identify regions needing smaller tools or tighter stepover. The analysis accounts for tool geometry, surface curvature, and local stepover. Adjust strategy based on the analysis before committing to a cut.
