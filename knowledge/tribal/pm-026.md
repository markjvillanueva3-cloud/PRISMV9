# Stock Model from Toolpath vs From Block

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:powermill-tutorials`

## Tip

Create stock models 'From Toolpath' when you need the actual machined result after specific operations. Create stock models 'From Block' when defining initial raw material (billet, casting, forging). The 'From Toolpath' method applies all selected toolpath operations sequentially to the input stock, producing a precise representation of remaining material. Use 'From Multiple Toolpaths' to apply roughing, semi-finishing, and finishing in sequence for comprehensive rest analysis.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `rest_machining`
