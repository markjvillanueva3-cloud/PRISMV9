# Custom Cycle Creation for Repeated Operations

**Category:** `automation` · **Subcategory:** `lights_out` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:edgecam-automation`

## Tip

Create custom cycles in Edgecam for shop-specific operations not covered by standard cycles: specialized deburring routines, custom probing sequences, machine-specific warm-up programs, and pallet change procedures. Custom cycles combine PCI macro logic with standard Edgecam operations and post processor customization. Once created, they appear in the operations menu alongside standard cycles for easy programmer access.
