# Aluminum High-Speed Machining with Adaptive Roughing

**Category:** `material_specific` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:bobcad-aluminum`

## Tip

For aluminum (6061/7075), BobCAD Adaptive Roughing enables extreme parameters: 20,000+ RPM, 10,000+ mm/min feed, 2xD axial at 8-12% radial. Use 3-flute uncoated or DLC-coated carbide for chip evacuation. Aluminum welds to cutters at high temps — Adaptive's constant engagement prevents thermal spikes. Use air blast or MQL over flood coolant for highest speeds. BobCAD's HSM roughing pattern is optimized for aluminum's high MRR capability.

- Material groups: `N`
- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
