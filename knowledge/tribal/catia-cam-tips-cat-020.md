# Geodesic Machining for Complex Curvature Uniformity

CATIA Geodesic machining computes tool paths along geodesic lines (shortest paths on the surface), producing the most uniform scallop height on complex double-curvature surfaces. It is computationally more expensive than Sweeping but delivers superior finish on automotive Class-A surfaces and aerospace skin panels. Use it as the final finishing pass after Sweeping semi-finish, limiting the machining zone to the most critical visible surfaces.
