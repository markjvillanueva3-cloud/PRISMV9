# Facing Operation Optimization

SprutCAM's facing operation machines the top surface of stock to a reference plane. Use a face mill or large-diameter flat end mill. Set step-over to 65-75% of cutter diameter for full coverage. Enable 'One Way' cutting to avoid conventional milling on the return pass. For interrupted cuts (bolt holes, keyways in the face), reduce feed 20% at entry to prevent insert chipping.
