# BMA for Multi-Material Tool Life

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `75` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-forum`

## Tip

Weight Taylor/Archard/empirical by posterior per material class. SolidCAM iMachining applies across all materials — BMA adapts model weights per iMachining material level. Steel level 5 may favor Taylor while aluminum level 7 favors empirical.
