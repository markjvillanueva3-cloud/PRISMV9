# Wire EDM Slug Management for Safe Unattended Operation

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `process_engineering`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:esprit-wire-edm`

## Tip

ESPRIT's slug management classifies each profile's slug by weight and size. Large slugs (>50g) require tab stops — small uncut sections that hold the slug in place until an operator can remove it manually. Small slugs can be allowed to drop into the work tank. Program tab stops at 2-3 locations per profile, with tab width of 0.3-0.5mm. After all rough cuts, return to remove tabs in a separate pass. This prevents slug pinching the wire during unattended operation.
