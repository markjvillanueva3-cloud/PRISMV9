# Geodesic 5-Axis Finishing for Constant-Length Passes

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-geodesic`

## Tip

TopSolid's geodesic finishing generates toolpath passes that follow geodesic curves on the surface—the shortest path between two points on a curved surface. This produces constant-length passes that maintain uniform cutting conditions regardless of surface curvature. The result is more consistent chip load and better surface finish compared to UV-based or planar strategies on highly curved surfaces like turbine vanes or automotive body panels.
