# Thread milling vs tapping decision

**Category:** `tooling` · **Subcategory:** `tool_selection` · **Domain:** `shop_floor`

**Confidence:** `95` · **Source:** `operator:shop_foreman`

## Tip

Use thread mills (not taps) for: blind holes in expensive parts, hole diameters >M12, exotic materials (Inconel, Ti), and any single-piece prototype. Taps are faster but if they break in the hole, the part is scrap. Thread mill breaks? Just replace and re-run the path.

- Material groups: `S`
- Operation types: `thread`
