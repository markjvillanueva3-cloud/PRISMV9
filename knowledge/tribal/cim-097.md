# Plunge Roughing for Deep Narrow Pockets

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `pocket` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Plunge (Z-axis) roughing drills vertically into stock, then repositions. Set step-over to 60-70% of tool diameter. Plunge roughing is effective for: narrow deep slots (L/D > 4), hard materials (>45 HRC), and weak spindles. Forces are primarily axial (strongest machine direction). Slower MRR than lateral roughing but dramatically safer for extreme conditions.

- Material groups: `H`
- Operation types: `roughing`
