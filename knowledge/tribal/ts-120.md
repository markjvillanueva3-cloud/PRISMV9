# Runner System Machining with Specialized Strategies

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-runner`

## Tip

TopSolid machines runner systems (sprue, runners, gates, cold slugs) with strategies tailored to the circular cross-sections and smooth flow requirements. Use ball-nose endmills matched to the runner half-round profile for single-pass finishing. For hot runner manifold bores, use gun drilling with pecking cycles and through-tool coolant. Program the runner with extra attention to surface finish—any roughness in the runner channel creates flow disturbance that affects part quality.

- Operation types: `finishing`, `drilling`
