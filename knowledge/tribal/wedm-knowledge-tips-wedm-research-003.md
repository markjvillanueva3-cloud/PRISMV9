# Multi-objective Jaya outperforms GA and TLBO for stainless steel WEDM

Comparative study on stainless steel wire-cut EDM optimization: Multi-objective Jaya (MO-Jaya) algorithm outperforms both Genetic Algorithm (GA) and Teaching-Learning-Based Optimization (TLBO) for simultaneously optimizing surface finish and machining time. MO-Jaya requires no algorithm-specific parameters, making it easier to implement in production settings.
