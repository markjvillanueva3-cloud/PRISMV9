# Standard Workflow Definition for Shop Consistency

**Category:** `automation` · **Subcategory:** `tool_presetter` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:edgecam-strategy-manager`

## Tip

Define standard workflows in Strategy Manager that enforce your shop's best practices: always spot drill before drilling, always deburr after milling, always measure after finishing. Workflows prevent steps from being skipped and ensure every programmer follows the same process. Include mandatory simulation and collision checking as workflow steps. Workflows can be locked by engineering and assigned to specific machine groups.
