# Stainless Steel with Constant Chip Load to Prevent Hardening

**Category:** `material_specific` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:surfcam-stainless`

## Tip

Austenitic stainless steels (304, 316) work-harden during machining, forming a hard surface layer that accelerates wear on subsequent passes. TrueMill's constant engagement prevents the intermittent cutting that causes the worst work-hardening. Set cutting speed to 80-120 m/min (lower for 316 than 304) with 0.08-0.12mm/tooth feed. Never let the tool rub — maintain positive chip thickness at all times. Use TiAlN-coated carbide tools for optimal heat resistance.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `M`
- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
