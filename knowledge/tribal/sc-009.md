# iMachining 3D Wall and Floor Offsets — Control Stock Allowance

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `83` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

The iMachining 3D toolpath first accounts for the tool plus Wall offset, then offsets along the tool axis by the Floor offset distance. Set Wall offset to 0.2–0.5 mm for semi-finish passes and Floor offset to match your finishing allowance. These offsets ensure consistent stock for the HSM finishing pass that follows.
