# SURFCAM Tangent Barrel Cutter for Floor-Wall Blends

**Category:** `tooling` · **Subcategory:** `tool_selection` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:surfcam-docs`

## Tip

The tangent barrel cutter variant has its barrel profile tangent to both the tip radius and the shank taper, creating a smooth convex cutting profile. In SURFCAM, use this for finishing floor-to-wall transition zones where a ball-nose would leave scallops and a flat end mill can't follow the blend radius. Program a 5-axis finishing pass with the tangent barrel tilted to maintain contact at the blend radius. Step-over can be 5-8x larger than with a ball-nose for equivalent scallop height.
