# Surface Extension for Clean Tool Exit

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.88` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Extend machining surfaces 2-5mm beyond part boundaries using Cimatron's 'Extend Surface' tool. This ensures the cutter exits the part cleanly without deceleration marks at edges. For parting surfaces, extend in the split direction. For freeform surfaces, use 'Tangent Extension' to maintain surface continuity. Remove extension surfaces from the final model after programming.
