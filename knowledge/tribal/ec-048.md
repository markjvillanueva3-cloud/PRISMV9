# Wire EDM 2-Axis Profile with Lead Strategy

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `process_engineering`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:edgecam-wire-edm`

## Tip

Edgecam's 2-axis wire EDM generates profile cutting toolpaths with automatic lead-in/lead-out placement. Place leads on non-critical surfaces or in corners where witness marks won't affect function. Use tangential leads with 0.5-1mm approach distance for precision dies. For multiple profiles, optimize the wire threading sequence to minimize non-cutting time. Enable automatic start hole placement in waste material.
