# Stainless Steel Strategies to Prevent Work Hardening

Austenitic stainless steels (304, 316) work-harden rapidly in ESPRIT programming. Key strategies: maintain minimum chip thickness (never let feed drop below 0.03mm/tooth), use climb milling exclusively, avoid re-cutting chips (ensure adequate chip evacuation). ProfitMilling's constant engagement prevents the intermittent cutting that causes work hardening. Surface speed: 80-120 m/min with coated carbide. Use positive-geometry inserts for turning to reduce cutting force and heat generation.
