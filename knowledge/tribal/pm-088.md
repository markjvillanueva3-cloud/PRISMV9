# Sensitivity Analysis for Parameter Prioritization

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.79` · **Source:** `web:powermill-forum`

## Tip

Vary each machining parameter ±10% and measure impact on surface finish and accuracy. Typical sensitivity ranking: (1) step-over (35% of finish variation), (2) feed rate (25%), (3) cutting speed (20%), (4) depth of cut (15%), (5) tool runout (5%). Focus optimization on the top 2 parameters — they account for 60% of the variation. Use PowerMill's parameter sweep to generate comparison toolpaths.
