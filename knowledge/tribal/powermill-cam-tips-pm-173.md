# Cooling Channel Intersection Cleanup

After drilling cooling channels, program cleaning operations at intersections. Chips collect where channels cross. Use long-reach tools with through-coolant flushing. Verify flow with air pressure test before closing the mold. Blocked channels cause hot spots, warpage, and cycle time increase.
