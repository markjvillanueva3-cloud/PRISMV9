# Trochoidal Milling for Hard Material Slots

8-15% radial, full depth, 3-5× feed. Constant engagement. Slot width independent of tool. For >45 HRC. hyperMILL HSC roughing handles trochoidal. Work-free chips improve tool life by preventing re-cutting of work-hardened material.
