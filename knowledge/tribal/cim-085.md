# Stainless Steel with Work-Hardening Prevention

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

For 304/316 stainless in Cimatron: coated carbide (TiAlN), 80-120 m/min, 0.08-0.12mm feed/tooth, 0.5-1×D axial depth. Never dwell or reduce feed below 0.04mm/tooth — stainless work-hardens. Use climb milling exclusively. Cimatron constant chip load roughing prevents intermittent engagement. Flood coolant mandatory. Adjust tool life expectations down 30% vs standard steel.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `M`
- Operation types: `roughing`
