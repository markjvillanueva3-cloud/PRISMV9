# Plunge Roughing for Extreme L/D

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `drilling` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Vertical drilling motions. 60-70% step-over. For L/D>4, >45 HRC, weak spindles. Axial forces only. Slower but dramatically safer. PowerMill generates efficient plunge patterns. Use when lateral roughing produces unacceptable vibration on deep thin ribs.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `H`
- Operation types: `roughing`
