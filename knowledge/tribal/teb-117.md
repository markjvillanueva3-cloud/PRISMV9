# Cost Optimization with Taguchi Loss Function

**Category:** `optimization` · **Subcategory:** `cycle_time` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `78` · **Source:** `web:tebis-forum`

## Tip

Total cost per part: C_total = C_machine_time + C_tool_cost + C_setup + C_quality_losses. Quality losses follow Taguchi loss function: L = k(y-m)². The optimal parameters minimize C_total, not individual components. Typically requires 5-10% longer cycle time than time-optimized parameters to avoid quality costs from surface finish variation and dimensional drift.
