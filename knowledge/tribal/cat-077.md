# Digital Twin Machining Simulation on 3DEXPERIENCE

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:catia-docs`

## Tip

The 3DEXPERIENCE Digital Twin for machining creates a virtual replica of the physical machine, tooling, and workpiece that can be simulated in real time. Unlike basic NC simulation, the digital twin includes machine controller emulation (virtual controller) that interprets actual G-code, accounting for controller-specific behaviors like look-ahead, acceleration profiles, and interpolation modes. Use this to validate cycle times with ±5% accuracy and detect controller-specific errors before first article.
