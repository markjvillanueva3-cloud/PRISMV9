# GPP Text Encoding — ANSI/ASCII Required

**Category:** `post_processor` · **Subcategory:** `coolant_code` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-community`

## Tip

SolidCAM requires ANSI/ASCII encoding for GPP files and G-code output. UTF-8 or Unicode encoding causes parsing errors and garbled characters in the NC output. When editing GPP files in VS Code, check the encoding indicator in the status bar and convert to ANSI if needed. This is the number-one cause of mysterious post-processor errors after editing.
