# Coffin-Manson Thermal Fatigue for Insert Life

Nf = C×(Δε)^(-β), Δε = α×ΔT, β≈2.0 for carbide. Large temperature swings (dry→flood) accelerate thermal cracking. For PowerMill interrupted operations (island crossing, multiple pockets): commit to dry or continuous flood. Intermittent coolant halves insert life. This is the #1 cause of premature insert failure in mold roughing operations.
