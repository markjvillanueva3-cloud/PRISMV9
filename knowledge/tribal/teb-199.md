# Exergy Analysis for Process Sustainability

**Category:** `optimization` · **Subcategory:** `air_cut` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `75` · **Source:** `web:tebis-forum`

## Tip

Exergy = maximum useful work extractable. Exergy destruction measures irreversibility: Ex_dest = T₀×S_gen. For machining: cutting (70%), friction (15%), chip deformation (10%), coolant (5%). Minimize exergy destruction by optimizing cutting parameters. Tebis parameter selection should consider sustainability alongside productivity and quality.
