# Inconel Requires Dual Ceramic/Carbide Approach

**Category:** `material` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:worknc-inconel`

## Tip

For Inconel (718, 625) in WorkNC, use two strategies: ceramic roughing at 200-400 m/min with round inserts and 0.5-1 mm depth (no coolant, use air blast), or carbide roughing at 15-30 m/min with 6-8% engagement waveform paths (flood coolant at max pressure). Never stop the tool in-cut with either approach. Ceramic cutting intentionally generates heat to soften the workpiece ahead of the cut.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `S`
- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
