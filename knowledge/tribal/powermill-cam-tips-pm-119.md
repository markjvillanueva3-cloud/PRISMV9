# Inconel with Ceramic Insert Roughing

Inconel 718: ceramic at 200-400 m/min, 0.1-0.15mm fz, 1-2mm DOC. No coolant — thermal shock cracks ceramics. Air blast only. Tool life 10-20 min but MRR 3-5× carbide. PowerMill constant engagement essential — interrupted cuts destroy ceramic. Reserve for roughing; finish with carbide. Program tool change at fixed time intervals, not wear-based.
