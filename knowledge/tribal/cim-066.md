# Cimatron Mold Design to NC Programming Integration

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.88` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Cimatron's integrated mold design and NC programming share the same model. When the mold design changes (parting surface, cooling channels, ejector pins), NC operations update automatically. Use 'Manufacturing Link' for associativity. This eliminates reprogramming for design changes — typically saving 2-3 hours per design iteration on complex mold tools.
