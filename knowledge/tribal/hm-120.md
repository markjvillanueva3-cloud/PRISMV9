# 5-Axis Swarf Cutting for Ruled Surfaces

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-docs`

## Tip

hyperMILL swarf cutting uses tool flute length on ruled surfaces in a single pass. 5-10× faster than Z-level for draft walls. Verify surface is developable — swarf on doubly-curved surfaces gouges. Set tilt limits ±3° from surface normal. hyperMILL's collision avoidance auto-tilts when holder interference is detected while maintaining swarf contact.
