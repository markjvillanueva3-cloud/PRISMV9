# Probe Geometry for Tool Wear Compensation

Use Probe Geometry (Manufacturing Extension) to measure critical features like bores and faces between machining operations. When a feature drifts out of tolerance due to tool wear, the probing cycle quantifies the deviation and applies automatic tool wear compensation. This lets you maximize tool life while maintaining accuracy — essential for unattended or lights-out machining of precision parts.
