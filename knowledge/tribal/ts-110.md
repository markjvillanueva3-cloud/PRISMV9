# Best-Fit Alignment Optimizes Part Position in Stock

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-bestfit`

## Tip

TopSolid's best-fit alignment probing measures multiple points on a casting or forging and calculates the optimal part position within the available stock to ensure adequate machining allowance on all surfaces. The algorithm minimizes the maximum stock removal while ensuring minimum wall thickness requirements are met. This is critical for near-net-shape parts where stock distribution is uneven and a poor alignment could expose insufficient material on critical surfaces.
