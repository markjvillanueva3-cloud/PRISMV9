# Material Removal Visualization Shows Stock Progress

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:worknc-removal`

## Tip

WorkNC's material removal simulation displays progressive stock removal color-coded by remaining thickness. Red indicates unmachined areas; green shows target thickness. The stock comparison overlay highlights under-cuts (gouges) in red and excess material in blue. Target zero red areas and minimal blue areas in the final result. Use this to verify uniform finishing allowance before the finish pass.
