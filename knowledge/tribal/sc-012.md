# HSM Smooth Linking — Maintain Feed Rate Through Retracts

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

SolidCAM HSM generates smooth, tangential lead-in/lead-out moves and avoids sharp angles in the toolpath to maintain continuous machine motion. This is essential for high-speed finishing where any sudden direction change causes the controller to decelerate. Always enable smooth linking and set minimum arc radius to match your machine's minimum radius capability.
