# Chamfering Operations

Chamfer mills (45/60/90°), ball-end tracing, spot drills. 3D chamfers: 5-axis normal. SprutCAM edge detection automates paths. Verify width. On turned parts: chamfer tool in turret, C-axis indexing for off-center features. Standard 0.3-0.5mm on all sharp edges.
