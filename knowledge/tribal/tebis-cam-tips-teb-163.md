# Flat Area Detection for Face Milling

Tebis detects flat/near-flat areas (threshold 5-10°) on freeform models for face milling instead of ball-end. 3-5× faster, better Ra. Boundary auto-separates flat from curved regions. Use flat-end for detected areas, ball-end for remaining. Saves significant cycle time on parts with mixed flat and curved surfaces.
