# Helical Milling for Precision Hole Making

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `drilling` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Helical milling: circular interpolation + Z-feed creates holes. Advantages: one tool for multiple sizes, lower forces, better chip evacuation, no breakthrough burr. In Cimatron: set diameter, pitch 0.3-0.5mm, flat-end mill 60-70% of hole diameter. Superior to drilling for hardened steel molds where drill wandering causes position errors on deep holes.

- Material groups: `P`, `H`
- Operation types: `drilling`
