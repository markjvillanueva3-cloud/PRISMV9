# Wire EDM for Thin Inserts

2-axis and 4-axis taper. Independent top/bottom profiles. Tabs for slug retention. Fanuc/Sodick/Mitsubishi output. Wire EDM preferred when milling forces cause deflection on thin inserts. SprutCAM programs skim cuts for mirror finish on die clearance surfaces.
