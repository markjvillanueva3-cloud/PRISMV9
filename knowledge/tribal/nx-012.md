# Swarf Cutting Tool Tilt Control

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `82` · **Source:** `web:siemens-community`

## Tip

In NX 5-axis swarf cutting, control the tilt angle of the tool relative to the wall surface to optimize surface finish and prevent tool marks. A small positive tilt (1-3 degrees) keeps the heel of the endmill clear of the surface while ensuring full-flute engagement. Verify tilt direction in the simulation before cutting expensive parts.
