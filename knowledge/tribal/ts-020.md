# High-Feed Roughing Exploits Face Mill Geometry

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-highfeed`

## Tip

TopSolid supports high-feed milling strategies using button cutters or high-feed face mills with large nose radii and shallow axial depths (0.5-1.5 mm). The strategy uses very high feed rates (3-5 m/min) with minimal axial engagement, converting cutting forces primarily into the spindle axis. Set the stepover to 50-65% of effective cutting diameter and enable chip thinning compensation. Particularly effective for roughing large flat-bottomed cavities in mold bases.
