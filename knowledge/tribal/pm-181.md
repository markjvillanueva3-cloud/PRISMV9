# Constant Scallop vs Fixed Step-Over Decision

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Constant scallop: varies step-over by curvature, 20-30% shorter, uniform quality. Fixed: simpler, predictable. For production molds: constant scallop. For prototypes: fixed step-over. PowerMill supports both per operation. Combine: constant scallop on freeform, fixed on flat regions.

- Operation types: `finishing`
