# Renewal Theory Optimal Replacement

Minimize C(T) = (Cp + Cf×F(T)) / (T×R(T) + M×F(T)). For mold work: Cf >> Cp (scrapped mold component). Replace early. MAXX barrel cutters: higher tool cost (Cp) but even higher Cf means optimal replacement is still early. Track economics per job type.
