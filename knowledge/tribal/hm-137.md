# Constant Scallop Height Finishing

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `adaptive` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-docs`

## Tip

Varies step-over by curvature for uniform scallop. Target 0.005mm for polish-ready. 20-30% shorter than fixed step-over. Essential for mold surfaces. hyperMILL computes local effective radius at each point. Combine with MAXX Finishing on flat regions for maximum cycle time reduction.
