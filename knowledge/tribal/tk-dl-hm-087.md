# Side electrode for inaccessible erosion areas

**Category:** `design` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `document:hypercad-s-v33@p455`

## Tip

Enable the Side electrode option when vertical erosion (Z direction) cannot reach the target area. Erode in XZ, YZ, or XYZ direction. Control joint geometry via: Joint angle (optimized = half the angle between erosion direction and Z), Min. lateral/vertical joint length. Set Lateral path length for collision avoidance. Note: rotational electrodes cannot be combined with side electrodes.
