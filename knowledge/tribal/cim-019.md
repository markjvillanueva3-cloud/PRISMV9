# Boundary Containment for Selective Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Use boundary curves to restrict machining to specific areas of the mold. Create 'Machining Boundaries' from surface edges, sketched curves, or silhouette projections. Set 'Boundary Mode' to 'Inside' for pocket areas, 'Outside' for surrounding regions. Add 0.5mm boundary extension to prevent tool marks at the boundary edge. Chain multiple boundaries for complex containment regions.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
