# Lead/Link Strategy for Smooth Tool Entry

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.89` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-tutorials`

## Tip

Configure leads and links to ensure smooth tool entry and exit. For finishing: use tangential arc leads (radius = 2×tool radius), normal retract at 45°, and 'Smooth Links' between passes. For roughing: use helical or ramp entry (max ramp angle 3° for carbide in steel). Avoid perpendicular plunge entries — they cause tool breakage and leave marks on finished surfaces.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`
- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
