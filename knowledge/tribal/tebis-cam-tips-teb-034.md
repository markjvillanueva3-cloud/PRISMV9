# Steep/Shallow Split Combines Z-Constant and Equidistant Strategies

Tebis automatic steep/shallow detection splits the part into regions and applies Z-constant finishing to steep areas and 3D-equidistant to shallow areas. Set the transition angle (default 30°, adjustable 25-50°). Enable overlap at the transition boundary — typically 2-3 tool-diameter overlap — to eliminate witness lines where strategies meet. The combined approach produces optimal surface quality on complex mold geometries with mixed steep and shallow regions.
