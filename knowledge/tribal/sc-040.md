# iMachining 2D Moating — Break Large Pockets into Efficient Zones

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

Enable Moating in iMachining 2D to subdivide large open pockets into smaller spiral zones separated by thin walls (moats). The Technology Wizard then treats each zone independently, maintaining optimal engagement angles throughout. Moating is especially effective on pockets wider than 5x tool diameter, where a single spiral would lose cutting efficiency in the center.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `2d_pocket`, `roughing`
