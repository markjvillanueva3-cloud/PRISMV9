# Gamma Process for Monotonic Degradation Modeling

**Category:** `optimization` · **Subcategory:** `cycle_time` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `76` · **Source:** `web:tebis-forum`

## Tip

Tool wear is monotonically increasing — Gamma process is more appropriate than Wiener for this constraint. Increments are non-negative with Gamma distribution. RUL = first passage time to threshold. Gamma process prevents the physically impossible 'negative wear' that Wiener can produce. Better for Tebis tool life management on unattended operations.
