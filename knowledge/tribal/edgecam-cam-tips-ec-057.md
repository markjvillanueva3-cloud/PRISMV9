# Knowledge Capture from Expert Programmers

Strategy Manager's knowledge capture records the decision process of expert programmers as they work. Which tool for this feature? What speeds and feeds for this material? What strategy for this pocket shape? These decisions become rules that less experienced programmers can apply. The system effectively multiplies expert knowledge across the team. Review and update captured knowledge quarterly as tooling and techniques evolve.
