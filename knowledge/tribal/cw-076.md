# No-Core Cutting — Eliminate Slug Drop for Small and Fragile Features

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

No-core (coreless) cutting programs the wire path to consume the entire slug through multiple overlapping cuts rather than dropping a solid core. Use no-core for small openings (< 5mm) where a falling slug could damage the wire or workpiece, or for very thin workpieces where the slug might tilt and jam the wire. No-core adds cycle time (3-5x longer than conventional cutting) but eliminates slug-related risks and the need for slug retainers.
