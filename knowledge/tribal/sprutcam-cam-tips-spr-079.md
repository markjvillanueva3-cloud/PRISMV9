# Geodesic Finishing for Complex Freeform Surfaces

Geodesic finishing follows the shortest path along the surface (geodesic curves), producing uniform tool contact patterns. Unlike raster, geodesic adapts to surface curvature naturally. Use for: turbine blades, automotive dies, and aerospace fairings. Set step-over based on target scallop height. SprutCAM computes the geodesic paths numerically — computation time increases with surface complexity.
