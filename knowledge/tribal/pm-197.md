# Collision with Full Assembly Safety Check

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.88` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Include shank, holder, taper, spindle + 0.5mm margin. Shrink-fit for min profile. Extended: 50% feed at 7:1 L/D. Gouge check every finish. Machine simulation catches machine-level interference. Critical for hardened steel where gouge = expensive rework.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `H`
- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
