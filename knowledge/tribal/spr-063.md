# Inconel Machining with Ceramic Tools

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

For Inconel 718 roughing with ceramic inserts: 200-400 m/min surface speed, 0.1-0.15mm feed/tooth, 1-2mm DOC. No coolant — ceramics thermal shock crack under coolant. Use air blast for chip clearing only. SprutCAM's constant engagement roughing is essential — interrupted cuts destroy ceramic inserts. Tool life is short (10-20 min) but MRR is 3-5× higher than carbide. Reserve ceramic for roughing only.
