# Pencil Tracing for Corner Cleanup

After HSM finishing, pencil trace cleans corners/fillets. Ball-end 50-70% of smallest fillet. Both-ways halves cycle time. Essential transition between Z-level and raster. Run as final operation. iMachining roughing + HSM finishing + pencil = complete SolidCAM workflow.
