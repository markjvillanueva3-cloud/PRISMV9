# Assembly Mode for Fixtures — Visualize Workholding in Simulation

**Category:** `fixturing` · **Subcategory:** `vise` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

SolidCAM works in CAD assembly mode to graphically show fixtures, tooling, and vises during simulation. Import your actual fixture models as SolidWorks assembly components and assign them as fixture bodies in SolidCAM. This enables realistic collision checking against vise jaws, clamps, and custom fixtures — not just the workpiece.
