# Pencil Tracing for Corner Cleanup

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `pencil` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.88` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

After main finishing passes, use pencil tracing to clean internal corners and fillets. SprutCAM detects concave regions where the previous tool couldn't reach. Use a ball-end mill 50-70% of the smallest fillet radius. Enable 'Both Ways' for symmetric corners to halve cycle time. Pencil tracing is essential between Z-level and raster finishing — it targets the exact transition zone these strategies miss.
