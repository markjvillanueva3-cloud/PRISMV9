# Dynamic Contour converts to dynamic motion where tool would bind or overload

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `contour` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `document:mastercam-dynamic-milling`

## Tip

Dynamic Contour mills material off walls supporting closed or open chains. The toolpath automatically converts to dynamic motion in areas where a standard contour would cause tool binding or encounter too much material. Use for walls with small radii, variable stock conditions, or forged/cast parts with inconsistent allowance. Does not require closed chains — works on open geometry.

- Operation types: `contouring`, `finishing`
