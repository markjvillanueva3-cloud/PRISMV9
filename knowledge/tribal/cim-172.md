# Plunge Roughing for Deep Narrow Ribs

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `drilling` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Vertical drilling, 60-70% step-over. For L/D>4, >45 HRC, weak spindles. Axial forces (strongest). Slower MRR but safer. Cimatron generates efficient patterns. Use when lateral roughing vibrates on deep thin rib cavities in mold tools.

- Material groups: `H`
- Operation types: `roughing`
