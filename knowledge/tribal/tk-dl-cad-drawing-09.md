# Siemens NX PMI for Model-Based Definition

**Category:** `setup` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `82` · **Source:** `document:cad_drawing_standards@section7`

## Tip

Siemens NX leads the industry in PMI (Product Manufacturing Information) — dimensions, tolerances, and annotations stored directly on the 3D model. This enables Model-Based Definition (MBD) per ASME Y14.41-2019, eliminating 2D drawings entirely. PMI feeds automated CMM programming for inspection. Export as STEP AP242 for interoperability with other CAD systems.
