# Thickness Allowance for Tool Life

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Roughing 0.5mm, semi 0.15mm, finish 0.0mm. Hardened: 0.3→0.15→0.05→0.0mm. Each removes only its layer. 40-60% longer tool life vs 2-pass. PowerMill stock model tracks remaining material accurately for each progressive operation.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
