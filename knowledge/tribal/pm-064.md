# Composite Trimming with 5-Axis Routing

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:autodesk-university`

## Tip

For CFRP/GFRP composite trimming: use PCD or diamond-coated compression routers, 200-400 m/min, 0.02-0.05mm feed/tooth. Program 5-axis tool normal to the surface edge for clean trim. Use PowerMill's 'Curve Machining' strategy along the trim line. Vacuum clamping is essential — composites can't handle mechanical clamp forces. Dust extraction required — composite dust is hazardous.
