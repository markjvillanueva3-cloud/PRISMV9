# Feature Recognition for Hole Automation

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `pocket` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

Recognize through/blind/countersink/counterbore/tapped holes. Batch assign: G81 through, G83 peck, G84 tap. Sort by diameter for tool changes. Tolerance 0.01mm. Review — filleted pockets occasionally misidentified. SprutCAM batch hole processing saves significant time on complex parts with 50+ holes.
