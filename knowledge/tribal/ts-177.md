# TopSolid Multi-Material Additive — Gradient Structures

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `82` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-docs`

## Tip

TopSolid supports multi-material DED where different alloys are deposited in the same part — for example, Inconel 625 for corrosion resistance on the outer surface and carbon steel for the structural core. Program material transitions with overlap zones (2-3 layers) where both powders mix for metallurgical bonding. TopSolid assigns different deposition parameters (laser power, feed rate, powder flow) per material zone. After building, machine the final geometry with tools and parameters appropriate for each material zone.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `S`
- Operation types: `general`
