# Burnishing Pass for Mirror-Quality Mold Surfaces

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:worknc-burnish`

## Tip

WorkNC can generate burnishing (spring pass) toolpaths that run at zero stock allowance with very light engagement to improve surface finish through material compression rather than cutting. Use a ball-nose cutter at 50-70% of normal cutting speed, zero depth of cut, and fine stepover (50% of finishing stepover). This can improve surface finish from Ra 0.4 to Ra 0.1 on pre-hardened mold steel without manual polishing.
