# Swarf Cutting for Ruled Surfaces and Thin Walls

SURFCAM Swarf cutting uses the side of a flat or tapered end mill to machine ruled surfaces in a single pass. This is far more efficient than ball-nose finishing for straight-ruled surfaces such as turbine vanes and thin walls. The tool axis tilts to maintain full side contact with the ruled surface. Set the tool axis lead/lag angle to 0° for true ruled surfaces. For near-ruled surfaces with slight curvature, add 0.5-1° lead angle to prevent gouging at concave regions.
