# Multi-Axis Contour for Complex Edge Trimming

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `contour` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `83` · **Source:** `web:fusion360-docs`

## Tip

Multi-Axis Contour follows a curve or edge while the tool tilts to maintain a specified lead/lag and side-tilt angle. Use it for deburring, edge breaking, or trimming composite parts where the tool must stay normal (or at a fixed angle) to the surface edge. Set collision checking against the part model to prevent holder interference on deep features.
