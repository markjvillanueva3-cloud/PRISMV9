# iMachining Channel Stepping — Narrow Feature Strategy

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `adaptive` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `82` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-community`

## Tip

When geometry contains narrow channels between islands, iMachining automatically switches to channel-stepping mode with modified trochoidal cuts. The acceleration and deceleration rates are controlled at material engagement/disengagement points. For very narrow slots (< 1.5x tool diameter), ensure your tool stick-out is minimized and consider a smaller tool to allow the morphing spiral room to work.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `slot_milling`, `pocket_milling`
