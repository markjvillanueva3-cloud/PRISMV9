# Uncertainty Budget for Mold Cavity Dimensions

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `81` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-forum`

## Tip

RSS: machine (±0.003mm), tool (±0.005mm), deflection (±0.008mm), thermal (±0.005mm), measurement (±0.002mm). Total: ±0.012mm. MAXX barrel cutters reduce deflection component (stiffer than ball-end at equivalent reach) — lower RSS total enables tighter tolerances from the same setup.
