# Plunge Roughing for Deep Narrow Features

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-docs`

## Tip

Vertical motions, 60-70% step-over. Axial forces (strongest). For L/D>4, >45 HRC, weak spindles. hyperMILL generates efficient patterns. Use when lateral roughing vibrates on deep thin ribs. Follow with light finishing passes.
