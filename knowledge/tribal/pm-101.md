# Sobol Sensitivity Indices for Parameter Ranking

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.77` · **Source:** `web:powermill-forum`

## Tip

First-order Si and total-order STi. Finishing: step-over (Si=0.35, STi=0.42), feed (0.25/0.33), speed (0.18/0.25). Gap reveals interactions. High STi + low Si = important through interactions only. Focus DOE on top 2-3 STi parameters. For PowerMill programs, step-over is almost always the most sensitive parameter for surface finish quality.
