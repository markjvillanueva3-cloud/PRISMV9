# Inconel Machining with Ceramic Inserts

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:tebis-docs`

## Tip

For Inconel 718 roughing in Tebis: ceramic inserts at 200-400 m/min, 0.1-0.15mm feed/tooth, 1-2mm DOC. No coolant — ceramics thermal shock crack. Air blast for chip clearing only. Tool life is short (10-20 min) but MRR is 3-5× higher than carbide. Use Tebis constant engagement strategy — interrupted cuts destroy ceramic inserts. Reserve ceramic for roughing only, finish with carbide.
