# Hybrid DED for Turbine Blade Repair

Alternate deposition and machining. 30-50% overlap, 3-5 layer intervals. Reduces buy-to-fly 20:1 → 3:1. For turbine blade tip repair: DED deposits superalloy, NX machines to airfoil profile. Saves $10K+ per blade vs replacement.
