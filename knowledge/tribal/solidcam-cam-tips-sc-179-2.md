# Flat Area Detection for Strategy

SolidCAM detects flat areas (5-10° threshold). Use flat-end finishing on detected areas, ball-end on curved. 3-5× faster on flat regions. Combine in same program. Critical for parts with mixed flat and freeform surfaces.
