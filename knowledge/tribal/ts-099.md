# Stainless Steel Strategy Prevents Work Hardening

**Category:** `material` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-stainless`

## Tip

For austenitic stainless steels (304, 316, 321) in TopSolid, the primary challenge is work hardening. Never allow the tool to rub—maintain positive chip load at all times by using feed rates above 0.05 mm/tooth. Use adaptive roughing with 10-15% engagement and 1.5-2x diameter axial depth. Enable constant chip load compensation in corners. Cutting speed: 80-120 m/min for carbide. Always use flood coolant and avoid interrupted cuts where possible.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `M`
- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
