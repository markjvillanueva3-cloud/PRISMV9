# Disc Cutter for Slots and Grooves

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `boring` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.82` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

Define disc geometry: diameter, thickness, bore, teeth. 3+2 positioning per slot. Speed based on outer diameter. Feed accounts for high tooth count (20-60). Flood coolant for evacuation. Disc cutters produce more consistent slot widths than end mills. SprutCAM supports full disc cutter simulation.
