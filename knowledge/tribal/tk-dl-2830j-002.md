# EWMA λ selection: smaller λ for smaller shifts

**Category:** `quality` · **Subcategory:** `spc` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `document:mit2830j@lecture9`

## Tip

EWMA smoothing parameter λ (called r in some texts): use λ=0.05-0.1 for detecting shifts <0.5σ, λ=0.1-0.25 for 0.5-1.5σ shifts, λ=0.4+ for large shifts (but at that point Shewhart may be better). Lower λ gives more memory of past observations. Trade-off: smaller λ = slower false alarm rate but longer detection delay for large shifts.
