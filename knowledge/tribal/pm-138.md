# Composite CFRP/GFRP Trimming

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.83` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

PCD or diamond-coated compression routers, 200-400 m/min, 0.02-0.05mm fz. 5-axis tool normal for clean edges. Vacuum fixturing only. Zig cutting for consistent fiber direction. Dust extraction mandatory (hazardous). PowerMill programs along trim curves with automatic approach/retract. Delamination-free cuts require sharp tools and controlled feed.

- Material groups: `N`
