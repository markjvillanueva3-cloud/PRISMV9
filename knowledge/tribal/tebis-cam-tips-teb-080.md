# Stainless Steel with Work-Hardening Prevention

For 304/316 stainless in Tebis: coated carbide (TiAlN/AlCrN), 80-120 m/min, 0.08-0.12mm feed/tooth, 0.5-1×D axial depth. Stainless work-hardens — never dwell or reduce feed below 0.04mm/tooth. Use climb milling exclusively. Tebis constant chip load roughing prevents the intermittent engagement that causes work-hardening. Flood coolant mandatory for chip evacuation and heat management.
