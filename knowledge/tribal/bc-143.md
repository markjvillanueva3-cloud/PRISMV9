# BobCAM for SOLIDWORKS Design-to-Manufacturing Workflow

**Category:** `setup` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:bobcad-docs`

## Tip

BobCAM for SOLIDWORKS enables a seamless design-to-manufacturing workflow where the CAD model and CAM program live in the same file. When the designer modifies the SOLIDWORKS model, BobCAM flags affected operations for regeneration. The programmer reviews changes, regenerates toolpaths, and re-posts without re-importing geometry. For concurrent engineering teams, this eliminates the STEP/IGES translation step that causes 40-60% of CAM programming rework. Lock finished operations to prevent accidental regeneration from unrelated design changes.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
