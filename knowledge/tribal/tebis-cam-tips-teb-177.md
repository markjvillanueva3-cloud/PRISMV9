# Additive DED Path Planning for Repair

Tebis DED: contour+fill patterns, 30-50% bead overlap, 60-80% layer height. Interleave subtractive every 3-5 layers. For mold repair, feature addition, near-net-shape. Reduces machining on expensive materials 60-80%. Define deposition speed based on wire/powder feed rate.
