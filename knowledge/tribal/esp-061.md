# Sinker EDM Vector Erosion for Non-Vertical Surfaces

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `process_engineering`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:esprit-sinker-edm`

## Tip

ESPRIT's vector erosion drives the electrode along a defined vector direction rather than straight Z-plunge. This allows machining angled surfaces, undercuts, and complex 3D cavities with a single electrode setup. Define the vector angle and depth, and ESPRIT calculates the compound XYZ motion. Vector erosion eliminates multiple electrode orientations needed with conventional plunging. Combine with orbital motion for 3D vector-orbital paths in complex mold cavities.
