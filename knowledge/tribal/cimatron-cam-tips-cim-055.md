# Barrel Cutter Strategies for Large Step-Over Finishing

Barrel cutters (segment radius 100-500mm) allow 3-5× wider step-over than ball-end mills for the same scallop height. In Cimatron, define barrel geometry precisely: barrel radius, tip fillet, taper angle. Use automatic tilt to maintain the barrel contact zone on the surface. Verify contact pattern — incorrect tilt causes gouging. Best for large automotive dies and mold surfaces.
