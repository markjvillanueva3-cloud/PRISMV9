# SURFCAM Multi-Axis Automatic Tool Axis Control Methods

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.89` · **Source:** `web:surfcam-docs`

## Tip

SURFCAM provides multiple tool axis control methods for multi-axis machining: Normal to Surface (default), Lead/Lag angles, To/From Point, To/From Line, Interpolate Between Two Axes, and Relative to Surface Normal with limits. Choose 'Normal to Surface' for uniform ball-nose finishing, 'To Point' for convergent turbine blade patterns, and 'Interpolate' for smooth transitions between two orientations across a surface. Each method suits different part geometries — incorrect choice causes poor surface finish or collisions.
