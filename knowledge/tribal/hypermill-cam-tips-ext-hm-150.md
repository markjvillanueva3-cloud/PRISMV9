# Stochastic Chatter Avoidance with Stability Lobes

Chatter stochastic: damping ±15%, hardness ±5%, overhang ±0.5mm. P(chatter) contours via Monte Carlo of stability lobes. Select RPM/DOC with P(chatter)<5%. MAXX Machining's barrel cutters have different stability characteristics than ball-end — the contact pattern affects the regenerative chatter mechanism.
