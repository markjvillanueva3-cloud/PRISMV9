# 5-Axis Boundary Control for Region Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

Use boundary curves to restrict 5-axis machining to specific surface regions. SprutCAM supports: 'Inside Boundary' (machine only within), 'Outside Boundary' (exclude region), and 'Between Boundaries' (machine a band). This enables selective finishing of specific zones without regenerating the entire toolpath. Combine multiple boundaries with Boolean operations for complex region definitions.
