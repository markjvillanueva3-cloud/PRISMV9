# Plunge Roughing for Deep Pockets and Slots

Plunge (Z-axis) roughing drills into the stock vertically, then repositions and plunges again. In Tebis, set plunge step-over to 60-70% of tool diameter for coverage. Plunge roughing is effective for: narrow deep slots (L/D > 4), hard materials (>45 HRC), and weak machine spindles. Forces are primarily axial (strongest direction). Slower MRR than lateral roughing.
