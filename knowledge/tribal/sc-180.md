# Helical Milling for Holes

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

Circular + Z-feed. One tool for multiple sizes. Lower forces, no burr. Flat-end 60-70% of hole. SolidCAM helical milling operation. Superior for hardened steel. iMachining doesn't handle helical holes — use standard helical milling operation.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `H`
- Operation types: `drilling`
