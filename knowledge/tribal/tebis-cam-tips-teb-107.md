# Uncertainty Budget for Mold Cavity Dimensions

Total uncertainty for mold cavity machining: machine positioning (±0.003mm), tool diameter tolerance (±0.005mm H6), tool deflection (±0.008mm for 6mm ball at 100N), thermal growth (±0.005mm over 4hr cycle), measurement (±0.002mm CMM). RSS total: ±0.012mm. This determines achievable tolerance. For ±0.01mm specs, improve largest contributor (tool deflection — larger diameter or shorter).
