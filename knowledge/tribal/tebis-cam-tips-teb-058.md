# Flowline Finishing for Turbine and Aerofoil Surfaces

Flowline finishing follows user-defined flow curves across the surface. Define start and end boundary curves — Tebis interpolates intermediate toolpath lines between them. Ideal for turbine blades (hub-to-shroud), automotive body panels, and aerofoils where the tool should follow the natural surface flow. Step-over is measured perpendicular to the flow direction for uniform scallop height.
