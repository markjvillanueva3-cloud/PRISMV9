# Hub Finishing with Constant-Scallop Step-Over

In NX Turbomachinery Hub Finishing, use Constant Scallop step-over rather than constant distance to maintain uniform surface quality across the hub's varying curvature. NX adjusts the physical distance between passes based on local surface curvature, producing tighter spacing in concave regions and wider spacing on convex areas. Target scallop height of 0.005 mm for production turbomachinery and 0.01 mm for prototypes.
