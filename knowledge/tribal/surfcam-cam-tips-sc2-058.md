# No-Core Wire EDM Eliminates Slug Removal

SURFCAM no-core wire EDM disintegrates the slug into small pieces during cutting rather than producing a single droppable slug. This is used when the slug cannot fall away safely (e.g., large slugs that could damage the lower guide, or complex shapes that would jam). The toolpath alternates between profile segments, cutting the slug into strips that are flushed away. Cycle time is 2-3x longer than conventional drop-slug cutting but eliminates manual slug handling.
