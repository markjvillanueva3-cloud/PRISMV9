# Tube Milling for Internal Passages

Cimatron tube milling machines internal passages and ports using 5-axis access through openings. Define centerline curve and cross-section profiles. Tool axis follows centerline tangent for smooth motion. Verify tool length vs passage depth — holder collision in deep ports is the most common failure. Use for cylinder heads, manifolds, and mold cooling channels.
