# 5-Axis Multi-Surface Finishing with Lead/Lag Control

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:esprit-5axis`

## Tip

For complex multi-surface 5-axis finishing, control the lead and lag angles to optimize cutter contact. A 10-15 degree lead angle (tool tilted forward in feed direction) with a ball-nose cutter moves the contact point away from the tool tip where surface speed is zero, improving surface finish and tool life. Avoid lag angles that cause the heel of the cutter to gouge the surface. ESPRIT's automatic tilt optimization can set these per-surface.
