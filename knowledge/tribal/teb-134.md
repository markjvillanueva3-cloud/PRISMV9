# Plastics and PEEK Medical Device Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `83` · **Source:** `web:tebis-tutorials`

## Tip

For plastics (PEEK, Delrin, nylon) in Tebis: single-flute or 2-flute uncoated tools with high positive rake, 200-500 m/min, 0.1-0.2mm feed/tooth. Avoid excessive heat — plastics melt, not cut. Use compressed air cooling. For PEEK medical implants, use diamond-coated tools (no metal contamination for biocompatibility). Down-cut direction prevents lifting thin plastic features.
