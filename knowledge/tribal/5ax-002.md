# M126 Shortest Angular Traverse prevents long rotary moves on Hurco

**Category:** `cnc_programming` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `94` · **Source:** `document:hurco-5axis-post`

## Tip

Hurco M126 (Shortest Angular Traverse) forces the control to take the shortest rotational path to commanded position regardless of sign. Output M126 in program header after safety line. Cancel with M127 when specific rotary direction is required (e.g., winding around a helix). Without M126, a C-axis move from C350 to C10 takes 340 degrees the long way instead of 20 degrees the short way.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `5axis`, `multiaxis`
