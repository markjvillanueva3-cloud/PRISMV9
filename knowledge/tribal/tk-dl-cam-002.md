# Rest machining from top-down beats pencil milling in corners

**Category:** `strategy` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `document:inventorcam-3d-hsm@ch2.11`

## Tip

Pencil milling in vertical corners forces both the flute and radius into full contact simultaneously, creating adverse cutting conditions. Rest machining picks corners from the top down with proper cutting engagement. Use rest machining instead of pencil milling for internal corners. Pencil milling is still appropriate for fillet blending along edges.
