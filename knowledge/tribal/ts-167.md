# TopSolid Gear Machining — Hobbing, Shaping, and Skiving

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-docs`

## Tip

TopSolid'Cam includes gear machining modules for hobbing (cylindrical gears), gear shaping (internal gears), and power skiving (both internal and external gears on mill-turn machines). Define the gear parameters (module, number of teeth, pressure angle, helix angle, profile shift) and TopSolid generates the toolpath and calculates the synchronization between workpiece rotation and tool motion. For power skiving on multi-task machines, TopSolid calculates the crossing angle and feed rate from the gear geometry. This eliminates dedicated gear cutting machines for small-batch production.

- Operation types: `turning`, `milling`
