# Robust Material Hardness Tolerance in Parameters

P20 mold steel varies ±2 HRC across a block (28-34 typical). H13 varies ±1.5 HRC (48-52). Design Tebis cutting parameters that tolerate this range: moderate speeds (120-160 m/min for P20) with consistent chip load produce stable results. Extreme high-speed parameters work at nominal hardness but fail unpredictably at the hard end of the distribution.
