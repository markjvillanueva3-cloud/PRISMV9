# Sensitivity Analysis for Parameter Ranking

Vary each parameter ±10%, measure impact. Typical ranking: step-over (35% of finish variation), feed (25%), speed (20%), DOC (15%), runout (5%). Focus on top 2 parameters — 60% of variation. For Cimatron mold programs, step-over selection is the single most impactful decision for finish quality, followed by feed rate. DOC and speed are secondary optimization targets.
