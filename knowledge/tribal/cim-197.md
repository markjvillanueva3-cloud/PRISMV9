# Boundary Management with Boolean Operations

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Create from edges, silhouettes, sketches, steep/shallow. Chain with union/intersection/subtraction. 0.5mm extension to prevent marks. Named sets for reuse. Boundary-based selective machining reduces cycle 20-40% when reworking specific regions only.

- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
