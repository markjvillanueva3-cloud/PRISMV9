# Thickness Allowance for Progressive Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-docs`

## Tip

Roughing 0.5mm, semi 0.15mm, finish 0.0mm. Hardened: 0.3→0.15→0.05→0.0mm. 40-60% longer tool life. hyperMILL IPW tracks remaining material accurately. MAXX barrel finish operates on the final pass with zero allowance.

- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
