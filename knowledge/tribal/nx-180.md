# Plunge Roughing for Thin-Wall Titanium

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `pocket` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.84` · **Source:** `web:siemens-nx-docs`

## Tip

Axial forces only. For deep pockets in thin-wall structures where lateral forces cause wall deflection. 60-70% step-over. Slower MRR but wall integrity preserved. NX programs efficient plunge patterns. Follow with light finishing passes.
