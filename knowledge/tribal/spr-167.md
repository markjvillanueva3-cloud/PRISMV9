# Chance-Constrained Turning Design

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.76` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

P(diameter_error ≤ tol) ≥ 99.7% for medical. Convert: μ + 3σ ≤ tol. Tighter than typical 95% — medical devices require Cpk ≥ 1.33 minimum, often 1.67. SprutCAM parameters must account for bar stock variation, thermal drift, and tool wear simultaneously.
