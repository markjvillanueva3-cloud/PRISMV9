# Point Distribution Finishing for Complex Freeform

Point distribution finishing maps tool contact points uniformly across freeform surfaces regardless of surface parameterization. Unlike raster or Z-level, it produces consistent scallop height on surfaces with varying curvature. Set 'Target Scallop Height' directly (e.g., 0.005mm). Best for aerospace and automotive die surfaces where uniform finish quality is critical.
