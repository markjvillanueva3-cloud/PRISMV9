# Graphite Electrode Machining Strategies

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `process_engineering`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

For graphite electrodes: uncoated carbide, 2-flute, 15,000-24,000 RPM, 0.05-0.08mm feed/tooth. No coolant — vacuum dust extraction mandatory (graphite dust is hazardous and conductive). Roughing: offset area clear with 0.1mm stock. Finishing: 3D offset or raster with 0.02mm step-over. Cimatron tracks electrode undersizing automatically per roughing/finishing designation.
