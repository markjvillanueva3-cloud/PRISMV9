# Radial Finishing for Circular and Dome Shapes

TopSolid's radial finishing generates passes radiating from a central point, ideal for dome-shaped, circular, or rotationally symmetric features. The passes fan out like spokes, and the stepover is measured as angular spacing at the center. Use 1-3° angular increments depending on surface quality requirements. Combine with a final spiral pass to clean up the hub area where radial passes converge.
