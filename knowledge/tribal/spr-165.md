# Mutual Information for SPC Feature Selection

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.76` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

I(X;Y) selects top-k features with highest dependence on quality. Reduces false alarms. For turned parts: 3-5 features (diameter, roundness, Ra, concentricity, taper) capture 90% of quality info. Focus monitoring on these, ignore irrelevant measurements.
