# Exergy Analysis for Sustainable Machining

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `75` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-forum`

## Tip

Exergy destruction = T₀×S_gen. Cutting 70%, friction 15%, deformation 10%, coolant 5%. MAXX barrel cutters: wider cut = more material per pass = better exergy efficiency per unit removed. Compare barrel vs ball-end exergy to quantify sustainability benefit of MAXX Machining.
