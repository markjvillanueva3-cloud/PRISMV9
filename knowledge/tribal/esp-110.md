# Titanium Machining Strategy with Controlled Engagement

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:esprit-materials`

## Tip

Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) requires strict engagement control in ESPRIT. Use ProfitMilling with 10-15% radial engagement, moderate axial depth (1x diameter), and consistent chip load (0.05-0.1mm/tooth). Surface speed: 40-80 m/min with carbide, 150-250 m/min with ceramic (roughing only). High-pressure through-spindle coolant (50-70 bar) is essential for chip evacuation and thermal management. Never let the tool dwell in the cut — always maintain feed to prevent work hardening.
