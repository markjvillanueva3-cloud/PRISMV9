# Eccentric Turning with Polar Interpolation

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `contour` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.81` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

X+C interpolation for cam lobes, ellipses, polygons. SprutCAM generates polar toolpath from 2D contour. Max eccentricity limited by X-axis acceleration. For pump rotors and automotive cam shafts. Verify motion profile before cutting — exceeding axis limits causes following error.
