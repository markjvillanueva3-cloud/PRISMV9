# Titanium Roughing with Vortex and Flood Coolant

For Ti-6Al-4V: use Vortex with 8-10% radial engagement, 1×D axial depth, 45-60 m/min cutting speed, 0.08-0.12mm feed/tooth. Flood coolant at 70+ bar through-spindle is essential — titanium's low thermal conductivity causes heat buildup. Use AlTiN-coated carbide or ceramic inserts. Never recut chips — titanium chips work-harden and destroy cutting edges. Monitor spindle load continuously.
