# Z-Level Finishing with Constant Cusp Height

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.88` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

In Z-Level finishing, enable 'Variable Step-Down' to maintain constant cusp height across varying surface slopes. For steep walls (>60°), use 0.15mm step-down; for shallow areas, the system automatically increases step-down. Set 'Overlap' to 10% of step-over to eliminate witness lines between Z-levels.
