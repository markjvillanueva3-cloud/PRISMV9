# ProfitTurning Hard Turning with CBN/Ceramic Inserts

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `web:esprit-profitturning`

## Tip

For hard turning (>50 HRC) in ESPRIT ProfitTurning, use CBN or ceramic inserts with 0.05-0.15mm depth of cut and high surface speed (150-250 m/min for CBN, 300-600 m/min for ceramic). ProfitTurning's constant engagement is critical here — engagement spikes that a carbide insert would survive will instantly fracture CBN. Enable 'constant speed control' to maintain SFM as the diameter changes, and avoid interrupted cuts with ceramic inserts.
