# iMachining Morphing Spiral — Maximize Engagement

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `adaptive` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

iMachining 2D uses a patented morphing spiral that gradually conforms to pocket geometry instead of simple racetrack offsets. This keeps the cutter engaged in material nearly 100% of the time, inserting trochoidal loops around corners and islands to maintain constant chip load. Always let the wizard generate the spiral — manual offset patterns cannot match its efficiency.

- Operation types: `pocket_milling`, `roughing`
