# Contour Turning Nose Radius Compensation

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `contour` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

G41/G42 for nose radius compensation. Set TNR precisely — errors cause dimensional inaccuracies on tapers and curves. CNMG: TNR 0.4/0.8/1.2mm. Larger TNR = better finish but can't do tight corners. SprutCAM applies compensation to complex contours automatically.
