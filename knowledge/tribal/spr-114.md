# Morris Screening for Many Factors

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.76` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

Elementary effects: μ* and σ per factor. High μ*=important, high σ=interactions. Screen 10+ SprutCAM parameters, then detailed DOE on top 3-4. For mill-turn with many adjustable parameters (speed, feed, DOC per turret + milling spindle), Morris identifies which actually matter before expensive testing.
