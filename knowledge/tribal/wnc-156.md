# Waveform Entry Strategy — Helical and Ramp Approach

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `spiral` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:worknc-docs`

## Tip

Waveform roughing requires controlled entry into the material to establish the constant-engagement condition. WorkNC uses helical entry (spiral down into a pocket) or ramp entry (linear descent at 2-5° angle) based on the geometry. Set the helical entry diameter to 1.5-2x tool diameter for adequate chip evacuation during the entry spiral. For blind pockets, the helical entry creates a pilot hole first, then the waveform path expands outward. Never use plunge entry with waveform roughing — it creates instantaneous full engagement that can break the tool.
