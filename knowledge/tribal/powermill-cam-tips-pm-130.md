# Spiral Finishing for Flat Surfaces

Continuous spiral eliminates direction changes that leave witness marks on flat pocket floors. Set step-over for target scallop. Climb milling direction. PowerMill spiral auto-computes center start and expands to boundary. Best for visible flat surfaces on consumer products and mold shutoff faces where surface quality directly affects part appearance.
