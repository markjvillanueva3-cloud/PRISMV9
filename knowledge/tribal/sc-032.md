# GPP Hexadecimal Character Codes — Handle Special Characters

**Category:** `post_processor` · **Subcategory:** `controller_quirk` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `79` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-community`

## Tip

In GPPL, use hexadecimal ASCII conversion to output special characters that would otherwise be interpreted as GPPL syntax. This prevents encoding issues when your G-code requires percent signs, brackets, or other reserved characters. Always test special character output against your controller's requirements — some Fanuc controls need specific comment delimiters.
