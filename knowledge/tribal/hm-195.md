# Surface Extension for Clean Exit

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-docs`

## Tip

Extend 2-5mm for clean exit. hyperMILL creates extensions automatically. Global Fitting improves extension quality. Prevents deceleration marks and corner deviation. Mandatory on visible surfaces and Class-A automotive die faces.
