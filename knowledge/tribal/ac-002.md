# Automation Center colouring functions identify feature types by surface geometry

**Category:** `automation` · **Subcategory:** `lights_out` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `90` · **Source:** `document:hypermill-automation-center`

## Tip

hyperMILL Automation Center includes smart colouring functions: Colouring holes, Colouring planes, Colouring lines/arcs, Colouring fillets (inner/outer), Colouring rotational elements, Colouring cylinders/chamfers. These automatically assign colours based on geometry analysis, enabling feature-based machining rules. Use Search inner/outer fillet to find blend radii. Colour assignments persist and drive downstream toolpath selection.
