# Adaptive Roughing with Constant Chip Load

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `adaptive` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.89` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

SprutCAM's Adaptive roughing maintains constant chip load by adjusting the toolpath to keep radial engagement below a maximum percentage (typically 8-15% of tool diameter). Set 'Max Engagement' in the 'Adaptive' roughing strategy parameters. This allows full axial depth (1×D or more) with 3-5× higher feed rates than conventional roughing, dramatically extending tool life in steel and titanium.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `S`
- Operation types: `roughing`
