# GibbsCAM process templates standardize operation sequences across programmers

Create process templates in GibbsCAM that define the operation sequence, tool selection rules, and cutting parameters for common part types. A 'Prismatic Part Roughing Template' might specify: (1) face with 80mm facemill at 200 SFM, (2) VoluMill rough with 20mm endmill at 300 SFM, (3) rest-rough with 10mm endmill, (4) finish contour with 10mm ball nose. Store templates as .vnc files that programmers load and adapt. This ensures consistency across shifts and programmers — especially important for AS9100/ISO shops where process standardization is audited.
