# GPR outperforms other ML models for MRR prediction (RMSE 0.9234)

Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) consistently outperforms ANN, SVM, and Random Forest for predicting Wire EDM Material Removal Rate across different materials. Research shows average RMSE values of 0.9234 for MRR and 3.0216 for surface roughness (Sa). GPR's uncertainty quantification also provides confidence intervals on predictions, valuable for production planning.
