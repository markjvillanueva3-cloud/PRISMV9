# Waveform Roughing Optimizes Tool Load for Longer Life

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `94` · **Source:** `web:worknc-waveform`

## Tip

WorkNC Waveform Roughing maintains constant tool engagement by dynamically adjusting the toolpath to prevent engagement spikes in corners and narrow regions. The algorithm computes intermediate Z-step calculations that account for tool load, enabling 2-3x deeper axial cuts and 2-3x higher feed rates compared to conventional Z-level roughing. Set maximum engagement to 8-15% of cutter diameter for steel and 15-25% for aluminum.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `N`
- Operation types: `roughing`, `3d_roughing`
