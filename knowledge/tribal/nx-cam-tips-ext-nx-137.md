# NX CAM Express vs NX CAM Advanced Features

NX CAM Express provides: 2.5-axis milling, basic 3-axis, turning, and drilling. NX CAM adds: 5-axis simultaneous, advanced roughing strategies, turbomachinery, wire EDM, additive, and VBM. Key decision point: if your work requires simultaneous 5-axis or complex multi-axis strategies, CAM Advanced is necessary. Express is sufficient for 3+2 positional work, prismatic milling, and standard turning.
