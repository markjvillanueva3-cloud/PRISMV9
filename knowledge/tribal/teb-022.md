# Plunge Roughing Removes Deep Pocket Material Efficiently

**Category:** `roughing` · **Subcategory:** `step_over` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:tebis-tutorials`

## Tip

For deep cavities with depth > 4xD, consider Tebis plunge roughing that moves the tool axially like a drill, stepping over in XY. This puts cutting forces along the spindle axis where the machine is strongest. Step-over is typically 60-75% of tool diameter. Works best with indexable insert drills or plunge mills. Not suitable for thin walls or floors — switch to conventional roughing for the last 2-3 Z levels.
