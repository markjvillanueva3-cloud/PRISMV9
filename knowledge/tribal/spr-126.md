# Helical Milling for Precision Holes

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `drilling` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.86` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

Circular interpolation + Z-feed. One tool for multiple sizes. Lower forces, no burr, better evacuation than drilling. Diameter = hole size, pitch 0.3-0.5mm, flat-end 60-70% of hole. SprutCAM helical milling operation handles geometry automatically. Superior for hardened steel where drills wander.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `H`
- Operation types: `drilling`
