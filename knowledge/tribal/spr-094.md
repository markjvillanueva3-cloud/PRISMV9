# Predictive Maintenance from SprutCAM Cycle Data

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.79` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-forum`

## Tip

Track cycle time trend across production runs. A gradual increase (5-10% over 50 parts) indicates: spindle bearing wear (increased settling time), ball screw backlash growth (more compensation moves), or guideway wear (slower acceleration). Cross-reference with machine maintenance logs. Use this data to schedule preventive maintenance before accuracy degrades. SprutCAM's estimated vs actual cycle time gap is the key indicator.
