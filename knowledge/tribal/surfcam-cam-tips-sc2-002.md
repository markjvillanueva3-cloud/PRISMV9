# TrueMill High-Efficiency Roughing with Full Flute Depth

TrueMill enables aggressive axial depths (up to full flute length) by maintaining controlled radial engagement at 5-15% of cutter diameter. The combination of shallow radial / deep axial engagement spreads heat and wear uniformly across the entire cutting edge. For a 12mm 4-flute carbide end mill in 4140 steel, use 12mm axial depth, 1.2mm radial engagement, 8000 RPM, and 3200 mm/min feed rate. This achieves higher MRR than conventional 1xD axial with 50% radial.
