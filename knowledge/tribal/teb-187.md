# Spiral Finishing for Flat Pocket Floors

**Category:** `finishing` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:tebis-docs`

## Tip

Continuous spiral eliminates direction-change witness marks on flat surfaces. Set step-over for target scallop. Climb milling. Tebis auto-computes center start and expands to boundary. Best for visible flat surfaces on consumer product molds and shutoff faces where quality directly affects appearance.
