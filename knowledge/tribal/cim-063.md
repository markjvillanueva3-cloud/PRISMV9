# Geodesic Finishing for Uniform Coverage

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.83` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Geodesic finishing follows the shortest path along surfaces (geodesic curves), producing uniform tool contact regardless of surface parameterization. Unlike raster, geodesic adapts to curvature naturally. Set step-over based on target scallop height. Best for doubly-curved surfaces where raster produces inconsistent scallop. Computation time increases with surface complexity.
