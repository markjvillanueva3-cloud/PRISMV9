# Probing for In-Process Verification

SolidCAM programs probe cycles. Verify stock, WCS, dims between ops. Output for Renishaw/Heidenhain/Blum. Store for SPC. Verify iMachining rough allowance before HSM finish to prevent overload. iMachining's predictable stock makes probing results more consistent.
