# Swarf Cutting for Ruled Surfaces and Draft Walls

Tebis swarf cutting uses the tool's flute length to machine ruled surfaces in a single pass. Define the drive surface (wall) and check surface (floor). The tool axis follows the surface ruling direction. Swarf cutting is 5-10× faster than Z-level for draft walls. Verify the surface is truly developable — swarf on doubly-curved surfaces causes gouging. Set tool tilt limits to ±3° from surface normal.
