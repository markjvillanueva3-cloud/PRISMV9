# Spiral for Shutoff Face Quality

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Continuous spiral eliminates witness marks on flat shutoff faces. Step-over per scallop formula. Climb milling. Auto center-start expanding to boundary. Surface quality directly affects mold sealing and part appearance. Best finishing strategy for flat-to-near-flat shutoff regions.
