# Dynamic Mill maintains constant chip load via radial engagement control

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `pocket` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:mastercam-docs`

## Tip

Mastercam Dynamic Mill toolpaths limit radial engagement to 5-15% of cutter diameter while using full flute-length axial depth (1-2x Dc). The engine continuously recalculates stepover to maintain constant chip load through corners and direction changes. This reduces side load on the tool, enabling 60-70% higher metal removal rates than conventional pocket milling with 50% stepover.

- Operation types: `roughing`, `2d_pocket`
