# Probing for NADCAP Traceability

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:siemens-nx-docs`

## Tip

Probe before, during, after machining. Store all measurements in Teamcenter with part serial number. Wall thickness verification on structural parts. NADCAP auditors require measurement traceability. NX probe macros output Renishaw/Blum compatible code.
