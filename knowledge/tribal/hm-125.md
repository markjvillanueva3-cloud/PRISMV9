# Flowline Finishing for Turbine Surfaces

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-docs`

## Tip

Flowline follows user-defined curves across surfaces. Define start/ end boundaries — hyperMILL interpolates intermediate lines. Ideal for turbine blades (hub-to-shroud), aerofoils, automotive panels. Step-over perpendicular to flow. Produces directional patterns that can improve aerodynamic performance.
