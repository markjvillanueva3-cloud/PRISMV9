# Pencil Finishing for Internal Corner Cleanup

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `pencil` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.89` · **Source:** `web:powermill-docs`

## Tip

Pencil finishing traces internal corners and fillets left by larger tools. PowerMill automatically detects corner geometry and generates the pencil trace path. Use a ball-end mill 50-70% of the smallest fillet radius. Set 'Corner Detection Angle' to filter which corners are traced. Run pencil finishing as the final operation — it produces the best surface quality in concave transitions.
