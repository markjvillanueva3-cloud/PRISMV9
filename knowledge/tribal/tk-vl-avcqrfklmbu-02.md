# Mastercam 2024 tool selection for 2D milling job

**Category:** `tooling` · **Subcategory:** `tool_selection` · **Domain:** `video_learned`

**Confidence:** `70` · **Source:** `video:aVcqrFkLMbU`

## Tip

Recommended tool set from Mastercam tutorial: 3" face mill for facing, 1/2" 3-flute end mill for roughing and finishing contours, 1/4" drill for bolt holes. The 3-flute end mill is preferred over 4-flute for aluminum and general-purpose steel milling due to better chip evacuation in slotting and pocketing.

- Material groups: `P`, `N`
- Operation types: `face`, `pocket`, `profile`, `drill`
