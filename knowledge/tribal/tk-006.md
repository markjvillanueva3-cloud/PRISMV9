# Aluminum face mill chatter fix

**Category:** `speeds_feeds` · **Subcategory:** `surface_speed` · **Domain:** `shop_floor`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `operator:hsm_specialist`

## Tip

If you get chatter face-milling aluminum, before reducing speed: try INCREASING speed to 15000+ RPM with high feed. The light cuts at high speed often eliminate resonance that occurs at mid-range RPMs. Also check that your face mill has unequal tooth spacing.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `N`
- Operation types: `face`
