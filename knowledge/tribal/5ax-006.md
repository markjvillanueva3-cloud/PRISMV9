# G43.4 enables toolpath linearization for 5-axis simultaneous on Hurco

**Category:** `cnc_programming` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `92` · **Source:** `document:hurco-5axis-post`

## Tip

Hurco G43.4 activates toolpath linearization for 5-axis simultaneous machining with IJK tool vectors. Output G43.4 after TCPM (M128) and before cutting moves. The control interpolates smooth 5-axis motion using the IJK direction cosines. Required for machining with tool perpendicular to sculptured surfaces. Without G43.4, 5-axis moves may produce faceted surface finish.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `5axis`, `multiaxis`
