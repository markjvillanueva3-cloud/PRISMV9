# iMachining Composites — Level 2-3 with Diamond Tooling and Dust Control

Carbon fiber (CFRP) and glass fiber (GFRP) composites require iMachining Level 2-3 with PCD or CVD diamond-coated tools. Set cutting speed to 100-200 m/min with feed per tooth of 0.04-0.08mm. The Technology Wizard must use reduced engagement angles to prevent delamination between plies. Use compression-style end mills (up-down flute) to push fibers toward the mid-plane from both sides. Critical: disable coolant and use vacuum extraction — composite dust is highly abrasive and a respiratory hazard. Monitor tool wear with acoustic emission sensors since CFRP abrasion wear is linear and predictable.
