# 3D Engraving from Image to CNC Toolpath

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:bobcad-3d-engraving`

## Tip

BobART converts 2D images (JPEG, PNG, BMP) into 3D relief models for CNC machining. The image brightness maps to relief height — bright areas are raised, dark areas are lowered. Up to 64 vectorization color levels provide fine control over the height mapping. After conversion, apply standard 3D roughing and finishing toolpaths. For detailed engravings, use a 1mm or smaller ball-nose tool with 0.05-0.1mm stepover. Best results come from high-contrast, well-lit source images.
