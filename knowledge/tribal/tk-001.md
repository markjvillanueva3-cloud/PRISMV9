# Stainless 304 work hardening prevention

**Category:** `speeds_feeds` · **Subcategory:** `chip_load` · **Domain:** `shop_floor`

**Confidence:** `95` · **Source:** `operator:senior_machinist`

## Tip

Never dwell in the cut with 304/316 stainless. Use climb milling, positive rake angles, and maintain constant chip load. If you hear the pitch change, you've already work-hardened the surface — increase speed 15% and take a fresh cut below the hardened layer.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `M`
- Operation types: `pocket`, `profile`
