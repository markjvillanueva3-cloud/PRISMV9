# Archard Wear Model for Flank Wear Prediction

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.78` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-forum`

## Tip

Archard: V = K × F × d / H. Doubling speed quadruples wear (distance doubles, K temperature-dependent also doubles). Use to predict tool life at different Cimatron speed settings. For roughing where tool cost matters: reduce speed 20% for 2.5× longer tool life. For finishing where time matters: accept shorter life at higher speed. Trade-off depends on tool cost vs machine rate.
