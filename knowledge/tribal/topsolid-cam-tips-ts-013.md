# Pocket Roughing with Island Detection and Stepover Control

TopSolid's pocket roughing automatically detects islands (bosses) within pockets and generates efficient spiral-out or zigzag patterns that avoid island collisions. Set the stepover to 60-70% of cutter diameter for conventional roughing or 8-12% for high-speed adaptive. Enable 'Island offset' to leave a consistent stock allowance around islands for the finishing pass. The system also supports multiple pocket depths within a single operation.
