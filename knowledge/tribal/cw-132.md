# VoluMill for Titanium — High Axial, Low Radial Strategy

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:camworks-docs`

## Tip

Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) benefits enormously from VoluMill's constant-engagement approach. Set max engagement angle to 45-60° (lower than steel due to titanium's low thermal conductivity), axial depth to 1.5-2x diameter, and use through-tool coolant at 40+ bar. VoluMill's controlled chip formation prevents the work hardening that occurs with conventional toolpaths. Typical parameters: Vc 40-60 m/min (carbide), fz 0.06-0.12mm/tooth. Monitor spindle load — if it exceeds 70%, reduce engagement angle rather than feed.
