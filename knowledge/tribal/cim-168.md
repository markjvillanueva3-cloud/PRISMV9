# Spiral Finishing for Flat Mold Shutoffs

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Continuous spiral eliminates direction-change witness marks. Step-over per scallop. Climb milling. Cimatron auto-computes center and expands. Best for shutoff faces where surface quality directly affects mold performance and part appearance. Mirror finish achievable with wiper geometry.
