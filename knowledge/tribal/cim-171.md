# Rapid Height Optimization for Cycle Time

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `pocket` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-docs`

## Tip

Incremental safe heights (10mm above stock) vs absolute. Safe-area rapids only when crossing obstacles. 5-15% savings on complex molds. Configure per-operation. Verify rapids clear fixturing in simulation. Critical for multi-pocket cavities with varying depths.
