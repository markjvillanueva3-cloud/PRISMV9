# Constant-Cusp Strategy Adapts to Surface Curvature

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `adaptive` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `91` · **Source:** `web:worknc-cusp`

## Tip

WorkNC's constant-cusp finishing measures actual theoretical cusp height at every point and adjusts local stepover accordingly. Convex surfaces get wider stepover (larger effective radius reduces cusp at same step); concave surfaces get tighter stepover. This produces truly uniform surface quality requiring consistent polishing effort across the entire part surface.
