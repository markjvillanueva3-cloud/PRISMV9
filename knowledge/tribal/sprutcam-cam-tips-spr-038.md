# Taguchi Robust Parameter Design

Apply Taguchi L9 orthogonal array to find robust machining parameters. Factors: cutting speed (3 levels), feed (3 levels), depth (3 levels). Noise factors: material hardness variation, tool wear state. Measure S/N ratio for surface finish. The Taguchi-optimal parameters are those where surface finish is least sensitive to noise factors — not necessarily the parameters that produce the best finish under ideal conditions.
