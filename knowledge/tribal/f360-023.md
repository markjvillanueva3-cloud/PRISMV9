# Stop on Collision for Real-Time Simulation Debugging

**Category:** `safety` · **Subcategory:** `coolant_safety` · **Domain:** `safety`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:fusion360-docs`

## Tip

Toggle the Stop on Collision feature in toolpath simulation to halt playback the instant a collision is detected between the tool, holder, or shaft and the stock, fixtures, or part. Red segments on the simulation timeline indicate collision zones — hover over them to jump directly to the problem. Fix the issue by adjusting retract heights, tilting the tool axis, or using a shorter holder assembly.
