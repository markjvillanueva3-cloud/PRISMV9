# Impeller 5-Axis Roughing Strategy

**Category:** `specialty` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `84` · **Source:** `web:tebis-docs`

## Tip

Plunge roughing between impeller blades removes bulk safely. Axial forces into hub (strongest direction). Step-over 50-60% of diameter. Then 5-axis contour roughing for passages. Tebis blade module manages plunge-to-contour transition automatically. Verify collision-free access between adjacent blades.
