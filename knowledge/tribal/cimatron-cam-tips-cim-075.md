# NC Template for Standardized Mold Programming

Cimatron NC Templates capture complete machining strategies: tool selection, cutting parameters, leads/links, boundary setup, and strategy sequence. Apply templates to similar molds — Cimatron remaps geometry references automatically. Templates enforce shop standards and reduce programming time by 50-70% for repeat mold types. Version-control templates for process improvement tracking.
