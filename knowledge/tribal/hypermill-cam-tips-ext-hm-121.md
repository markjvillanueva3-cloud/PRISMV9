# 5-Axis Automatic Indexing vs Simultaneous

hyperMILL automatic indexing (3+2) locks rotary axes per operation. Higher rigidity, better accuracy, simpler post. Use when simultaneous isn't needed. hyperMILL can automatically determine optimal index angles from the geometry. For mold work, 80% of operations can use 3+2 — reserve simultaneous for undercuts and complex freeform.
