# iMachining 3D Floor Offset — Protect Finish Floor Surface

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

Set a Floor Offset of 0.3-0.5mm in iMachining 3D roughing to protect the finish floor surface from tool marks and work hardening. The floor is the most visible surface on many mold and die components. Without floor offset, the last roughing pass contacts the final surface at varying engagement, creating inconsistent work-hardened zones that cause the finishing tool to deflect unpredictably. The floor offset ensures a uniform semi-finish stock for the HSM finishing pass.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `3d_roughing`, `mold_die`
