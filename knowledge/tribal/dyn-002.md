# Dynamic Mill Stay Inside strategy machines pockets from inside to outside efficiently

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `document_learned`

**Confidence:** `93` · **Source:** `document:mastercam-dynamic-milling`

## Tip

Dynamic Mill with 'Stay Inside' strategy (default) machines pockets starting from the inside and working outward. Requires at least one closed chain as Machining region. Additional geometry becomes Avoidance, Containment, or Air regions. Best for: enclosed pockets, complex multi-island geometry. The toolpath maintains constant engagement angle throughout, preventing tool overload at corners and narrow areas.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `pocketing`, `roughing`
