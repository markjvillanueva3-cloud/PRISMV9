# 5-Axis Geodesic Finishing for Uniform Coverage

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `83` · **Source:** `web:hypermill-docs`

## Tip

Geodesic follows shortest surface path, producing uniform coverage regardless of parameterization. Better than raster on doubly-curved surfaces. Set step-over by scallop height. hyperMILL geodesic is computation-intensive but produces superior finish quality on optical and automotive Class-A surfaces.
