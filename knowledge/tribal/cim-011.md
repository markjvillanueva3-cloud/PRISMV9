# Spiral Finishing for Flat and Near-Flat Areas

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `spiral` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:cimatron-tutorials`

## Tip

For flat-bottomed pockets and near-horizontal surfaces, spiral finishing produces superior surface quality compared to raster. Set 'Path Type' to 'Spiral' with constant step-over. This eliminates the direction changes inherent in raster patterns that leave witness marks. Use climb milling direction for best finish on aluminum and pre-hardened steel.

- Material groups: `P`, `N`, `H`
- Operation types: `finishing`
