# SPC Control Charts for Production Monitoring

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `82` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-forum`

## Tip

After stable SolidCAM programs, implement X-bar/R on critical dims. First 25 parts establish limits. Trends (7 same-side) = tool wear. Shifts = fixture/WCS. Increasing range = vibration. iMachining's consistent cutting forces make SPC more stable — less variation in the controlled variables means easier detection of assignable causes.
