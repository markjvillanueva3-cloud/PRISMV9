# View modes: wireframe, shaded, shaded+edges (most useful), edge-only, dashed hidden lines

**Category:** `setup` · **Domain:** `video_learned`

**Confidence:** `82` · **Source:** `video:hypermill-day1-interface@700-900s`

## Tip

hyperMILL display modes for the 3D viewport: Wireframe (edges only, see-through), Shaded (solid faces no edges), Shaded with Edges (most commonly used — solid faces with visible edge lines), Shaded with Dashed hidden lines (invisible edges shown as dashed), Edge-only (no shading), Edge with dashed hidden lines. Use Shaded+Edges for general CAM work. Use Wireframe to inspect internal features. Hide/Unhide individual faces to see inside cavities without switching to wireframe.
