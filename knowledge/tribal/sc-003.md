# iMachining Constant Chip Thickness — Eliminate Chatter

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `88` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

The patented motion-control algorithm continuously varies radial engagement angle (10°–80°) and feed rate to sustain constant chip thickness throughout the cut. This prevents force surges at corners that cause chatter and tool breakage. Do not override the feed correction — let the wizard handle the dynamic feed adjustment for each segment of the toolpath.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `pocket_milling`
