# Uncertainty Budget for Mold Cavity Dimensions

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.81` · **Source:** `web:powermill-forum`

## Tip

Total uncertainty for mold cavity machining: machine positioning (±0.003mm from calibration), tool diameter tolerance (±0.005mm H6), tool deflection under cutting forces (±0.008mm for 6mm ball at 100N), thermal growth (±0.005mm over 4hr cycle), and measurement (±0.002mm CMM). RSS total: ±0.012mm. This budget determines the tightest tolerance achievable. For ±0.01mm specs, improve the largest contributor (tool deflection).
