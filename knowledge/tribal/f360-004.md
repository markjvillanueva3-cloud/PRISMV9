# Helical and Ramp Entry for Adaptive Roughing

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `adaptive` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:fusion360-docs`

## Tip

Always prefer Helical or Ramp entry over Plunge in Adaptive Clearing. Helical entries distribute cutting forces radially and prevent shock loading on the tool tip. Set the helical ramp diameter to 80-110% of tool diameter and ramp angle to 2-5 degrees for carbide end mills. This dramatically extends tool life in hardened steels and stainless.

## Applies to

- Material groups: `M`
- Operation types: `3d_adaptive`, `2d_adaptive`
