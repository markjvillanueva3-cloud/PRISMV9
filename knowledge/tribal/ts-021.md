# Z-Level Finishing Produces Excellent Steep Wall Quality

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `93` · **Source:** `web:topsolid-finishing`

## Tip

TopSolid's Z-level finishing generates constant-Z contour passes ideal for steep surfaces (>45° from horizontal). Set the Z-step based on desired scallop height rather than a fixed increment—typically 0.05-0.15 mm scallop for semi-finish and 0.005-0.02 mm for final finish. Enable 'Smooth Z-step' to vary the increment slightly to avoid synchronization marks. Use ball-nose cutters with the largest practical diameter to minimize cusp height.
