## Tip

Exergy destruction = T₀×S_gen. Cutting (70%), friction (15%), deformation (10%), coolant (5%). Minimize by optimizing parameters. Compare PowerMill strategies: Vortex typically better exergy efficiency than offset area clear at same MRR due to reduced air cutting.
