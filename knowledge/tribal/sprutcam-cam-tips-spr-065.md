# Copper and Brass Machining Parameters

Copper and brass: use sharp uncoated tools (diamond-polished for mirror finish), 200-500 m/min, 0.05-0.15mm feed/tooth. Brass with high zinc content (>35%) can be machined at very high speeds with positive-rake geometry. SprutCAM's high-speed finishing works well for copper EDM electrodes. Warning: some lead-free brass alloys produce long stringy chips — use chipbreaker geometry or high feed to manage them.
