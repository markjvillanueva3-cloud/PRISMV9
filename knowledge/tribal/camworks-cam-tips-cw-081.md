# Material Removal Simulation — Visual Stock Verification at Each Operation

Material removal simulation shows a 3D animation of stock being cut away by each operation. Use it to verify: no stock remains in unreachable areas, roughing leaves uniform finish allowance, and finishing operations contact all required surfaces. Enable color-coded stock thickness display — red indicates excessive remaining stock, blue indicates minimal stock, and magenta indicates gouge (material removed below target surface). Check for magenta at each operation stage.
