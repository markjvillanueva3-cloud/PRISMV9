# Machining Templates Capture Best-Practice Processes

TopSolid's machining templates capture complete process sequences (operation order, tool assignments, cutting parameters, linking strategies) that can be applied to new parts with similar features. Create templates for common part families: prismatic housings, round covers, bracket families, etc. Templates can include conditional logic—if a feature exceeds a threshold dimension, use a different tool or strategy. Store templates in the PDM vault with descriptive names and revision control.
