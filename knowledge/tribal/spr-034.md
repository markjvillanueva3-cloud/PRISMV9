# Hardened Steel HSM Strategy

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `hsm` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

For hardened steel (50-65 HRC) in SprutCAM: use CBN or AlTiN-coated carbide, cutting speed 100-200 m/min, feed per tooth 0.03-0.06mm. Radial DOC ≤5% of diameter with full axial depth. Use SprutCAM's constant engagement roughing to maintain stable chip load. No coolant — use air blast only. Target Ra 0.4-0.8μm from machining to reduce or eliminate polishing. Chip should be orange-red colored (not blue/black).

## Applies to

- Material groups: `P`, `H`
- Operation types: `finishing`
