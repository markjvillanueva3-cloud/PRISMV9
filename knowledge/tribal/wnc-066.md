# Bridge Machining with Multi-Unit Connector Management

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `rest_machining` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `89` · **Source:** `web:worknc-bridge`

## Tip

WorkNC Dental machines multi-unit bridges with automatic connector placement and management. The software calculates optimal connector positions and sizes to hold the bridge during machining while minimizing cleanup after separation. Use 0.5-1.0 mm connectors placed at non-critical surfaces. The machining sequence rough-finishes each unit then processes connectors last for maximum rigidity.

- Operation types: `5_axis`, `finishing`
