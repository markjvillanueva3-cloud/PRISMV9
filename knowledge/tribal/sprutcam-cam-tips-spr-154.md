# Electrode Machining for EDM

Graphite: uncoated 2-flute, 15-24K RPM, 0.05-0.08mm fz. No coolant — vacuum dust extraction. Copper: sharp tools, flood coolant for chip evacuation. SprutCAM tracks undersizing per operation (roughing 0.3mm, finishing 0.05mm). Program datum pads for CMM on EROWA/3R pallets.
