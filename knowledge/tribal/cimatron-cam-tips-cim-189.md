# Additive DED for Mold Repair and Modification

Cimatron DED: deposit material on worn areas, machine to spec. Contour+fill, 30-50% overlap. For: worn parting surfaces, damaged edges, adding conformal cooling to existing molds. Reduces lead time vs new mold build from 8 weeks to 2 weeks for typical repair.
