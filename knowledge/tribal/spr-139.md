# Collision Checking with Full Assembly

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.88` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

Include: shank, holder body, holder taper, spindle nose. Shrink-fit for min profile. Extended tools: 50% feed at 7:1 L/D. Gouge check after every finish operation. 0.5mm safety margin. SprutCAM machine simulation catches holder/machine collisions that toolpath-only checking misses.

## Applies to

- Operation types: `roughing`, `finishing`
