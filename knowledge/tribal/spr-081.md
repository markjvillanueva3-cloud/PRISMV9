# Barrel Cutter Finishing for Large Step-Over

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.85` · **Source:** `web:sprutcam-docs`

## Tip

Barrel cutters (segment radius 100-500mm) allow 3-5× wider step-over than ball-end mills for the same scallop height. In SprutCAM, define the barrel geometry precisely: barrel radius, tip radius, body taper. Use 5-axis tilt to maintain the barrel contact zone on the surface. Verify the contact pattern — incorrect tilt causes gouging or air cutting. Best for automotive dies and large mold surfaces.
