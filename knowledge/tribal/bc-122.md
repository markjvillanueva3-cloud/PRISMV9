# Verification Probing with SPC Data Output

**Category:** `probing` · **Subcategory:** `part_inspection` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `86` · **Source:** `web:bobcad-verification-probing`

## Tip

BobCAD programs final verification probing that measures all critical dimensions after the last operation. Compare against tolerance and generate pass/fail via macro variables. Output measured values through DPRNT for SPC (Statistical Process Control) data collection. The digital inspection record accompanies the part through quality. BobCAD supports automated reporting formats compatible with major SPC software packages.
