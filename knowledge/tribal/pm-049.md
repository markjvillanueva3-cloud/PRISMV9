# Barrel Cutter Strategies for Large Step-Over Finishing

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `0.87` · **Source:** `web:autodesk-university`

## Tip

Barrel cutters (segment, tangent, taper barrel) have a large effective cutting radius (100-500mm) allowing step-over 3-5× wider than ball-end mills for the same scallop height. In PowerMill, define barrel cutter geometry precisely: barrel radius, tip radius, taper angle. Use 'Automatic Tilt' to maintain the barrel contact zone on the surface. Verify the contact pattern in simulation — incorrect tilt causes gouging.
