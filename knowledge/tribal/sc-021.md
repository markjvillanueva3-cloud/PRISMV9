# Mill-Turn Channel Synchronization — Eliminate Idle Time

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `85` · **Source:** `web:solidcam-docs`

## Tip

SolidCAM Mill-Turn coordinates multiple spindles, turrets, and tool channels to run simultaneously. Proper channel synchronization ensures operations across channels are timed to avoid collisions and eliminate idle time. Use the Sync Manager to define wait points and handoff sequences — never assume channels will self-synchronize from the post alone.

- Operation types: `mill_turn`, `turning`
