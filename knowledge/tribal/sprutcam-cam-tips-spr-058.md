# Raster Finishing Direction Selection

Choose raster direction based on part geometry: (1) align with longest surface dimension for fewest direction changes, (2) align perpendicular to the polishing direction for cross-hatch pattern, (3) use 45° angle for balanced coverage. Set 'One Way' for best finish quality, 'Zig-Zag' for 30% shorter cycle time. For mold cavities, align raster perpendicular to the mold opening direction.
