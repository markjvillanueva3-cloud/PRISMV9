# Steep and Shallow Remove Cusps at Junctions

**Category:** `cam_strategy` · **Subcategory:** `scallop` · **Domain:** `cam_software`

**Confidence:** `87` · **Source:** `web:fusion360-docs`

## Tip

Enable Remove Cusps at Junctions in Steep and Shallow finishing to add an extra pass at the transition zone where contour and parallel/scallop regions meet. Without this option, small ridges of material (cusps) remain at the boundary because the two strategy types approach from different directions. The cusp removal pass traces the boundary line and removes the ridge, eliminating the need for manual polishing on mold surfaces where the transition would otherwise leave a visible witness line.

- Operation types: `steep_and_shallow`
