## Free college courses

Full course / lecture-series homepages — open courseware that is maintained and re-offered, so the curriculum stays current.

- **MIT OpenCourseWare — 2.008 Design and Manufacturing II** — https://opencw.aprende.org/courses/mechanical-engineering/2-008-design-and-manufacturing-ii-spring-2004/ — Full MIT course homepage (OCW mirror) covering modern manufacturing across four areas: manufacturing processes, equipment/control, systems, and design-for-manufacturing, with lecture notes, labs, assignments, and exams. The canonical free-courseware framing of where turning sits in a formal machining education (Cutting I/II: tools, fixtures, machine tools, precision engineering).
- **NPTEL — Manufacturing Processes II (IIT Kharagpur)** — https://nptel.ac.in/courses/112105126 — A complete, free, CC-licensed (gov-funded, MoE India) university course on machining and metal-cutting processes, including turning/lathe operations, tool geometry, and tool life. Continuously available self-study course with recorded lectures; the deepest free single-course treatment of turning fundamentals.
- **NPTEL — Manufacturing Processes II (alternate course id)** — https://nptel.ac.in/courses/112105127 — Same IIT Kharagpur "Manufacturing Processes II" content under a sibling course id; useful as a redundant entry point if the primary id is unavailable.
- **edX — Manufacturing topic catalog** — https://www.edx.org/learn/manufacturing — The edX manufacturing course catalog page; a living index of university/industry manufacturing courses (most offer a free audit track). Use it to discover currently-offered manufacturing/machining courses as new ones are published.

## Free textbooks & references

Open-license textbook homepages — maintained editions, not frozen PDFs.

- **Open Oregon — Manufacturing Processes 4-5** — https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/manufacturingprocesses45/ — A free, CC-BY open textbook whose **Chapter 2 is dedicated entirely to the lathe** (engine-lathe fundamentals, speeds and feeds, chucks, turning procedures, tapping, threading), plus milling, grinding, heat-treat, and CNC chapters. The single best free *operations-level* turning textbook homepage for the galaxy.
- **OpenStax — University Physics Volume 1** — https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/1-introduction — Free, peer-reviewed, openly-licensed college physics textbook (full online edition). Volume 1 contains the rotational-dynamics chapters (angular velocity, torque, moment of inertia) that are the first-principles basis for CSS/G96, spindle torque, and workpiece-inertia reasoning in turning.
- **LibreTexts — Mechanical Engineering bookshelf** — https://eng.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Mechanical_Engineering — A living, openly-licensed bookshelf of mechanical-engineering open texts (statics, dynamics, mechanics of materials, thermodynamics, numerics). The supporting-theory reference layer under turning mechanics; continuously expanded by the LibreTexts community.

## Archives & open data / gov reports

Government and public-archive portals — the "data reports" surface, continuously updated.

- **NIST — Manufacturing portal** — https://www.nist.gov/manufacturing — The U.S. national-metrology institute's manufacturing hub, covering machining, quality assurance, process improvement, and smart manufacturing, with links to publications, projects, and benchmark test series. Authoritative entry point for manufacturing metrology and standards-adjacent research.
- **NIST — Publications database** — https://www.nist.gov/publications — Free, searchable database of NIST technical reports, special publications, and journal articles (filterable by topic incl. manufacturing). The living feed of authoritative U.S. metrology/manufacturing research as new reports are released.
- **NASA — Technical Reports Server (NTRS)** — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/ — Free full-text repository of NASA technical reports, papers, and patents — including wear/tribology, materials, and manufacturing-process compilations relevant to tool-wear and surface-integrity reasoning. Continuously updated as new reports are released.
- **Internet Archive — Texts / Library** — https://archive.org/details/texts — Massive free/borrowable and public-domain text archive; the place to find out-of-copyright machine-shop, lathe, and metalworking practice manuals plus historical engineering references. Living archive, continuously expanded.
- **Project Gutenberg — Bookshelves** — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/bookshelf/ — Curated public-domain ebook collections (including a Technology category). Source for free, legal, out-of-copyright machine-shop and mechanical-trade texts that frame the historical foundations of turning practice.

## Standards & authoritative bodies

Standards-organization landing pages — the governing-standards surface for lathe safety and surface-texture metrology (landing/index pages that stay current as editions revise).

- **ASME — Codes & Standards** — https://www.asme.org/codes-standards — The American Society of Mechanical Engineers standards portal. The authoritative landing page to locate the standards that govern turned-part specification and metrology — notably the Y14.5 GD&T family and B46.1 (Surface Texture) that define how a print's diameter/finish callouts are specified and measured. Continuously updated as editions revise.
- **OSHA — Machine Guarding (safety topic)** — https://www.osha.gov/machine-guarding — The U.S. OSHA machine-guarding safety-topic hub. Governing landing page for turning-machine safeguarding (lathes fall under general industry; chip-ejection and rotating-member hazards). Entry point to the hazard-recognition, standards, and solutions sections that frame ANSI B11.6 (Lathes) and 29 CFR 1910 Subpart O guarding requirements. Living regulatory surface.

## Maintenance

This atlas is a **link directory** — its single failure mode is **link rot** (sources move, standards bodies restructure, course mirrors retire). The freshness mechanism is therefore **periodic re-verification**, not a content rebuild:

- **Re-verify every URL** here on a recurring cadence (suggest quarterly, or whenever a fetch from this list 404s in use). Each entry was live-confirmed on 2026-06-10; treat any entry older than ~90 days as needing a re-fetch before it is relied upon.
- **Drop, don't guess.** If a URL stops resolving, remove it (and find the relocated canonical homepage) rather than leaving a dead link — same discipline used to build this atlas (404/403/redirect-loop candidates were dropped this pass, including several MIT-OCW modern-slug URLs, ISO direct standard pages behind anti-bot 403s, and YouTube channel pages that could not be confirmed on-topic).
- **Prefer the most-living form.** When refreshing, always prefer a maintained *homepage / course series / portal* over a single article page — that is what keeps this curriculum non-stagnant.
- **Owner gate unchanged.** Re-verifying a link never promotes a number from that page; cutting constants remain owner-gated in `mcp-server/src/physics/constants.ts`.
