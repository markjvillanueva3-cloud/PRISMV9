# Academy Open-Source Atlas

A curated, VERIFIED directory of the best **free + legal LIVING** resources for manufacturing / machining **education, pedagogy, and workforce training** — the academy galaxy's "keep-learning" curriculum. Every source here is a **continuously-updated root** (a course catalog, a textbook library, a data portal, a standards landing page, a video-lecture host) so the academy's curriculum stays current by pointing at sources that refresh themselves, rather than at a frozen snapshot.

**Scope vs. the foundations file.** This atlas is DISTINCT from [[academy-pedagogy-foundations]] (`knowledge/wiki/academy/academy-pedagogy-foundations.md`). The foundations file cites *specific leaf URLs* it extracted pedagogy/institutional facts from (a single OCW course page, one OpenStax book edition, a specific NIST e-Handbook subsection, the Cornell-LII text of one CFR section). This atlas instead curates the *broader living ROOTS* those leaves hang off — the catalog/library/portal/standards-body homepages that surface fresh courses, new editions, and updated reports over time. Read the foundations file for the *what-to-teach* spine; read this atlas for the *where-to-keep-pulling-fresh-material* directory.

**R8 / no-duplication.** The bulk flat-pointer corpus lives at `state/shared/specs/GALAXY-FREE-SOURCE-CORPUS-2026-06-09.md` (not auto-invoked). This atlas does NOT re-dump it — it adds value by VERIFYING each link resolves, CURATING to the strongest ~20 living roots, and TYPE-ORGANIZING them for the academy galaxy specifically.

## Free college courses

Full course-series / lecture-series catalogs — audit-free, open-courseware, or open-license. These refresh as new courses and re-runs are published.

- **MIT OpenCourseWare** — https://ocw.mit.edu/ — Free lecture notes, exams, and videos from 2,500+ MIT courses, no registration; the mechanical-engineering set (2.008/2.810/2.830J/2.852) is the strongest free structured manufacturing-process + manufacturing-systems spine for an academy curriculum.
- **NPTEL (IIT / IISc open courseware)** — https://nptel.ac.in/ — 3,200+ free self-study courses from India's IITs/IISc; the faculty-authored "Manufacturing Processes I/II" sequence gives a second national engineering-education system to cross-check the MIT spine against.
- **edX — Manufacturing catalog** — https://www.edx.org/learn/manufacturing — University/industry manufacturing courses, most auditable for free; good for production planning, quality control, lean, and supply-chain modules that supplement the process-physics core.
- **Coursera — Manufacturing catalog** — https://www.coursera.org/courses?query=manufacturing — Manufacturing courses (production planning, quality control, supply chain, lean) with many courses auditable for free; a living catalog the academy can scan for new workforce-relevant offerings.

## Free textbooks & references

Openly-licensed (CC-BY) or public-domain textbook libraries and authoritative free references — the academy may excerpt/adapt these at zero licensing cost (preserving required attribution).

- **OpenStax** (Introductory Statistics 2e shown) — https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/1-introduction — Peer-reviewed, Creative-Commons-licensed college textbook; the statistics titles are a zero-cost, freely-redistributable spine for the academy's data / SPC / quality leg.
- **LibreTexts** — https://libretexts.org/ — Non-profit open-access library with 17 subject libraries (engineering, math, statistics, business) of free customizable interactive textbooks — a broad OER base for any academy module that needs a free reference text.
- **Project Gutenberg** — https://www.gutenberg.org/ — 75,000+ free public-domain ebooks; the source for out-of-copyright machine-shop / engineering classics the academy can reproduce verbatim with no license constraint.
- **NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods** — https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/ — Free, continuously-hosted federal reference on statistical methods (control charts, process capability, DOE) — the canonical primary-source citation for the academy's SPC + metrology teaching, instead of vendor training decks.

## Archives & open data / gov reports

Government and archive portals that publish continuously-updated public reports and datasets — the "data reports" spine for evidence-based academy modules (labor outlook, manufacturing economics, technical research).

- **Internet Archive — Texts** — https://archive.org/details/texts — Digital library of free and borrowable texts (many public-domain), including scanned machining/engineering references; a living archive the academy can mine for historical and out-of-print source material.
- **Data.gov** — https://www.data.gov/ — The U.S. government's open-data portal (360,000+ datasets); the entry point for labor, manufacturing, and economic datasets feeding any data-driven academy module.
- **NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS)** — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/ — Free access to NASA technical reports, full-text documents, images, and videos; a deep well of aerospace-grade manufacturing, materials, and systems-engineering research.
- **NIST Publications** — https://www.nist.gov/publications — Searchable portal of free NIST technical publications and reports (manufacturing economics, model-based definition, metrology) — primary federal sources for the academy's standards-and-measurement legs.
- **U.S. DOE — Industrial Efficiency & Decarbonization Office** — https://www.energy.gov/eere/iedo/industrial-efficiency-decarbonization-office — DOE office page with freely accessible software tools, research data, and project databases on manufacturing competitiveness and industrial efficiency.
- **Apprenticeship.gov (U.S. DOL)** — https://www.apprenticeship.gov/ — Official Registered Apprenticeship portal; the authoritative living source for apprenticeship-occupation listings, standards, and the time-based/competency-based/hybrid models the academy maps credentials onto.
- **O*NET OnLine — Machinists (51-4041.00)** — https://www.onetonline.org/link/details/51-4041.00 — Continuously-updated federal occupation database for the machinist trade (tasks, skills, software, wage data, apprenticeship links) — the canonical competency reference for academy curriculum alignment.

## Lecture series & video

Reputable, verifiable hosts of free recorded lectures. (Listed only where the video-hosting root itself resolves and is confirmable — channel/playlist handles that could not be independently verified were deliberately omitted per R12.)

- **MIT OpenCourseWare (video lectures)** — https://ocw.mit.edu/ — Many OCW courses include full free recorded video lectures alongside notes and exams; the academy can embed/point to these as the video leg of its deliberate-practice + flipped-classroom modules.
- **NPTEL (video courses)** — https://nptel.ac.in/ — IIT/IISc courses are delivered substantially as free recorded video lecture series; a second large, faculty-authored free video corpus for cross-institutional curriculum coverage.

## Standards & authoritative bodies

Standards-body and regulatory landing pages relevant to machining education and shop-floor workforce safety training. These pages are the living entry points; specific standards may sit behind purchase, but the bodies' free landing/guidance pages anchor the academy's "why this rule exists" modules.

- **OSHA — Machine Guarding** — https://www.osha.gov/machine-guarding — Federal machine-guarding safety landing page; the primary-source justification for guard/safeguard requirements in any hands-on academy shop-safety module.
- **OSHA — Welding, Cutting & Brazing** — https://www.osha.gov/welding-cutting-brazing — Federal process-safety landing page (fume, UV, burn, electrical hazards) for the welding/cutting safety leg of workforce training.
- **ASME — Codes & Standards** — https://www.asme.org/codes-standards — ASME's standards landing page (pressure technology, GD&T/Y14 series, and more); the authoritative body the academy's drawing-interpretation and tolerancing modules should reference.
- **NIMS — National Institute for Metalworking Skills** — https://www.nims-skills.org/ — The U.S. metalworking-credentialing body; the standards-and-credentialing source the academy maps its machining competency levels and performance evaluations against.

## Maintenance

This atlas is a **link directory**, and link-rot is its primary failure mode. **Freshness mechanism: periodic re-verification.** On a recurring cadence (recommend quarterly, or whenever the academy galaxy is audited), re-WebFetch every URL above; drop any that 403/404 or redirect off-host, and bump `status` / `verified_by` with the re-verification date. New living roots should be added the same way — verified-live before listing, never pasted from the bulk corpus unchecked. The bulk superset (`state/shared/specs/GALAXY-FREE-SOURCE-CORPUS-2026-06-09.md`) is the candidate pool to promote fresh entries from. Several authoritative bodies (BLS OOH, ISO, ANSI, eCFR) bot-block automated fetches and so are intentionally absent here despite being legitimate — a future maintainer with an authenticated/browser path may add them after manual confirmation.
