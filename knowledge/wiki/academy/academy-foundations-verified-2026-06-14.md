## Synthesis
PRISM Academy's next layer rests on four pillars: (1) **Explainable knowledge tracing** (ante-hoc transparent + post-hoc explanation) — adaptive tutoring must expose its reasoning to instructors/learners, not merely predict skill state. (2) **Attention-based process-data KT** (EDM Cup 2023 winner) — feeding clickstream + curricula-graph structure into a Transformer beats response-sequence-only models, and attention weights indicate which prior concepts to remediate. (3) **Item Response Theory** (Rasch/2PL/3PL) — sample-free, difficulty-calibrated ability estimates enabling computerized adaptive testing with statistically defensible competency gates for machinist certification. (4) **Bloom's revised 2-D taxonomy + EDM/LA stack** — objectives encoded as measurable cognitive verbs at the right level (Apply/Analyze for procedural machinist skills) across factual/conceptual/procedural/metacognitive dimensions, with learning-analytics mining interaction data to close the design-vs-outcome gap.

> "algorithms need to achieve high accuracy, and users need to understand the internal operating mechanism and provide reliable explanations for decisions"

**Knowledge:** XKT models split into ante-hoc (transparent by design) and post-hoc (black-box + explanation layer); both required for stakeholder trust in adaptive machinist curricula.

### [Advanced Knowledge Tracing: Process Data + Curricula via Attention (JEDM v16n2, 2024; EDM Cup 2023 winner)](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1445645) — paper

**Knowledge:** Transformer KT ingesting clickstream + curricula graph beats response-sequence models; attention weights give interpretable, targeted remediation signals.

**Knowledge:** IRT (Rasch/2PL/3PL) yields sample-free, item-difficulty-calibrated ability estimates → adaptive testing + defensible competency gates.

### [Bloom's Taxonomy of Educational Objectives (Anderson & Krathwohl 2001 revision, UIC CATE)](https://teaching.uic.edu/cate-teaching-guides/syllabus-course-design/blooms-taxonomy-of-educational-objectives/) — article
