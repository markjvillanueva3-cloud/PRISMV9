# Quality Galaxy Open Source Atlas

A curated, verified directory of the best FREE + LEGAL + LIVING (continuously-updated) resources for quality engineering / SPC / metrology / process capability. The goal is a non-stagnant keep-learning curriculum: every entry points to a homepage / course series / data portal / standards landing that stays current on its own, so the galaxy's knowledge does not freeze on the day it was written.

Scope note (R8 -- no duplication): this is NOT `quality-foundations.md` (read that first for the WebFetch-confirmed numeric/methodology claims). The foundations file cites single pages for single facts; this atlas curates the broader living sources -- full course homepages, textbook libraries, gov data portals, recorded lecture galleries, and standards/authority hubs.

## Free college courses

Full course / lecture-series homepages. Audit-free or open-license only.

- **MIT OCW 18.650 -- Statistics for Applications (Fall 2016)** -- https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-650-statistics-for-applications-fall-2016/ -- Graduate-level theoretical foundations of statistical inference, hypothesis testing, and regression with 22 recorded video lectures; the statistical bedrock under SPC, capability, and DOE. (Prof. Philippe Rigollet.)
- **NPTEL -- Six Sigma (IIT Kharagpur, course 110105039)** -- https://nptel.ac.in/courses/110105039 -- Full video-based university course on the Six Sigma DMAIC methodology, process-variation reduction, and statistical quality control; directly on-domain for quality-engineering practice. (Profs. J. J. Thakkar and Tapan Bagchi.)
- **NPTEL -- Total Quality Management I (IIT Kanpur, course 110104080)** -- https://nptel.ac.in/courses/110104080 -- Full video-based course on TQM principles, continuous improvement, and quality-system organization; complements the SPC/Six-Sigma tooling with the management/organizational layer. (Prof. Raghu Nandan Sengupta.)
- **Coursera -- Six Sigma Advanced Define and Measure Phases** -- https://www.coursera.org/learn/six-sigma-define-measure -- Audit-free enrollment ("Enroll for free") course covering the Define and Measure phases of DMAIC; certificate is paid but all learning content is free to audit.
- **NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook, Chapter 6 -- Process or Product Monitoring and Control** -- https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/pmc.htm -- The chapter table-of-contents that organizes control charts (univariate/multivariate, EWMA, CUSUM), acceptance sampling, process capability, and time-series monitoring into a self-paced reference course; the canonical free SPC curriculum.

## Free textbooks & references

- **LibreTexts -- Introductory Statistics bookshelf** -- https://stats.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Introductory_Statistics -- A living shelf of ~16 free, openly-licensed statistics textbooks (OpenStax 1e/2e, Lane, OpenIntro, Statistical Thinking for the 21st Century) covering the normal curve, inference, confidence intervals, and hypothesis tests -- the statistical literacy base for SPC and capability work.
- **OpenStax -- Introductory Business Statistics 2e (free web reader)** -- https://openstax.org/books/introductory-business-statistics-2e/pages/1-introduction -- Free CC-licensed business-statistics textbook (Rice University); the broader living textbook home for descriptive statistics, distributions, sampling, and inference applied to operational/quality decision-making.
- **NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook, Chapter 2 -- Measurement Process Characterization** -- https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/mpc.htm -- The free reference chapter on metrology-as-a-process: gauge R&R study design, calibration designs, bias/variability control, and Type A/B uncertainty analysis -- the primary-source foundation under MSA and measurement-uncertainty practice.

## Archives & open data / gov reports

- **Internet Archive -- Texts collection** -- https://archive.org/details/texts -- Free, borrowable, and public-domain books; the home of the primary-source SPC literature (e.g. Shewhart's 1931/1939 monographs) so the galaxy can cite originals rather than secondary summaries.
- **NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS)** -- https://ntrs.nasa.gov/ -- Free searchable archive of NASA technical reports, standards, and papers; source for aerospace-grade workmanship standards, reliability methods, and measurement/inspection practice.
- **NIST Office of Weights and Measures (OWM)** -- https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm -- The living legal-metrology hub: traceability, SI use, calibration procedures, and laboratory-metrology training -- the authority layer under any measurement/inspection quality system.
- **NIST Statistical Engineering Division (SED)** -- https://www.nist.gov/itl/sed -- The maintaining division (founded 1946) behind the e-Handbook and NIST measurement-statistics work; an actively-updated hub for experimental design, modeling, and uncertainty references plus the NIST publications database.

## Lecture series & video

- **MIT OCW 18.650 -- Lecture Videos gallery** -- https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-650-statistics-for-applications-fall-2016/video_galleries/lecture-videos/ -- 22 recorded full-length statistics lectures (Introduction to Statistics through Generalized Linear Models); a free, watchable lecture series for building the inferential-statistics intuition SPC and capability indices rest on.
- **NPTEL (SWAYAM) video courses** -- via the course homepages above (e.g. https://nptel.ac.in/courses/110105039) -- NPTEL is a government-of-India platform whose Six Sigma and TQM courses are delivered as free, sequential recorded video lectures by IIT/IISc faculty; a continuously-refreshed lecture-series source for quality methodology.

## Standards & authoritative bodies

- **OSHA -- Law and Regulations landing** -- https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs -- The U.S. workplace-safety standards hub (General Industry, Construction, Maritime, Agriculture) plus current rulemaking; the regulatory-compliance dimension of a quality/EHS management system, kept current as rules change.
- **NIST Uncertainty Machine** -- https://uncertainty.nist.gov/ -- A free, maintained NIST web application that evaluates measurement uncertainty for a user-defined measurement model via GUM propagation and Monte Carlo; the living tool that operationalizes the metrology-uncertainty doctrine for real gauge/inspection data.

## Maintenance

This atlas is a link directory, so its single failure mode is link rot -- a homepage moves, a course year is retired, or a host adds a bot-block. The freshness mechanism is therefore periodic re-verification: re-WebFetch every URL here on a recurring cadence (suggested: quarterly, and opportunistically whenever the quality galaxy is next worked), drop any that no longer resolve to on-topic content, and promote net-new living sources discovered in the meantime. When re-verifying, bump `verified_by` with the new agent + date and keep `status: VERIFIED-PARTIAL` until every listed link has been re-confirmed in a single pass (then it may be raised to `VERIFIED`). Sources that render only as JS/footer shells under WebFetch (some YouTube channel pages, some standards-body pages behind 403 bot-blocks) were intentionally excluded rather than listed unverified -- prefer a fetchable representative page (a concrete course/video-gallery/tool URL) over a bot-blocked landing page.
