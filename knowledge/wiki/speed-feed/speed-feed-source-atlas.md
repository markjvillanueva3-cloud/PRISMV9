# Speed-Feed Open-Source Atlas

A curated, link-verified directory of the strongest **free + legal LIVING resources** for metal-cutting mechanics / speeds-and-feeds physics. Each entry was WebFetched on 2026-06-10 and confirmed to resolve to relevant content. The goal is a **non-stagnant keep-learning curriculum**: these point at continuously-updated source HOMES (full course series, textbook/bookshelf homepages, government data portals, standards landing pages) rather than a single article — so the curriculum stays current as the sources update.

This is **distinct from** [`speed-feed-foundations.md`](speed-feed-foundations.md), which promotes verified formula *structure* from specific source pages. The atlas does **not** repeat that Sources list; it curates the broader living homes those (and new) facts come from. No numeric cutting constant is listed here — this is a link directory only; PRISM sources all cutting constants from `mcp-server/src/physics/constants.ts`.

## Free college courses

- **NPTEL — Manufacturing Processes II (IIT Kharagpur, 112105127)** — https://nptel.ac.in/courses/112105127 — Full recorded video-lecture course (Profs. A.B./A.K. Chattopadhyay, S. Paul) covering machining and metal-cutting processes; the deepest free Indian-institute treatment of cutting mechanics for an SFC keep-learning track.
- **NPTEL — Manufacturing Processes II (IIT Kharagpur, 112105126)** — https://nptel.ac.in/courses/112105126 — Companion IIT Kharagpur manufacturing-processes course page (machining/metal-cutting); free video lectures, useful as a second-instructor cross-check on cutting theory.
- **NPTEL — Manufacturing Processes I (IIT Roorkee, 112107144)** — https://nptel.ac.in/courses/112107144 — Free recorded video course on foundational manufacturing/machining processes; good entry-level on-ramp before the deeper cutting-mechanics courses above.

## Free textbooks & references

- **OpenStax — University Physics (free, CC-licensed)** — https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/10-7-newtons-second-law-for-rotation — Openly-licensed university physics with the rotational dynamics (torque = I*alpha, P = torque*omega), friction, and heat-transfer chapters that under-pin spindle power, tool-chip friction, and cutting-zone thermal limits in any speed/feed model.
- **LibreTexts — Engineering Bookshelf** — https://eng.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Mechanical_Engineering — Continuously-updated open mechanical-engineering textbook library (statics, thermodynamics, materials, and more); the broad free OER home for the mechanics + materials background behind cutting-force and tool-life behavior.
- **Open Oregon — Manufacturing Processes 4-5 (CC-BY 4.0)** — https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/manufacturingprocesses45/ — Free open-license shop-practice textbook covering the lathe, mill, drill press, grinder, and CNC G-code; legal, paywall-free entry-level method reference for turning/milling/drilling speed-and-feed practice.
- **Machining Doctor — calculators index + glossary** — https://www.machiningdoctor.com/calculators/ — Free, continuously-maintained hub of speed/feed, cutting-force, chip-thinning, tool-life, power, and turning calculators plus a machinist glossary; the most actively-updated free reference home for speeds-and-feeds method (use for structure/method; never inline its numbers).
- **Project Gutenberg — machine-shop-practice (public domain)** — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/search/?query=machine+shop+practice — Free public-domain machining classics (e.g. Rose, "Modern Machine-Shop Practice"; "Elementary Lathe Practice"); historical-method depth on shop cutting practice with no paywall risk.
- **Internet Archive — Machinery's Handbook (1924, public domain)** — https://archive.org/details/machineryshandbo00indu — Full public-domain scan of the canonical machinist reference (machine design + shop practice); free historical baseline for cutting-speed/feed tables and tool geometry vocabulary.

## Archives & open data / gov reports

- **NIST — Engineering Laboratory** — https://www.nist.gov/el — Living home of NIST manufacturing measurement-science + standards programs (advanced manufacturing data/analytics, model-based engineering); the gov hub to track for authoritative machining-process measurement reports.
- **NIST — Intelligent Systems Division** — https://www.nist.gov/el/intelligent-systems-division-73500 — NIST division on measurement science/standards for advanced manufacturing processes and equipment optimization; a continuously-updated source of gov manufacturing research relevant to process control.
- **NIST/SEMATECH — e-Handbook of Statistical Methods** — https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/index.htm — Free authoritative gov handbook on process modeling/monitoring + statistical methods; the standards-grade basis for treating speed/feed recommendations as distributions (confidence bands, capability) rather than single numbers.
- **NIST — Chemistry WebBook (SRD 69)** — https://webbook.nist.gov/chemistry/ — Free NIST Standard Reference Data portal for thermophysical/thermochemical material properties; the authoritative gov data home for material thermal data behind cutting-zone heat and thermal-tool-wear limits.
- **NIST — Standard Reference Materials / Data** — https://www.nist.gov/srm — Over 1,200 certified reference materials + linked reference-data databases; the gov source for traceable material-property data that calibration of cutting-physics constants should bracket against.
- **U.S. DOE — OSTI.gov (Office of Scientific & Technical Information)** — https://www.osti.gov/ — Free searchable archive of DOE-funded science/engineering research reports + data; deep gov-report well for machining, materials, and manufacturing-process studies.
- **U.S. GPO — GovInfo / Government Publishing Office** — https://www.gpo.gov/ — Free authenticated access to federal publications (incl. the FDLP + Catalog of U.S. Government Publications); the canonical free portal for federal standards, handbooks, and technical reports.

## Lecture series & video

- **NPTEL — recorded video lecture courses (IIT/IISc)** — https://nptel.ac.in/courses/112105127 — NPTEL courses are full recorded video-lecture series; the Manufacturing Processes II (IIT Kharagpur) course above is a verified, free, continuously-hosted video curriculum on machining/metal-cutting mechanics — the strongest free, link-stable video source for the galaxy (preferred over un-verifiable third-party uploads).

## Standards & authoritative bodies

- **ASME — Codes & Standards (landing)** — https://www.asme.org/codes-standards — Landing page for the ASME standards portfolio (machine tools B5, cutting tools B94, drawing/GD&T Y14 families live one level deeper via "Find Codes & Standards"); the authoritative-body home for machine-tool and tooling standards relevant to speeds/feeds practice.
- **OSHA — Machine Guarding** — https://www.osha.gov/machine-guarding — Authoritative U.S. safety standards home for machine hazards; the regulatory landing every speed/feed/process decision is bounded by (operator + machine-tool safety), continuously maintained.
