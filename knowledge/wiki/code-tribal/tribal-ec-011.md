# Profiling with Lead-In/Lead-Out Arcs

Always use tangential arc lead-in and lead-out for Edgecam profiling operations to prevent witness marks at the entry/exit point. Set arc radius to 50-100% of the tool radius and approach tangentially to the profile contour. For critical surfaces, place the lead on a non-functional face or corner. Enable overlap (1-2mm past the start point) to ensure the profile closes completely without a visible seam.
