# Aluminum HSM for Die Casting Dies

For aluminum die casting die machining: 3-flute uncoated carbide, 300-500 m/min, 0.15-0.25mm feed/tooth, 50% radial, 2×D axial depth. Chip evacuation critical — air blast or high-volume flood. Cimatron adaptive roughing controls engagement in corners. MRR target: 500-1000 cm³/min on high-speed machines. HSM produces better surface quality, reducing manual finishing time.
