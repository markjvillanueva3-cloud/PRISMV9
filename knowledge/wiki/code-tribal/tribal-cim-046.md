# Uncertainty Budget for EDM Electrode Positioning

Total electrode positioning uncertainty budget: machine repeatability (±0.002mm), electrode holder (EROWA/3R: ±0.002mm), electrode deflection from flushing (±0.005mm for tall electrodes), thermal expansion of electrode (α×ΔT×L: ±0.003mm for graphite at 20°C ambient variation). Combined RSS: ±0.007mm. This must be added to the spark gap tolerance when designing electrode undersizes in Cimatron.
