# Barrel Cutter Lead and Tilt Angle Optimization

Barrel cutter 5-axis finishing requires precise lead and tilt angles to position the barrel segment tangent to the surface. In Edgecam, set the lead angle to match the barrel geometry — typically 10-20° from the surface normal. The tilt angle controls which portion of the barrel profile contacts the surface. Use Edgecam's 'automatic lead/tilt' mode which optimizes contact for maximum stepover while maintaining gouge-free cutting. Verify contact point in the toolpath simulation view.
