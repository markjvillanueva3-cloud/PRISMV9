# Aluminum HSM with Maximum Material Removal Rate

For aluminum (6061/7075) in Tebis: 3-flute uncoated carbide, 50% radial engagement, 2×D axial depth, 300-500 m/min, 0.15-0.25mm feed/tooth. Chip evacuation is critical — use air blast or high-volume flood. Tebis adaptive roughing with engagement control prevents overload in corners. At these parameters MRR reaches 500-1000 cm³/min on high-speed machines.
