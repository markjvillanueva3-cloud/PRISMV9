# PEEK Medical Device Machining

PEEK implants: 1-2 flute uncoated, high positive rake, 200-500 m/min, 0.1-0.2mm fz. Compressed air only — no liquid coolant for biocompatibility. Diamond-coated prevents metal contamination. Down-cut prevents lifting thin features. FDA traceability: link PowerMill program version to every implant. Validate with CMM per 21 CFR Part 820.
