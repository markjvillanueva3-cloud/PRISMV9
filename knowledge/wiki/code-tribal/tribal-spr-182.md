# Thread Milling on Mill-Turn for Shoulder Proximity

Thread mill when thread is close to shoulder, material is difficult, or diameter large (>M30). Helical interpolation + C-axis. Lower forces than single-point threading — reduces deflection on thin-walled parts. SprutCAM programs helical thread milling with automatic pitch calculation.
