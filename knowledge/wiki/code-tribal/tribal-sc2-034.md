# Cleanup Passes with Small Tools for Residual Material

SURFCAM cleanup operations target small pockets of material left by the semi-finish tool in tight radii and narrow channels. Use a ball-nose or bull-nose tool 50-75% smaller than the semi-finish tool. Enable 'Minimum material threshold' (0.05mm) to skip areas where the stock is already within tolerance. Set the toolpath overlap to 0.2mm beyond the detected rest material boundary to blend seamlessly. Limit Z-range to focus on problem areas rather than re-machining the entire part.
