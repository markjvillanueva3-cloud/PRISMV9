# Merchant Shear Angle for Chip Formation

Merchant model: higher positive rake → higher shear angle → thinner chips → lower forces → better finish. For mold steel: rake angle increase from 0° to 10° reduces cutting force by ~15%. Use this to validate Cimatron tool selection — tools with higher positive rake geometry produce better results but have weaker cutting edges for interrupted cuts.
