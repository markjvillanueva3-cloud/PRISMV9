# Hardened Steel Finishing Parameters

50-62 HRC: ball-end CBN or nano-coated, 100-200 m/min, 0.03-0.06mm fz. Step-over per target Ra. Air blast only. Constant scallop for uniform quality. Target Ra 0.4-0.8μm from machining to minimize polishing. SprutCAM finishing produces direct-to-polish results on P20 and H13.
