# FreeShape Solid Manipulation Adapts Imported Models

TopSolid's FreeShape functionality allows direct modification of imported solids without requiring a full parametric rebuild. You can adjust models to average dimensions, reconstruct problematic surfaces, and apply local modifications to make parts more machinable. This is particularly valuable when receiving customer models with tight draft angles or thin walls that need slight adjustments for manufacturing feasibility.
