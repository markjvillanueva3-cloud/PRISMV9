# Adaptive Clearing Stock-to-Leave for Clean Finishing

Leave 0.2-0.5mm radial and 0.1-0.3mm axial stock-to-leave on Adaptive Clearing roughing passes. This ensures your finishing toolpath has consistent material to remove, producing even cutting pressure and better surface finish. Inconsistent leftover stock from roughing is the number one cause of chatter marks on finish passes.
