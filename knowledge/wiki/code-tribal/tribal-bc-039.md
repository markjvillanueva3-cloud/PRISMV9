# Tool Axis Control: Lead, Lag, and Side-Tilt

BobCAD 5-axis provides lead angle (tilt in feed direction), lag angle (tilt opposite feed), and side-tilt (perpendicular to feed) controls. For ball-nose finishing, use 10-15° lead angle to engage ahead of the zero-speed center point, improving finish and preventing center rubbing. For side milling, use 1-3° side-tilt to keep the non-cutting side clear. The Multiaxis Wizard provides visual feedback of the tool axis orientation during setup.
