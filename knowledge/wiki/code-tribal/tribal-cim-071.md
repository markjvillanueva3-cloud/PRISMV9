# Pencil Tracing for Corner Cleanup

After main finishing, pencil tracing cleans internal corners and fillets left by larger tools. Cimatron detects concave regions automatically. Use ball-end mill 50-70% of the smallest fillet radius. Enable 'Both Ways' for symmetric corners to halve cycle time. Pencil tracing is essential between Z-level and raster — it targets the exact transition zone these strategies miss.
