# Copper EDM Electrode Finishing

Copper electrodes: sharp uncoated or diamond-polished, 200-500 m/min, 0.05-0.15mm fz. Flood coolant for chip evacuation — copper makes long stringy chips. Better Ra than graphite — preferred for precision EDM. PowerMill tracks electrode spark gap offsets per operation. Use chipbreaker geometry or high feed to manage chip length on deep pockets.
