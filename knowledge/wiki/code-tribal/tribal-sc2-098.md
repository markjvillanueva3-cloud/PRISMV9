# Aluminum High-Speed Machining with TrueMill

For aluminum (6061/7075), TrueMill enables extreme speeds: 20,000+ RPM, 10,000+ mm/min feed, 2xD axial depth at 8-12% radial engagement. Use 3-flute uncoated carbide or polished DLC-coated tools for best chip evacuation. Aluminum's tendency to weld to the cutter at high temperatures is mitigated by TrueMill's constant engagement — heat generation stays below the adhesion threshold. Enable air blast or MQL rather than flood coolant for highest speeds.
