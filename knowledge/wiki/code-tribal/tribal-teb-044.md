# Constant Scallop Adapts Step-Over to Local Surface Curvature

Tebis constant-scallop finishing automatically varies step-over based on local curvature to maintain identical scallop height everywhere. On flat areas, step-over increases (up to 3-5mm); on highly curved areas, it decreases (down to 0.1mm). This eliminates over-machining on flat regions and under-machining on curved regions. Set the target scallop height and the system optimizes. Compared to fixed step-over, cycle time reduces 20-40% with identical or better surface quality.
