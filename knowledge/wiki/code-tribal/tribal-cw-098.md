# Center Drilling — Short Rigid Pilot for Deep Holes

Use center drills (combined drill and countersink) for pilot holes that also need a chamfer. Unlike spot drills, center drills produce a chamfered entry and a shallow pilot hole simultaneously. For deep holes (L/D > 5), the center drill pilot must be deep enough to fully guide the twist drill point — at least to the point where the twist drill's full diameter engages. CAMWorks can sequence center → pilot → full drill automatically via TechDB operation mapping.
