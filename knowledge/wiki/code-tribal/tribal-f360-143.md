# Lens-Shape Barrel for Shallow Floor Finishing

The lens-shape barrel cutter (double-radius profile) is optimized for shallow and near-flat surfaces. Its symmetric profile contacts the surface at a low angle, sweeping a wide effective band on floors and gentle slopes. In Fusion, use Scallop or Parallel finishing with a lens barrel tool. The effective stepover for a lens barrel with 200mm radius on a flat surface can reach 8-12mm while maintaining Ra 0.4um finish — compared to 0.8mm stepover with a 10mm ball end mill for the same finish. Critical: verify the tool vendor's minimum and maximum tilt angles to ensure proper contact geometry.
