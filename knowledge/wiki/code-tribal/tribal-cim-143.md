# PCA for Multi-Response Process Optimization

When optimizing Ra, accuracy, tool life, and cycle time simultaneously, PCA reduces dimensionality. Extract PCs capturing 85-90% of variance. Optimize first 2-3 PCs to find balanced compromise. Avoids conflicting optima across individual responses. For mold machining with 6+ quality attributes, PCA typically captures the essential variation in 2-3 components.
