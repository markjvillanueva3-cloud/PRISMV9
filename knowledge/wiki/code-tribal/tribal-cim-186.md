Cimatron automates pin hole machining from mold design: center drill → pilot → ream to H7. Sort by diameter for tool changes. Single-flute reamer at low RPM for tight tolerance. Complete ejector plate program from mold BOM automatically. Saves 2-3 hours per plate.
