# Guide Bushing Management for Bar Feeding

ESPRIT automatically manages guide bushing position for Swiss-type lathes, coordinating the Z-axis slide with the guide bushing to maintain proper support. For operations close to the guide bushing face, ensure a minimum standoff distance of 1-2mm to prevent tool/bushing collision. When machining behind the guide bushing on the sub-spindle, remember there is no bushing support — reduce feed rates by 20-30% to compensate for the increased deflection.
