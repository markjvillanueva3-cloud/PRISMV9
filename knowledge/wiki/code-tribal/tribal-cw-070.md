# Cut-Off Operation — Part Separation with Chip Control

Cut-off (parting) operations separate the finished part from bar stock. Set the cut-off tool width to minimize material waste (typically 2-3mm for steel, 1.5-2mm for aluminum). Reduce feed rate by 30-50% at the center (last 2mm of diameter) to prevent the part from snapping off and generating a pip. Use a part catcher or sub-spindle to secure the part during cut-off. Pecking cut-off with 0.5mm retract cycles helps break chips on gummy materials like stainless steel.
