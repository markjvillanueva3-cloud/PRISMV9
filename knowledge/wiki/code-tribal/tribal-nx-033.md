# Rotary Roughing for Cylindrical Turn-Mill Parts

NX Rotary Roughing automatically determines the optimal turn-mill strategy for cylindrical and conical workpieces using ball, bull-nose, or flat endmills. The tool cuts in cylindrical levels with its axis perpendicular to the cut level, producing 4-axis motions. This is faster than conventional turning for interrupted cuts and hard materials where insert breakage is a concern.
