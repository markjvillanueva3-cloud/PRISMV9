# Boring — Internal Feature Machining with Proper Tool Selection

CAMWorks boring operations support rough boring, finish boring, and back-boring. For rough boring, ensure the boring bar diameter allows chip evacuation — bar diameter should be < 70% of bore diameter to leave clearance for chips. Use through-tool coolant for deep bores (L/D > 3). For finish boring, use a single-point diamond or CBN insert at low feed (0.02-0.05mm/rev) for mirror-finish bores. Anti-vibration boring bars are essential for L/D > 5.
