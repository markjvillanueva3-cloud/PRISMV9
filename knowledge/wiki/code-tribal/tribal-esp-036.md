# 5-Axis Trimming for Composite and Sheet Parts

ESPRIT's 5-axis trimming drives a router or end mill along the trim boundary of sheet metal, composite, or thermoformed parts while maintaining perpendicular orientation to the surface. Set the tool axis normal to the surface at each point and enable 'tilt correction' for draft angles. For composites, use diamond-coated or PCD tools at high speed (10,000-20,000 RPM) and low feed to minimize delamination. Enable 'corner radius compensation' for sharp trim corners.
