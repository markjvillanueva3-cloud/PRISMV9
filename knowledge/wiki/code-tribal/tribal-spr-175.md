# Guide Bushing Positioning for Deflection Control

Position guide bushing close to cutting zone to minimize unsupported length. Rule: max unsupported = 3× bar diameter for tight tolerance. For cross-drilling: bushing at drill location. SprutCAM programs should sequence operations to keep cutting forces near the bushing support point.
