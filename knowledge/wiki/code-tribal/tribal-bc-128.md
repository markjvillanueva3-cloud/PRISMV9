# Model Wrapping for Cylindrical and Curved Surface Art

BobART's model wrapping maps flat relief designs onto cylindrical or curved surfaces for rotary axis machining. This enables engraving on columns, rings, gun stocks, and other non-flat parts. The wrapping algorithm accounts for the surface curvature to prevent distortion of the original design. For 4th-axis rotary work, set the wrap diameter to match the part OD. Combine with BobCAD's 4-axis indexing or continuous rotary toolpath for complete cylindrical coverage.
