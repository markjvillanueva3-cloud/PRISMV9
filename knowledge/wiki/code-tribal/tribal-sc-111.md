# Wizard Customization — Tailor AFRM Templates to Shop Standards

Customize AFRM's default machining templates to match your shop's tooling inventory and process standards. By default, AFRM selects generic strategies and tool sizes that may not match your available tools. Create custom AFRM templates that specify your standard tool numbers, preferred roughing strategies (iMachining vs. conventional), and finishing methods. Store customized templates in the SolidCAM user folder so they persist across software updates. This makes AFRM output directly usable without manual editing for 60-80% of standard features.
