# Surface Extension for Clean Exit

Extend 2-5mm beyond edges. SolidCAM creates extensions. Prevents deceleration marks. Also prevents corner radius deviation. Critical for visible mold surfaces. Always extend when finishing near part edges — it costs seconds but saves hours of polishing.
