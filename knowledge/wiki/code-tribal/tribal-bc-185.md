# BobART Lithophane Creation for Translucent Materials

BobART creates lithophanes — translucent panels where image depth modulates light transmission. Convert a grayscale image to relief with inverted depth mapping (dark areas become thicker, blocking more light). Typical material: white acrylic, 3-5mm total thickness, 0.5-1.0mm minimum wall at highlights. Machine with a small ball-nose (1-3mm) at 0.1-0.2mm step-over for fine image detail. Set the flat-bottom thickness to 0.5mm to prevent breakthrough at the brightest image areas. BobCAD machines the back side of the panel; the front remains flat for even light distribution.
