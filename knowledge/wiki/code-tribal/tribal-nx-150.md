# Uncertainty Budget for Titanium Aerospace Components

Total machining uncertainty for Ti-6Al-4V structural parts: machine positioning (±0.005mm for large gantry), tool deflection (±0.010mm for long-reach tools at 150N cutting force), thermal growth (±0.008mm over 6hr cycle), material spring-back (±0.003mm from residual stress), and fixture compliance (±0.004mm). RSS total: ±0.015mm. This determines the feasible tolerance class. For ±0.025mm specs, Cpk ≈ 1.11 — marginal.
