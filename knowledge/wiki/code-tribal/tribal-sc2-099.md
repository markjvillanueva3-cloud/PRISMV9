# Titanium Machining with Low Speed and High Feed

Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) requires low cutting speed (40-60 m/min for carbide) with high feed per tooth (0.1-0.15mm/tooth) to generate sufficient chip thickness for heat removal through the chip. Use TrueMill at 10-15% radial engagement with 1.5xD axial depth. High-pressure coolant (70+ bar through-tool) is essential. Avoid dwelling or stopping in the cut — titanium work-hardens rapidly when the tool rubs without cutting. Use 4-5 flute variable-helix tools to suppress chatter.
