# Multi-Machine Energy Optimization for Green Manufacturing

ESPRIT's energy module estimates power consumption per operation based on cutting force models and machine efficiency data. For multi-machine shops, the scheduler can optimize job assignment to minimize total energy: (1) assign roughing to high-power machines running near optimal load (65-80% spindle capacity is most energy-efficient per unit MRR), (2) assign finishing to smaller machines that consume less standby power, (3) schedule energy-intensive operations during off-peak electricity hours. ESPRIT reports kWh per part for each program, enabling ISO 50001 energy management compliance. Typical energy savings: 10-15% through intelligent scheduling alone.
