# iMachining Aluminum — Level 6-8 with High RPM and Chip Evacuation

Aluminum (6061, 7075, 2024) benefits from aggressive iMachining levels (6-8) with high spindle speeds (12,000-24,000 RPM) and large depths of cut (up to 2xD). The Technology Wizard optimizes for aluminum's low cutting forces by increasing feed rates to 5,000-15,000 mm/min. Use 3-flute uncoated carbide or ZrN-coated end mills — TiAlN coating creates aluminum adhesion. Critical: enable air blast or MQL rather than flood coolant to prevent chip re-cutting. Set chip load per tooth to 0.10-0.20mm for optimal chip formation and surface finish.
