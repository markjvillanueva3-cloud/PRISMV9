# Wiener Process for Stochastic Wear Modeling

dVB = μdt + σdW. Predict RUL distribution P(VB > threshold at time t). Update μ and σ from measurements. More accurate than deterministic Taylor for variable conditions. For Cimatron multi-operation mold programs where cutting conditions vary significantly between operations.
