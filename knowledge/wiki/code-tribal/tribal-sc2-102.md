# Inconel and Superalloy Machining with Aggressive Coolant

Nickel-based superalloys (Inconel 718, Waspaloy) require very low surface speeds (20-40 m/min for carbide) with aggressive coolant delivery (through-tool at 70+ bar). TrueMill's controlled engagement is critical — Inconel generates extreme heat at the cutting zone and any engagement spike causes rapid notch wear. Use ceramic inserts at 200-300 m/min for roughing (with round inserts to distribute notch wear) and carbide at 25-35 m/min for finishing. Tool life is typically 5-15 minutes.
