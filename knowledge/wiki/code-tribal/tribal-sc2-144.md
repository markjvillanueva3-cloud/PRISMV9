# SURFCAM Multi-Axis Lead and Lag Angles for Surface Finish

In 5-axis ball-nose finishing, the lead angle (tilt forward in the feed direction) and lag angle (tilt backward) control the effective cutting radius and chip evacuation. A lead angle of 10-15° moves the cutting zone away from the tool tip where surface speed is zero, dramatically improving surface finish. Lag angles of 5-10° are used for climb milling on concave surfaces. Never set lead angle to 0° with ball-nose tools — the tool center has zero speed, causing rubbing and poor finish.
