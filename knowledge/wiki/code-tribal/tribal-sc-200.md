# Sensitivity: iMachining Level is the #1 Lever

For roughing: iMachining material level is the single most impactful parameter (40% of MRR variation). For finishing: step-over is #1 (35% of Ra). For iMachining, the level slider replaces speed/feed/DOC optimization — it controls all three simultaneously. Focus on level calibration first, then finishing parameters.
