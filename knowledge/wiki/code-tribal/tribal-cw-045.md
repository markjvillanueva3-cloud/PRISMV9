# Simultaneous 5-Axis — Continuous Tool Orientation for Complex Surfaces

Simultaneous 5-axis machining continuously adjusts all five axes during cutting, keeping the tool optimally oriented relative to the surface normal. This maintains consistent chip load and surface finish across compound-curved surfaces. Set the lead angle to 10-15° (tool tilted forward in feed direction) and tilt angle to 0-5° (sideways tilt) for ball end mills. Excessive lead angle causes the tool tip to disengage, losing surface quality; insufficient lead causes center-cutting with zero surface speed.
