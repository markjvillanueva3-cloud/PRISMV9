# Aluminum HSM with Maximum MRR

For aluminum (6061/7075): Vortex or offset area clear with 50% radial engagement, 2×D axial depth, 300-500 m/min surface speed, 0.15-0.25mm feed/tooth. Use 3-flute uncoated carbide or polished DLC-coated tools. Chip evacuation is the primary concern — use air blast or high-volume flood. PowerMill's 'Feed Rate Optimization' can push MRR to 500-1000 cm³/min on high-speed machines.
