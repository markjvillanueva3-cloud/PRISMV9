# Hardened Steel Strategies (>45 HRC)

For hardened steels above 45 HRC in ESPRIT, switch from conventional to high-speed machining approach: light radial engagement (3-8% of tool diameter), shallow axial depth (0.1-0.3mm for finishing), and high surface speed (150-300 m/min with AlTiN or TiSiN coated carbide). ProfitMilling is essential — any engagement spike in hardened steel fractures the cutting edge. For finishing, use bull-nose cutters at a 10-15° tilt angle rather than ball-nose to avoid zero-speed at the tool tip.
