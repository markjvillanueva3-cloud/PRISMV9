# Smooth Transitions — Avoid Witness Lines at Strategy Boundaries

Witness lines appear where two toolpath strategies meet (e.g., steep-area Z-level meets shallow-area scallop). Set overlap between strategies to 1-2mm and blend the transition zone. Use consistent cutting direction across the boundary. For critical cosmetic surfaces, extend both strategies 3-5mm past the boundary and use the better result in the overlap zone. CAMWorks' boundary smoothing option helps, but manual verification of transition quality is still recommended for A-surface molds.
