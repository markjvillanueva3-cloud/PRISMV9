# Hardened Steel Finishing for Mold and Die

50-62 HRC: CBN or nano-coated ball-end, 100-200 m/min, 0.03-0.06mm fz, step-over per target Ra. Air blast only. hyperMILL constant scallop for uniform quality. Target Ra 0.4-0.8μm directly from machining. MAXX Finishing with barrel cutters reduces polishing time by additional 30-50% on suitable surfaces.
