# Hardened Steel Machining Strategies by Hardness Range

In WorkNC, hardened steel strategy depends on hardness. Below Rc 45: carbide at 100-150 m/min with adaptive roughing at moderate depths. Above Rc 55: CBN or ceramic-coated carbide at 150-300 m/min, very light depths (0.05-0.2 mm radial, 0.1-0.5 mm axial), and HSM strategies. For H13 mold steel at Rc 48-52, use AlCrN-coated carbide with constant engagement paths.
