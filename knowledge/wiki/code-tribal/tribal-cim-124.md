# Digital Twin for Continuous Process Improvement

Build digital twin: simulate forces from toolpath, predict thermal deformation, estimate finish from feed marks/vibration. Compare to CMM data. When error >10%, update parameters. After 10 iterations, predictions converge to ±3%. Enables predictive quality — flag bad parts before CMM. Especially valuable for multi-cavity molds where consistency is critical.
