# ProfitMilling Constant Engagement Eliminates Load Spikes

ESPRIT ProfitMilling maintains a constant tool engagement angle throughout the entire roughing toolpath by dynamically adjusting stepover. Unlike conventional roughing where engagement spikes in corners cause tool breakage, ProfitMilling limits radial engagement to a user-defined maximum (typically 8-15% of cutter diameter for steel), enabling 2-3x deeper axial depths and 2-3x higher feed rates. This can reduce cycle times by up to 75% and extend tool life by 500%.
