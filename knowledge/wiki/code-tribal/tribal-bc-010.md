# Advanced Rough with HSM Pattern and Flatland Detection

BobCAD's Advanced Rough operation offers three cut patterns: offset, parallel, and HSM (high-speed machining) with automatic tool engagement control. The HSM pattern is equivalent to Adaptive Roughing within the 3D roughing context. Enable 'Flatland Detection' to automatically identify flat-bottomed regions and apply optimized facing passes rather than the general roughing strategy. This reduces cycle time on parts with mixed 3D and flat geometry by 15-25%.
