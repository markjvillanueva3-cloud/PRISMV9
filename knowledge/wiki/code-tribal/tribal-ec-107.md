# Brass and Copper Alloy Machining Tips

Brass and copper alloys in Edgecam are easy to machine but have unique requirements. Use sharp, uncoated carbide or PCD tools. For free-machining brass (C360), high speeds (200-400 m/min) and aggressive feeds work well. For copper (C110), reduce speed to 100-200 m/min and use positive rake to prevent smearing. Avoid re-cutting chips — brass chips are small but can embed in the surface. Use flood coolant for copper to prevent material welding to the tool.
