# Tolerance Settings Best Practices

Roughing 0.1mm, semi 0.02mm, finishing 0.005-0.01mm. For hardened finishing: 0.005mm + constant scallop = polishing-ready. Don't over-relax. MAXX barrel: may need tighter tolerance due to curvature sensitivity of barrel contact zone.
