# iMachining Titanium — Level 2-3 with Controlled Heat and Engagement

Titanium alloys (Ti-6Al-4V, Ti-6Al-2Sn) require conservative iMachining levels (2-3) due to low thermal conductivity and high chemical reactivity. Set cutting speed to 40-60 m/min with TiAlN or AlCrN coated carbide. The Technology Wizard limits radial engagement to prevent rubbing, which causes work hardening. Use depth of cut equal to 1-1.5xD and maintain chip thickness above 0.06mm — thin chips in titanium generate excessive heat without removing material efficiently. High-pressure coolant (70+ bar) through-tool is essential to flush chips from the cutting zone and prevent chip re-cutting that causes notch wear.
