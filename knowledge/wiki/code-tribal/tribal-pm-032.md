# Vortex Trochoidal Roughing for Hard Materials

PowerMill's Vortex strategy uses trochoidal tool motion to maintain constant engagement angle. For hardened steel (>45 HRC): set engagement angle to 40-60°, full axial depth (1×D), and feed rates 3-5× higher than conventional. This extends carbide tool life by 200-300% compared to traditional offset roughing. Enable 'Adaptive Feed' to automatically reduce feed in tight corners.
