# Titanium 5-Axis Machining with Trochoidal Roughing

For Ti-6Al-4V in Tebis: trochoidal roughing with 8-10% radial engagement, 1×D axial depth, 45-60 m/min, 0.08-0.12mm feed/tooth. Flood coolant at 70+ bar through-spindle essential. Use AlTiN-coated carbide. Never recut chips — titanium chips work-harden. Tebis constant engagement roughing prevents the intermittent loading that destroys inserts. Monitor spindle load continuously.
