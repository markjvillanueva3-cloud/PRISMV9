# BobCAD Barrel Cutter vs Ball-Nose Cost-Benefit Analysis

Evaluate barrel cutter ROI in BobCAD by comparing cycle times: a barrel cutter with 100mm radius typically finishes a 500x500mm surface in 15 minutes vs 120 minutes for a 10mm ball-nose at equivalent scallop. Barrel cutters cost 3-5x more than ball-nose tools but the cycle time savings pay back within 2-5 parts on large surfaces. The break-even point depends on machine hourly rate and part size. For surfaces smaller than 100x100mm, ball-nose tools are more economical due to shorter lead-in/out and simpler 3-axis programming. Factor in the 5-axis machine requirement for barrel cutters.
