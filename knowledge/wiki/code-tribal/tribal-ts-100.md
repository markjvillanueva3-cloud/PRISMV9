# Hardened Steel Machining Below Rc 45 vs Above Rc 55

In TopSolid, hardened steel machining strategy varies dramatically with hardness. Below Rc 45: use carbide endmills at 100-150 m/min, conventional adaptive roughing, and moderate depths. Above Rc 55: use CBN or ceramic-coated carbide at 150-300 m/min, very light depths (0.05-0.2 mm radial, 0.1-0.5 mm axial), and high-speed strategies with constant engagement. For mold steels (H13 at Rc 48-52), use the high-hardness strategy with AlCrN-coated carbide.
