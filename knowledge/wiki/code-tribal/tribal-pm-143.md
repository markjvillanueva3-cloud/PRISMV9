# Polishing-Ready Surface from Machining

Achieve Ra 0.4-0.8μm directly: (1) step-over per scallop formula, (2) constant scallop mode, (3) sharp tools (<50% life), (4) lead/lean angles for ball-end, (5) air blast for hardened steel. Reduces polishing 50-70% on mold surfaces, saving 20-40 hours per mold. PowerMill's 3D offset finishing with constant scallop is the preferred strategy.
