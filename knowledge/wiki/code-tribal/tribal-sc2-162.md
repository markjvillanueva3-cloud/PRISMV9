# SURFCAM Swiss-Type Bar Stock Remnant Optimization

SURFCAM's Swiss-type bar optimization calculates the number of parts per bar and minimizes the remnant length. Set the bar length, grip length, cutoff width, and part length in the stock definition. SURFCAM reports the remnant length and can adjust part spacing to minimize waste. For expensive materials (titanium, Inconel), optimize cutoff width — switching from a 1.5mm to 1.0mm cutoff blade saves 0.5mm per part, yielding 1-2 additional parts per 3m bar. The savings compound across production volumes of 10,000+ parts.
