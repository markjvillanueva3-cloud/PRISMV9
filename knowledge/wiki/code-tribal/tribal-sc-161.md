# Wiener Process for Stochastic iMachining Wear

dVB = μdt + σdW. iMachining's constant engagement makes μ more predictable and σ smaller than conventional. Wiener parameters are more stable for iMachining programs — faster convergence of RUL predictions. Calibrate separately from conventional programs.
