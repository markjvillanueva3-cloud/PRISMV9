# Facing Operations for Mold Plates

Facing mold plate surfaces: use face mill or large flat-end mill. Step-over 65-75% of cutter diameter. One-way cutting to avoid conventional on return. For interrupted faces (pin holes, pockets), reduce feed 20% at entry. Wiper inserts give single-pass mirror finish. Critical for parting surface flatness — tolerance ±0.01mm across the entire plate face.
