# Gutowski Energy Model for Benchmarking

P = P₀ + k×MRR. Specific energy e = P₀/MRR + k. At low MRR, P₀/MRR dominates — energy efficiency drops. Maximize MRR within limits. Benchmark Cimatron programs by comparing e across strategies. Higher MRR roughing (iMachining-style) always more energy-efficient per unit of material removed.
