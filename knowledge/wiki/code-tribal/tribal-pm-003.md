# Offset Area Clear Helical Entry Prevents Plunge Shock

Always configure helical entry in Offset Area Clear for carbide end mills. Set helix diameter to 110-130% of tool diameter and ramp angle to 2-5° for steel, 5-8° for aluminum. The helical entry distributes axial load across the cutting edges rather than concentrating it on the tool tip, extending tool life by 200-300% compared to plunge entry. For hardened materials above 45 HRC, reduce helix diameter to 105% and ramp angle to 1-3°.
