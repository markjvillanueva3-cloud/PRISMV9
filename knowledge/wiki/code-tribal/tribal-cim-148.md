# Copper Electrode Machining Parameters

Copper electrodes: sharp uncoated or diamond-polished tools, 200-500 m/min, 0.05-0.15mm feed/tooth. Flood coolant for chip evacuation — copper produces long stringy chips. Better surface finish than graphite — preferred for precision EDM with fine Ra requirements. Cimatron tracks electrode spark gap offsets per operation type (roughing/finishing).
