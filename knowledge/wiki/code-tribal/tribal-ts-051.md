# Multi-Turret Synchronization via Chronogram

TopSolid's chronogram (timing diagram) enables synchronization of operations across multiple turrets, spindles, and channels on complex mill-turn machines. Drag operations in the timeline to create simultaneous or sequential execution, with automatic detection of interference zones. Use the 'Wait' synchronization point to ensure one channel pauses until another completes a critical operation. The chronogram visually displays idle time, allowing optimization for minimum cycle time.
