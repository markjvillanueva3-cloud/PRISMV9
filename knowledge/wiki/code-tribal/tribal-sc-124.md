# iMachining Copper and Brass — Level 6-7 with Sharp Geometry Tools

Copper and brass alloys machine easily with iMachining Levels 6-7. Free-cutting brass (C360) allows speeds up to 300 m/min; pure copper requires 150-200 m/min due to gummy chip formation. Use uncoated polished carbide or PCD tools with sharp positive rake angles (12-15 degrees). The Technology Wizard increases feed per tooth to 0.12-0.18mm for brass. For copper electrode machining (EDM applications), prioritize surface finish by reducing the iMachining level by one step and enabling the finishing pass option. Avoid TiAlN coatings — aluminum in the coating causes adhesion with copper alloys.
