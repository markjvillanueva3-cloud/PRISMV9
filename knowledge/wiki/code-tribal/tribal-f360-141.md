# Tangent Barrel Finishing on Steep Walls

Use the tangent barrel (also called tangent-plane or conical barrel) cutter for finishing steep walls and near-vertical surfaces. The tangent barrel's cutting edge contacts the wall surface tangentially, covering a large surface band per pass. In Fusion, select a Steep and Shallow or Multi-Axis strategy and set the tool type to Barrel - Tangent. The effective cutting band width is determined by the barrel radius and the wall angle. For walls between 60-90 degrees from horizontal, a tangent barrel with 100mm radius and 5mm stepdown can match the finish quality of 30+ conventional contour passes.
