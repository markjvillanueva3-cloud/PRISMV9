# Pencil Trace — Clean Internal Fillets and Blend Regions

Pencil trace machining follows the intersection lines between surfaces, targeting internal fillets and concave blends that other strategies miss. Use pencil trace after Z-level or scallop finishing to remove the residual cusp material in tight corners. Tool diameter should be ≤ 2x the fillet radius for effective contact. Enable multi-pass pencil for deep fillets where a single pass cannot reach full depth. Pencil trace adds minimal cycle time but dramatically reduces hand polishing.
