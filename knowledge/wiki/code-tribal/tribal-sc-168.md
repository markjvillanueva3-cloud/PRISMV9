# Exergy Analysis Comparing iMachining vs Conventional

iMachining: higher MRR = lower P₀/MRR = better energy efficiency per unit removed. Constant engagement also reduces friction losses. Total exergy destruction 20-30% lower than conventional roughing at same total material removed. Quantify for green manufacturing certifications.
