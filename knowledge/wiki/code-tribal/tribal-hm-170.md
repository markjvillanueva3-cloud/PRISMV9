# Flat Area Detection for MAXX Strategy

hyperMILL detects flat areas (5-10° threshold). MAXX Finishing barrel cutters are most effective on flat and gently curved regions. Use flat detection to automatically assign MAXX to suitable regions and ball-end to highly curved regions. Hybrid strategy maximizes cycle time reduction.
