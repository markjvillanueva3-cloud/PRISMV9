# Facing with Wiper Inserts

Face mill with 65-75% step-over. One-way cutting. Wiper inserts give single-pass mirror finish. For interrupted faces, reduce feed 20% at entry. Critical for parting surface flatness on molds — tolerance ±0.01mm across entire plate. PowerMill's facing operation handles irregular stock shapes and can avoid clamping regions automatically.
