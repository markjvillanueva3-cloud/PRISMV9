# Plastics and Engineering Polymers Machining

For plastics (PEEK, Delrin, nylon): use single-flute or 2-flute uncoated tools with high positive rake. Surface speed 200-500 m/min, feed 0.1-0.2mm/tooth. Avoid excessive heat — plastics melt, not cut, when too hot. Use compressed air cooling. SprutCAM's 'Down Cut' direction prevents lifting thin plastic features. For PEEK medical implants, use diamond-coated tools for biocompatibility (no metal contamination).
