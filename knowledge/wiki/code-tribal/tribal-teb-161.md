# Polishing-Ready Surface from Machining

Achieve Ra 0.4-0.8μm directly: (1) scallop formula for step-over, (2) constant scallop mode, (3) sharp tools <50% life, (4) lead/lean for ball-end, (5) air blast for hardened steel. Reduces polishing 50-70% on mold surfaces. Tebis 3D offset with constant scallop is the preferred strategy for polishing-ready results.
