# Conformal Cooling Channel Programming

Cimatron designs and machines conformal cooling channels that follow the mold cavity shape. Compared to straight-drilled channels, conformal channels reduce cooling time 20-40% and part warpage 30-50%. Program with 5-axis contour milling or additive DED. Verify flow connectivity at channel intersections.
