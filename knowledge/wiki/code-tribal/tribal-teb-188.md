# Facing with Wiper Inserts for Mirror Finish

Face mill 65-75% step-over. One-way cutting. Wiper inserts give single-pass mirror finish. Interrupted faces: reduce feed 20% at entry. Critical for mold parting surface flatness ±0.01mm across entire plate. Tebis facing handles irregular stock and avoids clamping regions automatically.
