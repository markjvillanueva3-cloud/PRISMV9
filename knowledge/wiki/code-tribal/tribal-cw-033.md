# Z-Level Roughing — Waterline Strategy for 3D Cavity Stock Removal

Z-level roughing machines a 3D cavity in horizontal slices, each at a constant Z depth. Set the step-down to 50-70% of axial depth capability to balance cycle time and stock uniformity. For steep-walled cavities (draft > 15°), Z-level roughing leaves minimal stock variation. On shallow areas (draft < 10°), the staircase effect leaves excessive stock — follow up with a flat-area or scallop finishing pass to address these zones.
