# Automated Tool Selection via Machining Advisors

The Manufacturing Extension includes Machining Advisors that analyze your part geometry and suggest tools, strategies, and parameters. The advisor examines pocket depths, fillet radii, and wall heights to recommend appropriate cutter diameters and flute lengths. Accept advisor suggestions as starting points, then refine — the advisor optimizes for safety margins and may recommend more conservative parameters than an experienced programmer would use. Always verify the suggested tool exists in your physical inventory.
