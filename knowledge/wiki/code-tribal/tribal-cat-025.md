# Multi-Axis Sweeping Lead/Lag Angle for Surface Quality

In CATIA Multi-Axis Sweeping, set a lead angle of 10-15 degrees to tilt the ball-nose tool forward along the feed direction. This moves the cutting point away from the tool tip (where surface speed is zero) to a region with actual cutting velocity, dramatically improving surface finish. For concave surfaces, also add 3-5 degrees of tilt (side angle) to prevent the tool shank from colliding with adjacent walls.
