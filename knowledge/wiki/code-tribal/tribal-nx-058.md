# Area Milling for Flat and Near-Flat Finishing

Area Milling is the most efficient strategy for finishing flat and near-flat surfaces (slope < 10 degrees). Use a flat or bull-nose endmill with step-over at 60-70% of the flat contact width for maximum material removal rate on planar regions. Enable Follow Part for the cut pattern so the tool follows floor contours rather than using a fixed zig-zag direction. Area Milling out-performs Contour Area by 40% on predominantly flat cavity floors.
