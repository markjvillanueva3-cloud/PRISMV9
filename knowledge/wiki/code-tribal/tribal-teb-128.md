# Taper Ball Nose for Draft Wall Finishing

Taper ball nose cutters combine a ball tip with tapered shank for improved rigidity on deep cavity walls. In Tebis, define ball radius and taper half-angle precisely. Use for Z-level finishing on draft walls — the taper provides a larger effective cutting zone while the ball tip handles floor transitions. Taper angle must match wall draft angle to prevent interference.
