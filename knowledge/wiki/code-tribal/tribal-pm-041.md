# Flowline Finishing for Natural Surface Flow

Flowline finishing follows the natural flow of surfaces between two boundary curves. Define 'Start Boundary' and 'End Boundary' — the toolpath interpolates between them. Ideal for: turbine blades (hub-to-shroud), automotive body panels (front-to-rear), and aerofoils. Step-over is measured along the cross-flow direction. Produces superior surface quality compared to raster on doubly-curved surfaces.
