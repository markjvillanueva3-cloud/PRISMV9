/diagnose-fix — Forensic Fix Pipeline (U-CK22)

Composed front-end for "something broke, here's the artifact, fix it." Classifies the failure, proposes a fix grounded in error-pattern memory, applies under per-file scrutiny, runs tests, traces the verdict. R8: adds 0 logic.
