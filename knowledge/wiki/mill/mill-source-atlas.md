# Mill Galaxy Open-Source Atlas (Verified Living Resources)

A curated, verified directory of the best FREE + LEGAL **living** (continuously-updated) resources for milling / machining processes. The purpose is a non-stagnant "keep-learning" curriculum: every entry points at a source that stays current on its own, so the galaxy's knowledge does not rot.

## Free college courses

Full course / lecture-series homepages with free, openly-licensed materials.

- [MIT OpenCourseWare — 2.008 Design and Manufacturing II (Spring 2025)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-008-design-and-manufacturing-ii-spring-2025/) — Free MIT course (CC license): manufacturing processes, equipment/control, quality (SPC), and design-for-manufacturing; metal-cutting taught as mechanics -> forces/power -> practice. Lecture notes, problem sets, and exams with solutions. The canonical living teaching-order reference for the mill galaxy.
- [MIT 2.810 — Manufacturing Processes and Systems (course homepage)](https://web.mit.edu/2.810/www/) — Free MIT course homepage: machining alongside assembly, injection molding, casting, thermoforming; frames a process through its physics AND statistical variation, and as one node in a larger production SYSTEM. Refreshed each term it runs.
- [NPTEL — Manufacturing Processes II (IIT Kharagpur, Profs. Chattopadhyay/Chattopadhyay/Paul)](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/112105126) — Free full Indian-Institute-of-Technology course on metal cutting and machining processes, with recorded video lectures and notes. A complete, structured machining curriculum at zero cost.
- [TITANS of CNC Academy](https://academy.titansofcnc.com/) — "The world's largest free CAD/CAM and CNC machine training online." Free, regularly-expanded course tracks for milling (and turning, 5-axis, fixturing, inspection, G&M code). The most practitioner-current free living curriculum for shop-floor mill programming.

## Free textbooks & references

Openly-licensed textbook homepages and continuously-maintained reference handbooks.

- [LibreTexts — Mechanical Engineering bookshelf](https://eng.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Mechanical_Engineering) — Free open-access ME texts (Statics, Mechanics of Materials, Thermodynamics, Dynamics) underpinning the force/deflection/material reasoning behind milling. Community-maintained, grows over time.
- [LibreTexts — Industrial and Systems Engineering bookshelf](https://eng.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Industrial_and_Systems_Engineering) — Free open-access texts on industrial engineering, control systems, and simulation/lean (e.g. "Introduction to Industrial Engineering", "Beyond Lean - Simulation in Practice") — the process/throughput layer around a mill operation.
- [LibreTexts — Introductory Statistics bookshelf](https://stats.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Introductory_Statistics) — Free open-access statistics texts (OpenStax, OpenIntro, Statistical Thinking for the 21st Century, etc.) — the foundation for SPC / process-capability / control-chart QC on milled parts.
- [OpenStax — Introductory Statistics 2e](https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/1-introduction) — Free, CC-licensed, peer-reviewed statistics textbook (read online or download). Directly supports the mill galaxy's SPC and tolerance-verification logic.
- [NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods (homepage)](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/) — Free U.S. Government statistical reference handbook; the authoritative living source for control charts, process capability, and DOE used in machining quality control.
- [NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook — 6.1.6 Process Capability Indices (Cp/Cpk)](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section1/pmc16.htm) — The specific gov section on Cp/Cpk/Cpm capability indices — the measured-vs-spec-limits math behind a milled part's "is this process capable" gate.
- [Machining Doctor — machining technical hub](https://www.machiningdoctor.com/) — Continuously-updated free machining reference: calculators (speeds/power/hardness), a 700+ material database, charts, a machinist "glossary/wikipedia", and G-code learning. The single most-current free general machining reference for milling.

## Archives & open data / gov reports

U.S. government portals and open archives — the "data reports" layer.

- [Data.gov — U.S. Government open data portal](https://www.data.gov/) — 360k+ free federal datasets (manufacturing, economic, materials), continuously updated. The catalog entry point for any gov data the mill galaxy needs.
- [NIST Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP)](https://www.nist.gov/mep) — U.S. Department of Commerce / NIST program for small/medium manufacturers; living source of manufacturing best-practice guidance, training, and assessments relevant to a machine shop.
- [NIST — SI Units: Length (the meter)](https://www.nist.gov/pml/owm/si-units-length) — NIST Office of Weights & Measures metrology reference: the speed-of-light meter definition and measurement traceability that every milled-part dimensional tolerance is ultimately verified against.
- [NASA STEM / Learning Resources](https://www.nasa.gov/stem/) — Free U.S. Government STEM education portal (videos, interactive tools, activities) spanning engineering and manufacturing topics — a no-cost, continuously-refreshed learning feed.
- [Internet Archive — Texts collection](https://archive.org/details/texts) — Free digital library of public-domain and openly-licensed texts (older machining/engineering handbooks, government technical reports). Living mirror that keeps out-of-copyright machining literature accessible.

## Standards & authoritative bodies

Standards-organization landing pages relevant to milling / machining process control and safety.

- [ASME — Codes & Standards portal](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards) — The American Society of Mechanical Engineers' authoritative standards hub (e.g. the Y14 GD&T / digital-product-definition family and B-series dimensional standards) governing how milled-part geometry and tolerances are specified.
- [OSHA — Machine Guarding (safety topic page)](https://www.osha.gov/machine-guarding) — U.S. Government (OSHA) workplace-safety landing page for machine guarding; the authoritative living reference for milling-machine point-of-operation hazards, guard requirements, and the four guard types. Doubles as the milling safety/compliance authority for the galaxy.
