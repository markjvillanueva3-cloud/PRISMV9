## Why

A domain with N engines and no aggregator has no way to fuse predictions. The
inverse-variance (Kalman-style) ensemble weights each engine by 1/var so a noisy
engine is automatically down-ranked — better than a fixed average and far better
than picking one engine arbitrarily.
