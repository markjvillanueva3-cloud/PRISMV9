## What it checks (sampled describe/it titles)

- SpeedFeedOrchestrator boring uses the BORE diameter (U-SFC-ORCH-BORE-DIAMETER)
- boring rpm is computed from bore_diameter_mm: rpm == 1000*Vc/(pi*D_bore)
- boring falls back to the workpiece OD when no bore diameter is given (prior behavior, unchanged)
- a smaller bore yields a higher rpm than the workpiece-OD fallback (the bore drives rpm)
- bore_diameter_mm is ignored for non-boring ops (turning still uses the workpiece OD)
- boring Vc is physically plausible, not the collapsed ~1-2 m/min tool-diameter bug
