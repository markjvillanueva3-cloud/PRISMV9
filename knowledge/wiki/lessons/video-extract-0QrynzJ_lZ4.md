## Opening snippet

Almost everything made of metal is machined with an insert. The insert has to withstand extreme heat and force, so it's made of some of the hardest materials in the world. A typical insert is made of 80% tungsten carbide and a metal matrix that binds the hard carbide grains together, where cobalt is the most common.
