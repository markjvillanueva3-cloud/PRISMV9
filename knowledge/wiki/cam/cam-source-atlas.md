# CAM Open Source Atlas

A curated, link-verified directory of the best **FREE + LEGAL + LIVING** resources for computer-aided
manufacturing / toolpath strategy. The goal is a non-stagnant "keep-learning" curriculum for the CAM
galaxy that stays current because it points to **continuously-updated source homepages** (full course
catalogs, textbook homepages, gov data programs, standards landing pages, active lecture channels)
rather than one-off article snapshots.

**Scope vs. siblings (R8 — no duplication):**
- This atlas is DISTINCT from [`cam-foundations.md`](cam-foundations.md), which promotes specific
  WebFetch-confirmed *method/formula-structure facts* from individual pages. The atlas instead curates
  *broader living homepages* you return to repeatedly as they update.
- It is the curated + verified + per-galaxy form of the flat bulk pointer list in
  `state/shared/specs/GALAXY-FREE-SOURCE-CORPUS-2026-06-09.md` (which is NOT auto-invoked and is not
  link-verified). This atlas adds value by *verifying each link resolves*, *curating the strongest
  continuously-updated sources*, and *organizing by type* — it does not re-dump that corpus.

Every URL below was confirmed live + on-topic by an actual WebFetch on **2026-06-10**. No URL is listed
that was not fetched and confirmed in this pass (R12). No physics/cutting constants appear here — this
is a link directory, not a number source.

## Free college courses

Full free course / catalog homepages (continuously expanded by the institution) for the
manufacturing-process theory that CAM toolpath strategy sits on top of.

- **MIT OpenCourseWare (homepage)** — https://ocw.mit.edu/ — Free MIT lecture notes, exams, and videos,
  no registration; 2,500+ courses including Course 2 (Mechanical Engineering) manufacturing-process and
  metal-cutting material that underpins chip-thinning, climb-vs-conventional, and finishing-stepover
  reasoning. The canonical free-college-course entry point for the galaxy.
- **NPTEL — "Manufacturing Processes I," IIT Roorkee (course 112107144)** —
  https://nptel.ac.in/courses/112107144 — Free Indian open-courseware unit (Prof. H.S. Shan / Prof. S.R.
  Gupta / Dr. Pradeep Kumar) covering manufacturing processes and machining; a non-MIT lineage so the
  curriculum is not single-institution-dependent.

## Free textbooks & references

Open-license / freely-readable textbook homepages and free formula/calculator references that update at
the source.

- **Open Oregon Pressbooks — "Manufacturing Processes 4-5"** —
  https://openoregon.pressbooks.pub/manufacturingprocesses45/ — CC-BY 4.0 free textbook; dedicated
  chapters on Milling Machines, Lathe Machines, Surface Grinders, and a full **CNC** chapter (machine
  axes, VMCs, CNC language, Haas control + Mastercam). The closest free open textbook to the CAM galaxy's
  exact domain.
- **OpenStax — University Physics Volume 1** —
  https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/1-introduction — Free CC-licensed physics
  textbook; the mechanics/forces/friction chapters are the first-principles basis for the heat, wear, and
  cutting-force trade-offs a CAM strategy balances.
- **LibreTexts — Industrial & Systems Engineering bookshelf** —
  https://eng.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Industrial_and_Systems_Engineering — Open (Dept. of Education /
  NSF funded) library of free engineering texts on manufacturing-process management, lean, and systems
  optimization — the planning/optimization layer above raw toolpath generation.
- **Machining Doctor — Calculators & Formulas index** —
  https://www.machiningdoctor.com/calculators/ — Free, continuously-maintained index of milling/turning
  calculators (chip thinning, ball-nose surface finish, MRR, machining power, SFM/RPM) with the formula
  STRUCTURE behind each. Good for the geometry/relationship forms CAM models (numbers stay owner-gated).
- **CNCCookbook — Complete Guide to CAM Toolpaths and Operations for Milling** —
  https://www.cnccookbook.com/complete-guide-to-cam-toolpaths-and-operations-for-milling/ — Free 9-chapter
  reference on toolpath strategy itself (adaptive/HSM constant-engagement, pocketing, 2D/3D profiling,
  rest machining, entry methods) — the single most galaxy-on-topic free reference here.

## Archives & open data / gov reports

US-government and open-standard program homepages — the "data reports" / standards programs that stay
live and updated, which a model-based CAM pipeline interoperates with.

- **NIST — Digital Thread for Manufacturing (program)** —
  https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/digital-thread-manufacturing — Live, ongoing NIST program (last
  updated 2025) on STEP (ISO 10303), QIF, and MTConnect conformance testing — the design-to-manufacturing
  data-thread standards CAM emits into.
- **NIST — Smart Manufacturing Operations Planning and Control program** —
  https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/smart-manufacturing-operations-planning-and-control-program —
  NIST program documenting STEP AP242, QIF, and MTConnect milestones; the standards-context backbone for
  feature-based CAD->CAM->CNC interoperability.
- **MTConnect Institute (homepage)** — https://www.mtconnect.org/ — The open ANSI/MTC1.4 standard for
  machine-tool data streaming (no proprietary format); the live source for the machine-data vocabulary a
  CAM/shop-floor feedback loop consumes (GitHub at github.com/mtconnect).
- **Internet Archive — Texts collection** — https://archive.org/details/texts — Free / borrowable /
  public-domain digital library; the place to find out-of-copyright machining and manufacturing
  engineering texts and historical machinist references.

## Standards & authoritative bodies

Standards landing pages relevant to a safety-critical CAM/toolpath pipeline (machine safety, dimensioning,
and the G-code interface CAM posts into). Landing pages are listed because they stay current as the body
revises the standard.

- **OSHA — 29 CFR 1910.212 (General requirements for all machines)** —
  https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.212 — Live US federal machine-guarding
  standard (point of operation, rotating parts, flying chips) — the safety floor any CAM/shop pipeline
  must respect; mills and saws are named.
- **OSHA — Machine Guarding (safety-topic landing)** — https://www.osha.gov/machine-guarding — Live OSHA
  topic hub linking standards, hazard recognition, and solutions for moving-machine-part hazards relevant
  to CNC shops.
- **ASME — Codes & Standards (landing)** — https://www.asme.org/codes-standards — The authoritative entry
  point to ASME standards, including the Y14.5 GD&T / Y14.36 surface-texture family that a finishing/
  profiling toolpath exists to hold (navigate via "Find Codes & Standards").
- **LinuxCNC — G-code reference documentation** — https://linuxcnc.org/docs/html/gcode/g-code.html —
  Free/open (GPL), actively-maintained (last updated 2025-12-15) reference for the G/M-code,
  work-offset, and canned-cycle semantics a CAM post-processor emits — an open, vendor-neutral spec for
  the controller interface CAM targets.

## Maintenance

This atlas is a **link directory**, so its primary failure mode is **link rot** — URLs go 404/403, sites
restructure, courses move term-slugs (several MIT OCW term-specific course slugs already 404'd in this
pass and were deliberately dropped in favor of the stable OCW + NPTEL homepages). **Freshness mechanism:**
re-verify every URL here on a periodic cadence (recommend quarterly, or before any galaxy "keep-learning"
sweep that relies on it) by re-running a live WebFetch against each link; drop any that no longer resolve
or that drift off-topic, and add newly-discovered living sources via the same verify-before-list rule. A
link is only allowed in this atlas after a fetch confirms it resolves to the described content (R12) —
never restore a dropped link without re-verifying it. Owner: kilo (cam galaxy). When re-verifying, prefer
homepages/catalog pages over deep article URLs — they rot far less and stay current at the source.
