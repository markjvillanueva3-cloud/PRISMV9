# Business Open-Source Atlas

A curated, **verified** directory of the best FREE + LEGAL **living** resources for the **business** galaxy — manufacturing business, accounting, ERP, and operations management. The point of this atlas is a *non-stagnant keep-learning curriculum*: it points at continuously-updated homepages, full course series, textbook catalogs, gov data portals, lecture channels, and standards-body landing pages — sources that stay current on their own, so the galaxy never freezes on a one-time snapshot.

## Free college courses

Full course / lecture-series homepages — complete syllabi, lecture notes, assignments, and exams, free under Creative Commons.

- **MIT OpenCourseWare — 15.760A Operations Management (Sloan)** — https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-760a-operations-management-spring-2002/ — the canonical operations-management topic spine (inventory, process flow, queueing, process capability, Toyota Production System, constraint management, supply-chain design); use as the coverage checklist for what a "world-leader" business/ops engine must eventually cover.
- **MIT OpenCourseWare — 15.066J System Optimization and Analysis for Manufacturing (Sloan / MechE)** — https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-066j-system-optimization-and-analysis-for-manufacturing-summer-2003/ — the optimization + simulation methodology layer beneath capacity planning and scheduling: linear/integer/non-linear programming, sensitivity analysis (which constraint binds), Monte-Carlo and discrete-event simulation for manufacturing decision support.

## Free textbooks & references

Open-license textbook catalogs and public-domain text homes — continuously republished, free to read and download.

- **OpenStax — Principles of Financial Accounting (Rice University, CC BY-NC-SA)** — https://openstax.org/books/principles-financial-accounting/pages/3-1-describe-principles-assumptions-and-concepts-of-accounting-and-their-relationship-to-financial-statements — free, peer-reviewed financial-accounting textbook covering GAAP recognition principles, the accounting equation, and double-entry bookkeeping — the accounting contract a job-costing/quote engine sits on.
- **LibreTexts — Business / Accounting bookshelf (DoE Open Textbook Pilot)** — https://biz.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Accounting — a live open-education library aggregating multiple free financial + managerial accounting texts (OpenStax, Lumen, Arnold & Kyle), continuously maintained — the broadest single home for free accounting curriculum.
- **Project Gutenberg — subject / bookshelf browsing** — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/subject/487 — public-domain text catalog; the home of foundational management/economics primary texts (e.g. Taylor's *Principles of Scientific Management*, the 1911 root of process standardization) — browse by subject for the historical doctrine layer.

## Archives & open data / gov reports

Government statistics portals and open archives — the continuously-refreshed "data reports" layer for benchmarking labor cost, manufacturing output, and economic context.

- **U.S. Census Bureau — Annual Survey of Manufactures / Annual Integrated Economic Survey (AIES)** — https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/asm.html — official manufacturing establishment statistics (employment, value of shipments, value added) refreshed on benchmark cycles; the authoritative free source for industry-level manufacturing economics.
- **Data.gov — U.S. government open-data portal** — https://www.data.gov/ — 360k+ free datasets across economics, manufacturing, and labor; the GSA-managed catalog for pulling fresh public datasets into a business/ERP analytics surface.
- **NIST — Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP)** — https://www.nist.gov/mep — the canonical free U.S. institutional resource for small/medium manufacturers: operational-improvement consulting, value-stream mapping, and the impact reports to cite when justifying a capex (e.g. elevating a constraint per TOC).
- **NIST — Baldrige Performance Excellence Program** — https://www.nist.gov/baldrige — the public-private organizational-performance framework with free sector-specific Excellence Frameworks (Manufacturing, Small Business) and self-assessment tools — the gov-backed structure for a whole-shop performance-improvement program.
- **Internet Archive — Texts** — https://archive.org/details/texts — a live free + borrowable digital library of books and documents; the fallback archive for out-of-print management/manufacturing references not in OpenStax or Gutenberg.

## Standards & authoritative bodies

Authoritative landing pages relevant to manufacturing business operations, HR, and safety programs.

- **OSHA — Safety and Health Programs (Recommended Practices)** — https://www.osha.gov/safety-management — the U.S. Dept. of Labor's seven-element safety-program framework (management leadership, worker participation, hazard ID/assessment, hazard prevention/control, education/training, program evaluation, communication) — the canonical free + authoritative structure for the EHS dimension of any scheduling/overtime/process-change decision. (NIST Baldrige, listed under Archives & open data, doubles as the authoritative performance-excellence framework body.)
