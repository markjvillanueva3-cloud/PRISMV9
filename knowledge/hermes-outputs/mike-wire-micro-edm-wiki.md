# Micro EDM (MIKE)

**Galaxy:** MIKE (Wire)
**Status:** Core Strategy - Master Level

## Description
Micro EDM for very small features, high-aspect-ratio holes, and precision components.

## Key Considerations
- Electrode materials (tungsten, copper-tungsten)
- Very small spark gaps
- High-precision electrode manufacturing
- Process stability at micro scales

## PRISM Implementation
- EDMProgramAssemblerEngine supports micro EDM parameters
- Integration with high-precision CAD and electrode design

## JM Die Notes
- Micro EDM is used for high-precision components and small features
- Rule: Electrode manufacturing and alignment are critical at micro scales
