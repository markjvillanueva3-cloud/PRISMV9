# Fixture and Setup Best Practices (FOXTROT)

**Galaxy:** FOXTROT (Mill)
**Status:** Core Strategy - Master Level

## Description
Proper fixturing and setup are critical for accuracy, rigidity, and repeatability in milling operations.

## Key Considerations
- Workpiece support and clamping to minimize deflection
- Fixture design for accessibility and rigidity
- Datum selection and repeatability
- Thermal stability and coolant management
- Setup time vs. accuracy trade-offs

## PRISM Implementation
- Fixture recommendations integrated into Milling Wizard
- DFM feedback on fixture implications

## JM Die Notes
- Many quality issues trace back to poor fixturing rather than cutting parameters
- Rule: Design fixtures with the same rigor as the part itself
