## Assessment
This topic requires maximum variability. Nose radius effects change significantly with brand, geometry, and material.

## Overview
Nose radius affects chip thickness, surface finish, and force distribution. Different brands use different standard radii.

## Common Nose Radii by Brand & Geometry

| Brand       | Common Radii (mm)          | Typical Use                          | Effect on Chip Thickness |
|-------------|----------------------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| Sandvik     | 0.2, 0.4, 0.8, 1.2, 1.6    | General turning & milling            | Standard reference       |
| Kennametal  | 0.2, 0.4, 0.8, 1.2         | High feed and general                | Slightly more aggressive |
| OSG         | 0.2, 0.4, 0.8, 1.0         | End mills and drills                 | Conservative on finish   |
| Mitsubishi  | 0.2, 0.4, 0.8, 1.2, 1.6    | Broad range                          | Very close to Sandvik    |

## Brand-Specific Recommendations

- **Sandvik**: Prefers larger radii (0.8–1.6) on CoroTurn for stability.
- **OSG**: Often uses smaller radii (0.2–0.4) on end mills for better finish.
- **Tungaloy / Seco**: More aggressive with high-feed geometries (larger effective radius).
