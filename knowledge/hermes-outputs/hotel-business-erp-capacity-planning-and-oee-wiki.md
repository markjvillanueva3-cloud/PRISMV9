# Capacity Planning and OEE (HOTEL)

**Galaxy:** HOTEL (Business ERP)
**Status:** Core Capability - Master Level

## Description
Capacity planning and Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) tracking to optimize shop floor utilization and quoting accuracy.

## Key Metrics
- OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality
- Theoretical vs. actual capacity
- Bottleneck identification
- Long-term capacity forecasting

## PRISM Implementation
- Capacity planning engine integrated with business and quoting systems
- Real-time OEE data from machine monitoring

## JM Die Notes
- Accurate capacity data is essential for realistic lead times and quoting
- Rule: Never quote lead times based on theoretical capacity without OEE adjustment
