# Quote-to-Ship 21-Stage Pipeline (CHARLIE)

**Galaxy:** CHARLIE (Quoting)
**Status:** Core Capability - Master Level

## Description
The 21-stage Quote-to-Ship pipeline that takes a customer inquiry through quoting, order processing, scheduling, manufacturing, quality, and shipping.

## Key Stages
- Inquiry receipt and DFM review
- Cost modeling and pricing
- Quote generation and approval
- Order entry and confirmation
- Capacity planning and scheduling
- Material procurement
- Manufacturing execution
- Quality control and inspection
- Shipping and invoicing

## PRISM Implementation
- QuoteToShipOrchestratorEngine manages the full pipeline
- Integration with business engines, ERP, and shop floor systems

## JM Die Notes
- The 21-stage pipeline is the backbone of the quoting and delivery process
- Rule: Every stage must have clear ownership, data requirements, and handoff criteria
