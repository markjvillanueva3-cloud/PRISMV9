## Key Variability Factors

- **Substrate**: Micro-grain carbide usually gives 10–20% longer life than standard carbide.
- **Edge Prep**: Honed edges improve life on cast iron and hardened steel; sharp edges better on aluminum.
- **Coolant**: Through-tool coolant can increase tool life 15–40% on P and M materials.
- **Coating Thickness**: Thicker coatings (8–12 µm) improve wear resistance but can reduce toughness.
