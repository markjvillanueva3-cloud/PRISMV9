### Ethical Governance Principles
The ethical governance principles are derived from NIST AI RMF 1.0 and ISO/IEC 42001:2023. The key controls include:
- **Transparency:** Ensure that AI systems are understandable to all stakeholders [NIST AI RMF GOVERN].
- **Accountability:** Establish clear roles and responsibilities for the development, deployment, and maintenance of AI systems [ISO/IEC 42001 Clause 7].

### Regulatory Impact Models
The `RegulatoryImpactAssessorEngine` will model regulatory changes by:
- **Identifying Affected Controls:** Cross-reference regulation changes with existing NIST controls.
- **Flagging Customers/Parts:** Identify customers and parts that are affected by the regulation change, particularly those relevant to ITAR and EAR compliance.
- **Gap Actions:** Suggest actions required to close gaps introduced by regulatory changes.
- **Cost Exposure:** Estimate cost exposure associated with non-compliance.

### Compliance Models
The compliance models include:
- **NISTAIRMFComplianceEngine:** Implements NIST AI RMF 1.0 controls [mcp-server/src/engines/NISTAIRMFComplianceEngine.ts].
- **ITARComplianceTaggerEngine:** Handles ITAR, EAR, CMMC v2, and DFARS compliance [mcp-server/src/engines/ITARComplianceTaggerEngine.ts].
