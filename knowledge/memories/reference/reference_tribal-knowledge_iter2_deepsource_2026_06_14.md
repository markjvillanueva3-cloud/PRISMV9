## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Organizational Knowledge Conversion, Epistemic Game Theory, and Codification Strategies in Software Engineering Knowledge Management**

A world-leading expert in tribal-knowledge mastery next internalizes the formal mechanisms that convert tacit engineering insight into persistent organizational software assets. This layer draws from knowledge management theory applied to large-scale software organizations, epistemic game-theoretic models of knowledge sharing, and codified standards for knowledge asset management.

Key next-tier competencies include:
- Nonaka’s SECI model extensions into software engineering contexts, particularly how “ba” enables knowledge spiral acceleration in distributed engineering teams (Nonaka & Konno, 1998).
- Epistemic game theory applied to knowledge contribution dilemmas in open-source and internal platforms, modeling engineer contribution as a Bayesian game with incomplete information about peer expertise (D’Aspremont et al., 2007; von Hippel & von Krogh, 2003).
- IEEE and ISO standards governing knowledge management systems design, specifically the requirements for knowledge lifecycle processes in systems and software engineering.
- Measurement frameworks for knowledge asset valuation and codification efficiency drawn from software engineering economics and information systems literature.
- Advanced pedagogical frameworks from MIT’s organizational learning courses that treat tribal knowledge as a dynamic capability in agile and DevOps environments.

This layer shifts from descriptive tacit/explicit theory to prescriptive, measurable, and game-theoretic governance of engineering tribal knowledge at enterprise scale.
