**Next Layer: Advanced Thermo-Mechanical Coupling, Stochastic Stability, and Closed-Loop In-Process Metrology for Safety-Critical Turning**

World-leading expertise advances into stochastic thermo-mechanical delay systems with state-dependent time delays under combined regenerative and frictional process damping, including uncertainty propagation in stability boundaries. This layer covers:

- Chebyshev spectral continuous-time approximation and Lyapunov-based stability analysis for nonlinear time-periodic DDEs with thermal softening and state-dependent cutting force coefficients (Insperger & Stépán frameworks extended).
- Coupled thermomechanical finite-element models that integrate transient heat partition, tool-workpiece thermal contact resistance, and plastic deformation-induced heating to predict chatter onset under varying engagement (Munoa-type models with full thermo-viscoplastic material behavior).
- Probabilistic stability prediction using Gaussian process regression on noisy in-process metrology (force, vibration, acoustic emission, and laser tool-tip displacement) to generate stability lobe confidence intervals under parametric uncertainty (tool wear, temperature-dependent properties, material inhomogeneity).
- Closed-loop adaptive control using real-time laser metrology and piezoelectric actuators for in-process stability margin regulation, including robust MIMO controllers accounting for sensor-actuator latency and thermal drift (NIST closed-loop metrology protocols).
- High-order semi-discretization and cluster treatment of characteristic roots (CTCR) methods for multi-delay systems with thermo-mechanical coupling and process damping identified via inverse stability solutions from operational modal analysis.

These topics represent the current frontier beyond the listed MIT OCW 2.096/2.097/2.094, NIST TN 2256/2300/IR 8482, and cited CIRP/ASME papers.
