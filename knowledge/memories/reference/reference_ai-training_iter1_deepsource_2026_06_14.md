**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Machine Learning Systems & Training Infrastructure**

A world-leading expert in ai-training next masters the engineering of large-scale, reliable, and observable training systems beyond core algorithms. This includes distributed training architectures, optimization at scale, reproducibility, efficient memory management, and production-grade ML infrastructure.

**Key uncovered topics:**

- Model parallelism, pipeline parallelism, and 3D parallelism strategies (Megatron-LM, DeepSpeed)
- Mixed-precision training mechanics, loss scaling, and FP8/FP16 convergence theory
- Advanced optimizers (Lion, Sophia, Adafactor) and their theoretical convergence properties
- Deterministic training, experiment reproducibility, and versioning of data/model/code (MLflow, DVC)
- Training observability, telemetry, and performance debugging at cluster scale
- Gradient compression, communication-efficient SGD, and decentralized training
- Hardware-software co-design for training (CUDA Graphs, FlashAttention-2, custom kernels)
