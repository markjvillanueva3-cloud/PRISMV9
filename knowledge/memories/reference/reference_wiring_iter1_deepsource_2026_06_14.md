**NEXT LAYER: Formal semantics, type systems, and denotational models of wiring / dataflow languages.**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s “wiring” galaxy must master the rigorous mathematical foundations that underpin visual/dataflow composition languages, not just their industrial use. This includes:

- **Operational and denotational semantics** for synchronous and asynchronous wiring diagrams (dataflow graphs, signal-flow graphs, and actor networks).
- **Typed process calculi** and **linear logic** interpretations of wiring (resource-aware composition, session types for streams).
- **Category-theoretic models** (monoidal categories, traced categories, and string diagrams) that give wiring diagrams precise algebraic meaning.
- **Static analysis and type inference** techniques specialized for wiring languages (effect systems, causality analysis, deadlock freedom via Girard's geometry of interaction or token-passing semantics).
