A world-leading expert in pdf-corpus-mill next masters the rigorous construction of high-fidelity, structure-preserving corpora at billion-token scale, emphasizing layout-aware tokenization, logical reading-order enforcement, artifact-free PDF-to-text normalization, and statistical validation of corpus quality for domain-specific LLM pretraining.

Key uncovered competencies include:

- **Layout-aware PDF parsing pipelines** that combine PDF 2.0 structure tree traversal (Tagged PDF / Marked Content) with geometric post-correction using learned reading-order models, ensuring semantic fidelity beyond simple raster or stream extraction (ISO 32000-2:2020 and extensions).
- **Corpus-level quality metrics** grounded in NIST-style evaluation frameworks: measuring token reconstruction accuracy, layout fidelity (reading order error rate), entity boundary preservation, and cross-document structural consistency.
- **Scalable deduplication and sanitization** at corpus scale using MinHash/LSH combined with PDF-specific content hashing that respects logical structure rather than raw bytes (avoiding pitfalls highlighted in modern extraction literature).
- **Multimodal corpus construction theory**: aligning PDF text, vector graphics, bitmap regions, and semantic tags into unified sequence formats suitable for Document Transformer or DiT-style pretraining, including handling of mathematical expressions, tables, and complex layouts.
- **Standards-driven validation**: systematic use of PDF/UA-2 and PDF/VRA conformance testing to guarantee reliable reuse, plus automated structure-tree validation tools.

These build directly on prior document-layout and extraction work but focus on repeatable, auditable, production-grade corpus manufacturing pipelines used in frontier LLM research.
