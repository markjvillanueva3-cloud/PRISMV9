## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer masters sociomaterial enactments of knowing-in-practice and narrative methodologies for tracing how tribal knowledge is performed, stabilized, or eroded in digitized organizations. This extends prior boundary-object and infrastructure scholarship by treating knowledge as recurrent sociomaterial performances (not static repositories) and deploys shadowing, videorecording, and narrative analysis to surface ephemeral tribal interactions beyond ISO 30401 codification or Wenger communities.

Core elements: Orlikowski’s practice lens on knowing as enacted capability; Lave/Wenger situated learning trajectories; Orr’s machine-talk ethnographies revealing undocumented diagnostic storytelling; and Nicolini’s interventionist practice-theory methods for making invisible tribal regimes visible without reducing them to explicit KM artifacts.

These provide theory depth (sociomateriality), methodology (multi-site ethnography of practices), and institutional insight (how digital tools reconfigure knowledge persistence).
