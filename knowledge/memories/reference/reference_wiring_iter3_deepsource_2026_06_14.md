**Next Layer: Resource-Aware Synchronous Dataflow, Linear Type Systems for Wiring, and Certified Hardware Synthesis Pipelines**

A world-leading expert next masters the integration of **linear and bunched type theories** with **synchronous dataflow languages** to enforce resource bounds, causality, and reversibility at the wiring scale, together with mechanized verification pipelines that connect high-level synchronous models to provably correct RTL and netlist realizations.

Core topics include:
- **Categorical semantics of synchronous wiring** using string diagrams and profunctors to model clocked resource flow (Pavlovic, 2012; Ghica & Jung, 2017).
- **Quantitative type theory and graded modal types** for tracking wiring latency, power, and area as semiring-graded resources (Atkey, 2018; Orchard et al., 2019).
- **Dependent temporal types and clock calculus extensions** that eliminate causality loops while preserving static schedulability (Baudart et al., 2019; Guatto, 2018).
- **Mechanized verified compilation stacks** for Lustre-like languages down to VHDL/Verilog, building on CompCert and CakeML techniques but targeting dataflow hardware (Bourke et al., 2017; Rieu et al., 2022).
- **Reversible circuit synthesis and linear logic embeddings** that guarantee no information loss in wiring networks (James & Sabry, 2014; Di Meglio et al., 2023).

These layers provide the formal infrastructure for PRISM’s wiring galaxy to statically certify resource composition, causality, and correctness of generated hardware descriptions at scale.
