Key topics include:
- **PAC verification of hyperproperties** under distribution shift for learning-augmented autoscalers.
- **Relational refinement types** for stochastic contracts.
- **Compositional PAC bounds** via approximate probabilistic bisimulation metrics on hyper-distributions.
- **Hypercontract synthesis** via counterexample-guided inductive synthesis (CEGIS) lifted to quantitative settings.

This layer sits beyond standard PAC contract satisfaction and single-property stochastic model checking, focusing on **relational decomposition** and **hyperproperty contract algebra** tailored to database elasticity (workload-adaptive scaling policies that must satisfy quantitative non-interference-style guarantees between observed load and internal resource states).
