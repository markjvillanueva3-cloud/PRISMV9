## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Institutional Theory of Infrastructure + Sociotechnical Memory Systems**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s tribal-knowledge galaxy must next master the **institutional mechanisms** that stabilize (and destabilize) knowledge infrastructures over decades, moving beyond boundary objects and STS ethnographies into explicit theories of **institutional work**, **infrastructural inversion**, and **organizational forgetting**.

Core next-layer topics include:
- Institutional entrepreneurship and maintenance in cyberinfrastructure (how actors create, maintain, and disrupt rules, norms, and resource distributions that sustain knowledge infrastructures).
- The tension between **infrastructural invisibility** and required **infrastructural inversion** (the deliberate foregrounding of normally invisible work).
- Theories of **organizational memory** and **forgetting** as active infrastructural processes, not mere side-effects.
- The role of **classification infrastructures** and **standards** as carriers of institutional logic.

**Concrete next sources:**

- Lawrence, T.B., Suddaby, R. & Leca, B. (2011) Institutional Work: Refocusing Institutional Studies of Organization. Journal of Management Inquiry.
- Scott, W.R. (2014) Institutions and Organizations: Ideas, Interests, and Identities (4th ed.). Sage.
- Bowker, G.C. (2000) Biodiversity Datadiversity. Social Studies of Science 30(5).
