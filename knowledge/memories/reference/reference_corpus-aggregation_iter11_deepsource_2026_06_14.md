**Next Layer: Tractable Aggregation Circuits, Semiring Factorization, and Certified Compilation for Aggregate Queries**

A world-leading expert in corpus-aggregation next masters the theory and algorithmic foundations of **tractable circuits for aggregation under semirings**, including d-DNNF, OBDD, and structured decomposable negation normal form (d-SDNNF) representations that support linear-time aggregation. This layer focuses on **knowledge compilation lower bounds**, **semiring homomorphism properties for factorized aggregates**, and **certified incremental view maintenance** using provenance circuits with formal correctness guarantees.

Key uncovered topics include:
- Algebraic factorization of aggregate queries via semiring-valued provenance and its connection to hierarchical and #-hierarchical queries (beyond basic factorized databases).
- Compilation of aggregate queries into circuits with **constant-delay enumeration** and **random-access** capabilities.
- Dichotomy results for aggregate query tractability under different semirings (tropical, boolean, probability, etc.).
- Use of **functional aggregate queries (FAQ)** framework and its generalization to FAQ-SS for subsumption-free aggregation.
- Certified compilation pipelines that produce **verifiable circuits** with formal proofs of equivalence (interactive or zero-knowledge style).
