**Next Layer: Advanced Orchestration Synthesis, Role-Dynamic Session Types, and Verified Timed Multi-Agent Coordination**

A world-leading expert in agent-orchestration next masters the rigorous synthesis of reactive orchestrators from temporal specifications, dynamic role inference in multiparty session systems, and model-checking toolchains for timed and probabilistic agent ensembles.

Key areas include:

- **Correct-by-construction synthesis** of distributed orchestrators from ATL and LTL specifications, extending Pnueli & Rosner’s reactive-module synthesis to multi-agent settings with explicit role mobility.
- **Dynamic multirole session types** (Deniélou & Yoshida 2013) and their integration with dependent and graded type theories for runtime role adaptation in asynchronous orchestrations.
- **Symbolic model checking** of timed and hybrid systems using UPPAAL’s zone-based reachability algorithms, combined with probabilistic extensions for quantitative verification of orchestrator policies.
- **Program-synthesis techniques** for reactive and distributed controllers, including counterexample-guided inductive synthesis (CEGIS) and syntax-guided synthesis (SyGuS) tailored to agent coordination protocols.
- **Ethical-by-design governance** of autonomous orchestrators via IEEE 7000-2021 processes integrated with formal verification workflows.

These build directly on already-covered session types, ATL, UPPAAL, and synthesis foundations, moving into algorithmic synthesis, role polymorphism, and standards-driven verifiable autonomy.
