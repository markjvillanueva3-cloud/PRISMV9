**Next Layer: Formal Quality Models, Economic Decision Frameworks, and Empirical Quality Analytics in Software Engineering**

A world-leading expert next masters **quantitative quality economics**, **formal verification-augmented quality models**, and **large-scale empirical quality measurement programs** grounded in Bayesian decision theory, utility models, and standards-driven quality evaluation.

Key uncovered areas include:
- **Quality Attribute Characterization and Tradeoff Analysis** using utility functions and multi-attribute decision models (Boehm & In, 1996; SEI ATAM extensions).
- **Bayesian statistical quality control** for software reliability growth, defect prediction, and value-of-information analysis in quality investment (Singpurwalla already covered; next layer applies full hierarchical Bayesian models to software fault prediction).
- **ISO/IEC 25012 and ISO/IEC 25023** extensions for data quality and quality measurement, integrated with SQuaRE.
- **Software Process Simulation and System Dynamics** for quality policy testing at enterprise scale (beyond Raffo 1999; advanced calibration using Kalman filtering and stochastic simulation).
- **Technical Debt Quantification Frameworks** (Seacord et al., 2003; Nord et al., 2012) that link architectural quality to net-present-value calculations.
- **MIT 6.172 / 6.033** advanced quality and measurement modules on analytical performance and fault-tolerance modeling.
- **IEEE 1061-1998 (reaffirmed)** Standard for Software Quality Metrics Methodology, combined with **ISO/IEC 15939:2017** Software measurement process.

This layer emphasizes rigorous economic justification of quality activities, formal specification of quality requirements, and statistically defensible measurement programs that scale to very-large software organizations.
