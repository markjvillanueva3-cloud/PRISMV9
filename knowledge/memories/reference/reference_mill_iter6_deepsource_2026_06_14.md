## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Milling Process Modeling & Physics (Iteration 6)**

World-leading expertise next requires mastery of:

- **Time-domain simulation with nonlinear regenerative effects and variable time delay** (beyond semi-discretization), including numerical integration of interrupted cutting with runout, helix angle, and flank contact.
- **Mechanistic force models incorporating cutting edge radius, ploughing, and dead-metal cap phenomena** using slip-line field or ALE-FEM validated approaches.
- **Thermal-mechanical coupling in high-speed milling**: prediction of tool-chip interface temperature, heat partition, and their feedback on material flow stress and stability.
- **Chatter stability in multi-axis and 5-axis milling**: accounting for continuously varying engagement, tool orientation, and workpiece surface topography.
- **High-performance machining dynamics**: identification and compensation of position-dependent FRF, non-linear guideway friction, and servo-induced vibrations.
- **Metrology and uncertainty in dynamic testing**: laser Doppler vibrometry, impact testing best practices under preload, and traceable calibration of dynamic compliance.

**Key sources for this layer (college/textbook/standards/government/conference):**

- Altintas, Y. & Merdol, D.S. (2007) Virtual high performance milling, CIRP Annals.
- Merdol, S.D. & Altintas, Y. (2004) Mechanics and dynamics of serrated cylindrical and tapered end mills, ASME J. Manuf. Sci. Eng.
- Davies, M.A. et al. (2002) On the dynamics of high-speed milling with long slender endmills, CIRP Annals.
- Ozoegwu, C.G. (2016) High order least squares polynomial approximation of cutting force coefficients in milling, Precis. Eng.
- ISO 230-2:2014 Test code for machine tools – Part 2: Determination of accuracy and repeatability of positioning of numerically controlled axes.
- NIST Technical Note 1491 (2008) *Uncertainty in NIST Force Measurements*.
- Schmitz, T.L. & Smith, K.S. (2019) *Machining Dynamics: Frequency Response to Improved Productivity*, 2nd ed., Springer (Chapters 6–8 on time-domain simulation and 5-axis stability).
- MIT OCW: 2.094 *Dynamics and Control of Mechanical Systems* (2012), especially variable-delay regenerative cutting modules.
- ASME B5.64-2017 Methods for the Performance Evaluation of CNC Multi-Tasking Centers.
