**NEXT LAYER: Formal Methods for Live CAD Data Fusion in Collaborative Engineering Systems**

A world-leading expert in cad-fusion-live must master the rigorous application of **incremental constraint solving**, **persistent data structures for geometric streams**, and **formal semantic interoperability** between heterogeneous CAD kernels under live update conditions. This layer focuses on typed functional-reactive models, bidirectional transformation theory, and verified geometric constraint satisfaction that scale to real-time multi-user CAD sessions.

Key uncovered topics include:
- **Constraint propagation networks** using incremental SAT/SMT solvers for live parametric models (e.g., incremental versions of the DeltaBlue and Cassowary algorithms adapted to 3D B-rep).
- **Categorical approaches** to CAD data fusion via lenses, open games, and profunctors for bidirectional synchronization between feature trees and boundary representations.
- **Temporal logic specifications** (LTL/CTL*) and model checking for correctness of live fusion protocols under concurrent engineering workflows.
- **Persistent geometric data structures** (persistent octrees, kinetic data structures, and versioned B-rep graphs) that maintain topological invariants during streaming updates.
- **Semantic web and ontology-based** fusion layers (OWL-DL + geometric ontologies) for unambiguous interchange between dissimilar CAD systems in real time.

**Institutional depth** draws from MIT’s graduate PL and geometric computing curricula, IEEE standards on digital product representation, and formal methods literature applied to engineering design systems.
