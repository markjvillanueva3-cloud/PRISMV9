**NEXT LAYER: Mechanism Design, Information Design, and Dynamic Contracting in Multi-Sided Platform Ecosystems (Advanced Graduate Level)**

A world-leading expert in the business of software platforms next masters the integration of **dynamic mechanism design with information design** under endogenous participation and forward-looking agents on both sides of the platform. This layer extends beyond static two-sided pricing and basic Myersonian auctions into Bayesian persuasion, dynamic revelation mechanisms with interdependent values, and optimal information structures that shape platform participants’ beliefs over time while preserving incentive compatibility and individual rationality across heterogeneous sides.

Core uncovered competencies include:
- DesigningMenus of dynamic contracts that solve the **dynamic incentive compatibility (DIC)** and **dynamic individual rationality (DIR)** constraints when platform users learn about match quality or network effects over multiple periods.
- Applying **Bayesian persuasion** frameworks to platform information architecture—deciding what performance, recommendation, or quality signals to reveal to which user cohorts to maximize platform surplus while preventing adverse selection spirals.
- Modeling **intertemporal price discrimination** and **sequential screening** in the presence of forward-looking buyers and sellers who strategically time participation based on expected future information and prices.
- Using **information design** to optimally coarsen or refine signals in matching platforms to balance thickness, congestion, and strategic misrepresentation.
