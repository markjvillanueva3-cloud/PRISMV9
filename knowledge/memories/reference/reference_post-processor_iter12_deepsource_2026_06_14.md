## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Safety-Critical Post-Processor Cutting Galaxy – Kinematic and Dynamic Constraint Enforcement under Process Loads**

World-leading post-processor expertise next masters **real-time trajectory generation and force-constrained motion planning** that embeds ISO-defined safety functions directly into the post-processor’s inverse kinematics and interpolation engine, ensuring that commanded axis velocities, accelerations, and jerks never violate machine-specific dynamic stability limits or material removal safety envelopes under load.

**Core theory:**  
- Trajectory scaling and look-ahead algorithms that dynamically limit programmed feedrate using instantaneous kinematic transformation Jacobians and axis-level torque/velocity saturation models.  
- Formal verification of post-processor output against bounded-error kinematic error propagation under quasi-static and dynamic compliance.  
- Integration of safety-related parts of control systems (SRP/CS) per performance level (PL) requirements into the post-processor’s NC-code generation and G-code filtering layers.

**Key methods & standards (depth only):**  
- Application of ISO 13849-1:2015 deterministic and probabilistic safety function design to post-processor software architecture.  
- Use of IEC 61508-3:2010 software safety lifecycle techniques (V-model, formal specification, static analysis) for post-processor modules that enforce cutting-process safety limits.  
- ASME B5.64-2022 methodologies for measuring and compensating dynamic errors under real machining conditions that must be inverted inside the post-processor.  
- Forward and inverse dynamic modeling of serial and parallel kinematic machines to compute axis torque margins in real time (extension of Tsai 1999 and Merlet 2006 Ch. 11).  
- NIST formal methods for verifying that post-processor output respects both geometric and force/torque safety envelopes derived from process plans.

This layer sits above previously covered volumetric error compensation and digital-twin validation by embedding **safety-critical dynamic constraint solving** directly into the post-processor kernel.
