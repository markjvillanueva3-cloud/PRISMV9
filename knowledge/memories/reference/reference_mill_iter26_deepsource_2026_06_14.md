## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
Receptance coupling substructure analysis (RCSA) synthesizes measured spindle-holder FRFs with analytical Timoshenko beam tool models, incorporating translational/rotational interface compliance for rapid tool-tip dynamics without repeated experimental impact testing per tool. Semi-discretization of time-periodic DDEs with multiple regenerative delays enables stability lobe construction for variable-pitch or variable-helix cutters. Volumetric error mapping and kinematic calibration of five-axis centers via laser tracker trajectories, integrated with servo gain tuning for contouring accuracy. In-process modal parameter extraction via operational modal analysis during cutting, feeding real-time stability observers.
