## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer centers on formalizing neural tangent kernel (NTK) parametrizations and mean-field limits for infinite-width training dynamics, using verified convergence proofs in dependent type theory together with Wasserstein gradient flows and empirical process theory. This supplies rigorous, mechanized guarantees for stable generalization and scaling in overparameterized stochastic optimizers, extending RG-based analyses to kernel regimes while preserving PAC-style bounds under non-convex landscapes. Institutional depth draws from CMU probabilistic modeling sequences, MIT statistical learning curricula emphasizing Rademacher complexity, and textbook treatments of information-theoretic generalization.
