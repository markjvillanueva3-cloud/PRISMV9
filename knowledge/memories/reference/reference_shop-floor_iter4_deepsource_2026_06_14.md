## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Formal Verification, Compositional Scheduling, and Predictable Middleware for Distributed Shop-Floor Cyber-Physical Systems**

A world-leading expert next masters **compositional real-time scheduling theory for multiprocessor and distributed systems**, **contract-based design and assume-guarantee reasoning**, **timed automata-based model checking at scale**, and **certifiable middleware architectures** that integrate safety, security, and timing predictability.

Key uncovered areas include:

- **Compositional Scheduling Frameworks**: Using interface-based design and resource models (periodic, sporadic, server-based) to enable hierarchical scheduling and independent verification of subsystems (Lipari & Bini, 2009; Shin & Lee, 2003).
- **Timed Automata & Symbolic Model Checking**: Beyond basic UPPAAL usage — mastering difference-bound matrices (DBM), zone abstraction refinement, and scalability techniques for networks of timed automata (Bengtsson & Yi, 2004; Behrmann et al., 2006).
- **Contract-Based Design for CPS**: Assume-guarantee contracts, semantic foundations using modal interfaces, and integration with AADL for co-verification of timing, safety, and security properties (Benveniste et al., 2018; Sangiovanni-Vincentelli et al., 2012).
- **Real-Time Java & RTSJ Extensions**: Memory-area models, asynchronous event handling, and Ravenscar-Java profile for high-integrity systems (Wellings & Burns, 2006; Dibble, 2008).
- **Predictable Communication & Middleware**: Data Distribution Service (DDS) QoS policy composition for end-to-end latency bounds, and Real-Time CORBA portable priorities mapping (Object Management Group, 2015 extensions; Schmidt & Kuhns, 2000).

These build directly on already-covered standards (IEC 61499, TSN, DDS, AADL, RTSJ) by providing the formal methods and compositional theory required for verifiable, certifiable shop-floor distributed intelligence.
