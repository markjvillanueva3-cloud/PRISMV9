## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer in dormant-data expertise focuses on searchable symmetric encryption (SSE) and structured encryption enabling queries over long-dormant archives without bulk retrieval, attribute-based encryption (ABE) and functional encryption for policy-controlled access across decades of dormancy, and fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) permitting computation directly on encrypted inactive data. Theory depth includes adaptive security definitions, leakage-abuse tradeoffs, and simulation-based proofs reducible to standard assumptions (DDH, LWE). Methodology emphasizes machine-checked implementations via F*, Coq, or TLA+ for retention-policy automata, plus crypto-agility frameworks that schedule algorithm migration without decrypting massive archives. Institutional coverage draws from IEEE standards for at-rest protection, NIST storage-encryption guidelines, and graduate curricula stressing protocol composition for cold-storage adversaries.

This layer extends covered POR/PDP/regenerating codes by adding selective disclosure and verifiable computation primitives while preserving OAIS-style provenance across key rotations.
