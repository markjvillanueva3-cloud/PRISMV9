**Next Layer: Blueprint-Vision Knowledge Mastery**

A world-leading expert in blueprint-vision next masters **constraint-based declarative modeling**, **incremental graph rewriting for model synchronization**, **stochastic image grammars with attribute propagation**, and **rigidity-theoretic diagnosis of under/over-constrained engineering drawings** at the intersection of algebraic graph transformation, geometric constraint solving, and perceptual parsing.

Key uncovered depth includes:

- **Constraint query languages** extended to spatial databases with algebraic varieties (Kanellakis et al. already covered; next is Grumbach et al.’s spatial constraint databases using semi-algebraic sets).
- **Categorical foundations** of model synchronization via triple graph grammars (TGGs) for incremental blueprint-to-model consistency.
- **Probabilistic attributed graph grammars** that combine stochastic context-free grammars with geometric constraint satisfaction for parsing ambiguous engineering drawings.
- **Algebraic diagnosis** of geometric constraint graphs using matroid theory and combinatorial rigidity (beyond Sitharam 2021’s basic chapters: Laman’s theorem generalizations and Henneberg constructions in 3D).
- **Declarative geometric constraint solvers** using symbolic algebraic rewriting and Gröbner bases for under-constrained sketch recognition.
- **Formal semantics** of STEP schema mappings to constraint databases via institution theory (beyond ISO 10303-221).
