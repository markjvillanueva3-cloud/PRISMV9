## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Synchronous Dataflow Semantics, Clock Calculus, and Polyhedral Compilation for Verified Hardware/Software Wiring**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s “wiring” galaxy next masters the rigorous clock calculus and causality analysis of synchronous languages, the polyhedral model for scheduling and resource allocation in dataflow graphs, and the categorical treatment of affine/graded modalities that control latency and buffer sizing in bounded reactive systems. This layer provides the formal infrastructure for guaranteed absence of causality loops, static schedulability, and provably bounded-memory compilation from high-level Lustre-like specifications to VHDL or C.

Key concrete topics include:
- Lucid Synchrone clock calculus and its extension to n-synchronous clocks (Caspi et al., 2003)
- The polyhedral model for automatic parallelization and memory allocation of affine recurrence equations (Feautrier, 1992; Bastoul, 2004)
- Graded modal types for tracking data ages and buffer requirements in synchronous functional programming (Krishnaswami & Benton, 2011; Bahr et al., already covered but extended in later work)
- Denotational semantics of synchronous dataflow using sheaf theory and presheaves over time (Manna & Pnueli, 1992; Benveniste et al., 2018)

These results are taught in advanced courses on synchronous programming and verified compilation (e.g., ENS Paris “Synchronous Languages” and “Polyhedral Compilation” seminars) and appear in IEEE and ACM standards-track literature on high-level synthesis.
