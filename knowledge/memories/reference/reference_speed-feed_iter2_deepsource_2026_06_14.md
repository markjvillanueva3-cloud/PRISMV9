## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Machining Mechanics & Process Physics (Safety-Critical Galaxy)**

**Finite Element Modeling of Chip Formation and Strain-Rate Dependent Behavior**  
Oxley’s predictive machining theory is extended by rigorous orthogonal and oblique cutting models incorporating temperature- and strain-rate-sensitive flow stress using Johnson-Cook constitutive models. Key focus is on prediction of shear-zone thickness, segmented chip formation, and adiabatic shear banding under high-speed conditions.

**Thermo-Mechanical Coupling and Heat Partition Models**  
Advanced analytical and numerical treatment of heat generation in primary, secondary, and tertiary deformation zones, including moving heat-source theory and partition ratios at tool-chip and tool-work interfaces. Critical for tool-life prediction beyond ISO 3685 empirical limits.

**High-Speed Machining Dynamics and Stability**  
Chatter stability lobes derived from frequency response functions of the machine-tool system coupled with regenerative force models. Includes time-domain simulation of interrupted cutting and nonlinear stability analysis.

**Material Microstructure Effects on Machinability**  
Relationship between workpiece metallurgical state (grain size, phase transformations, work-hardening, residual stress) and cutting forces, chip morphology, and surface integrity. Draws from materials science treatment of machining Inconel, titanium, and hardened steels.

**Standards-Driven Uncertainty Quantification in Machining Tests**  
ASME B5.54 and ISO 230 series methods for machine-tool performance evaluation integrated with cutting-process metrology; NIST traceability protocols for force dynamometry and thermal measurement in safety-critical applications.
