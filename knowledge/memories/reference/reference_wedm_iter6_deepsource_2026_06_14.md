**NEXT LAYER: Advanced WEDM Knowledge for PRISM Galaxy (Iteration 6)**

World-leading mastery next requires command of:

- **Plasma channel formation dynamics and dielectric ionization physics** beyond basic breakdown, including streamer propagation theory and Townsend avalanche coefficients in hydrocarbon vs. deionized water dielectrics (Kunieda et al., 2005).
- **Wire electrode vibration modal analysis and tension-frequency mapping** under spark-induced impulsive loading, incorporating Euler-Bernoulli beam theory with distributed stochastic forcing (Guo et al., 2013; Chen et al., 2016).
- **Thermo-mechanical white-layer formation mechanisms** with phase transformation kinetics, martensitic/austenitic retention models, and micro-crack initiation criteria in ferrous and nickel-based alloys (Cusanelli et al., 2004; Klocke et al., 2014).
- **Debris particle transport and dielectric flushing hydrodynamics** inside the narrow kerf using two-phase CFD with particle-laden flow, bubble expansion, and pressure wave propagation (Wang et al., 2019; Zhang et al., 2021).
- **Adaptive servo control using real-time gap impedance spectroscopy and pulse-type classification** via FPGA-based high-speed waveform feature extraction (Altpeter & Perez, 2004; Yan & Liao, 2009).
- **Surface integrity metrology standards** including ISO 4287/4288 profile analysis, SEM/EDX white-layer thickness protocols, and residual stress depth profiling via XRD sin²ψ method for WEDM'd aerospace components (NIST SRM 2100 series references).
- **High-precision wire path compensation algorithms** accounting for wire lag, corner error, and kerf taper via geometric-simulation and CNC interpolation correction (Hsue et al., 1999; Sanchez et al., 2006).

These layers integrate multi-physics (electro-thermo-hydro-mechanical) modeling validated against high-speed imaging, spectroscopy, and acoustic emission data.
