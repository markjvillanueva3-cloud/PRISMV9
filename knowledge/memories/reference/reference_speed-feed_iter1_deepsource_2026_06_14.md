## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Machining Process Mechanics & Thermal Modeling**

A world-leading expert in speed-feed next masters the underlying continuum mechanics and thermal-physics governing chip formation, force prediction, and heat partition in orthogonal and oblique cutting, before any proprietary constants are applied.

Core topics include:
- Merchant’s force circle and minimum energy principle, including shear angle prediction from plasticity theory.
- Oxley’s predictive machining theory incorporating strain-hardening, strain-rate, and temperature-dependent flow stress (Johnson-Cook type constitutive models).
- Moving heat source solutions for tool-chip interface and tool-workpiece interface temperatures (Jaeger, Rosenthal, Hahn, Loewen-Shaw).
- Friction modeling at the tool-chip interface: sticking-sliding zones, Coulomb vs. constant shear friction models.
- Oblique cutting transformation mechanics, Stabler’s chip flow rule, and 3D force resolution.
- Thermal conductivity and diffusivity effects on heat partition ratio (Boothroyd, Trigger, Chao & Trigger).
- High-strain-rate material behavior characterization via Split-Hopkinson Pressure Bar (SHPB) data mapped to machining regimes.

**METHOD/THEORY/ STANDARDS DEPTH ONLY**

Mastery requires ability to derive temperature fields from Fourier’s heat equation with moving sources, couple plastic work with heat generation via Taylor-Quinney coefficient, and apply finite-difference or analytical solutions for transient tool temperatures. Standards govern test methods for machinability data collection and thermal property measurement.
