**Next Layer: Categorical Semantics of Synchronous Reactive Systems and Linear Resource-Aware Circuit Synthesis**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s “wiring” galaxy next masters the **categorical axiomatization of synchronous dataflow and Kahn networks** together with **linear-logic-based resource typing for hardware synthesis**, emphasizing string-diagram rewriting, graded comonads for latency and causality, and verified compilation pipelines from Lustre-like languages down to provably correct RTL.

Concrete topics include:
- **Categorical models of clocked and multi-clocked synchronous programs** using traced monoidal categories and dinaturality (Boussinot-style reactive kernels interpreted in Prof or Para categories).
- **Graded modal types for quantitative resource analysis** in hardware (static timing, power, and causality) via graded comonads and quantitative realizability.
- **String-diagram rewriting systems** for verified RTL optimization, extending Ghica–Jung diagrammatic semantics with equational theories drawn from directed linear logic.
- **Mechanized meta-theory** of synchronous compilers that prove bisimulation or refinement between dataflow equations and generated VHDL/Verilog using Coq or Isabelle/HOL.

This layer sits directly above the already-covered Ghica, Atkey, Baudart–Mandel–Pouzet, Bourke–Pouzet, and Rieu et al. results.
