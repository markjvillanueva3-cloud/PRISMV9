## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer centers on agent-oriented software engineering (AOSE) methodologies and normative organizational frameworks for scalable orchestration. This layer specifies roles, interaction protocols, institutional rules, and deontic extensions to temporal/epistemic logics for obligations, permissions, and sanctions. It addresses verifiable coordination in open, dynamic societies via model-driven design, self-organization, and compliance checking—preventing emergent violations while enabling adaptive orchestration beyond BDI or process calculi foundations.

Core concepts include Gaia-style role models, Tropos goal-oriented requirements, electronic institutions for norm enforcement, and logic-based governance of agent societies. These are taught in advanced AOSE and distributed AI curricula, emphasizing traceability from specification to verified multi-agent ensembles.
