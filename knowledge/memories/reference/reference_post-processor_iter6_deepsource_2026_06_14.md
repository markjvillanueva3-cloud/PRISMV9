**NEXT LAYER (Safety-Critical Post-Processor Galaxy – Iteration 6)**

World-leading post-processor experts next master **kinematic error modeling under functional-safety constraints**, specifically the integration of **ISO 230-1:2012** geometric error mapping with **IEC 61508-2:2010** and **ISO 13849-1:2015** safety-related position monitoring for five-axis tool-path generation. This includes formal treatment of **homogeneous transformation matrix (HTM) uncertainty propagation** through rotary axes under non-orthogonal configurations when safety functions (STO, SS1, SS2, SLS) are active.

Key uncovered knowledge:

- **Error-sensitive post-processor synthesis** using screw theory and Lie-group methods to bound positional deviation within Performance Level (PL) and Safety Integrity Level (SIL) envelopes (Merlet, 2006; Tsai & Joshi, 2000).
- **Safety-constrained inverse kinematics** that enforces **IEC 61800-5-2** safe motion limits during rotary-axis singularity traversal and kinematic redundancy resolution.
- **Real-time contour-error monitoring** fused with safety PLC feedback loops per **ISO 13849-1:2015 Category 3/4 architectures**, ensuring post-processor output never violates safe speed/position zones even under dynamic machine compliance.
- **Probabilistic HTM error budgeting** derived from **ISO/TS 23131:2021** (laser tracker uncertainty) and **ASME B89.4.19-2005** performance evaluation of laser trackers, propagated through the post-processor’s kinematic chain.

This layer shifts focus from pure geometric post-processing to **certifiable safety-aware post-processing** required for lights-out or human-collaborative manufacturing cells.
