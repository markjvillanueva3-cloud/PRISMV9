**Next Layer: Ontology-Driven Architectural Knowledge Graphs Integrated with 4C/ID Instructional Blueprints for Adaptive, Continuous Learning in Software Architecture**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s Academy galaxy next masters the **formal ontological modeling of architectural knowledge** and its embedding into **dynamic knowledge graphs** that support automated reasoning, traceability, and personalized learning paths. This layer explicitly links ATAM outputs to evolving AKGs, uses semantic web technologies for continuous learning, and aligns knowledge-graph navigation with 4C/ID learning-task sequences and just-in-time information scaffolds.

Key competencies include:
- Constructing domain ontologies for architectural decisions, quality attributes, and design patterns (using OWL, RDF(S), and SPARQL).
- Building and querying Architectural Knowledge Graphs that ingest ATAM scenarios, trade-off points, and sensitivity points as first-class nodes with provenance.
- Designing adaptive learning environments where the AKG drives personalized 4C/ID task classes, cognitive-load-aware information delivery, and automated generation of pedagogical ADRs.
- Applying formal reasoning over the graph to detect knowledge gaps, suggest refactoring of decision records, and maintain curriculum coherence in CS programs.
