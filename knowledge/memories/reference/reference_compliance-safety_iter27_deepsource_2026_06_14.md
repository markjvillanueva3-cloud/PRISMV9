## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer involves language-based security via type theory (static enforcement of safety invariants and noninterference properties), abstract interpretation for sound over-approximation of program behaviors to prove compliance with safety policies, dependent types for machine-checked proofs of adherence to specifications, and software-specific lifecycle standards that embed verification, validation, and assurance activities at every process stage. These provide compositional, semantics-driven techniques that extend beyond assurance cases, model checking, and systems-level standards by guaranteeing properties at the programming-language level and integrating them into repeatable engineering workflows.

This layer equips experts to formally prove type safety theorems, mechanically verify functional correctness and information-flow compliance, and apply standardized processes that link artifacts to safety requirements without runtime overhead.
