**Next Layer: Formal Treatment of Quotation Model Uncertainty, Calibration, and Decision-Theoretic Scoring in Software Analytics**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s quoting galaxy next masters the rigorous integration of **proper scoring rules**, **Bayesian model averaging under model uncertainty**, and **value-of-information** frameworks specifically applied to predictive quoting and cost-estimation models. This layer moves beyond basic uncertainty quantification to formal calibration of probabilistic quotes, decision-theoretic model selection, and quantification of the economic value of reducing quote uncertainty.

Core topics include:
- Strictly proper scoring rules (logarithmic, Brier, spherical) for calibration and sharpness assessment of quoting distributions.
- Bayesian model averaging (BMA) and Bayesian model selection for handling competing COCOMO-style or parametric quoting models.
- Utility-based evaluation of quoting models via decision-theoretic loss functions rather than purely statistical metrics.
- Value-of-Information (VoI) analysis to prioritize data collection or model refinement that most improves quoting accuracy.
- Formal propagation of epistemic and aleatory uncertainty through hierarchical Bayesian quoting models.

This knowledge is drawn from graduate-level courses on probabilistic modeling, decision analysis under uncertainty, and advanced software metrics.
