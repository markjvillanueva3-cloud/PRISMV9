## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Pedagogical Content Knowledge (PCK) for Software Engineering, Ontology Engineering for Curriculum Design, and Evidence-Based Curriculum Architecture in Higher Education Computing.**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s Academy galaxy must master how to design, validate, and evolve domain-specific computing curricula using **Pedagogical Content Knowledge (PCK)** adapted to software architecture, distributed systems, and data-intensive applications. This includes constructing **ontologies for learning outcomes**, applying backward design at the course and galaxy (curriculum) level, and using rigorous curriculum assessment frameworks drawn from computing education research.

Key next-layer topics include:
- **Constructing ontologies for curriculum knowledge domains** beyond Gruber’s foundational work, using formal concept analysis and ontology alignment techniques for modular “galaxy” structures.
- **Software Engineering Curriculum Patterns** and **Curriculum Design Studios** as reflective practice for SE educators.
- **Computing Education Research (CER)** methodologies for measuring learning at Bloom’s revised taxonomy levels in complex engineering topics (architecture description, DDD tactical patterns, data systems trade-offs).
- **Curriculum accreditation and outcomes-based assessment** aligned with ABET and CS2013 extensions.
- **Cognitive Task Analysis** and **Learning Progression** frameworks applied to software engineering concepts.
