**Next Layer: Mastery of Cognitive Task Analysis (CTA), Instructional Design Models, and Curriculum-as-Engineering for Computing Education**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s Academy galaxy next masters **structured cognitive task analysis techniques** applied to software engineering knowledge, advanced **instructional systems design (ISD)** models tailored to computing, and **curriculum engineering** as a formal design discipline. This layer moves beyond general PCK and learning progressions into rigorous elicitation of expert mental models, hierarchical task decomposition, and evidence-based curriculum architecture that treats learning outcomes as engineered artifacts with measurable performance specifications.

Key areas include:
- **Cognitive Task Analysis methods** (CTA) for uncovering implicit software architecture decision-making, performance engineering trade-offs, and debugging strategies (Crandall et al., 2006; Clark et al., 2008).
- Application of **ADDIE**, **Backward Design**, and **Learning Engineering** frameworks to computing curricula, with emphasis on iterative validation against competency rubrics.
- **Curriculum engineering** methodologies that apply systems engineering principles (requirements analysis, modularity, verification & validation) directly to curriculum design, treating courses as engineered systems (Lucke et al., 2022; Seemann & Tartsch, 2021).
- Integration of **software performance engineering** pedagogical content knowledge from advanced systems courses into curriculum architecture (MIT OCW 6.172).

This knowledge enables construction of academy modules whose internal structure mirrors high-reliability software systems—traceable, measurable, and evolvable.
