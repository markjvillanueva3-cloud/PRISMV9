## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next tier of mastery in dormant-data centers on long-term cryptographic lifecycle controls for inactive archives, proxy re-encryption for delegated access without bulk decryption, and formal specification of integrity invariants in cold-storage systems. This layer integrates NIST SP 800-57 key-management practices (generation, escrow, rotation, and cryptoperiod enforcement for decades-long confidentiality), ISO/IEC 27040 storage-security controls for media classification and protection, and proxy-based cryptographic access structures that preserve forward security. Theory emphasizes reduction-based proofs tying IND-CPA confidentiality to archival integrity against adaptive adversaries. Methodology draws from TLA+ model checking to verify liveness and safety of distributed archival protocols, extending verified-system techniques while avoiding overlap with covered PDP/PoR or updatable-encryption results. Depth comes from MIT 6.824 labs on fault-tolerant storage layers that embed these primitives.
