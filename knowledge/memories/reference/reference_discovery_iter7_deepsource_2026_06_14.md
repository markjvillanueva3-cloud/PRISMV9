**Next Layer: Ontology Evolution, Modular Forgetting, and Justification-Based Repair in Large-Scale Knowledge Graphs**

A world-leading expert in discovery next masters the formal theory and algorithmic machinery for **ontology evolution under modular extraction**, **justified forgetting**, and **axiom-pinpointing-based repair** at industrial scale. This layer focuses on how belief revision operators, conservative extensions, and uniform interpolation interact with justification-oriented debugging to enable safe, incremental updates in dynamic knowledge graphs without global recomputation.

Key next competencies include:
- Forgetting-based modularization and uniform interpolation in expressive DLs (SROIQ, ALCHOIQ) to compute minimal forgetting signatures that preserve query entailments.
- Axiom weakening and refinement operators grounded in justification-based repair, including laconic justifications and pinpointing formulas.
- Ontology evolution frameworks that integrate AGM-style belief revision with DL entailment preservation, using kernel contraction and remainder sets.
- Computational complexity results for uniform interpolation and forgetting in Horn and non-Horn fragments, plus practical approximations via forgetting signatures and modularization heuristics.
- Tooling-level mastery of justification-oriented proof infrastructures and their use in incremental reasoning engines for evolving knowledge bases.

This builds directly on already-covered justification finding, OWL API modular extraction, and conservative extensions by adding controlled evolution, safe forgetting, and repair that scales to enterprise discovery graphs.
