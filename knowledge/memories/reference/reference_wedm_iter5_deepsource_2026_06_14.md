## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Plasma Channel Dynamics, Dielectric Breakdown Kinetics, and Wire Vibration–Gap Interaction in WEDM under Safety-Critical Aerospace Constraints**

World-leading WEDM expertise next masters the multi-physics coupling between transient plasma channel expansion, shock-wave propagation in the dielectric, thermoelastic wire vibration modes, and servo-controlled gap impedance under sub-10 µm kerf precision and zero-defect surface integrity requirements.

Key uncovered domains include:
- Theoretical modeling of plasma channel pressure and shock front propagation using compressible two-phase flow (Van Dijck-type continuum extended by high-speed imaging validation).
- Dielectric liquid breakdown delay statistics, streamer-to-arc transition times, and statistical distribution of ignition delay (t_d) under nano-second pulse conditions.
- Transverse and axial wire electrode vibration eigenmodes, their coupling to servo gap control, and resulting kerf wall straightness error in tall aerospace workpieces.
- Real-time pulse discrimination algorithms distinguishing open-circuit, normal discharge, short-circuit, and abnormal arcing via gap voltage/current waveform feature extraction (beyond basic Liao-type classifiers).
- Quantitative evaluation of recast layer microstructure, microcrack depth, and residual stress gradients in nickel-based superalloys and titanium alloys as functions of pulse energy, dielectric flushing pressure, and wire tension.

These topics appear in advanced manufacturing physics curricula and standards for high-reliability components.
