## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: State-dependent delay modeling with nonlinear regenerative effects, thermo-mechanical tool-workpiece interface compliance, and probabilistic stability prediction under process damping variability in turning.**

World-leading CAM experts master advanced semi-discretization and full-discretization methods for DDEs with state-dependent delays including nonlinear chip thickness and tool flank contact loss. Key extensions cover time-periodic systems with simultaneous structural and cutting-force nonlinearities, thermo-elastic deflection of the workpiece, and stochastic parameter variation (material inhomogeneity, damping fluctuation).

**Core uncovered topics:**
- Numerical construction of stability charts for turning with continuously varying spindle speed using Chebyshev collocation and Lyapunov-Floquet theory for periodic DDEs with distributed delays (process damping).
- Modeling of flank-wave contact and ploughing force as a nonlinear, velocity-dependent additional stiffness that modulates the effective regenerative delay.
- Bayesian inference and Monte-Carlo-based uncertainty propagation on stability lobe diagrams that incorporate measured FRF variability, temperature-dependent modulus, and identified process-damping coefficients.
- ISO and ASME-compliant volumetric error mapping integrated with dynamic substructuring for prediction of position-dependent stability in long-bed CNC lathes.

These build directly on prior process-damping and SSV literature by treating the system as a nonlinear time-periodic delay differential equation with state-dependent delay and stochastic coefficients, solved via high-order collocation or piecewise polynomial methods.
