## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Process Physics, Dielectric Dynamics, and Metrology of Subsurface Integrity in WEDM**

World-leading expertise advances to:

- **Thermo-electric plasma channel modeling** including anode/cathode energy partition, plasma flushing efficiency, and time-dependent dielectric breakdown strength under high-pressure flushing (Jain, 2002; Kunieda et al., 2011).
- **Gap phenomena and debris transport**: hydrodynamic simulation of dielectric flow, bubble expansion/collapse, and their effect on secondary discharges and wire lag (Wang & Han, 2013; Okada et al., 2010).
- **Subsurface metallurgy and integrity**: white-layer (recast) thickness prediction, heat-affected zone microstructure evolution, residual stress profiles, and micro-crack formation mechanisms in tool steels, titanium alloys, and nickel-based superalloys (Aspiwall et al., 2001; Newton et al., 2009; Li et al., 2015).
- **Wire vibration dynamics and servo control**: modal analysis of wire electrode under tension, transverse vibration suppression, and real-time servo gap control using discharge pulse discrimination (Yan & Liao, 2009; Chen et al., 2015).
- **Dielectric fluid chemistry and breakdown**: deionization kinetics, conductivity control, and the role of deionized water vs. hydrocarbon oils on MRR, surface finish, and electrolysis suppression (Schumacher, 2004; Masuzawa, 2001).

These topics are drawn from graduate-level manufacturing process physics, precision engineering metrology, and non-traditional machining courses.
