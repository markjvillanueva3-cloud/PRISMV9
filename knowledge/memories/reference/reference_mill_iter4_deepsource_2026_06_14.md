## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Advanced Process Damping, Nonlinear Cutter Dynamics, and Uncertainty Propagation in Stability Lobes for Safety-Critical Milling**

World-leading expertise next requires mastery of:

- **Frequency-domain process damping models** that incorporate flank-wave indentation and velocity-dependent damping coefficients derived from interference between tool flank and machined surface (Tlusty & Ismail, 1981; Altintas, 2012).
- **Nonlinear regenerative chatter with multiple regenerative effects and variable time delays** using full-discretization with state-dependent delays and Runge-Kutta integration for interrupted cutting (Insperger et al., 2015; Dombovari & Stepan, 2010).
- **Receptance coupling substructure analysis (RCSA) extensions** to nonlinear tool-holder interfaces including gyroscopic and centrifugal effects at high spindle speeds (Schmitz & Smith, 2019 extension; Grossi, 2021).
- **Uncertainty quantification and propagation** through milling stability lobes using Monte Carlo, Polynomial Chaos Expansion, and Generalized Polynomial Chaos on modal parameters and process damping (Zaloga et al., 2020; ASME B5.54 uncertainty annex updates).
- **High-speed spindle metrology and dynamic performance mapping** under load per ISO 230-7:2015 and NIST best practices for speed-dependent FRF variation and thermal growth effects on stability (NIST IR 8260 extensions; ISO 230-7:2015).

These build directly on already-covered semi-discretization, basic RCSA, and force-measurement uncertainty by adding nonlinear time-delay physics, probabilistic stability margins, and standards-mandated uncertainty budgets required for aerospace/defense safety-critical certification.
