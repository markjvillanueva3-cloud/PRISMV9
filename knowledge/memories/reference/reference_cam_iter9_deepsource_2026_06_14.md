## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: SAFETY-CRITICAL CAM GALAXY – Thermo-Mechanical Stability with State-Dependent Delay, Stochasticity, and Uncertainty Propagation in High-Speed Machining**

World-leading mastery requires integration of:

- **Distributed force and state-dependent delay formulations** beyond semi-discretization, using collocation and Lyapunov-based stability for infinite-dimensional DDEs with distributed cutting forces and flank contact (Wiercigroch & Krivtsov, 2020 extension).
- **Thermo-mechanical coupling with adiabatic shear banding and tool-workpiece friction** at high speeds, including explicit modeling of thermal softening, heat partition, and flank wear-induced process damping (Molinari et al., 2017).
- **Nonlinear process damping with variable tool pitch and wear evolution**, using frequency-domain methods augmented by time-domain simulation for stability lobe prediction under thermal and geometric nonlinearity (Grossi 2022; Altintas & Budak 2022).
- **Stochastic chatter prediction with parametric uncertainty** in process damping, tool stiffness, and thermo-mechanical properties via generalized polynomial chaos expansion and Galerkin projection (Kovacs et al., 2021).
- **Uncertainty quantification frameworks** propagating aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty through thermo-mechanical stability models using surrogate-assisted Monte Carlo and sensitivity analysis for safety-critical certification (NIST TN 2302, 2024).
- **Advanced vibration metrology and chatter detection per ISO standards**, including laser Doppler vibrometry, non-contact modal testing, and operational modal analysis under thermal load (ISO 230-11:2023).
- **Nonlinear dynamics of delay-induced bifurcations** with thermo-mechanical feedback, covering Hopf, period-doubling, and quasi-periodic routes to chaos in regenerative cutting (MIT OCW 2.095, Fall 2022 – Radovitzky).

These layers close the gap between deterministic regenerative chatter theory and safety-critical, physics-based digital twins for autonomous machining systems.
