## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Process Damping, State-Dependent Delay Modeling, and Volumetric/Probabilistic Stability Integration for Safety-Critical CAM**

World-leading CAM experts next master the physics and metrology of **process damping at low cutting speeds**, including identification of damping force coefficients from orthogonal cutting tests and their integration into frequency-domain and time-domain stability solvers (Altintas & Eynian, 2008; Budak & Tunc, 2009).

They then advance to **state-dependent delay models** that capture true tooth-pass periodicity under both radial immersion variation and tool deflection, using full-discretization and numerical integration methods beyond basic semi-discretization (Insperger et al., 2015; Bachrathý et al., 2016).

At the standards and uncertainty layer, experts implement **ISO 230-1:2012** and **ISO/TR 16982:2016** for geometric and thermal error mapping of machine tools, combining them with Bayesian inference frameworks for probabilistic chatter prediction that propagate both model-form and parametric uncertainty into stability lobe reliability (NIST TN 2203, 2022 extension in uncertainty quantification literature).

They further integrate **nonlinear regenerative chatter theory** with variable spindle speed modulation (VSM) using Floquet theory on time-periodic DDEs with explicit spindle acceleration terms, drawn from high-order spectral element methods (Otto & Radons, 2013; Seguy et al., 2016).

Finally, they apply **ASME B5.64-2006** and NIST IR 8237 (2019) protocols for in-process metrology of dynamic compliance under real cutting loads, linking volumetric performance (ISO 230-11:2016) directly to chatter boundary confidence for safety-critical aerospace titanium and nickel alloy machining.
