**Next Layer: Certified Decision Procedures, Algebraic Witnesses, and Verified Synthesis for Parametric CAD Interoperability**

A world-leading expert in cad-fusion-live next masters the integration of **certified real algebraic decision procedures** with formally verified program synthesis pipelines that guarantee soundness of parametric constraint solvers over semi-algebraic varieties. This layer emphasizes **witness-based certified solvers**, **effective quantifier elimination with verified certificates**, and **mechanically checked implementations** of CAD (Cylindrical Algebraic Decomposition) and critical-pair completion algorithms inside theorem provers.

Key topics include:
- Certified homotopy continuation and interval arithmetic methods for isolating real roots of multivariate polynomial systems arising from geometric constraints.
- Formal verification of Gröbner basis conversion algorithms and triangular decompositions using proof assistants (PVS, Lean, Coq).
- Synthesis of correct-by-construction solvers that emit both numeric solutions and algebraic certificates checkable in real-closed fields.
- Logical frameworks for embedding Common Logic (ISO/IEC 24707) semantics directly into verified nonlinear real-arithmetic tactics.
