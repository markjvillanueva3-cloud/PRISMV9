## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Advanced Quality Models, Formal Verification, and Empirical Software Quality Engineering**

A world-leading expert in software quality next masters quantitative quality attribute modeling, formal methods for quality assurance, statistical process control adapted to software (via SPC for software), and rigorous empirical methods for quality improvement. This includes predictive quality models, architecture-based quality evaluation techniques, and standards for quality measurement programs.

Key areas:
- **Quality Attribute Scenarios & Tactics Frameworks** beyond basic SEI approaches, including quantitative reasoning frameworks (e.g., utility trees with cost-benefit analysis).
- **Model-Driven Quality Assurance**: Using formal specification languages (Z, Alloy, TLA+) and model checking (SPIN, NuSMV) to verify quality properties.
- **Software Reliability Growth Models (SRGMs)** extensions: non-homogeneous Poisson processes, Bayesian reliability models, and their predictive validity.
- **ISO/IEC 25012 and ISO/IEC 25023** for data quality and quality measurement elements.
- **Empirical Software Engineering** methods: replication studies, meta-analysis of quality metrics, and theory-building in SE (using guidelines from Wohlin, Runeson, and Kitchenham).
- **IEEE 1061 (Software Quality Metrics Methodology)** and its application in establishing organization-specific quality measurement frameworks.
- **CMMI Level 4/5** quantitative project management and causal analysis techniques applied specifically to quality outcomes.
- **Static Analysis & Symbolic Execution** effectiveness studies for quality defect detection (from IEEE TSE and ICSE).

These represent the post-2014 layer focused on measurable, verifiable, and empirically validated quality engineering at scale.
