**NEXT LAYER: Bounded Model Checking, Craig Interpolation, and Automated Abstraction Refinement for Bug-Hunting**

A world-leading bug-hunting expert next masters **bounded model checking (BMC)** combined with **interpolation-based refinement** and **counterexample-guided abstraction refinement (CEGAR)** at industrial scale. This layer focuses on practical decidability trade-offs, incremental SAT/SMT solving, and lazy abstraction techniques that scale beyond explicit-state or pure predicate-abstraction methods already covered.

Key competencies include:
- Encoding safety properties as SAT/SMT instances with loop unrolling and k-induction.
- Using Craig interpolants to compute over-approximations for refinement without full predicate discovery.
- Integrating IC3/PDR (Property-Directed Reachability) with interpolation for hardware and software bug-hunting.
- Understanding relative completeness results and convergence guarantees in lazy abstraction frameworks.
