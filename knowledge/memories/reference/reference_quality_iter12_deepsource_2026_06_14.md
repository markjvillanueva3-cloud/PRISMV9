This includes rigorous treatment of confounding variables in quality metrics, do-calculus for intervention analysis on quality attributes, and quantitative linking of internal product quality to external business value under uncertainty.

Key next-layer knowledge includes:

- Formal multi-attribute value theory (MAVT) and multi-attribute utility theory (MAUT) for quality requirements negotiation and prioritization.
