**NEXT LAYER: Software Engineering & Systems Pedagogy for Intelligent Platforms**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s Academy galaxy must next master the pedagogical frameworks and advanced software-engineering theory used to design, scale, and teach large-scale intelligent manufacturing-intelligence platforms as enterprise software systems.

**Core uncovered topics include:**

- **Software architecture for data-intensive systems**: patterns for real-time analytics pipelines, domain-driven design in polyglot persistence environments, and evolvable microservice-based intelligence platforms (Evans 2003; Kleppmann 2017).
- ** pedagogy of computing systems**: how universities structure capstone project-based learning for building production-grade data platforms, including constructive alignment and backward design (Biggs & Tang 2011; MIT 6.172 / 6.824).
- **Standards for software quality, lifecycle, and systems engineering**: ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207:2017 (software life cycle processes), ISO/IEC 25010:2011 (SQuaRE quality model), and IEEE 42010 (architecture description).
- **Empirical software engineering and measurement**: rigorous approaches to defining, validating, and teaching software metrics and platform success indicators (Basili et al. 1994; ACM SIGSOFT Empirical Software Engineering papers).
- **Knowledge representation and ontology engineering for intelligent systems**: foundational theory for building teachable, evolvable domain models inside software platforms (Gruber 1995; Studer et al. 1998).

This layer supplies the institutional, standards-based, and pedagogical depth required to transform domain knowledge into scalable, maintainable academy curriculum and platform architecture.
