**Next Layer: Forgetting-Based Ontology Modularization, Conservative Extensions, and Logical Difference in Expressive DLs**

A world-leading expert in discovery next masters the algorithmic and model-theoretic foundations for computing uniform interpolants and forgetting operators that preserve entailments over signature-restricted vocabularies, including complexity results for EL, ALC, and SROIQ, as well as practical computation via Ackermann-style elimination, second-order quantifier elimination, and hyper-tableau techniques.

They further master the theory of conservative extensions and logical difference (⊥-difference, concept-difference), which provide rigorous semantic guarantees when modules or evolved ontologies are extracted or repaired. This includes fixed-parameter tractability results, approximation algorithms, and their integration into belief-change operators that satisfy the AGM postulates adapted to DLs.

Key next sources focus on:
- Model-theoretic characterizations of uniform interpolation
- Forgetting in Horn and Horn-SROIQ fragments
- Logical difference operators for ontology versioning and evolution
- Practical implementations in modularization and repair pipelines

These build directly on the listed forgetting/interpolation papers by addressing succinctness bounds, tractable approximations, and integration with justification-based repair and OWL API modularization.
