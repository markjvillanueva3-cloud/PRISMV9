The next layer in pdf-corpus-mill expertise centers on layout-aware document structure recovery, probabilistic graphical models for precise information extraction, and unified dataflow architectures guaranteeing exactly-once semantics during large-scale PDF-to-corpus ETL. This extends covered IR/NLP/distributed foundations with geometric page analysis to infer reading order from PDF content streams, CRF-based labeling for metadata and entity extraction from noisy layouts, and incremental corpus updating that tolerates out-of-order arrivals while preserving annotation provenance.
