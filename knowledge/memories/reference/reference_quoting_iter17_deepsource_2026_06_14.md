**Next Layer: Nonparametric Bayesian Sequential Design and Robust Information Relaxation Bounds for Adaptive Quoting under Model Uncertainty**

A world-leading expert in software quoting moves beyond parametric Bayesian updating and standard Gittins indices into **nonparametric Bayesian sequential decision theory**, focusing on Dirichlet process and Gaussian process priors for unknown cost/schedule distributions, combined with robust information relaxations that explicitly handle model misspecification and imprecise probabilities in dynamic programming formulations for service quoting.

This layer emphasizes **Bayesian nonparametric sequential sampling rules** that adaptively allocate simulation or data-collection effort when the functional form of the quoting model itself is unknown, using posterior sampling over function spaces rather than parameter spaces. It further integrates **distributionally robust information relaxations** that bound the value of information under ambiguity sets (Wasserstein or moment-based), providing tighter regret guarantees for online quoting engines than classical ADP or lookahead policies.

**Core next knowledge includes:**
- Optimal stopping and allocation rules under Dirichlet process priors for sequential Monte Carlo design in software cost modeling.
- Robust dynamic programming via information relaxations with ambiguity sets for imprecise priors (Walley-style).
- Nonparametric extensions of curve-fitting Bayesian experimental design to sequential quoting with unknown response surfaces.
- Theoretical regret bounds for bandit-like quoting problems with Bayesian nonparametrics and model robustness.
