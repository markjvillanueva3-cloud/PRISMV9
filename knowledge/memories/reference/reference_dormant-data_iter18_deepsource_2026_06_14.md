## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next knowledge layer — Proactive Secret‑Sharing & Erasure‑Coded Storage for Long‑Term Dormant Data**

Dormant‑data systems must survive decades of inactivity while remaining confidential, available, and tamper‑detectable even if a subset of storage nodes is compromised or offline. The literature that bridges *information‑theoretic secret sharing*, *proactive refresh* and *erasure‑coded distributed storage* has not yet been incorporated into the PRISM knowledge base. Mastery of this layer gives an expert:

| Sub‑area | Core concepts | Why it matters for dormant data |
|----------|---------------|---------------------------------|
| **Shamir’s Secret Sharing (SSS)** | Polynomial‑based (t,n) threshold sharing, information‑theoretic confidentiality. | Enables splitting archival keys or data into independent fragments that can be stored on sleeping nodes without exposing the secret when any ≤ t nodes are offline. |
| **Proactive Secret Sharing (PSS)** | Periodic share renewal without reconstructing the secret; resilience to mobile adversaries. | Guarantees that a dormant system whose nodes may be compromised at different times never accumulates enough shares to recover the secret, satisfying long‑term confidentiality under *sleepy* adversaries. |
| **Information Dispersal Algorithms (IDA) & Regenerating Codes** | Rabin’s IDA (1989), Dimakis et al.’s regenerating codes (2010); trade‑off between storage overhead and repair bandwidth. | Provides efficient, fault‑tolerant reconstruction of large archival files from a small subset of awakened nodes, reducing the cost of “wake‑up” operations. |
| **Proofs of Retrievability (PoR) & Auditable Storage** | Juels & Kaliski (2007), Ateniese et al. (2010); spot‑checking and homomorphic authenticators. | Allows a dormant client to verify that stored fragments remain intact without full retrieval, supporting *composable availability* while the system sleeps. |
| **Proactive Erasure‑Coding for Long‑Term Archival** | Herzberg, Jarecki & Krawczyk (1995) – proactive secret sharing; Wang et al. (2022) – proactive erasure‑coded storage
