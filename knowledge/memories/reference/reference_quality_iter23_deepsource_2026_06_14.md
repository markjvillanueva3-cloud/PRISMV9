## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer entails mastery of comprehensive software quality assurance (SQA) infrastructure and planning that operationalizes quality management systems within the software lifecycle. This includes formal SQA plans, audits, corrective action procedures, and supplier oversight per IEEE Std 730-2014; end-to-end SQA implementation covering pre-project component quality, in-process assurance, and post-project infrastructure per Galin (2004); and quantitative defect analysis plus reliability growth models for quality prediction and improvement per Tian (2005). Institutional depth comes from ISO/IEC 90003:2014 guidelines adapting ISO 9001 QMS requirements specifically to software engineering processes, emphasizing measurable quality objectives, risk-based thinking, and continual improvement aligned with empirical baselines. Theory extends to quality requirement elicitation integrated with architectural viewpoints (beyond covered SQuaRE models) and pedagogical emphasis on large-scale case analyses in graduate software engineering curricula that stress traceability from quality goals to verifiable artifacts.

These elements equip an expert to build auditable, economically justified quality programs that scale across enterprise software platforms.
