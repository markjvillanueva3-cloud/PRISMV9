## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next layer:** Crystal plasticity finite element (CPFEM) frameworks incorporating visco-plastic self-consistent homogenization to resolve grain-level slip system activation, texture evolution, and Hall-Petch strengthening under the extreme strain-rate, temperature, and stress triaxiality conditions of the primary shear zone. Coupling of CPFEM with non-local ductile damage (distinct from covered Miehe phase-field and Voyiadjis gradient models) for transgranular fracture and chip segmentation prediction. Arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian (ALE) adaptive meshing techniques that maintain element quality in steady-state orthogonal cutting without remeshing or Eulerian fixed-boundary artifacts. Implicit gradient regularization of damage coupled to thermo-mechanical inertia terms for correct shear-band spacing and propagation speed. Validation against high-speed imaging and residual stress measurements per areal surface metrology standards.

These close the gap between continuum thermoviscoplastic localization (already covered) and true microstructure-sensitive CAM physics.
