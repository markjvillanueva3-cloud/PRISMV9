**NEXT LAYER: Architectural Decision Records (ADR) as a Core Curriculum Governance Mechanism and Cognitive Load-Optimized Knowledge Evolution Framework**

A world-leading expert next masters the systematic integration of **Architectural Decision Records** as both a pedagogical governance artifact and a cognitive scaffolding tool for evolving curriculum architectures. This layer focuses on ADR-driven refactoring of learning trajectories, decision capture in capstone-style curriculum studios, and the application of cognitive load theory to minimize extraneous load when learners engage with evolving architectural knowledge.

Key advancements include:
- Using ADR templates to document curriculum design decisions with explicit rationale, context, and trade-offs (aligned with ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010 viewpoints).
- Treating curriculum evolution as an architecture refactoring process where ADRs serve as the primary change log, enabling traceability and institutional memory.
- Embedding just-in-time information presentation (van Merriënboer) inside ADR-driven learning tasks to support complex 4C/ID learning blueprints.
- Measuring the impact of decision visibility on learner failure rates in project-based software architecture courses.
