## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Formal Safety Assurance, Model-Based Safety Assessment, and Runtime Verification for Complex Software Systems**

A world-leading expert in compliance-safety must master **assurance-case patterns and compositional reasoning**, **model-based safety analysis (MBSA)** using formal architectural models, **contract-based design** for safety, and **runtime verification/monitoring** of safety properties in deployed systems. This layer emphasizes rigorous, tool-supported techniques that scale beyond Leveson’s STAMP and basic assurance cases.

Key areas include:
- Goal Structuring Notation (GSN) extensions for defeasible and modular assurance cases
- Contract-based frameworks (e.g., assume-guarantee contracts) for compositional safety verification
- Formal semantics of safety cases and their relation to architectural models (AADL, SysML)
- Runtime verification using temporal logics (LTL, STL) and monitors synthesized from formal specifications
- Quantitative confidence arguments and probabilistic assurance cases
