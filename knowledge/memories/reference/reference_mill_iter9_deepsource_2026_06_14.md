**NEXT LAYER: Probabilistic Uncertainty Propagation in Milling Stability and Machine Tool Accuracy under Combined Thermal-Mechanical-Temporal Effects**

World-leading expertise advances to stochastic modeling of uncertainty propagation from position-dependent process damping, thermal distortion, and spindle speed variation into stability lobe diagrams and finished-part geometric accuracy. This layer integrates:

- Bayesian inference frameworks for joint parameter identification of regenerative chatter, process damping, and position-dependent cutting force coefficients.
- Polynomial Chaos Expansion (PCE) and Generalized Polynomial Chaos for efficient forward propagation of aleatoric/epistemic uncertainties through time-periodic DDE stability operators.
- Metrological traceability chains that combine ISO thermal error mapping with Monte-Carlo-based uncertainty budgets for stability boundary prediction.
- Spectral-element and Chebyshev-collocation methods extended to stochastic delay differential equations with uncertain time-periodic coefficients.
- NIST-traceable uncertainty quantification protocols applied to in-process stability monitoring using audio/vibration signals under variable thermal states.

**Key theory/methods:**
- Stochastic semi-discretization and full-discretization methods with random coefficients.
- Probabilistic stability margin assessment replacing deterministic lobes with confidence-bounded stability regions.
- Temperature-dependent modal parameter updating coupled with position-dependent process damping models.
- ASME B5.54 / ISO 230 uncertainty budgeting extended to dynamic cutting stability prediction per NIST TN 1822 methodology.

This knowledge is drawn from graduate-level precision engineering and nonlinear dynamics curricula, CIRP keynote papers, and standards-body technical reports on manufacturing metrology.
