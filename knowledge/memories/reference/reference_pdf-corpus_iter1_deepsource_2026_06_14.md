## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Structured PDF Corpus Engineering & Document Intelligence**

A world-leading pdf-corpus expert masters the theory and tooling for large-scale, semantically rich PDF document corpora used in information retrieval, NLP pre-training, and enterprise search. This layer focuses on PDF parsing fidelity, layout-aware representation, corpus construction pipelines, and evaluation frameworks — drawn from computer science, digital libraries, and document AI literature.

Key uncovered topics include:
- PDF document model (ISO 32000-2), incremental updates, cross-reference streams, and tagged PDF (PDF/UA).
- Robust extraction pipelines that preserve reading order, logical structure, and vector graphics using layout analysis beyond simple text extraction.
- Corpus construction methodologies: deduplication at document and passage level, content hashing (MinHash/LSH), boilerplate removal, and language identification at scale.
- Evaluation metrics for PDF-to-text and PDF-to-structured conversion (e.g., geometric matching, tree-edit distance on logical trees).
- Standards-based accessibility and archival practices (PDF/A-3, PDF/UA-2) that impact corpus quality and legal admissibility.

This knowledge sits at the intersection of digital document standards, information retrieval system design, and modern document understanding models.
