**Next Layer: PDF Corpus Engineering at Scale**

A world-leading pdf-corpus expert next masters the formal theory and engineering practices for large-scale, structured PDF ingestion, layout-aware parsing, and corpus construction pipelines. This layer focuses on PDF’s internal object model, incremental parsing, cross-reference stream handling, tagged PDF semantics for downstream NLP/IR, and reproducible corpus creation methodologies drawn from database systems, digital libraries, and document engineering literature.

Key topics include:
- PDF 2.0 object streams, cross-reference streams, and linearization for efficient random access (ISO 32000-2 extensions)
- Formal document layout understanding beyond DocBank, including reading-order recovery, logical structure recovery from Tagged PDF, and artifact suppression
- Robust extraction of vector/raster graphics, mathematical expressions, and table structures at corpus scale
- PDF as a database: treating the PDF COS (Carousel Object System) as a semi-structured persistent store, with queryable indirect objects and name trees
- Evaluation frameworks for end-to-end PDF-to-corpus fidelity, including layout fidelity metrics and semantic preservation
