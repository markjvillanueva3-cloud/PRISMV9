## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next mastery layer in software quality centers on quality attribute scenarios/tactics for architecture-centric quality, orthogonal defect classification (ODC) for causal analysis, Personal Software Process (PSP) for individual defect prevention and measurement, and standardized SQA planning with independent verification. Experts use QAW/ATAM to elicit, prioritize, and tradeoff non-functional requirements (availability, usability, maintainability) via utility trees and architectural tactics, feeding directly into design before coding. ODC taxonomies classify defects by trigger, impact, and defect type to enable rapid process feedback loops without exhaustive root-cause data. PSP trains engineers in personal metrics baselines (defect density, yield, estimation accuracy) prior to TSP scaling. These integrate with IEEE 730 SQA plans that mandate quality planning, supplier oversight, and risk-driven audits across the lifecycle, extending ISO 25010 models with proactive, engineer-owned techniques.
