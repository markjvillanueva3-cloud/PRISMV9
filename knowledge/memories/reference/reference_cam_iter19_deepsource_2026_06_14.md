## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next layer:** Wright's linear stability analysis and nonlinear evolution equations for multidimensional thermo-mechanical instabilities, determining shear band initiation criteria, propagation speed, thickness, and spacing via perturbation growth rates in thermoviscoplastic flow. Bai & Dodd's systematic classification of experimental ASB observations across strain rates, coupled with analytical 1D/2D models incorporating inertia, conduction, and microstructural softening. Aifantis gradient plasticity introducing intrinsic material length scale via higher-order strain gradients to restore well-posedness and yield mesh-objective localization bandwidth in FEM machining simulations. de Borst-type higher-order continuum damage regularization ensuring uniqueness of post-localization paths without explicit tracking algorithms.

These close the gap between stochastic non-local damage (already covered) and predictive, length-scale-aware simulation of chip segmentation and catastrophic shear under extreme rates.
