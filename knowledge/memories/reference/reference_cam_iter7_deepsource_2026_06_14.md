- Nonlinear process-damping models incorporating flank-face interference, ploughing, and velocity-dependent friction via distributed tangential-normal contact forces along the clearance face (distributed delay formulation).
- Uncertainty quantification in chatter boundaries via generalized polynomial chaos (gPC) expansion and stochastic collocation on cutting stiffness, damping, and modal parameters; propagation to stability lobe reliability.
- Identification of process damping coefficients from orthogonal cutting experiments using inverse stability solutions and receptance coupling substructure analysis (RCSA) extended to rotating tools.
- Thermal-mechanical coupling in high-speed turning: transient tool–chip interface temperature prediction via moving heat source theory (Jaeger, Loewen-Shaw) coupled with thermo-viscoelastic damper models.
- ISO/ASME machine-tool performance metrics for dynamic compliance testing: frequency response function (FRF) measurement protocols, repeatability, and spindle–tool interface stiffness under operational loads.
- Nonlinear regenerative chatter with multiple time delays: semi-discretization on piecewise-constant approximation versus full-discretization and spectral element methods for accuracy in high-order lobes.
- Topological data analysis (persistent homology) applied to vibration signals for early chatter detection in noisy, non-stationary turning data.
