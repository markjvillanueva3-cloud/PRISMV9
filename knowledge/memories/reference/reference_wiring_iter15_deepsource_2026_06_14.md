**Next Layer: Denotational Semantics, Category-Theoretic Models, and Verified Synchronous Compilers for Reactive Systems**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s wiring galaxy next masters the denotational foundations that unify guarded recursion, sheaf semantics, and synchronous dataflow languages with constructive mathematics and synthetic domain theory. This includes:

- **Categorical semantics of clocks and guarded recursion** using topos-theoretic models (especially the topos of trees and presheaf toposes) to interpret modal fixed-point operators and productive corecursion.
- **Synthetic domain theory** and its application to reactive programming, providing internal languages for reasoning about causality, contractiveness, and step-indexed logical relations inside categories with fixed-point structure.
- **Clocked Type Theory (CTT)** and its cubical variants, with emphasis on how clock quantification encodes productivity and causality in dependent reactive programs.
- **Verified compilation pipelines** for Lustre-like languages that reach CompCert-level assembly, including end-to-end proofs of semantic preservation from synchronous equations to C or RTL.
- **Functorial and sheaf-theoretic models** that interpret signals as sections of sheaves over time domains, connecting the tagged-signal model to higher categorical structures.

These topics appear in advanced type-theory, semantics, and verified-systems curricula beyond the listed guarded/modal courses.
