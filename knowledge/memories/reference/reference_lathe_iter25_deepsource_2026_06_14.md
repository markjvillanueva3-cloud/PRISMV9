## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Spindle-Bearing Thermo-Elastic Metrology, Nonlinear Contact Dynamics, and In-Process Error Mapping under Combined Loads (Safety-Critical Domain)**

World-leading expertise advances to:

- Thermo-mechanical Jacobian coupling between bearing preload, centrifugal expansion, and non-uniform thermal gradients using finite-element-based thermo-elastic distortion models (Bossmanns & Tu, 1999).
- Frequency-domain and time-domain identification of nonlinear bearing stiffness and damping under simultaneous thermal and mechanical loading via Hilbert-Huang transform and stochastic subspace identification (SSI) methods.
- In-situ spindle error motion separation techniques distinguishing synchronous, asynchronous, and thermally induced radial/axial error motions per ASME B89.3.4 and ISO 230-7 Annex B.
- Kalman-filter and particle-filter fusion of multi-sensor data (capacitive probes, RTD arrays, strain-gauge preload sensors) for real-time prediction of spindle centerline drift under transient cutting loads.
- Uncertainty propagation frameworks for combined thermal-mechanical spindle metrology traceable to NIST and PTB standards, including sensitivity analysis of thermal expansion coefficients and contact angle variation.
- Nonlinear rotordynamic stability analysis of spindle systems exhibiting sub-harmonic and chaotic motion due to varying stiffness and thermal preload using continuation techniques (AUTO-07p / MATCONT).

These topics extend beyond previously covered linear FRF-based stability, basic Kalman thermal compensation, and static ISO/ASME spindle tests into coupled, nonlinear, safety-critical regimes required for high-reliability aerospace and medical-component turning.
