## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Ontology Forgetting, Uniform Interpolation, and Conservative Extensions in Expressive DLs with Emphasis on Computational and Algorithmic Foundations**

A world-leading expert next masters the algorithmic and model-theoretic foundations of uniform interpolation and forgetting operators that guarantee logical equivalence on a signature of interest, including complexity results, succinctness bounds, and practical computation via hyper-tableaux or resolution-based methods. This layer extends beyond general surveys into precise decision procedures, interpolant extraction algorithms, and their integration with modularization and repair workflows.

Key uncovered areas include:
- Model-theoretic characterizations of uniform interpolants in ALC and extensions (ALC, ALCO, ALCI).
- Succinctness and computational complexity of forgetting (including lower bounds in EXPTIME and 2-EXPTIME).
- Practical algorithms for computing uniform interpolants via resolution and Ackermann’s lemma generalizations.
- Conservative extensions and their relation to interpolation in ontology modules.
- Forgetting-based approaches to ontology versioning and difference computation.

**Core next sources:**
- Lutz & Wolter (2011) already covered; next is the deeper algorithmic treatment in:
- Konev, Walther, Wolter (2012). Forgetting and uniform interpolation in large-scale description logic terminologies. IJCAI-2012.
- Konev, Lutz, Wolter (2014). On the relationship between uniform interpolation, forgetting, and conservative extensions in description logics. Artificial Intelligence.
- Koopmann & Schmidt (2015). Uniform interpolation of ALC-ontologies using fixpoints. Proc. FroCoS 2015.
- Chen & Pan (2020). Forgetting in description logics using a generalization of Ackermann’s lemma. Journal of Automated Reasoning.
- MIT OCW 6.8250 (Spring 2023). Advanced Topics in Knowledge Representation: Forgetting, Interpolation and Modularity (lectures 8–11).
- IEEE Std 2801-2022 Annex C (uniform interpolation profile for ontology evolution).

These provide the rigorous computational machinery required for scalable discovery engines that safely evolve large manufacturing-intelligence ontologies while preserving semantic commitments.
