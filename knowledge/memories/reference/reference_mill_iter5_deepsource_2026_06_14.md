**NEXT LAYER (Iteration 5): High-Fidelity Physics & Uncertainty in Regenerative Chatter & Process Damping**

World-leading experts next master **stochastic stability prediction**, **nonlinear process damping identification at low speeds**, **integrated geometric-kinematic error propagation into stability lobes**, and **probabilistic uncertainty quantification** of stability boundaries under combined parameter variation.

**Key uncovered domains:**

- Semi-discretization and full-discretization methods extended to stochastic delay differential equations with parametric noise (multiple regenerative effects, runout, and variable time delay).
- Identification of process damping via inverse stability solution and wavelet-based decomposition of cutting force signals at low cutting speeds where ploughing dominates.
- Receptance coupling substructure analysis (RCSA) extended to non-linear tool-holder interfaces and spindle-bearing thermo-mechanical variation.
- Propagation of machine tool geometric errors (ISO 230-1, ISO 230-7), rotary axis error motions (ASME B5.57), and dynamic compliance uncertainty into stability lobe uncertainty bands using Monte-Carlo and generalized polynomial chaos.
- Nonlinear time-domain simulation coupling variable spindle speed actuation (act-and-wait, SSV) with measured process damping maps for safety-critical aerospace titanium and stainless steel milling.
