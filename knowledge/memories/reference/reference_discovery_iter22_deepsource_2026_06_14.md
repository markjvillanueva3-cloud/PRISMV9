## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer focuses on inseparability criteria and locality-based module extraction algorithms to support scalable semantic discovery and reuse in large ontologies. This extends uniform interpolation and forgetting to practical computation of minimal modules that preserve conservative extensions, with complexity results for EL/Horn fragments, syntactic locality checks, and integration into query-rewriting pipelines for ontology-mediated data access. Institutional depth draws from JAIR analyses of module quality metrics, fixpoint-based extraction implementations, and KR conference evaluations of entailment preservation in modular discovery frameworks.
