## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Hierarchical Skill Structures, Cognitive Simulation, and Policy Optimization in Adaptive Curricula**

A world-leading expert next masters **hierarchical Bayesian models of skill discovery and transfer**, **cognitive model simulation at scale**, and **partially observable Markov decision process (POMDP) curriculum planning** grounded in formal learning sciences.

Key uncovered areas include:

- **Knowledge component (KC) model discovery via nonparametric Bayesian methods** (Corbett & Anderson, 1995; Koedinger et al., 2012 extensions).
- **Additive Factors Model (AFM) and Q-Matrix refinement using logistic regression and EM** for cognitive model validation (Cen et al., 2006 is covered; next is Pavlik et al. 2012 AFM extensions and Liu et al. 2011 Q-matrix methods).
- **Deep Knowledge Tracing (DKT) limitations and successor architectures**: Dynamic Key-Value Memory Networks (DKVMN) and graph-augmented sequential models that explicitly represent skill topology (Zhang et al., 2017; Nakagawa et al. 2019 is covered; next layer is relational extensions).
- **Curriculum learning as Bayesian experimental design** at the instructional-policy level using expected information gain and value of information (VoI) (Zhang et al. 2022 is covered; next is Brunskill & Russell, 2011 POMDP teaching and Rafferty et al. 2018 policy optimization under hierarchical priors).
- **MIT-level cognitive architectures**: ACT-R cognitive tutors integrated with hierarchical Bayesian learning (Anderson, 2007; Ritter et al., 2007).
- **Standards-based competency frameworks**: IEEE 1484.11.1-2004 (CMI) and IMS Learning Design specification for reusable, hierarchical competency graphs.
- **MIT OCW 6.813/6.831 User Interface Design and Implementation (Spring 2022)** – focuses on adaptive scaffolding and structured belief tracking in tutoring interfaces (extension of 6.8260).

This layer emphasizes **skill graph induction**, **transfer-appropriate cognitive simulation**, and **optimal instructional policy search** using hierarchical POMDPs and relational Bayesian networks, moving beyond single-skill BKT/DKT into multi-scale curriculum engineering.
