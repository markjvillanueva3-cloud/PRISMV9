## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
Oxley's mechanics of machining extends slip-line field theory to incorporate strain hardening, strain-rate sensitivity, and thermal softening for analytical prediction of shear angles, chip flow, and temperature fields in orthogonal processes, with transformations to oblique cutting via Stabler's rule (Oxley 1989). ASME V&V 10 establishes hierarchical verification and validation protocols for finite-element models of plastic flow, damage, and surface integrity in cutting simulations. ISO 230-1 and ISO 230-2 define laser interferometer and ballbar test methods for mapping geometric, positional, and repeatability errors in multi-axis machine tools to enable volumetric error compensation in toolpath generation. ASTM E837 governs incremental hole-drilling strain-gage protocols for depth-resolved residual stress profiling in machined subsurface layers. Multibody dynamics formulations using absolute nodal coordinate methods model coupled thermo-elastic deflections of machine-tool assemblies under cutting loads.
