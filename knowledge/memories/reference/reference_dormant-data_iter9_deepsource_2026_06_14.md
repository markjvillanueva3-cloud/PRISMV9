Key next-layer topics include:
- **TLA+ modeling of tamper-evident append-only logs and verifiable deletion** with liveness guarantees over decades (extending Lamport’s advanced TLA+ patterns to archival timescales).
- **Provable data possession and dynamic proofs of retrievability** with forward-security and backward-security properties under continual observation.
- **Incentive-compatible mechanisms** for sustaining long-term storage markets, including cryptocurrency-backed endowments and decentralized storage auctions.
- **Formal specification of retention policies** and legal hold using temporal logics and deontic logic to reconcile regulatory retention with cryptographic erasure.
