## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Algorithmic Uniform Interpolation, Forgetting in Lightweight DLs, and Ontology Modularization for Large-Scale Semantic Integration**

A world-leading expert next masters the computational treatment of uniform interpolation in tractable description logics (especially EL and Horn fragments), second-order quantifier elimination techniques, and their integration into modular ontology engineering pipelines at industrial scale. This includes fixpoint-based algorithms, resolution-based methods for ALC, locality-based module extraction as preprocessing, and the tight relationship between uniform interpolation, conservative extensions, and inseparability checking.

Key uncovered depth includes:
- Practical implementations and complexity results for EL and EL++ forgetting (Konev et al., 2012; Nikitina et al., 2012 extensions).
- Second-order quantifier elimination frameworks (SOQE) that underpin forgetting (Doherty et al., 2006; Gabbay et al.).
- Advanced locality-based module extraction and its use in forgetting pipelines for scalability (Grau et al. extensions, Sattler et al.).
- Ontology versioning and difference computation via uniform interpolation (Konev et al., 2014 logical difference work builds directly here).
- Integration patterns from semantic web deployment experience (OWL 2 QL forgetting at scale).
