**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Multi-Physics Coupling in WEDM Plasma–Wire–Kerf System**

World-leading experts master the **coupled magneto-hydrodynamic (MHD) plasma channel dynamics with wire electrode thermo-mechanical response and dielectric two-phase turbulent flushing** at the sub-microsecond timescale, including:

- Self-consistent modeling of plasma column expansion under Lorentz forces, Joule heating, and radiation transport inside the kerf (not just empirical breakdown).
- Transient wire vibration induced by plasma pressure impulses, electrostatic forces, and MHD-induced electromagnetic damping.
- Phase-change-driven recast layer formation coupled with rapid solidification microstructure prediction in aerospace alloys.
- Real-time dielectric fluid hydrodynamics with bubble collapse, debris entrainment, and cavitation effects on flushing efficiency.
- Subsurface residual stress evolution due to steep thermal gradients and martensitic transformations, measured via synchrotron XRD and incremental hole-drilling.
