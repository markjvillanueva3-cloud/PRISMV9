## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Causal Decision Theory, Value of Information, and Economic Mechanisms for Software Quality Interventions**

A world-leading expert next masters the integration of **causal decision theory (CDT)** with **value of information (VOI)** analysis and mechanism design to optimize quality-related decisions under uncertainty in software engineering economics. This layer moves beyond Pearl’s do-calculus and basic real-options thinking into rigorous expected-utility maximization over causal graphs, Bayesian experimental design for quality interventions, and incentive-compatible mechanisms for quality investment.

**Key next bodies of knowledge:**

- **Causal decision theory** applied to software quality trade-offs (evidential vs. causal expected utility in release decisions, technical debt repayment, and process change).
- **Value of Information** calculation on causal models to decide which quality metrics, tests, or experiments are worth collecting (pre-posterior analysis).
- **Mechanism design** and principal-agent theory for aligning quality incentives across development teams, product management, and platform owners.
- **Bayesian decision analysis** fused with structural causal models (SCMs) for software economics.

**Concrete next sources to master:**

- Raiffa, H. & Schlaifer, R. (1961). *Applied Statistical Decision Theory* – foundational VOI framework.
- Howard, R.A. (1966). “Information Value Theory” / IEEE Transactions on Systems Science and Cybernetics.
- Savage, L.J. (1972). *The Foundations of Statistics* (2nd rev. ed.) – sure-thing principle and causal vs. evidential decision theory.
- Parmigiani, G. & Inoue, L. (2009). *Decision Theory: Principles and Approaches* (Wiley) – chapters on preposterior analysis and VOI for design of experiments.
- Laffont, J.J. & Martimort, D. (2002). *The Theory of Incentives: The Principal-Agent Model* (Princeton Univ. Press) – applied to quality effort elicitation.
- MIT OCW 6.8260 (Spring 2023). *Design and Analysis of Experiments* (especially Bayesian optimal design and VOI modules).
- Russell, S. & Norvig, P. (2020). *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (4th ed.), Chapter 16–17 on decision theory and multi-agent mechanisms under causal uncertainty.
- IEEE Std 730-2014. *IEEE Standard for Software Quality Assurance Processes* – risk and economic decision annexes.

These extend Pearl (2009), MIT 6.825/6.826, Boehm/Sullivan, and ISO/IEC 25010 with explicit VOI-driven quality intervention planning and incentive-aware economic quality models.
