## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer a world-leading expert masters is architectural knowledge management (AKM): systematic elicitation, codification, and reuse of tacit design rationale and decisions in evolving software systems. This adds concrete methodology via decision-capture templates, ontology-based representation for query/retrieval, versioning of knowledge artifacts, and evaluation metrics for retention across distributed teams—addressing stickiness at the codebase and architecture boundary levels.

It supplies institutional depth through ISO/IEC standards mandating rationale documentation, SEI-derived practices for knowledge infrastructures in CMMI-aligned organizations, and empirical SE conference results on tool-supported externalization. These extend general KM theory into software-specific mechanisms that prevent tribal knowledge evaporation during refactoring, onboarding, and platform evolution.
