**Next Layer: Nonlinear Dynamics, Subcritical Bifurcations, and Stochastic Thermo-Mechanical Stability in Turning**

World-leading expertise advances to **global nonlinear stability analysis** of turning under combined state-dependent delay, thermomechanical coupling, and stochastic parameter variation. This includes mapping **unsafe zones** generated by subcritical Hopf bifurcations, where stable cutting regions are surrounded by bistable unsafe pockets reachable by finite disturbances.

Key topics at this layer:
- Numerical continuation methods for direct tracking of subcritical Hopf branches and associated limit-cycle stability in DDEs with state-dependent delay.
- Bit excursion maps and their experimental construction to quantify safe operating envelopes beyond linear stability lobes.
- Stochastic bifurcation analysis using Gaussian-process surrogate models calibrated by in-process metrology under combined thermal and force uncertainty.
- Nonlinear time-periodic and quasi-periodic solutions in thermomechanically coupled turning systems.
- Advanced process-damping models that incorporate flank-wave contact geometry, ploughing, and velocity-dependent friction under varying rake and clearance angles.

This layer integrates **nonlinear dynamics theory** with **precision metrology** for closed-loop identification of unsafe zones, moving beyond linear or semi-discretization stability prediction.
