## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER:** Hierarchical Bayesian extensions of Knowledge Tracing, deep sequential models beyond DKT (including attention and graph-based variants), and formal computational rationality frameworks for optimal instructional planning.

World-leading expertise requires mastery of: (1) integrative hierarchical models that jointly infer student ability, skill discovery, and forgetting dynamics using fully Bayesian nonparametric approaches; (2) transformer-based and graph neural network architectures for modeling complex exercise–concept dependencies and temporal forgetting; (3) computational rationality / bounded optimality theories that treat teaching as sequential decision-making under uncertainty, linking directly to POMDP planning but at a deeper formal level; and (4) standardized evaluation methodologies using log-likelihood per opportunity, AUC on next-response prediction, and knowledge-component-level learning curves.

Key next sources include:

- **Hierarchical Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (HBKT)** formulations that place hierarchical priors over individual learning rates and initial knowledge.
- **Deep Knowledge Tracing** extensions using self-attention (SAKT, AKT) and graph-based propagation (GKT, GraphKT).
- **Optimal Teaching as Bayesian Experimental Design** and rational teaching models grounded in cognitive science.
- **Cognitive Model Discovery via Bayesian Model Comparison** using DIC, WAIC, and posterior predictive checks.

These layers move beyond classic BKT/PFA/AFM and the already-covered 2016-era deep KT critiques, into production-grade learner modeling used in modern adaptive learning systems.
