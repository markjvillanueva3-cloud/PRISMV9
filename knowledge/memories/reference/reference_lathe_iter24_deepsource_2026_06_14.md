**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Spindle-Bearing Thermo-Elastic Metrology, Nonlinear Contact Dynamics, and Process-Synchronized Error Mapping**

World-leading lathe experts next master frequency-domain thermo-elastic distortion modeling of spindle-bearing systems under simultaneous centrifugal, thermal preload, and cutting-force-induced loads, including non-stationary error motion trajectories synchronized to workpiece rotation. This layer emphasizes multi-physics simulation of bearing hysteresis, grease migration, and nonlinear Hertzian contact stiffness variation with speed and temperature, together with in-situ dynamic compliance mapping traceable to ISO standards.

Key un-covered topics include:
- Thermo-hydrodynamic lubrication analysis of angular contact ball bearings with thermal preload and centrifugal ball expansion (Harris & Kotzalas already cited; next layer extends to transient FE coupling).
- Nonlinear bearing stiffness identification via modal testing under operational thermal gradients and axial preload variation.
- Spindle metrology using capacitive or laser interferometric sensors under combined thermal-mechanical loading, with uncertainty propagation per GUM.
- Kalman-filter and observer-based real-time estimation of time-varying thermal error maps linked to cutting-force direction in turning.
- Stability lobe diagram reconstruction accounting for speed-dependent bearing stiffness and process damping in precision turning.
