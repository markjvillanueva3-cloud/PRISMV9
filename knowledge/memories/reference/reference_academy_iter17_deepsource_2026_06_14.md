## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Ontology-Driven Architectural Knowledge Graph Engineering and Pedagogical Integration**

A world-leading expert next masters the systematic engineering of **architectural knowledge graphs (AKGs)** as formal, queryable, and evolvable ontologies that link ATAM outputs (scenarios, trade-off points, sensitivity points, risks) directly into OWL-based reasoning structures for continuous learning and curriculum orchestration.

Core topics include:
- OWL 2 DL modeling patterns for architectural decision capture, including reification of decisions as first-class individuals, property chains for “affectsQualityAttribute” and “mitigatesRisk” relations, and SWRL rules for automated consistency checking of architectural trade-offs.
- Construction of AKG-centric learning paths using 4C/ID task classes mapped to ontology individuals, enabling adaptive retrieval of pedagogical ADR fragments.
- Use of SHACL for validation of AKG completeness against ISO/IEC 42010 conformance and ACM/IEEE CS2023 architectural knowledge units.
- Integration of SPARQL-based analytics for measuring knowledge diffusion across a CS curriculum represented as a knowledge graph.

This layer moves beyond capture and basic representation into executable semantic models that support automated reasoning, adaptive learning, and accreditation-level traceability.
