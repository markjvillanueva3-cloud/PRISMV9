A world-leading expert next masters the integration of **software architecture evaluation** with **quantitative process simulation** and **empirical quality measurement frameworks** that treat quality as a dynamic, feedback-driven property of large-scale systems. This layer emphasizes formal models for quality attribute trade-off analysis, statistical process control applied to software metrics, Bayesian belief networks for quality prediction, and controlled experimentation in software engineering.

Key areas include:
- **Architecture-centric quality evaluation** using utility trees, scenario-based analysis, and quantitative impact assessment of architectural tactics on quality attributes (availability, modifiability, performance).
- **Software process simulation and calibration** using system dynamics models tuned against empirical project data, including stochastic extensions and sensitivity analysis.
- **Empirical software engineering methods** for building and validating quality models through families of experiments, meta-analysis, and replication protocols.
- **Measurement theory applied to software quality** (representation theory, scale types, meaningfulness of metrics).
- **Value-based monitoring** of quality requirements and technical debt using real-options and multi-criteria decision analysis.
