**Next Layer: Formal Verification, High-Probability Guarantees under Heavy-Tails & Non-Uniform Smoothness, plus Implicit Bias in Over-Parameterized Non-Convex Settings**

A world-leading expert in AI-training next masters **formalized mechanized proofs** of optimizer correctness (beyond basic GD), **high-probability convergence theory** that replaces expectation bounds with explicit tail probabilities under infinite variance and non-uniform smoothness, **stability-based generalization** for adaptive methods with dependent noise, and **phase-space analysis** linking edge-of-stability dynamics to implicit bias and grokking in non-convex neural networks.

Key uncovered areas include:
- Mechanized verification of adaptive optimizers (Adam, SAM) in proof assistants using floating-point semantics and stochastic oracles.
- Refined high-probability bounds via truncated moments, robust clipping, and Lyapunov analysis that avoid moment explosion.
- PAC-Bayes and algorithmic stability theory extended to sharpness-aware minimization and heavy-tailed SGD with Markovian dependence.
- Dynamical systems view of EoS: progressive sharpening, catapults, and convergence to flat minima without explicit regularization.

**Concrete next sources:**
- Formal verification of Adam and SAM variants in Lean 4 / Isabelle with full floating-point models.
- Non-asymptotic high-probability convergence of clipped Adam under (L0,L1)-smoothness and α-stable noise.
- Stability and PAC-Bayes bounds for SAM under non-uniform smoothness.
- Implicit bias and edge-of-stability phenomena in non-linear networks via mean-field / neural tangent kernel limits with heavy tails.

These layers close the gap between theoretical guarantees published 2022–2024 and fully machine-checked proofs usable in safety-critical AI-training pipelines.
