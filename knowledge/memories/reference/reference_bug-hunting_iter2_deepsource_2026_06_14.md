**NEXT LAYER: SAT/SMT-based Bug Hunting, Decision Procedures, and Industrial-Scale Formal Verification Methodologies**

A world-leading bug-hunting expert next masters the theory and practice of modern SMT solvers, decidable fragments, Craig interpolation, and abstraction-refinement loops that power industrial formal verification beyond basic bounded model checking.

Key areas include:
- DPLL(T) architecture and theory of uninterpreted functions, linear arithmetic, bit-vectors, and arrays.
- Lazy and eager SMT solving, quantifier instantiation heuristics, and model-based combination.
- Interpolation-based model checking (IMPACT, IC3/PDR) and property-directed reachability for infinite-state bug hunting.
- CEGAR (Counterexample-Guided Abstraction Refinement) with predicate abstraction.
- Formal verification standards and methodology used in safety-critical and high-assurance software.

These techniques enable scalable detection of deep semantic bugs that symbolic execution and classic model checking cannot practically reach.
