## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Institutional Antitrust Enforcement, Platform Design Remedies, and Algorithmic Governance under Live Regulatory Regimes**

A world-leading expert next masters the concrete institutional mechanisms that translate theoretical information design and mechanism-design results into enforceable remedies, consent decrees, behavioral remedies, and ongoing compliance monitoring in multi-sided platform markets. This layer focuses on how antitrust authorities and courts operationalize information-design insights (transparency rules, API access mandates, data portability, algorithmic auditing) inside real-world institutional constraints.

Key uncovered areas include:
- Structural separation vs. behavioral remedies in platform gatekeeper cases
- Design and enforcement of “fair, reasonable, and non-discriminatory” (FRAND) access obligations for essential platform infrastructure
- Algorithmic collusion detection protocols and the evidentiary use of simulated bidding/price-tagging experiments in court
- Mandatory transparency and explainability requirements under DMA Article 15 and emerging U.S. state laws
- Institutional economics of regulatory sandboxes and ex-ante vs. ex-post platform governance

- **OECD (2022)** – “Ex-ante versus ex-post regulation of digital markets – lessons from the DMA and P2B Regulation”
