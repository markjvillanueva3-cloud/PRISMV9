## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer integrates quality attribute characterization with scenario-based elicitation, architectural tactics, and utility-tree prioritization to quantify tradeoffs in design decisions. This extends value-based SE and ATAM by applying Markov models for analytic evaluation of qualities (e.g., availability, security) and tactics catalogs for modifiability/performance. It adds test-adequacy criteria hierarchies, logic coverage, mutation analysis, and input-domain partitioning to verification/validation processes. Process institutionalization via staged maturity frameworks complements ISO 25010 and 12207 by defining measurable practice areas for quality management. Updated testing process standards provide concrete techniques and ontologies absent from prior reliability/metrics coverage.

These elements enable rigorous, decision-theoretic quality engineering at scale, linking causal models to testable architectural claims.
