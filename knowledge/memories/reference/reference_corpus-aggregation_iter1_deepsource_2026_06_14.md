## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Advanced Corpus Aggregation Theory & Systems**

A world-leading expert in corpus-aggregation next masters the architectural and algorithmic foundations of large-scale, heterogeneous data ingestion, deduplication, schema reconciliation, and quality-driven aggregation pipelines as treated in distributed systems and data management literature.

Key topics include:
- **Entity resolution and deduplication at corpus scale** using probabilistic record linkage, collective entity resolution, and blocking techniques (Getoor & Machanavajjhala, 2012).
- **Data integration and schema mapping** via Bayesian models, crowdsourcing, and knowledge-graph construction (Dong & Srivastava, 2015).
- **Incremental and streaming aggregation** architectures that maintain consistency and provenance under continuous corpus ingestion (Franklin et al., 2005; Stonebraker et al., 2014).
- **Quality-aware aggregation**, utility-based data cleaning, and truth discovery algorithms (Li et al., 2016; Rekatsinas et al., 2017).
- **Distributed pipeline orchestration** using dataflow models (e.g., Naiad, Spark Streaming, Apache Beam semantics) and formal lineage/provenance tracking (Foster et al., 2008; Amsterdamer et al., 2011).

These build on core database theory (Codd, relational algebra extensions) and modern big-data systems while emphasizing formal correctness, scalability, and measurable corpus quality — exactly the layer beyond basic ETL or simple warehousing.
