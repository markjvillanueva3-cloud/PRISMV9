**Next Layer: Advanced PDF Corpus Engineering & Document Intelligence**

A world-leading expert in pdf-corpus must next master the formal theory and engineering of PDF as a structured document interchange and corpus format, moving beyond core ISO specs into layout-aware parsing, semantic reconstruction, corpus-scale indexing, and reproducible extraction pipelines.

Key uncovered areas include:
- PDF’s graphics and content stream model as a PostScript-derived imaging model, including marked content, structure tree mapping, and tagged PDF artifact handling (ISO 32000-2:2020 Annexes are only entry point).
- Advanced document layout analysis taxonomies and evaluation metrics (reading order, logical structure recovery, zone segmentation).
- Vector-space and graph-based representations for PDF corpora, including inverted indexing of text, vector, and raster elements.
- Reproducible, deterministic PDF-to-XML/HTML conversion pipelines that preserve semantic fidelity at corpus scale.
- Multimodal corpus construction combining PDF content streams with rendered visual features for deep learning.
