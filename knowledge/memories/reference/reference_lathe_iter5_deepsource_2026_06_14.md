## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Advanced Nonlinear, Multiphysics, and Metrology-Driven Turning Stability & Process Modeling**

World-leading expertise advances into state-dependent delay models with 3D chip-flow geometry, nonlinear process damping from flank contact, thermo-mechanical coupling in high-speed turning, and uncertainty quantification using Bayesian inference on in-process metrology. Key topics include stability of systems with state-dependent time delays due to tool nose radius and variable chip thickness, stochastic chatter prediction under process variability, integrated force-thermal-vibration models for precision hard turning, and digital-twin calibration using high-bandwidth force and vibration data fused with NIST-traceable dynamic metrology.

**Core un-covered domains:**
- Chebyshev collocation and full-discretization methods for state-dependent DDEs in turning (beyond semi-discretization).
- Nonlinear regenerative chatter with velocity-dependent friction and ploughing forces at low chip thicknesses.
- Coupled thermo-mechanical stability analysis accounting for temperature-dependent material behavior and thermal expansion in the cutting zone.
- Bayesian uncertainty propagation and Gaussian Process Regression calibrated on operational modal analysis and spindle metrology data.
- In-process dynamic compliance identification and stability lobe updating using real-time sensor fusion under ASME and ISO performance protocols.

These build directly on prior linear stability, time-domain simulation, and basic virtual turning systems.
