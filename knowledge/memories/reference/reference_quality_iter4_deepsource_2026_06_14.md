## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Quality as a Measurable, Architecturally-Governed, Socio-Technical Feedback System**

A world-leading expert advances from foundational metrics, reliability models, and technical debt management into **formal verification of quality attributes**, **statistically rigorous longitudinal quality analytics**, and **organizational feedback control theory applied to continuous quality**.

Key next-layer knowledge includes:

- **Quantitative architecture evaluation and quality attribute reasoning**: Using utility trees, scenarios, and tactics-based evaluation beyond ADD (Bass et al., 2003; Clements et al., 2010).
- **Software quality economics and cost-of-quality models** grounded in empirical data (Boehm & Sullivan, 2000; Huang & Boehm, 2005).
- **Bayesian statistical methods for software reliability growth and quality forecasting** (Singpurwalla, 2006; Ruggeri et al., 2007).
- **Formal methods for quality specification and verification**: Alloy, TLA+, and model checking for quality properties (Jackson, 2012 extension; Lamport, 2019; Newcombe et al., 2015).
- **Process simulation and system dynamics modeling of quality behavior** at scale (Abdel-Hamid & Madnick, 1991; Raffo & Kellner, 1999).
- **ISO/IEC 25010:2011 quality model in depth** (product, quality-in-use, data) and its integration with **ISO/IEC 25023 measurement primitives**.
- **Empirical software engineering measurement theory** including Goal-Question-Metric + Measurement & Analysis (Basili et al., 1994; McGarry et al., 2001; CMU SEI MA course).

These represent the rigorous, institutionally anchored layer that integrates quality as a controllable, verifiable, economically optimized socio-technical system.
