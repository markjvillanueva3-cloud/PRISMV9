## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Algorithmic, Implementation, and Modular Aspects of Forgetting and Uniform Interpolation**

A world-leading expert next masters the algorithmic realization, optimized implementation techniques, modularity theorems, and practical tool architectures that enable scalable forgetting in expressive DLs beyond the foundational complexity and model-theoretic results already covered.

Key topics include:
- **Ackermann-style algorithms** and their generalizations to Horn and Horn-like fragments, including polynomial-time computability for EL and efficient second-order quantifier elimination.
- **Forgetting-based module extraction** and locality-based techniques for ontology modularization.
- **Practical optimization** via fixpoint iteration, symbol ordering heuristics, and interpolation templates.
- **Tool engineering** aspects: saturation-based vs. resolution-based forgetting engines, caching, and integration into OWL reasoners.
- **Uniform interpolation in lightweight profiles** (EL, DL-Lite, OWL 2 QL) with emphasis on query rewriting and datalog rewritability preservation.

This layer bridges theory to deployable systems, focusing on termination guarantees, size control of interpolants, and preservation of entailments under modular reuse—core to large-scale ontology evolution in knowledge representation platforms.
