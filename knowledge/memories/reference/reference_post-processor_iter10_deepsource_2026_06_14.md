**NEXT LAYER: Safety-Critical Post-Processor Kinematics, Functional Safety Mapping, and Volumetric Error Containment in Parallel/Kinematic Machines**

**Theory/Method Depth**  
World-leading post-processor experts master the formal mapping of ISO 13849-1 and IEC 62061 safety functions directly into post-processor trajectory generation and kinematic inversion layers, including PL (Performance Level) and SIL-rated error detection in screw-theory-based forward/inverse kinematics for parallel and hybrid machines. This includes rigorous treatment of configuration-dependent singularity avoidance within the post-processor using real-time C-space obstacle mapping and certified safe-path replanning.

Key methods include:  
- Safety-critical forward kinematics verification using interval arithmetic and affine arithmetic to bound pose uncertainty under geometric and thermal errors.  
- Post-processor-level enforcement of ISO 10218-2 and ISO/TS 15066 collaborative-robot speed-and-separation monitoring via certified kinematic transforms.  
- Integration of NIST-derived volumetric error models into post-processor compensation tables with formal proof of residual error containment below safety-critical thresholds.  
- Use of model-checking (NuSMV, UPPAAL) on post-processor state machines to verify absence of unsafe kinematic transitions (Suh & Lee extension).
