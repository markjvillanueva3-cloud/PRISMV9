Key uncovered knowledge includes:
- Theory of **Bayesian sequential design for nonlinear models** using expected utility of information with Markov chain Monte Carlo sampling of posterior predictive distributions (Müller et al. extensions).
- **Index policies for restless bandits** with Bayesian belief-state updates applied to dynamic quoting of software features under evolving uncertainty.
- **Information relaxation bounds** and **approximate dynamic programming (ADP)** techniques that convert perfect-information relaxations into tight VOI lookahead approximations suitable for real-time quoting engines.
