Key uncovered areas include:

- **Bayesian calibration and hierarchical modeling** of software cost drivers beyond basic COCOMO posterior updating (Boehm et al., 2000 is already covered; deeper treatment required).

- **Real options analysis (ROA)** applied to software project quoting under uncertainty, treating requirements and architectural decisions as financial options.
- **Institutional estimation maturity models** and organizational quoting process standards, including governance, auditability, and independent cost estimation (ICE) protocols.
