## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Automated Bug-Hunting via Decision Procedures, Craig Interpolation, and Software Model Checking with IC3/PDR**

A world-leading bug-hunting expert next masters the integration of **interpolant-based model checking**, **property-directed reachability (PDR/IC3)**, and **SMT-based invariant synthesis** as practical scalable alternatives to classical bounded model checking and abstract interpretation. This layer emphasizes algorithmic advances that avoid explicit state explosion through incremental SAT/SMT solving, over-approximation via interpolants, and syntax-guided synthesis of inductive invariants.

Key topics include:
- Craig interpolation in model checking (McMillan, 2003)
- The IC3/PDR algorithm family and its generalizations to infinite-state systems
- Syntax-guided synthesis (SyGuS) and its use in automated invariant generation
- Combination of k-induction with PDR
- Counterexample-guided abstraction refinement (CEGAR) with SMT solvers

These techniques form the core of modern industrial bug-hunting toolchains (e.g., extensions of CBMC, CPAchecker, and commercial formal apps).
