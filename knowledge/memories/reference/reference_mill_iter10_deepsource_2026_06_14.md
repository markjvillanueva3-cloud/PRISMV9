**Next Layer: Stochastic & Metrological Stability in Milling under Uncertainty**

A world-leading expert next masters stochastic time-delay differential equation (SDDE) formulations that incorporate both parametric uncertainty (tool dynamics, material heterogeneity) and additive process noise, moving beyond deterministic semi-discretization and spectral element methods. This includes Floquet-theory extensions to stochastic systems, moment-based stability criteria, and Lyapunov exponents for milling SDDEs.

Core topics include:
- Stochastic averaging and moment equations for milling with uncertain modal parameters and random cutting-force coefficients.
- Bayesian experimental design and uncertainty propagation pipelines that fuse in-process vibration data with prior stability lobe models.
- Metrological traceability of dynamic compliance and process-damping identification per ISO and ASME standards, including dynamic uncertainty budgets for frequency response function (FRF) measurements.
- NIST-recommended Monte Carlo and polynomial chaos approaches for stability boundary uncertainty quantification traceable to GUM and ISO 230-10.
- Advanced nonlinear process damping models identified via stochastic subspace identification and Gaussian-process regression under varying immersion and speed.

These build directly on covered deterministic DDE/SDM work and NIST uncertainty notes by treating stability as a probabilistic reliability problem suitable for safety-critical aerospace and medical milling.
