**NEXT LAYER: Memory-Efficient & Systems-Optimized Training at Scale (Iteration 2)**

A world-leading expert in ai-training next masters the theory and practice of **rematerialization, mixed-precision training dynamics, distributed optimizer states, and compilation-aware training pipelines** beyond the already-covered checkpointing and FlashAttention baselines.

Key uncovered areas include:

- **Optimal rematerialization algorithms** (static and dynamic) under memory budgets, using integer linear programming and dynamic programming formulations (Chen et al. already covered is only the 2016 baseline).
- **BFloat16/FP8 numerics** and their impact on convergence, gradient scaling strategies, and stochastic rounding theory.
- **Sharded optimizer states and hybrid parallelism schedulers** (3D parallelism, ZeRO-Infinity extensions, FSDP).
- **ML compilers for training**: operator fusion, kernel synthesis, and automatic differentiation graph optimization at the XLA/TorchDynamo level.
- **Training-aware model compression** co-optimized with quantization and sparsity during the forward-backward pass.

**Institutional depth sources** (post-2022 emphasis):
