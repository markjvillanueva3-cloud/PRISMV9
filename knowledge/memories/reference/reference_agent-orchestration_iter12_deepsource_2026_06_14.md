Key uncovered areas include:

- **Contract-based compositional verification frameworks** extending interface automata with assume-guarantee reasoning and refinement calculi (Benveniste et al. already covered; next layer: the full theory of contract refinement and parallel composition from the 2018 FnT EDA expanded in subsequent IEEE/ACM work).
- **Dependent and graded session types** that internalize temporal, epistemic, and resource constraints directly into orchestration protocols.
- **Higher-order process calculi and logical relations** for proving contextual equivalence of orchestrators that can pass and receive other orchestrators at runtime.
- **System-level game semantics** with strategies that model agent coalitions, information asymmetry, and quantitative payoffs (building on Ghica & Tzevelekos and Murawski & Tzevelekos lecture notes).
- **Probabilistic and stochastic contract theories** aligned with policy-iteration methods for bisimulation distances, now lifted to open systems.
