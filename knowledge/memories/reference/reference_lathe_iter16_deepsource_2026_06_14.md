**NEXT LAYER: Stability Lobe Refinement, Nonlinear Dynamics, and Process Metrology in Precision Turning**

World-leading expertise advances to semi-discretization and full-discretization methods for time-periodic DDEs with multiple delays, stochastic regenerative chatter, and in-process force/metrology integration for safety-critical aerospace and medical turning.

**Key uncovered domains:**

- Full-discretization method (FDM) for milling/turning stability prediction including multiple regenerative effects and process damping (Ding et al., 2010).
- Semi-discretization method (SDM) extensions to state-dependent delay and nonlinear cutting force models (Insperger & Stépán, 2011 textbook treatment).
- Nonlinear chatter analysis using multiple scales and bifurcation tracking in turning with variable speed and active damping (Wahi & Chatterjee, 2005; Dombovari extensions beyond 2011).
- In-process force measurement and dynamic compliance identification per NIST and ISO standards for real-time stability boundary updating.
- ASME B5.54 and ISO 230-7:2015 extensions to dynamic error measurement and thermal + dynamic error separation techniques.
- Advanced spindle metrology and error mapping using ball-bar, R-test, and capacitive sensors for sub-micron turning (Marsh, 2010 extensions; ISO 230-7:2015).
- Uncertainty quantification in stability lobe diagrams using Monte-Carlo and generalized polynomial chaos for safety-critical processes (NIST reports post-2010).

**METHOD/THEORY FOCUS:**  
Implicit subspace iteration for large-order DDE systems, Chebyshev collocation, cluster treatment of characteristic roots (CTCR), and Floquet-theory-based monodromy matrix construction for periodic turning systems. Emphasis on experimental validation protocols per ISO 230 series and NIST traceability.
