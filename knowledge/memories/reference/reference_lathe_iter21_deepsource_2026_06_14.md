## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Nonlinear Thermo-Mechanical Spindle-Bearing Interaction and Process-Driven Stability under Transient Thermal Gradients**

World-leading expertise advances to coupled nonlinear bearing preload migration, thermo-elastic spindle growth, and their direct influence on time-varying dynamic stiffness and process damping during long-duration turning. This includes:

- **Thermo-hydrodynamic bearing models** that capture viscosity-temperature dependence, centrifugal expansion, and non-Newtonian lubricant behavior under high-speed transient thermal loads.
- **Nonlinear time-variant stability solutions** using Floquet theory or semi-discretization on systems with periodically modulated stiffness and damping induced by thermal preload drift.
- **In-process spindle metrology** combining capacitive/ interferometric error motion measurement with real-time Kalman-filter or observer-based thermal error separation under active cutting heat flux.
- **Multi-physics finite-element reduced-order modeling** of spindle-bearing-housing assemblies that includes localized heat generation at ball-race contacts, heat partition into the chip/workpiece/tool, and feedback on cutting process stability lobes.
- **Standards-compliant transient thermal testing protocols** beyond ISO/TR 230-11, specifically addressing asynchronous error motion and thermal preload drift under combined external and internal heat sources.

This layer integrates manufacturing-physics coupling between the cutting process, bearing tribology, and structural dynamics at a depth required for predictive digital twins in safety-critical applications.
