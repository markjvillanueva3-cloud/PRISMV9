## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next mastery layer in tribal-knowledge focuses on knowledge stickiness, exploration-exploitation tradeoffs, transactive memory systems, and transfer impediments in distributed information systems and software organizations. This adds methodological depth via social network analysis for knowledge flows, knowledge audits, and diachronic case studies of retention/loss in digital infrastructures—extending prior coverage of boundary objects, CoPs, sensemaking, and absorptive capacity. It addresses how informal collective know-how persists or erodes without direct participation, informing design of experiential computing platforms.

Institutional emphasis includes KM maturity assessment frameworks and systems that reduce causal ambiguity in reuse. Theory draws from network-based search/transfer models and collective mind constructs suited to engineering teams.
