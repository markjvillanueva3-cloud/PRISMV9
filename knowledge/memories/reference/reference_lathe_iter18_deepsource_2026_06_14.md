**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Thermo-Mechanical Spindle-Bearing-Housing Interaction and In-Process Metrology for Stability & Error Mapping**

World-leading lathe experts master coupled thermo-elastic spindle-bearing dynamics under simultaneous thermal and cutting loads, including non-linear bearing preload variation, grease behavior, and housing compliance effects on both thermal error and chatter stability. This layer integrates:

- Thermo-mechanical bearing models that couple Hertzian contact, thermal preload, and viscous friction torque (Harris & Kotzalas, 2006; Stein & Tu, 1992).
- Finite-element reduced-order modeling of spindle-housing heat transfer and elastic deformation using modal condensation and experimental modal analysis (EMA) under operating temperatures (NIST, 2014).
- In-process spindle error motion and thermal drift measurement using non-contact capacitive or optical sensors with real-time FFT and synchronous/asynchronous error separation per ASME B89.3.4 and ISO 230-7.
- Stability prediction that incorporates measured speed- and temperature-dependent FRF variation caused by bearing stiffness change (dynamic stiffness matrix updating).
- Uncertainty propagation frameworks linking thermal model parameters, sensor noise, and compensation residual to final part tolerance using Monte-Carlo and GUM methods.

This knowledge is drawn from graduate precision engineering sequences and standards bodies, extending beyond previously covered MIT OCW 2.72/2.71, NIST TN 1677/1809/1822, Mayr et al. (2012), and Altintas (2012).
