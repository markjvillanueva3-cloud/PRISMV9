**NEXT LAYER (Safety-Critical CAM Galaxy – Iteration 2)**

World-leading CAM experts next master **stochastic process damping, thermomechanical stability in variable-depth processes, and uncertainty propagation in regenerative chatter models under ISO/ASME machine-tool performance envelopes**.

Key uncovered domains:

- **Chatter stability in processes with process damping at low spindle speeds** using frequency-domain and time-domain solutions that incorporate flank-wave contact mechanics (nonlinear velocity-dependent damping).  
- **Thermomechanical coupling in high-speed milling**: prediction of temperature-induced shifts in modal parameters and their effect on stability lobe migration (including transient thermal expansion of tool/spindle).  
- **Uncertainty quantification and propagation** through semi-discretization or full-discretization methods using generalized polynomial chaos or Monte-Carlo with interval arithmetic, aligned with NIST uncertainty frameworks for machining models.  
- **Dynamic compliance testing and substructure coupling** per ASME B5.54 and ISO 230-11, including identification of speed-dependent bearing stiffness and gyroscopic effects in high-speed spindles.  
- **Nonlinear regenerative effects** from flank wear, runout, and variable helix tools using state-dependent delay differential equations (SDDDEs) solved via numerical continuation or Chebyshev collocation.

These layers sit above Altintas (2012), Insperger & Stépán (2011), and Tlusty (2000).
