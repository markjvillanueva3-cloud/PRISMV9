Key uncovered domains:

- **Molinari-type hyperelastic-viscoplastic flow rules** coupled to **Johnson-Cook-type** thermal softening with explicit regularization of adiabatic shear bands via gradient or nonlocal terms (required for reliable high-speed stability maps when process damping vanishes).
- **Distributed state-dependent delay models** with both **cutting-force** and **flank-contact** contributions formulated as neutral delay differential equations (NDDEs) or implicit renewal-type DDEs; numerical treatment via spectral element or pseudospectral collocation on variable meshes.
- **Nonlinear thermo-mechanical regenerative chatter** including **temperature-dependent friction** on rake and flank, solved via **semi-discretization in augmented thermal state space**.
- **Polynomial-chaos and stochastic Galerkin** propagation of **aleatoric uncertainty** in thermal properties, friction coefficients, and damping through Hopf bifurcations, producing probability density of stability boundaries.
- **ISO/ASME-compliant metrology chains** for in-process chatter detection: laser vibrometry, capacitive displacement, and triaxial force dynamometry traceable to ISO 230-11:2023 and ASME B5.54, including uncertainty budgets for phase-sensitive stability lobe validation.
- **NIST uncertainty quantification frameworks** extending beyond TN 2302 to **Bayesian experimental updating** of thermo-mechanical model parameters using full-field infrared thermography and high-speed imaging of shear bands.

These close the gap between deterministic nonlinear DDE theory and industrial, traceable, safety-critical stability prediction under thermal and stochastic variability.
