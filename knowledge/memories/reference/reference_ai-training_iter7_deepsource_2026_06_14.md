**NEXT LAYER: Heavy-Tailed Noise, Non-asymptotic Analysis, and Formalized Optimization Theory**

A world-leading expert next masters non-asymptotic convergence theory under heavy-tailed noise, stability of adaptive optimizers, and mechanized verification of optimization algorithms in proof assistants.

Key topics include:
- High-probability convergence bounds for SGD under α-stable Lévy noise and fractional-order moments (instead of variance).
- Edge-of-stability phenomena analyzed via implicit bias and Hessian eigenvalue dynamics beyond the quadratic regime.
- Formal semantics and mechanized proofs (Coq/Lean) of distributed SGD variants, including Byzantine resilience and momentum methods.
- Lyapunov-function constructions and contraction analysis for adaptive methods (Adam, RMSprop) with biased second-moment estimates.
- Algorithmic differentiation correctness at the level of operational semantics and differentiable programming logics.
