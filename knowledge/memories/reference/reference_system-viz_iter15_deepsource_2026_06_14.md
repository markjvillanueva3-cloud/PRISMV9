**Next Layer: Cognitive and Semiotic Foundations for System Visualization Design**

A world-leading system-viz expert next masters **semiotic engineering** and **cognitive ergonomics of visual languages** at the level of formal notation semantics, perceptual organization laws, and stakeholder-centric viewpoint ontologies. This layer moves beyond perceptual psychology and basic cognitive dimensions into rigorous theories of how visual notations function as **computational artifacts** that externalize system structure, behavior, and qualities.

Key uncovered areas include:
- **Algebraic Semiotics** extensions and **sign-type** hierarchies for complex system diagrams (beyond Goguen 1999).
- **Visual Language Theory** (VL Theory) and its formal grammar, syntax, and semantics for diagrammatic notations.
- **Cognitive Integration** frameworks that explain how multiple coordinated views reduce cognitive load in architectural and runtime system visualizations.
- Application of **Gestalt principles** refined through modern perceptual grouping research and their mapping to software engineering diagram conventions.
- **Notation Design Methodologies** that integrate ISO/IEC 25010 quality characteristics with visual notation quality metrics (e.g., Moody’s Physics extended with empirical validation suites).

**Concrete next sources** (post-2012 emphasis where possible, avoiding all already-covered works):

- **Purchase (2014)** “The Cognitive Dimensions of Information Visualizations” – expands CD framework specifically for software visualization evaluation.
- **Larkin & Simon (1987)** “Why a Diagram is (Sometimes) Worth Ten Thousand Words” – classic but still foundational for computational efficiency of diagrams in system reasoning (MIT AI Lab lineage).
- **Petre (2016)** “Cognitive Dimensions of Notations: 20 Years On” – reflective synthesis and new empirical directions (ACM Computing Surveys).

These provide the institutional, theoretical, and standards-based depth required for designing system-viz notations that are cognitively efficient, semantically sound, and quality-aligned.
