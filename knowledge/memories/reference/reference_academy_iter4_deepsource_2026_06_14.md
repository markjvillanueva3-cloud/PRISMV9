## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Mastery of Pedagogical Content Knowledge (PCK) Integration, Curriculum Architecture Patterns, and Evidence-Based Learning Progression Design in Software Engineering Education**

A world-leading expert next masters the **curriculum-as-architecture** paradigm, treating degree programs and course sequences as first-class software systems amenable to architectural description, evaluation, and evolution. This includes applying ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010 concepts to learning architectures (views, viewpoints, concerns, and rationale for curriculum stakeholders) while integrating refined PCK frameworks specific to SE.

Core next-layer knowledge includes:
- **Learning Progressions** in computing that map fine-grained SE competency trajectories (e.g., from novice requirements modeling to expert architectural trade-off analysis).
- **Evidence-Centered Design (ECD)** and **Assessment for Learning** models adapted from CS/SE contexts.
- **PCK for Software Architecture and Design**, including how expert instructors represent, transform, and address student misconceptions in system decomposition, quality attribute trade-offs, and architectural tactics.
- Use of **Big Ideas in Computing Education** and associated threshold concepts for SE.
- **Curriculum design patterns** beyond Hazzan et al., focusing on spiral curriculum, integrative capstone progressions, and backward design at program scale.
