## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Platform Scaling, Monetization Architecture, and Ecosystem Governance (Iteration 3)**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s business galaxy next masters advanced platform pricing architecture, multi-sided network effects measurement, ecosystem health metrics, and defensive strategies against platform envelopment at enterprise scale. This layer moves beyond introductory two-sided market strategies and basic SaaS metrics into rigorous, quantitative governance and growth models drawn from economics, strategy, and software-industry literature.

Core topics include:
- **Value-based pricing and versioning** for complex B2B SaaS platforms (Shapiro & Varian, 1998).
- **Openness vs. control tradeoffs** and platform governance mechanisms that balance generativity with quality (Tiwana, 2014; Parker et al., 2017).
- **Ecosystem orchestration** and keystone/niche player dynamics at scale (Iansiti & Levien, 2004 – already covered; extended in Williamson & De Meyer, 2012).
- **Quantitative network effects measurement**, including same-side and cross-side elasticity estimation (Rochet & Tirole, 2006; Rysman, 2009).
- **Defensive strategies** against disintermediation and multi-homing warfare in enterprise software (Hagiu & Wright, 2020; Zhu & Iansiti, 2019).
- **Platform maturity models** and transition from early-stage to mature ecosystem governance (Cusumano et al., 2019 – extended in 2021 update).

These concepts are typically taught in:
- MIT Sloan 15.232 *Business Model Innovation* (2021–2023)
- Stanford MS&E 238 *Leading Trends in Platform and Ecosystem Innovation* (2022)
- Harvard Business School elective *Competing in the Age of Platforms* (course ID: 9-623-055, 2023)
