**Next Layer: Clock Calculus, Causality Analysis, and Denotational Semantics for Synchronous Languages with Dependent and Graded Types**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s wiring galaxy next masters the rigorous clock calculus extensions that integrate dependent types, quantitative resource reasoning, and ultrametric denotations to guarantee causality, bounded memory, and schedulability in reactive dataflow graphs.

Key topics include:
- **Higher-order clock calculus** and its polymorphic generalization (clock polymorphism and subclocking relations)
- **Causality loops in hybrid synchronous models** solved via type-based rejection using contractiveness in ultrametric spaces
- **Graded modal types** for quantitative reasoning about reactivity, latency, and buffer sizes
- **Fitch-style modal calculi** (⊳ modality) for reactive programming with explicit delay
- **Sheaf-theoretic and topos-theoretic semantics** that unify synchronous streams with guarded recursion
- **Verified compilation pipelines** that propagate clock information through polyhedral transformations to imperative C/VHDL

This layer sits directly above the already-covered Lustre/Lucid Synchrone compiler theory, Ptolemy SR semantics, and basic polyhedral scheduling.
