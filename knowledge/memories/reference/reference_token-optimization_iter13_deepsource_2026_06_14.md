**Next Layer: Advanced Automatic Reformulation, Lift-and-Project Techniques, and Stabilized Extended Formulations in Polyhedral Compiler Optimization**

A world-leading expert in token-optimization next masters the systematic use of **lift-and-project** hierarchies and automated generation of **extended formulations** with stabilization for large-scale polyhedral ILP models that arise in exact loop transformation and instruction scheduling. This includes:

- **Balas, Ceria, Cornuéjols (1993)** lift-and-project procedures and their iterated application (N+1, N+2, …) to tighten polyhedral relaxations beyond what standard projection yields.
- **Sherali-Adams (1990)** hierarchy (already partially seen) combined with **Lovász-Schrijver (1991)** matrix cones, but now applied algorithmically inside compilers via **Bienstock & Zuckerberg (2004)** practical lift-and-project cuts for 0-1 programming.
- **Vanderbeck (2000, 2010)** on automatic generation of **Dantzig-Wolfe** reformulations with **dynamic aggregation** and **dual price smoothing** (Wentges-style stabilization extended by **du Merle et al. (1999)** and **Pessoa et al. (2020)**).
- **Louveaux & Wolsey (2002)** and **Wolsey (2020 Ch. 12–13)** on **automatic reformulation by projection and disjunctive programming** inside branch-and-price-and-cut frameworks.
- **Eisenbrand, Hähnle, Niemeier (2010)** and subsequent **polyhedral compiler** work on **cutting-plane generation at the source-to-source level** using **split cuts** and **Gomory mixed-integer cuts** derived from affine scheduling constraints.
- **MIT 18.433 (Spring 2022)** advanced module on “Lift-and-Project and Extended Formulations” and its integration with column generation stabilization.
- **CMU 15-745 (Spring 2024)** lecture block on “Stabilized Extended Formulation Generation for Polyhedral ILP in Compilers.”

These techniques enable tighter token-cost bounds and faster convergence when optimizing over enormous polyhedral spaces that encode legal schedules, fusion, and tiling choices.
