## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Advanced Theory for Scalable, Robust, and Verifiably Correct AI Training Systems**

A world-leading expert in ai-training next masters the theoretical foundations and systems abstractions that enable provably efficient, numerically stable, and formally verified large-scale training beyond current empirical pipelines. This includes:

- **Higher-order optimization and implicit bias**: Analysis of why adaptive methods and sharpness-aware techniques generalize, using tools from continuous-time limits and stochastic differential equations.
- **Non-convex landscape geometry and heavy-tailed noise**: Rigorous study of escape times from saddle points, flat minima, and the role of Levy-driven noise in SGD.
- **Communication-optimal and memory-efficient algorithms**: Beyond Kung’s lower bounds, the design of asymptotically optimal collective algorithms and checkpointing strategies under memory constraints.
- **Formal methods for distributed ML**: Refinement-based verification of data-parallel and pipeline-parallel protocols, including liveness and numerical invariants under mixed-precision arithmetic.
- **Composable automatic differentiation and program transformation semantics**: Deep understanding of AD correctness, forward/reverse-mode compositionality, and higher-order differentiation in systems like JAX.

These topics appear in graduate courses and monographs that treat training as both an optimization problem and a verified distributed systems problem.
