**Next Layer: Tractable Aggregation via Arithmetic Circuits, OBDDs, and Certified Knowledge Compilation**

A world-leading expert in corpus-aggregation next masters the systematic use of **arithmetic circuit representations** (ACs, d-DNNFs, FBDDs) for exact and approximate evaluation of aggregate queries over joins, with emphasis on size bounds, compilation complexity, and certification.

Key uncovered topics include:
- **Circuit width and smoothness** for additive and multiplicative aggregates; algorithms for compiling FAQ (Functional Aggregate Queries) into deterministic structured decomposable circuits with polynomial-time evaluation guarantees.
- **Certified compilation**: producing succinct certificates that a computed aggregate is correct without re-executing the full query, using proof systems tied to circuit provenance.
- **Tractable lineages and weighted model counting** reductions that lift beyond #P-hard boundaries via knowledge compilation hierarchies (e.g., sd-DNNF vs. OBDD).
- **Incremental maintenance** of aggregate circuits under database updates, drawing on dynamic algorithms for circuit recomputation.
- **Approximation schemes** that exploit circuit structure for FPRAS-style guarantees on aggregate queries with inequalities.

This layer draws from advanced database theory, knowledge compilation literature, and standards on verifiable computation.
