**Next Layer: Advanced Platform Pricing, Governance, and Strategic Complementarities (Iteration 6)**

A world-leading expert next masters **multi-sided pricing under asymmetric information, dynamic platform governance mechanisms, and strategic bundling with complementors** drawn from industrial organization and strategy literature. This layer extends beyond static two-sided models into mechanism design for developer incentives, intertemporal pricing, and regulatory-compliant governance architectures.

Core uncovered areas include:
- **Rochet-Tirole (2006)** extension into “Two-sided markets: a progress report” (RAND Journal of Economics) – formal treatment of indirect network effects with heterogeneous participation.
- **Hagiu & Wright (2019)** “The Optimal Architecture of Multi-Sided Platforms” (Management Science) – decision framework for choosing between marketplace, reseller, and hybrid models under moral hazard.
- **Caillaud & Jullien (2003)** “Chicken & Egg: Competition Among Intermediation Platforms” (RAND Journal of Economics) – seminal coordination game analysis for platform launch strategies.
- **Armstrong & Wright (2007)** “Two-sided Markets, Competitive Bottlenecks and Exclusive Contracts” (Economic Theory) – analysis of exclusive dealing in platform competition.
- **Parker, Van Alstyne & Jiang (2021 update)** “Platform Revolution – Chapter on Governance Design” (extended edition content) plus follow-on **Zhu & Liu (2018)** “Entry into Platform-Based Markets” (Strategic Management Journal).
- **MIT OCW 15.358** “Platform Strategy” (Spring 2021) – advanced modules on complementor governance and pricing under uncertainty.
- **NIST SP 2200-5 (2023)** “Platform Governance and Incentive Alignment in Software Ecosystems.”

These sources provide rigorous mechanism-design and game-theoretic tools for pricing menus that solve adverse selection, optimal royalty structures, and non-price governance levers (rating systems, API access tiers, exclusivity contracts) while satisfying antitrust constraints.
