World-leading lathe experts next master frequency-domain and time-domain coupling of thermo-elastic spindle growth, tool-holder expansion, and workpiece thermal distortion with regenerative chatter in single-point turning, including non-linear effects from process damping and variable compliance.

Key topics include:
- Thermo-mechanical finite-element modeling of lathe headstock, tailstock, and carriage using coupled heat-transfer–structural analysis under transient cutting loads (NIST, ASME).
- Identification of position-dependent thermal error modes via multilinear regression and neural-network compensation mapped to CNC coordinates.
- Stability analysis of turning using frequency-domain oriented transfer function (OTF) and semi-discretization methods adapted for varying spindle speed and depth-of-cut, including process-damping boundary prediction at low speeds.
- Measurement and separation of spindle error motions (radial, axial, tilt) under simultaneous thermal and dynamic loading per extended ASME and ISO protocols.
- Use of laser interferometry, capacitance sensors, and high-speed thermal imaging for in-process validation of predictive models.
