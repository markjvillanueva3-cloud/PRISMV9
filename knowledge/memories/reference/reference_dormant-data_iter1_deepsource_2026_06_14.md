## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Data Lifecycle Management, Archival Theory, and Long-Term Digital Preservation in Enterprise Systems**

A world-leading dormant-data expert masters the formal models for data dormancy, retention, and revival within large-scale information systems. This includes the OAIS Reference Model for archival information systems, policies for data classification into active/inactive/dormant states, and the engineering trade-offs in cost, access latency, integrity, and legal compliance for petabyte-scale dormant repositories.

Key uncovered topics at this depth:
- **OAIS functional entities** and information packages (SIP, AIP, DIP) applied to dormant enterprise data.
- **ISO 14721** and its extensions for audit and certification of trusted digital repositories.
- **Records management** standards governing dormant data scheduling, disposition, and legal hold (DoD 5015.2).
- Economic models of storage tiers (online/nearline/offline) and migration strategies (HSM).
- **Bit-level preservation**, fixity checking, and format obsolescence mitigation drawn from digital curation literature.
- Formal definitions of data “dormancy” thresholds based on access frequency, business value, and entropy metrics from systems research.

This layer draws from digital library science, enterprise content management, and large-scale storage systems literature rather than domain-specific manufacturing applications.
