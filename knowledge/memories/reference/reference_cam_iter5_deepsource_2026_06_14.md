## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Nonlinear regenerative chatter with state-dependent and distributed delays in variable-speed turning/milling, including viscoelastic process damping, thermal effects, and Bayesian calibration under uncertainty.**

World-leading CAM experts master the following after the listed covered works:

- **Distributed delay modeling** arising from the finite cutting edge radius and flank-workpiece contact, leading to short-time viscoelastic damping kernels (non-instantaneous regeneration).
- **State-dependent delay differential equations (SDDDEs)** where instantaneous delay varies with both tool angular position and actual surface position due to tool deflection and runout.
- **Viscoelastic contact mechanics** at the flank face using fractional-derivative Kelvin-Voigt or Maxwell models to capture rate-dependent process damping without empirical constants.
- **Thermal-mechanical coupling** in high-speed turning: temperature-dependent shear flow stress and its influence on stability lobe migration (thermally induced gain variation).
- **Uncertainty propagation** via Bayesian inference on modal parameters, cutting edge geometry, and damping kernels; use of Markov-Chain Monte Carlo or Polynomial Chaos for robust stability boundaries.
- **Semi-discretization and spectral element methods extended to distributed and state-dependent delays** with convergence proofs for periodic systems having multiple time scales.
- **Standards-compliant circular test metrology** (ISO 230-4) to quantify position-dependent spindle error motions that modulate effective delay and excite parametric instability.

These topics form the current research frontier for physics-based digital twin stability prediction in PRISM’s CAM galaxy.
