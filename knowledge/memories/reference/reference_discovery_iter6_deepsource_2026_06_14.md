**Next Layer: Advanced Theory and Methods in Ontology Dynamics, Conservative Extensions, and Scalable Knowledge Graph Evolution**

A world-leading expert in discovery next masters formal foundations for safe ontology evolution, uniform interpolation, and modular repair techniques that preserve entailment under dynamic updates. This includes forgetting-based methods, justification-based debugging at scale, and the computational complexity of inseparability notions in expressive DLs beyond ALC and DL-Lite.

Key areas include:
- **Forgetting and uniform interpolation** in description logics as mechanisms for ontology contraction and modular extraction while preserving conservative extensions.
- **Axiom pinpointing and laconic justifications** for scalable OWL debugging.
- **Ontology revision operators** grounded in belief revision theory (AGM-style) adapted to DLs.
- **Temporal and dynamic description logics** for modeling evolving knowledge graphs.
- **Empirical evaluation methodologies** using large-scale synthetic and real-world knowledge graph benchmarks.
