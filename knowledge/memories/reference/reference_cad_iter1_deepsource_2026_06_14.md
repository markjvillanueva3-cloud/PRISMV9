**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Parametric & Feature-Based Modeling Theory**

A world-leading CAD expert in the software-engineering sense masters the formal algebraic and algorithmic foundations of parametric modeling, constraint solving, and feature semantics that underpin modern CAD kernels (ACIS, Parasolid, OpenCascade).

Key uncovered topics include:
- Variational geometric constraint solving (degrees-of-freedom analysis, graph-based decomposition, rigidity theory)
- Persistent naming algorithms for topological entities in parametric history trees
- Feature representation ontologies, semantic feature modeling, and design intent preservation
- Boundary representation (B-rep) validity maintenance and non-manifold topology
- Procedural and declarative modeling paradigms, including sketch solving with nonlinear constraint solvers
