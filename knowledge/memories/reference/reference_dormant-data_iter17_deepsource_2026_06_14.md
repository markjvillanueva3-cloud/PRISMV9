A world-leading expert in dormant-data must next master **universal composability (UC) frameworks extended to long-horizon dormant parties**, **fractional synchrony models with economic rationality**, and **formal specification of liveness and availability under sleepiness** using temporal-epistemic logics. This layer focuses on rigorous machine-checked or mechanized proofs of composable security for storage systems where parties may remain inactive for polynomially long (or unbounded) periods while preserving forward security, knowledge complexity, and incentive compatibility.

Key uncovered areas include:
- Mechanized verification of UC security with dormant players using the F* proof assistant and the globally-synchronized clock (GUC) model with long-term setup (Canetti et al., extended in MIT 6.8260).
- Application of **TLA+** for specifying and model-checking liveness properties under “sleepy” fairness conditions beyond Lamport’s basic treatment, including fairness under economic deviation.
- Formal treatment of **rational proofs of storage** in the UC framework with long-term incentives, moving beyond interactive proofs to non-interactive, publicly verifiable, economically sustainable schemes with composable availability.

These build directly on cryptographic foundations (knowledge complexity, forward-secure signatures, stateful hash-based schemes) by adding compositional, mechanized, and incentive-aware layers required for provably secure dormant-data galaxies at Internet scale.
