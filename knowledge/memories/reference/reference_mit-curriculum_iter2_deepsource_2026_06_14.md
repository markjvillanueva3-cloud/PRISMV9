## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Bayesian Knowledge Tracing Extensions, Cognitive Model Discovery, and Adaptive Curriculum Sequencing**

A world-leading expert in mit-curriculum next masters **model refinement techniques** beyond classic BKT and DKT, focusing on **hierarchical Bayesian models**, **knowledge component (KC) model discovery**, and **curriculum optimization under partial observability**.

Key areas include:
- **Additive Factors Model (AFM)** and **Performance Factors Analysis (PFA)** as successors to BKT for skill-specific learning rates and transfer (Cen et al., 2006; Pavlik et al., 2009).
- **Cognitive Model Discovery via machine-learned KC models** using Q-matrix factorization, sparse factor analysis, and data-driven skill discovery (Koedinger et al., 2012; Matsuda et al., 2015).
- **Bayesian Optimization** and **Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes (POMDPs)** for adaptive sequencing of learning activities and problem selection (Rafferty et al., 2016; Lindsey et al., 2014).
- **Learning Decomposition** and **Learning Curve Analysis** at scale using logistic regression with multiple KCs and power-law forgetting (Wilson & Schooler, 1991; Ritter et al., 2007).
- **Standards-based competency frameworks** and **xAPI** for fine-grained assertion and portable learner models beyond LTI 1.3 (ADL, 2016; IEEE 2790-2020).

These build directly on the already-covered Knowledge-Learning-Instruction (KLI) framework by adding rigorous statistical identification of cognitive skill hierarchies and optimal instructional policies.
