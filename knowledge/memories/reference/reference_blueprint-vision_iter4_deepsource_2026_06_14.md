**Next Layer: Blueprint-Vision Knowledge Mastery**

A world-leading expert in blueprint-vision next masters the integration of **sketch understanding**, **diagram parsing**, and **constraint-driven semantic reconstruction** within a formal software-engineering and AI pipeline. This layer emphasizes stochastic image grammars extended to engineering notation, error-tolerant graph rewriting for symbol and dimension recognition, and scalable constraint databases that couple geometric solvers with enterprise data models.

Key uncovered areas include:

- **Stochastic context-free grammars and And-Or graphs** for hierarchical parsing of mechanical drawings (Zhu et al., 2007; Han & Zhu, 2005).
- **Constraint databases and Datalog-based geometric reasoning** that embed spatial predicates directly into query engines, enabling declarative 2D-to-3D reconstruction (Kanellakis et al., 1995; Revesz, 2010).
- **Graph transformation systems and typed graph grammars** for formal rewriting of engineering symbol graphs, ensuring consistency with ISO/IEC 12207 and 15288 life-cycle processes (Ehrig et al., 2006).
- **Perceptual organization and Bayesian inference** layers that close contours and resolve ambiguous dimensioning using learned priors (Saund & Moran, 1995; Sarkar & Boyer, 1993).
- **Model-based engineering semantics** using STEP Application Protocols combined with semantic web technologies for ontology-driven interpretation of annotated drawings (ISO 10303-221, 2007; Pratt & Srinivasan, 2005).

These build directly on MIT 6.869 and 6.850 by adding rigorous database-theoretic and grammar-based methods suitable for production-grade blueprint-vision systems inside PRISM’s galaxy.
