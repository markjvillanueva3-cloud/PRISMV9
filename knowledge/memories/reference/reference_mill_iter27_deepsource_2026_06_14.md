## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Volumetric error synthesis via HTM kinematic chains for 5-axis mills.**  
Method combines 21 rigid-body geometric errors, position-dependent thermal distortions, and orientation-dependent dynamic compliance into a unified 6-DOF tool-tip error prediction across the workspace. Error parameters identified by multilateration laser tracker sequences at multiple stations; error components propagated through successive homogeneous transformation matrices that embed angular, linear, and squareness deviations plus thermal growth terms. Compensation realized by real-time 3-D error table interpolation in the CNC lookahead buffer. Uncertainty propagation follows GUM-adapted Monte Carlo sampling of tracker and machine repeatability contributions. Extends single-axis laser interferometer and ballbar protocols to full simultaneous 5-axis contouring trajectories while coupling with orientation-dependent FRFs obtained by receptance coupling substructure analysis.

**Advanced semi-discretization of time-periodic DDEs with time-varying directional factors.**  
Updates the Chebyshev and full-discretization methods already covered by treating both delay and periodic coefficients as piecewise constant over each tooth period, enabling direct inclusion of tool orientation changes in 5-axis paths without fixed-axis assumption.
