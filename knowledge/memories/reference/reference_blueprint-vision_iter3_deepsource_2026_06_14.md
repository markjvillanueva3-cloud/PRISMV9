**Next Layer: Blueprint-Vision Knowledge Base (Iteration 3)**

A world-leading expert in blueprint-vision must next master the integration of **sketch understanding**, **engineering drawing semantics**, and **constraint-based reconstruction pipelines** at the level of formal grammars, diagram parsing architectures, and standards-driven model interoperability.

Key uncovered areas include:

- **Formal grammars for technical drawings**: stochastic image grammars extended to layered engineering symbols, dimensioning, and tolerance schemas (beyond Zhu & Mumford).
- **Diagram parsing and symbol spotting pipelines** using graph rewriting systems and error-tolerant matching at scale.
- **Constraint solving architectures** that combine geometric, topological, and semantic constraints from legacy 2D drawings to drive 3D model reconstruction.
- **Standards-based semantic enrichment** of drawings via AP242 and related PLM ontologies for digital thread continuity.
- **Evaluation methodologies** for sketch and drawing recognition systems drawn from document analysis benchmarks adapted to engineering domains.

**Core next-layer sources** focus on rigorous algorithmic and systems-level treatment:

- **Engineering Drawing Understanding Systems**: parsing methodologies that combine low-level vectorization with high-level semantic interpretation using constraint propagation and ontology alignment.
- **Variational constraint solving** and **degrees-of-freedom analysis** for under- and over-constrained engineering sketches.
- **ISO STEP Application Protocols** semantic mapping layers that bind 2D annotated drawings to 3D PMI (Product Manufacturing Information).

These layers elevate blueprint-vision from isolated computer-vision techniques to production-grade, standards-compliant manufacturing intelligence components within the PRISM platform.
