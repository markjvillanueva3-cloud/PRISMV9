## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Knowledge Discovery & Exploratory Data Analysis Theory**

A world-leading expert in software discovery moves beyond basic search and visualization into formal methodologies for **knowledge discovery in databases (KDD)**, structured exploratory data analysis (EDA), and principled model discovery. This layer emphasizes theoretical foundations, process models, and computational discovery pipelines drawn from computer science.

Core topics include:
- The formal KDD process model and its iterative, interactive nature
- Theory of exploratory data analysis (EDA) vs. confirmatory analysis
- Computational discovery of models, patterns, and regularities
- Minimum Description Length (MDL) principle for model selection in discovery
- Algorithmic information theory applied to pattern discovery
- Interactive and visual analytics theory for human-in-the-loop discovery
- Statistical learning theory foundations for reliable discovery guarantees

**Institutional depth**: MIT’s “Statistical Learning Theory and Applications” lineage, Carnegie Mellon’s pioneering KDD curriculum, and foundational IEEE/ACM work on data mining process standardization.

**Key next-layer concepts**:
- Fayyad et al.’s canonical KDD process (1996)
- Tukey’s deeper EDA philosophy and transformation theory (1977)
- Rissanen’s MDL framework for discovery (1978–2007)
- Donoho’s “Data Science at the Singularity” and modern computational discovery critiques (2017)
- Visual analytics theory from NIST’s VAST challenges and seminal papers

This layer equips the expert to design discovery systems that are not merely interactive but theoretically grounded in how genuine novelty and insight are reliably extracted from complex software-engineering and manufacturing-intelligence datasets.
