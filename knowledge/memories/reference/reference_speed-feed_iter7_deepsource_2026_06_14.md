**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Constitutive Modeling, Adiabatic Shear Localization, and Thermomechanical Coupling at Extreme Machining Rates**

World-leading mastery next requires:
- Physically-based constitutive models beyond JC and Zerilli-Armstrong that incorporate dynamic recovery, recrystallization, and dislocation substructure evolution at strain rates >10^5 s⁻¹ and temperatures approaching melting.
- Rigorous treatment of adiabatic shear banding (ASB) instability criteria, perturbation analysis, and spacing/width prediction in high-speed machining.
- Coupled thermomechanical finite-element formulations with explicit treatment of thermal softening, momentum diffusion, and mesh-objective regularization techniques (non-local, gradient, or phase-field damage).
- High-strain-rate material characterization using Split-Hopkinson Pressure Bar (SHPB) with direct shear and pressure-shear plate impact, including temperature measurement via embedded thermocouples or high-speed infrared.
- Uncertainty quantification and verification/validation (VVUQ) protocols specific to machining simulations per ASME and NIST frameworks.

**METHOD/THEORY:**
