## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Quality Economics, Stochastic Process Modeling, and Empirical Software Quality Theory**

A world-leading expert next masters formal economic models of software quality under uncertainty, Bayesian updating of quality metrics, stochastic process simulation for defect dynamics, and empirical validation frameworks drawn from large-scale software datasets.

Key areas include:

- **Value-Based Software Engineering (VBSE) and quality ROI under uncertainty** using real-options theory and multi-attribute utility models (Boehm & Jain, 2006).
- **Bayesian statistical quality control for software** — moving beyond classical SPC to hierarchical Bayesian models for defect prediction and reliability growth (Singpurwalla, 2006 is baseline; next is Gelman et al. hierarchical modeling applied to software).
- **Software reliability growth models (SRGMs) with imperfect debugging and non-homogeneous Poisson processes (NHPP)**, calibrated via maximum likelihood and MCMC (Musa, 2005; Gokhale & Trivedi, 2002).
- **Empirical software engineering methodology**: rigorous experiment design, threats to validity, meta-analysis of quality metrics, and replication standards (Kitchenham & Charters, 2007; Shull et al., 2008).
- **Architectural quality debt quantification using probabilistic cost-benefit models** extending Nord et al. with real-options and technical debt interest formulas (Seacord et al., 2003; Kazman et al., 2015).
- **System dynamics extensions for quality management**: calibration of quality feedback loops using Kalman filtering and Bayesian inference on observed defect flows (Madachy, 2008).

These build directly on covered Boehm/Sterman/ISO foundations by adding stochastic formalisms, empirical standards, and decision theory under uncertainty.
