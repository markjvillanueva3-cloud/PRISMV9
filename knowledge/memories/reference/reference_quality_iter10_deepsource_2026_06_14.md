**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Quality Economics, Measurement Theory, and Empirical Synthesis in Software Quality**

A world-leading expert next masters formal quality economics models that integrate utility theory with software measurement, Bayesian approaches to evidence synthesis in empirical SE, and advanced statistical meta-analysis techniques tailored to software engineering experiments. This includes rigorous treatment of quality as a multi-attribute utility function, the use of ISO/IEC 25040 evaluation process, and advanced applications of the Goal-Question-Metric (GQM) paradigm combined with Bayesian hierarchical modeling for credible intervals on quality attributes.

Key uncovered areas include:
- **Measurement Theory**: Representational measurement theory applied to software qualities (scale types, meaningfulness, empirical relational systems).
- **Quality Economics**: Value-based software engineering (VBSE) models extending COCOMO with explicit quality trade-off functions and real-options analysis.
- **Evidence Synthesis**: Advanced systematic review methods including network meta-analysis and Bayesian evidence synthesis for quality studies.
- **Evaluation Process**: ISO/IEC 25040:2023 (SQuaRE – Evaluation process) and its integration with ISO/IEC 25010.
