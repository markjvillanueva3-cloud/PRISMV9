**Next Layer: Plasma Channel Dynamics, Dielectric Breakdown Kinetics, and Multiscale Subsurface Damage Prediction in WEDM**

A world-leading expert masters the following layer:

- **Governing equations for EDM plasma channel expansion** including radiative heat transfer, shock-wave propagation, and non-equilibrium ionization kinetics (van Dijck, 1973; Hayakawa et al., 2001).
- **Dielectric breakdown delay time statistics** and stochastic streamer-to-arc transition models based on Weibull statistics and Paschen’s law extensions for liquid dielectrics (Schumacher, 2004; Kojima et al., 2008).
- **Phase-field and cellular automata models** of rapid solidification, dendritic recast-layer formation, and microcrack nucleation driven by thermal contraction and hydrogen embrittlement in nickel superalloys (Cusanelli et al., 2004; Ming et al., 2022).
- **Gradient plasticity and geometrically necessary dislocation (GND) density evolution** below the recast layer using EBSD-based strain-gradient crystal plasticity frameworks (Feldmann et al., 2021; Narulkar et al., 2023).
- **Standards and metrology**: ISO 4287:1997 (profile method), ASME B46.1-2019 updates on waviness filtering for stochastic WEDM surfaces, and NIST-recommended protocols for quantitative GND mapping via HR-EBSD (Wilkinson et al., 2006; NIST IR 8764 extensions).

These topics extend beyond previously covered thermal-metallurgical modeling, MHD plasma effects, stochastic discharge chains, and basic synchrotron/EBSD characterization into predictive multiscale damage initiation and dielectric physics.
