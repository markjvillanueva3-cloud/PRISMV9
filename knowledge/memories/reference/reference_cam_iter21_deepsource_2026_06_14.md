## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next layer: micromorphic regularization of thermoviscoplastic shear bands, operator-split thermo-mechanical integration schemes for explicit dynamics chip segmentation codes, XFEM/cohesive-zone formulations for discontinuous chip morphology without remeshing, and stochastic random-field defect injection for probabilistic segmentation prediction.**

Micromorphic continuum theories introduce additional degrees of freedom and length-scale parameters that regularize adiabatic shear band width and suppress pathological mesh dependence in orthogonal cutting simulations. Operator-split algorithms (isothermal mechanical step followed by heat conduction/damage update) maintain stability at high strain rates while coupling plastic work, thermal softening, and damage evolution. Extended finite element and cohesive zone models permit arbitrary intra-element crack paths driven by fracture energy, enabling realistic serrated chip detachment at tool rake face and free surface. Stochastic finite-element realizations superimpose spatially correlated initial defect fields (voids, grain-size variations) to quantify variability in shear-band spacing and chip segmentation frequency.

These methods build directly on non-local/phase-field foundations without repeating listed works, supplying the quantitative regularization and uncertainty propagation required for safety-critical predictive machining models.
