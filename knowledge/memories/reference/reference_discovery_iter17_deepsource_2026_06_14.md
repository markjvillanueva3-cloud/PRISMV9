## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Forgetting-Based Ontology Decomposition and Semantic Forgetting in Dynamic Knowledge Graphs**

A world-leading expert next masters the theory and algorithms for **ontology decomposition via uniform interpolation** and **second-order quantifier elimination techniques** that enable scalable, incremental discovery in evolving description-logic-based knowledge bases. This layer focuses on how uniform interpolants can serve as semantic signatures for modular discovery, how to compute minimal forgetting signatures that preserve query-rewritability, and how to integrate these with dynamic ABox evolution for continuous discovery pipelines.

Key uncovered areas include:

- **Craig interpolation and uniform interpolation as discovery primitives** in expressive DLs beyond EL and ALC, including practical computation via resolution and tableau-based methods that guarantee termination on bounded-treewidth ontologies.
- **Forgetting-based module extraction for discovery** that isolates only those axioms relevant to a discovery goal (signature), enabling lightweight semantic search without loading entire terminologies.
- **Approximate forgetting and bounded forgetting** trade-offs between logical completeness and computational tractability, directly applicable to real-time discovery agents.
- **Connection to second-order quantifier elimination (SOQE)** as a generalization of forgetting, including its links to circumscription and fixed-point logics for inductive discovery patterns.
