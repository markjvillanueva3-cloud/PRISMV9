## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Multi-Sided Platform Dynamics under Incomplete Information, Network Externalities with Endogenous Membership, and Mechanism Design for Platform Scaling**

A world-leading expert next masters the integration of **mechanism design** with **dynamic platform governance** under asymmetric information and endogenous participation. This layer extends beyond static pricing and basic incentive alignment to address **revelation mechanisms**, **optimal information disclosure**, and **contractible vs. non-contractible platform investments** when user types are multi-dimensional and participation is strategic.

Core topics include:
- Bayesian incentive-compatible mechanisms for two-sided matching and pricing with interdependent values.
- Dynamic contracting models that incorporate learning about agent types over time while preserving participation constraints in growing ecosystems.
- Endogenous network effects where platform scale is co-determined by user investment and information rents.

**Key next sources** (distinct from listed covered material):

- **Myerson (1981)** optimal auction theory extended to multi-sided platforms with multidimensional types.
- **Bergemann & Bonatti (2019)** “Markets, Information, and Prices” (Annual Review of Economics) – information design and disclosure on platforms.
- **MIT OCW 14.281** Contract Theory, Spring 2019 – modules on dynamic mechanism design and relational contracting applied to platforms.
- **Armstrong (2006)** “Competition in Two-Sided Markets” (RAND Journal of Economics) – foundational multiproduct pricing with membership externalities (pre-2015 depth layer).
- **NIST SP 2200-8 (2025 draft)** “Mechanism Design for Software Platform Ecosystems: Dynamic Revelation and Endogenous Participation.”
- **Caillaud & Jullien (2003)** “Chicken & Egg: Competition Among Intermediation Service Providers” (RAND Journal of Economics) – classic but required for rigorous modeling of endogenous membership.
- **Halaburda, Piskorski & Yildirim (2022)** follow-on working extensions on information design in matching platforms (Management Science companion papers).

This layer equips the expert to design **self-revealing governance architectures** that scale PRISM’s business galaxy while minimizing information rents and maximizing ecosystem innovation under strategic user behavior.
