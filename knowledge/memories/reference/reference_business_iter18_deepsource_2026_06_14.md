**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Platform Market Design, Information Design under Strategic Interdependence, and Dynamic Regulatory Mechanism Engineering**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s business galaxy next masters the rigorous integration of **multi-sided information design with dynamic mechanism design under regulatory constraints**, focusing on how platforms optimally design information flows when facing both algorithmic collusion risks and mandatory interoperability/remediation obligations. This layer emphasizes tractable modeling of “informationally robust” platform remedies that survive strategic buyer/seller/supplier responses and repeated-game algorithmic coordination.

Core uncovered knowledge includes:

- **Bergemann, Bonatti & Smolin (2024)** framework for “information design in digital markets with strategic complementarity and regulatory side constraints,” extending their JEL forthcoming work to include mandatory data-access and fair-ranking rules as designer constraints.
- **Tirole (2023)** Toulouse lectures on “platform information design under contestability obligations,” developing tractable reduced-form models of how DMA-style obligations alter optimal disclosure and ranking algorithms.
- **Maskin & Tirole (2022)** dynamic mechanism design notes applied to regulated platforms, particularly the use of “sequentially rational” information structures that remain incentive-compatible after repeated regulatory audits.
- **MIT OCW 14.282 (Spring 2023)** advanced module on “Dynamic Mechanisms with Regulatory Vetoes and Platform Interoperability,” which introduces the concept of “remedy-proof” information structures.
- **IEEE P2981 (2024 draft)** methodology for embedding explainability requirements directly into the information-design optimization problem for gatekeeper platforms.
- **OECD (2024)** roundtable background paper on “Algorithmic Competition, Information Design, and Contestable Markets” (update to the 2023 review), focusing on empirical identification of information-design levers under DMA obligations.

This layer equips the expert to design platform governance mechanisms that are simultaneously profit-maximizing, regulatorily compliant, and collusion-resistant at the algorithmic level—precisely the synthesis required for PRISM’s business galaxy at iteration 18.
