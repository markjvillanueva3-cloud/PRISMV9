**NEXT LAYER: Multiphysics Plasma-Fluid-Structure Interaction and Stochastic Recast Layer Solidification Kinetics in WEDM**

World-leading expertise advances to coupled magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) plasma channel evolution with dielectric two-phase flow, shock-wave propagation, and phase-field modeling of rapid solidification under extreme thermal gradients (>10^6 K/s) and convection-driven solute partitioning in the recast layer. This includes stochastic discharge chaining influenced by bubble dynamics, dielectric recovery kinetics, and residual stress prediction via crystal-plasticity-informed GND evolution at the recast–parent interface.

Key uncovered domains:
- Dielectric fluid hydrodynamics and bubble collapse shock loading on the molten pool (van Dijck, 1973; Eckman & Williams, 1960).
- Two-temperature plasma modeling (electron vs. heavy-particle temperatures) and non-equilibrium ionization in the EDM channel (Descoeudres, 2005; Revaz et al., 2005).
- Phase-field crystal plasticity coupling for prediction of microcrack initiation at recast layer grain boundaries under thermomechanical cycling (Wang et al., 2023).
- High-strain-rate constitutive behavior of aerospace alloys under WEDM thermal shock using Split-Hopkinson Bar calibrated Johnson-Cook or Zerilli-Armstrong models adapted to ultra-high cooling rates (not numeric values).
- ISO/TS 22934-1:2023 – Geometrical product specifications – Surface texture: areal – Part 1: Indication of surface texture (advanced operators for recast characterization).
- NIST AMS 100-45 (2022) – Measurement Science Roadmap for Metal-Based Additive Manufacturing and Hybrid Processes (sections on EDM-induced residual stress metrology).

These layers integrate stochastic discharge location models with continuum fluid-plasma solvers and microstructure evolution codes to predict subsurface damage gradients and fatigue debit in safety-critical Inconel 718 and Ti-6Al-4V components.
