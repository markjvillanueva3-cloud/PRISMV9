## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Post-Processor Kinematics, Compensation, and Safety-Critical Trajectory Generation for 5-Axis Machining**

World-leading post-processor expertise advances into **inverse kinematics with error compensation under dynamic constraints**, **real-time trajectory smoothing with jerk-limited blending**, and **safety-critical collision avoidance integrated with volumetric error mapping**.

**Core topics at this depth:**

- Kinematic transformation with non-orthogonal rotary axes, including singular-region handling and tool-tip/tool-axis decoupling (She and Huang, 2010).
- Post-processor algorithms for RTCP (Rotary Tool Center Point) and TCP (Tool Center Point) modes with synchronized rotary-linear error compensation.
- Integration of ISO 230-4 circular interpolation tests and ASME B5.54 laser-based dynamic contouring diagnostics into post-processor validation loops.
- Jerk-limited corner smoothing and feed-rate scheduling considering servo bandwidth and structural dynamics (Altintas & Erkorkmaz, 2003; Erkorkmaz et al., 2011).
- Safety-critical aspects: redundant kinematic checking, axis-limit monitoring, and collision-free tool-path regeneration using configuration-space methods.
- Mapping of machine-specific volumetric error (from ISO 230-1/6 and NIST laser-tracker protocols) into post-processor compensation tables.

**Method/Theory Emphasis:**
- Closed-form vs. iterative inverse kinematics solvers for table-on-table, head-on-table, and head-on-head 5-axis machines.
- Real-time interpolation error prediction and correction within the post-processor (beyond CAM CL-data).
- Integration of finite-element-derived compliance models into trajectory generation without exposing gated constants.
- Standards-driven acceptance testing of post-processor output using ASME B5.64 rotary table performance and ISO 10303-238 STEP-NC compliant toolpaths.

This layer bridges classical post-processing with modern model-based error compensation and functional safety per IEC 61508 / ISO 13849 principles applied to CNC motion control.
