**Next Layer: Advanced Discovery in Semantic Systems, Ontology Alignment, and Scalable Knowledge Graph Engineering**

A world-leading expert in *discovery* next masters the theory and practice of **ontology alignment, modularization, and federated knowledge discovery** at enterprise scale, including probabilistic and machine-learning-augmented reasoning over evolving linked data.

Key areas include:
- Ontology matching and alignment algorithms, evaluation campaigns, and logical repair techniques.
- Modular ontologies, ontology partitioning, and conservative extensions for safe reuse.
- Scalable reasoning with existential rules, Datalog±, and hybrid neuro-symbolic discovery methods.
- Provenance, trust, and change management in distributed knowledge graphs.
- Formal methods for knowledge graph construction, refinement, and automated discovery from heterogeneous sources.
