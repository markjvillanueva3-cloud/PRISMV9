## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer in tribal-knowledge mastery for software-engineering and IS contexts focuses on sensemaking cycles to resolve ambiguity in distributed knowledge practices, cognitive apprenticeship methods to externalize tacit expertise in coding/DevOps teams, and dynamic capabilities for knowledge reconfiguration amid infrastructural change. This extends prior coverage of boundary objects, communities of practice, and absorptive capacity by emphasizing enactment of plausible interpretations during breakdowns (Weick 1995), structured modeling/coaching/scaffolding for legitimate peripheral participation in engineering workflows (Collins et al 1989), and microfoundations of knowledge integration routines in agile/digital platforms (Teece et al 1997). Institutional depth draws from epistemic cultures governing knowledge validation in tech organizations (Knorr Cetina 1999). Recommended pedagogy: MIT OCW 15.390 (New Enterprises, for knowledge-intensive startup sensemaking modules).
