**NEXT LAYER: Formal Vision Architectures & Scene Understanding Theory**

A world-leading expert in blueprint-vision masters the computational geometry and structured scene parsing layers that bridge pixel-level perception to symbolic manufacturing-intent graphs. This includes projective geometry for 2D-to-3D blueprint normalization, constraint-based diagram understanding, and hierarchical graph grammars for engineering drawing semantics.

Key uncovered topics at this depth:

- **Projective and multi-view geometry** applied to orthographic and isometric blueprint standardization (Hartley & Zisserman, 2004).
- **Constraint satisfaction and geometric constraint solving** for implicit dimensioning and tolerance inference in CAD-derived drawings (Joan-Arinyo et al., 2003; Hoffmann, 2005).
- **Attributed relational graphs (ARGs) and graph rewriting systems** for formal representation of engineering drawing syntax and functional intent (Grbavec & Samet, 1995; Lladós et al., 2001).
- **Stochastic image grammars and hierarchical compositional models** for parsing technical diagrams (Zhu & Mumford, 2006; Han & Zhu, 2009).
- **Knowledge-based vectorization and symbol recognition pipelines** grounded in formal ontology and description logics (IEEE Std 1012, ISO/IEC 15288 systems engineering lifecycle processes).

These layers sit above basic CNN detection and directly enable PRISM’s blueprint-vision galaxy to produce machine-interpretable, semantically rich models rather than raw detections.
