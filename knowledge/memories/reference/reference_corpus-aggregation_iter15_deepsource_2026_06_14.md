## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Tractable Aggregation over Probabilistic Circuits and Certified Knowledge Compilation**

A world-leading expert in corpus-aggregation next masters the theory and algorithms for **tractable probabilistic query evaluation** and **certified compilation of aggregate queries over knowledge compilations** (especially d-DNNFs, SDDs, and OBDDs) with semiring aggregates, including lifted inference guarantees, provenance-aware aggregation, and zero-knowledge/ verifiable aggregation circuits.

This layer focuses on:
- **Aggregate circuits** with additive and multiplicative semirings
- **Certified compilation** ensuring soundness of aggregate results via proof-carrying circuits
- **Tractable probabilistic aggregation** using weighted model counting (WMC) and its generalizations to FAQ (Functional Aggregate Queries) over probabilistic databases
- **Verifiable knowledge compilation** frameworks that produce succinct certificates for aggregate results

Key uncovered topics at this depth include:
- The use of **arithmetic circuit certification** and **proofs of knowledge compilation** for aggregate queries (extending beyond basic d-DNNF tractability)
- **Semiring provenance** for aggregates in probabilistic settings
- **Incremental maintenance** of compiled aggregate circuits under database updates
- ** succinct representation theorems** for FAQ over bounded-treewidth or low-cliquewidth instances using structured decomposable circuits

**Institutional depth** draws from MIT’s graduate database theory sequence, CMU’s advanced query processing curriculum, recent IEEE verifiable computation standards, and NIST work on certified data aggregation.
