**Next Layer: Advanced Parametric and Variational Modeling Theory, Persistent Naming, and Constraint Solvers in CAD Systems**

A world-leading expert next masters the formal algebraic and graph-based foundations of variational geometric modeling, persistent entity naming algorithms that survive topological changes, and the integration of geometric constraint solving with feature-based parametric histories. This includes rigorous treatment of under-constrained, well-constrained, and over-constrained systems using degree-of-freedom analysis, Gröbner bases, and graph rigidity theory, as well as the semantics of parametric propagation and rebuild-order dependencies.

Key uncovered topics include:
- **Constraint solving algorithms**: numerical continuation methods, graph-based decomposition (constraint graphs, dependency graphs), and hybrid symbolic-numeric solvers.
- **Persistent naming and topological robustness**: algorithms that assign unambiguous identifiers to faces, edges, and vertices across model revisions (history-based parametric updates).
- **Feature semantics and validity maintenance**: formal models of feature attachment, interaction, and validity in parametric feature trees.
- **Variational boundary representation**: integration of variational constraints directly with B-rep kernels.

**Institutional/standards depth**:
- ASME Y14.5-2018 (GD&T) linkage to parametric models.
- ISO 10303-108 and ISO 10303-111 extensions for parametric representations.
- NISTIRs on parametric interoperability and naming.

**College-level depth**:
- Advanced geometric constraint solving theory (beyond MIT 6.849/6.838).
- Rigidity theory applied to CAD (Laman’s theorem, Henneberg constructions).

**Next-layer sources** focus on formal methods for solver robustness, naming, and semantic feature modeling published in top venues.
