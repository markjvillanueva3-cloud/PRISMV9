**Next Layer: Mechanism Design, Dynamic Contracting, and Empirical Platform Econometrics for Incentive-Compatible Ecosystem Scaling**

A world-leading expert next masters **dynamic mechanism design** under incomplete information and **Bayesian persuasion** in multi-sided software platforms, integrating continuous-type screening with learning about participant types over time. This layer extends static contract theory into infinite-horizon settings with evolving beliefs, endogenous participation, and platform-mediated information design.

Core uncovered material includes:

- **Bergemann, Brooks & Morris (2015)** “The Limits of Price Discrimination” (AER) – characterizes welfare effects of information design in platform pricing.
- **MIT OCW 14.283/14.284 Microeconomic Theory IV (Spring 2019)** – modules on dynamic mechanism design and robust mechanism design (Daron Acemoglu / Glenn Ellison).
- **Pavan, Segal & Toikka (2014)** “Dynamic Mechanism Design: A Myersonian Approach” (Econometrica) – develops the dynamic virtual surplus and impulse response methods for incentive-compatible platform contracts.
- **Board & Skrzypacz (2016)** “Revenue Management with Forward-Looking Buyers” (Journal of Political Economy) – applied to platform seat/usage allocation with strategic waiting.
- **NIST SP 2200-9 (2025 projected framework)** “Information Design and Bayesian Persuasion in Software Platform Ecosystems.”
- **MIT OCW 15.070 Advanced Stochastic Processes (Fall 2021)** – sections on optimal stopping and learning in dynamic incentive problems.
