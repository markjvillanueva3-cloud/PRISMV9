## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer integrates Personal Software Process (PSP) for individual-level defect prevention and measurement discipline, Orthogonal Defect Classification (ODC) for causal analysis and in-process feedback, pre-SQuaRE quality factor hierarchies (McCall and Boehm models), IEEE-mandated Software Quality Assurance Planning, and ISO process assessment frameworks (successor to SPICE). These provide theory and institutional mechanisms for shifting from organizational CMMI/ISO 12207 compliance to quantitative, preventive quality at both personal and process-assessment levels, supported by empirical experimental software engineering methods.

This mastery emphasizes PSP's structured planning/tracking, ODC's orthogonal categorization for rapid process insight, explicit quality attribute taxonomies that ISO 25010 refined, and standardized assessment against process reference models—extending beyond covered testing/metrics/V&V into proactive engineering economics and repeatability.
