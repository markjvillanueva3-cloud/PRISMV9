## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Process Physics, Dielectric Hydrodynamics, and Metrology of Gap Phenomena**

World-leading WEDM expertise next requires mastery of sub-millisecond discharge channel physics, dielectric fluid breakdown kinetics, plasma channel expansion under high-pressure flushing, and quantitative debris evacuation hydrodynamics. This layer addresses stochastic gap contamination, wire vibration modal analysis, and servo-controlled spark frequency adaptation beyond basic thermal recast models.

Key domains include:
- Dielectric strength recovery and deionization time constants as functions of pulse off-time, pressure, and contamination level.
- High-speed camera and piezoelectric force transducer studies of single-discharge plasma pressure waves and bubble dynamics.
- Wire electrode lateral vibration eigenmode modeling under tension, dielectric flow, and spark-induced shock loading.
- On-line pulse discrimination algorithms distinguishing effective sparks, short circuits, and open gaps via voltage/current waveform signature analysis.
- Surface integrity metrology specific to WEDM: white-layer microstructure, microcrack depth distribution, and residual stress gradients measured by XRD and incremental hole-drilling.

These topics are covered in advanced manufacturing physics curricula and specialized research.
