## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Machining Dynamics, Process Stability, and Chatter Avoidance**

A world-leading expert in lathe processes next masters the predictive modeling of regenerative chatter, forced vibration, and stability lobe diagrams (SLDs) derived from frequency response functions (FRFs) of the tool-workpiece-machine system. This includes oriented transfer function (OTF) analysis, eigenvalue solutions for stability boundaries, and the effect of process damping at low cutting speeds. Theory centers on the delay-differential equation (DDE) formulation of chip thickness variation, Nyquist stability criterion applied to the closed-loop cutting system, and semi-discretization or full-discretization methods for solving time-periodic DDEs.

Key methods include:
- Measurement and modal fitting of tool-tip FRFs using impact hammer testing and experimental modal analysis.
- Construction of stability lobe diagrams in the spindle-speed vs. depth-of-cut plane.
- Analytical and numerical prediction of chatter frequencies and lobe hopping.
- Incorporation of variable pitch tools, spindle speed variation (SSV), and active/passive damping techniques.
- Surface location error (SLE) prediction due to forced vibration.

This layer is safety-critical: unstable cuts produce excessive forces, tool fracture, workpiece damage, and potential machine crashes.
