## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer centers on forward-secure key-evolving signatures with optimal reductions, compact/dynamic POR constructions supporting block-level updates with homomorphic authenticators, and the OAIS reference model for systematic long-term preservation planning. These supply concrete methodology for balancing audit bandwidth, repair under sleepy nodes, semantic migration across generations, and UC-style composability when clients remain dormant for decades while data must retain both bit-level integrity and interpretability.

**forward-secure signatures & key evolution** | Bellare & Miner (1999); optimal security reductions for key-update algorithms resistant to compromise over long horizons.  
**dynamic/compact POR** | Shacham & Waters (2008), Erway et al. (2009); BLS-based homomorphic tags plus Merkle-tree indexing for logarithmic verification and insertions on dormant archives.  
**OAIS archival framework** | CCSDS 650.0-M-2 / ISO 14721 (2012); functional model, information packages (SIP/AIP/DIP), representation networks, and preservation planning to guarantee usability beyond cryptographic lifetimes.
