## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Advanced Thermo-Elastic Rotor-Bearing Interaction and Metrology under Transient Multiphysics Loading**

A world-leading expert next masters the coupled transient thermo-elastic rotor dynamics of spindle-bearing-housing systems under time-varying cutting loads, including non-uniform heat generation in angular contact bearings, thermal preload variation, and its effect on nonlinear bearing stiffness and shaft centerline motion. This layer focuses on finite-element rotordynamic models that incorporate thermo-mechanical bearing boundary conditions derived from ISO standards, Kalman-filter or observer-based real-time thermal state estimation, and uncertainty propagation per GUM and ISO/TR frameworks.

Key topics include:
- Thermo-hydrodynamic lubrication models for high-speed angular contact ball bearings under combined radial, axial, and moment loading.
- Transient thermal preload prediction and its influence on rotordynamic stability margins.
- In-situ spindle error motion measurement and separation techniques under simultaneous thermal and mechanical loading.
- Model-order reduction techniques for real-time thermo-elastic compensation in precision CNC lathes.
