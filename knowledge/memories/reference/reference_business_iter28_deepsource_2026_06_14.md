## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)

Rochet and Tirole’s models specify optimal price structures and cross-subsidies across user groups to internalize network effects and solve chicken-egg coordination. Cohen and Levinthal’s absorptive capacity theory details the micro-processes (recognition, assimilation, exploitation) required to convert external platform complementor innovations into proprietary advantage.
