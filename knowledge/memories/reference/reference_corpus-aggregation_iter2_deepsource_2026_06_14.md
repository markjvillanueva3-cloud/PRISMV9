## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Advanced Theory & Systems for Scalable, Privacy-Aware Corpus Aggregation**

A world-leading expert in corpus-aggregation next masters formal models for data integration at web scale, probabilistic reasoning over heterogeneous corpora, and polystore/query-optimization architectures that respect both semantic heterogeneity and differential privacy.

Key uncovered areas include:

- **Knowledge Graph Construction & Canonicalization at Scale**: Techniques for automated schema alignment, entity linking, and relation extraction over massive, noisy corpora using embedding-based and graph neural methods (Koutra & Faloutsos, 2015; Dong et al., 2014 follow-on work).
- **Probabilistic Data Integration & Inference**: Mastery of factor-graph and Markov Logic Network approaches to jointly solve entity resolution, schema mapping, and data cleaning with uncertainty propagation (Getoor & Taskar, 2007; Singla & Domingos, 2006).
- **Polystore & Federated Query Optimization**: Advanced cost-based optimization across heterogeneous stores (RDBMS, array DBs, graph DBs, document stores) and incremental view maintenance in polystore environments (Tan et al., 2017; Duggan et al., 2015).
- **Privacy-Preserving Record Linkage & Secure Aggregation**: Differential-privacy mechanisms for corpus aggregation, secure multi-party computation for entity resolution, and formal privacy-utility tradeoffs (Dwork & Roth, 2014; Kifer & Machanavajjhala, 2011).
- **Provenance & Lineage in Heterogeneous Environments**: Fine-grained provenance models beyond basic annotations, including why-provenance, where-provenance, and dependency tracking across polystores (Cheney et al., 2009; Glavic, 2012).

These topics appear in graduate-level courses on probabilistic databases, advanced data integration, and privacy-preserving data management, as well as recent IEEE/ACM conference literature on scalable data systems.
