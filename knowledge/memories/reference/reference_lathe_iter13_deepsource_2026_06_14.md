**Next Layer: Nonlinear Regenerative Chatter with State-Dependent Delay, Thermomechanical Coupling, and Stochastic Safe-Zone Mapping under In-Process Metrology**

World-leading expertise advances to **bifurcation analysis of subcritical Hopf bifurcations**, construction of **bit excursion maps**, and experimental mapping of **unsafe zones** in the presence of state-dependent delay, process damping, and thermomechanical effects. This includes numerical continuation techniques for direct identification of unstable periodic orbits and their basins, stochastic collocation and Gaussian-process-enhanced stability lobes that incorporate real-time metrology feedback, and inverse identification methods that fuse laser tool-tip metrology with frequency-domain and time-domain solvers under combined thermomechanical loading.

Key theory covers:
- Chebyshev collocation versus semi-discretization for state-dependent delay differential equations (DDEs)
- Nonlinear time-periodic DDE solvers with thermomechanical coupling
- Probabilistic stability boundaries under parametric uncertainty using Gaussian processes updated by in-process measurements
- Experimental construction of unsafe zones via controlled bit-excursion mapping and numerical continuation on physical turning systems
- Closed-loop metrology-driven stability control architectures that shift stability limits in real time

These methods supersede classical linear stability lobe diagrams by quantifying the extent of bistable regions, subcritical chatter onset, and safe operating envelopes under realistic process damping and thermal expansion.
