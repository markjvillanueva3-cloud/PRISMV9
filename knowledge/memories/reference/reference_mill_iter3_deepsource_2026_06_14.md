## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer (Iteration 3 – SAFETY-CRITICAL Mill Galaxy)**

**Stability Lobe & Time-Delay Theory – Higher-Order Methods**  
- Full-discretization method (FDM) for direct integration of the delayed differential equation in state-space form, including multiple time-delays and runout.  
- Chebyshev collocation and spectral element methods for periodic DDEs applied to interrupted cutting.  
- Updated semi-discretization with higher-order interpolation and adaptive step-size control.

**Source:** Ding et al. (2010) “A full-discretization method for prediction of milling stability,” Int. J. Mach. Tools Manuf.

**Process Damping & Nonlinear Regenerative Effects**  
- Mechanistic identification of process-damping coefficients from tool-workpiece contact geometry and indentation force model.  
- Nonlinear stability analysis using multiple-regenerative effects and bifurcation tracking in milling.

**Source:** Budak & Tunc (2009) “A new method for identification and modeling of process damping in machining,” ASME J. Manuf. Sci. Eng.

**Machine Tool – Spindle & Joint Dynamics**  
- Receptance coupling substructure analysis (RCSA) extended to non-linear tool-holder interfaces and spindle-bearing thermal effects.  
- Experimental modal analysis standards and uncertainty propagation for FRF measurement in milling.

**Source:** Schmitz & Duncan (2005) “Three-component receptance coupling substructure analysis for tool point dynamics prediction,” ASME J. Manuf. Sci. Eng.

**Metrology & Performance Standards**  
- ISO 230-1:2012 test code for machine tools – geometric accuracy of machines operating under no-load or quasi-static conditions.  
- ASME B5.57-2012 Methods for performance evaluation of CNC rotary tables.  
- NIST IR 8260 (2022) – “Best Practices for Uncertainty Evaluation of Machining Center Dynamic Performance.”

**High-Performance Machining Physics**  
- Variable pitch and variable helix cutter stability prediction using Floquet theory and parallel slicing.  
- Thermal error modeling and compensation per ISO 230-3 and NASA machining protocols for aerospace alloys.
