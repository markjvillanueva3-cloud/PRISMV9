Key uncovered areas include:

- **Formal diagrammatic reasoning systems** and their soundness/completeness properties
- **Algebraic semiotics and sign systems** for evaluating representation quality
- **Cognitive dimensions applied to formal visual notations** with measurable metrics
- **Visual specification languages** in distributed and concurrent system modeling
