**Next Layer: Stochastic Delay Differential Equations with State-Dependent Switching and Non-Gaussian Lévy Noise for Safety-Critical Milling Stability**

World-leading experts advance beyond semi-discretization, polynomial chaos, persistent homology, and Bayesian boundary updating by mastering **stochastic DDEs with state-dependent regenerative delays, stochastic averaging under combined parametric and Lévy-type noise**, and **measure-theoretic uncertainty propagation** for guaranteed stability margins in safety-critical milling.

Core topics include:
- **Stochastic averaging and moment mapping** for DDEs with time-periodic coefficients and additive/multiplicative white noise (Khasawneh & Mann, 2013–2016 extensions).
- **Numerical continuation and pseudo-arclength methods** for tracking stochastic stability boundaries in parameter space of interrupted cutting systems (Dombovari et al.).
- **Lévy process modeling** of intermittent cutting forces and tool-workpiece impacts, replacing Gaussian assumptions with alpha-stable distributions for rare but catastrophic events.
- **Measure-theoretic probability** and **Donsker-Varadhan large deviation principles** applied to escape times from stable milling attractors (NIST-aligned advanced propagation).
- **Verification & validation under uncertainty (VVUQ)** frameworks per ASME V&V 20-2009 and NASA-STD-7009 for safety-critical CNC processes.

These methods enable provable safety margins when Gaussian or PCE assumptions break under highly interrupted, low-damping, or hard-to-model material conditions.
