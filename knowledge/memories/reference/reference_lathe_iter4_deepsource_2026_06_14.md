## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Turning Dynamics & Process Stability (Post-2020 Depth)**

World-leading expertise next requires mastery of:

- Time-domain simulation of regenerative chatter in turning with variable spindle speed and nonlinear process damping (non-smooth delay differential equations with state-dependent delay).
- Stability prediction for multi-directional coupled turning systems including tool nose radius, 3D chip flow, and oblique cutting force directionality.
- In-process identification of dynamic cutting force coefficients and process damping via inverse stability solution and operational modal analysis on the rotating workpiece.
- High-speed turning stability lobes accounting for gyroscopic effects, bearing nonlinearities, and thermo-mechanical spindle growth.
- Uncertainty quantification in stability boundaries due to material property variation, tool wear, and measurement noise using polynomial chaos expansion and robust optimization.
- ISO and ASME-compliant test procedures for dynamic compliance and chatter-free parameter selection on CNC lathes, including finished-part accuracy under dynamic loading.
- Application of Chebyshev collocation and full-discretization methods tailored to turning’s periodic delay structure (distinct from milling).

These build directly on semi-discretization and frequency-domain methods already covered.
