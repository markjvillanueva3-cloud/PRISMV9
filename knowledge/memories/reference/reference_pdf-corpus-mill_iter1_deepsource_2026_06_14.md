**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Document Engineering & Corpus Construction for PDF-native Knowledge Bases**

A world-leading expert in pdf-corpus-mill must master the theory and tooling layers that turn raw PDF streams into high-fidelity, semantically structured corpora suitable for downstream ML, retrieval, and manufacturing-intelligence pipelines.

**Core uncovered competencies include:**
- PDF document model internals (ISO 32000-2:2020 object syntax, incremental updates, cross-reference streams, content streams, marked content, and Tagged PDF / PDF/UA structure trees).
- Precise extraction pipelines that preserve layout semantics via spatial indexing and reading-order reconstruction rather than simple text scraping.
- Standards-driven document understanding: PDF/A-3, PDF/UA-2, WCAG 2.2 mapping to PDF tags, and the interplay between logical structure and presentation.
- Corpus construction methodologies: deduplication at document, passage, and structural-element levels; provenance tracking; version-aware ingestion; and creation of hybrid corpora that retain both raster, vector, and semantic layers.
- Evaluation frameworks for extraction fidelity (character accuracy, reading-order F1, table-structure metrics, figure-caption attachment) drawn from IR and document AI literature.

**Key next-layer knowledge domains:**
- PostScript/PDF imaging model and operator semantics (Adobe PostScript Language Reference, 3rd ed., 1999; ISO 32000-2).
- Layout-aware parsing algorithms and heuristics used in production-grade tools.
- Creation and validation of PDF/UA compliant tagged documents for reliable semantic corpus generation.
- Scalable corpus-building pipelines using Apache PDFBox, PDFium, or MuPDF with custom structural post-processing.
- NIST and IEEE work on document image analysis metrics that translate directly to corpus quality scoring.
