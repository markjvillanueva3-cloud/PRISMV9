## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Constraint Solvers, Variational Modeling, and Persistent Naming in Parametric CAD Systems**

A world-leading expert next masters the algebraic and numerical foundations of geometric constraint solving, the theory of variational boundary representation, and persistent naming mechanisms that guarantee topological robustness under parametric editing.

Key topics include:
- Degrees-of-freedom analysis and solution selectors in variational geometric constraint systems
- Graph-based decomposition algorithms (maximum matching, rigid clusters) and their complexity
- Gröbner-basis and homotopy-continuation methods versus Newton–Raphson with sparse matrix techniques
- Parametric continuity (C0–C1) between feature histories and B-rep updates
- Persistent naming schemes (cell, face, edge identifiers) that survive topological modifications
- Semantic feature modeling and design intent preservation beyond pure geometry

**Institutional depth**
- Owen’s foundational work on algebraic constraint solving and the commercial solver approach used in most CAD kernels
- Hoffmann’s later extensions on constraint decomposition by degree-of-freedom analysis
- The NIST-funded “Persistent Naming” project results that influenced ASME and ISO standards committees
- Formal semantics of feature-based modeling and the role of constructive solid geometry (CSG) trees versus boundary evaluation order

This layer sits between basic solid modeling (Shapiro 2002) and applied evolutionary solvers (Bettig & Shah 2010), providing the mathematical engine that makes parametric history-based CAD both robust and editable.
