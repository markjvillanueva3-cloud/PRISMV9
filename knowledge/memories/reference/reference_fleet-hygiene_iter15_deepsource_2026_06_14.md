**Next Layer: Quantitative Hyperproperty Inference, Epistemic Fleet Repair, and Stochastic Game-Theoretic Enforcement**

A world-leading fleet-hygiene expert next masters **statistical inference of hyperproperties from observed fleet traces** and **game-theoretic repair strategies** under partial observability. This layer extends beyond monitoring and synthesis to active inference of unknown quantitative hyperproperties across heterogeneous distributed configurations and coordinated repair via stochastic multi-agent games.

Key areas include:

- **Hyperproperty inference and learning**: algorithms that infer both qualitative and quantitative hyperproperties directly from execution traces of large fleets when explicit specifications are unavailable.  
- **Epistemic hyperproperty repair**: techniques that synthesize repairs while reasoning about agents’ knowledge of other agents’ states (common knowledge, distributed knowledge).  
- **Stochastic game models for fleet enforcement**: modeling configuration repair as turn-based or concurrent stochastic games where the environment and multiple fleet nodes act adversarially or cooperatively, with quantitative objectives (mean-payoff, ratio, percentile).  
- **Compositional verification of hyperproperties under uncertainty**: using assume-guarantee reasoning and abstraction-refinement for quantitative hyperproperties in partially observable Markov decision processes (POMDPs).
