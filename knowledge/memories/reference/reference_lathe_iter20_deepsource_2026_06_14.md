## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Nonlinear Thermo-Mechanical Spindle-Bearing Interaction and Process-Driven Stability in Hard Turning**

World-leading experts advance to coupled nonlinear thermo-elasto-hydrodynamic (TEHD) bearing models that capture transient preload migration, thermo-viscous feedback, and their bidirectional interaction with regenerative chatter under variable cutting stiffness. This includes:

- **Thermally coupled bearing stiffness and damping matrices** derived from Reynolds equation solutions with temperature-dependent viscosity and thermal expansion of inner/outer rings and shaft (Palmgren-type extensions to transient cases).
- **Nonlinear time-varying delay differential equations (DDEs)** for turning that incorporate spindle compliance, bearing nonlinearity, and process damping from flank-wave interference at low speeds.
- **Stability lobe generation using semi-discretization or full-discretization methods** that embed measured or simulated spindle frequency response functions (FRFs) updated in real time by thermal preload state.
- **In-process thermal error compensation via state observers** (Kalman or Luenberger) driven by bearing temperature and spindle power, accounting for non-stationary thermal time constants.
- **Metrology and uncertainty propagation** for combined error motion, thermal growth, and dynamic compliance under simultaneous thermal and cutting loads per extended ASME and ISO frameworks.

This layer sits beyond static thermal error mapping or linear regenerative theory and is required for predictive digital twins in safety-critical hard-turning applications.
