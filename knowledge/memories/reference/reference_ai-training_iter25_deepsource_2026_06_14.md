## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer in ai-training mastery is machine learning systems design for scalable, efficient, and trustworthy training at production scale. This integrates 3D parallelism (data + pipeline + tensor), mixed-precision arithmetic with loss scaling, communication-efficient federated optimization, sparsity/pruning schedules, and embedding NIST risk functions (map/measure/manage) directly into training pipelines for bias, robustness, and privacy controls. It extends covered NTK/mean-field theory and scaling laws with numerical stability analysis under quantization noise, memory-communication trade-offs, and lifecycle standards for auditability—emphasizing end-to-end engineering beyond pure optimization or statistical generalization.
