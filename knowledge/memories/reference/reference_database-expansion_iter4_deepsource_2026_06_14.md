**Next Layer: Automated Elasticity, Formal Models, and Verifiable Reconfiguration**

A world-leading expert in database-expansion next masters formal methods for **predictive elasticity**, control-theoretic approaches to scaling, and verifiable online reconfiguration protocols that guarantee consistency and SLOs under dynamic workloads.

Key areas include:
- **Control theory for database elasticity**: feedback-driven provisioning using PID controllers and model predictive control (MPC) adapted to transactional systems.
- **Formal verification of elastic transitions**: using TLA+ and refinement proofs to ensure safety during shard splits, merges, and live migrations.
- **Workload forecasting and proactive scaling**: integration of time-series models (ARIMA, LSTM) with query graph analysis for anticipatory elasticity.
- **Elasticity-aware query optimization**: cost models that incorporate reconfiguration latency and transient inconsistency windows.
- **Policy languages and declarative elasticity**: frameworks for specifying SLO-driven scaling rules with formal semantics.
