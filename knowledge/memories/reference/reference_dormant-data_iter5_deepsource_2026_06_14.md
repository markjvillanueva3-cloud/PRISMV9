## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Economic Models, Policy Formalization, and Long-Term Bitstream Integrity in Distributed Archival Systems**

A world-leading expert in dormant-data advances beyond OAIS, PROV-DM, LOCKSS, and basic bit-preservation reliability by mastering formal economic lifecycle costing, verifiable policy languages for preservation processes, and mathematically-grounded integrity mechanisms that scale over decades under explicit threat and cost models.

Core next-layer knowledge includes:

- **Economic modeling of indefinite retention**: Understanding TCO models that incorporate media obsolescence, energy, migration labor, and actuarial risk rather than simple MTTDL. This draws on stochastic renewal theory applied to storage fleets.

- **Formal specification and model-checking of preservation policies**: Using process algebras, temporal logics, and security-aware workflow languages to verify that archival policies (ingest, AIP management, dissemination) are free of deadlock, unauthorized disclosure, or policy violation over infinite time horizons.

- **Advanced cryptographic and information-theoretic approaches to long-term integrity**: Techniques such as forward-secure signatures, proof-of-retrievability protocols adapted for cold storage, and locally decodable codes that tolerate both bit-rot and Byzantine repository failure.

These topics are treated rigorously in graduate distributed-systems curricula, formal-methods literature, and specialized digital-preservation economics research.
