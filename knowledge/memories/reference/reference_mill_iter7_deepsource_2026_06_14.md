## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Regenerative Chatter Modeling, Nonlinear Process Damping, Multi-Dimensional Stability, and Uncertainty in High-Speed Milling**

World-leading experts advance beyond semi-discretization and basic variable-speed methods into:

- **Full-discretization methods** for periodic delay-differential equations with multiple regenerative effects (Ding et al., 2010).
- **Nonlinear process damping models** incorporating flank-wave contact geometry and velocity-dependent damping at low speeds (Huang & Wang, 2010; Grossi, 2020).
- **Multi-frequency solution** of the milling stability problem accounting for harmonics of the tooth-passing frequency (Merdol & Altintas, 2004 extended; Budak & Altintas, 1998).
- **Time-domain simulation with nonlinear cutter-workpiece engagement** and trochoidal paths for low-radial-immersion and 5-axis cases (Ferry & Altintas, 2008; Lazoglu et al., 2011).
- **Probabilistic stability prediction** using Monte-Carlo, polynomial chaos, or Bayesian inference on uncertain modal parameters and cutting coefficients (Zaloga et al., 2020 extension; Hamadi et al., 2018).
- **ISO/ASME uncertainty evaluation frameworks** applied to FRF measurements and stability lobe identification (ASME B5.54-2005 Annex, ISO/TS 230-11:2016).
- **Chebyshev collocation and spectral element methods** for high-order accurate DDE solution in milling (Khasawneh & Mann, 2011; Butcher et al., 2013).
- **Stability of variable-pitch and variable-helix tools** using clustered semi-discretization or Floquet multipliers on averaged systems (Otte et al., 2022; Sims, 2014).

These layers integrate nonlinear dynamics, advanced numerical delay solvers, and metrological uncertainty propagation required for safety-critical aerospace/medical milling digital twins.
