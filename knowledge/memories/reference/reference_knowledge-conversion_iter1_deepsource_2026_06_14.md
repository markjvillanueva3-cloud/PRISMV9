## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Knowledge Representation, Acquisition, and Formal Conversion Theory**

A world-leading expert in knowledge-conversion must next master formal models for elicitation, representation, and verifiable transformation of tacit/explicit knowledge within software systems. This layer focuses on structured knowledge acquisition methodologies, ontological engineering, and semantic interoperability techniques drawn from AI and software engineering.

Core areas include:
- **CommonKADS** methodology for structured knowledge modeling and conversion between human and computational forms.
- **Ontology engineering** (OWL, description logics) for formal semantic conversion and reuse.
- **Knowledge elicitation protocols** ( repertory grids, laddering, protocol analysis) and their validation.
- **Model-Driven Knowledge Engineering** and transformation rules between knowledge models and executable software artifacts.
- **Semantic Web technologies** and linked-data principles for scalable knowledge conversion pipelines.
- **Knowledge graph construction** and embedding techniques for automated conversion.
