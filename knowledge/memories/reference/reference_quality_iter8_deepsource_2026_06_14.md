**Next Layer: Advanced Software Quality Modeling, Economic Valuation, and Empirical Synthesis**

A world-leading expert next masters **quantitative quality economics**, **architecture-driven quality prediction**, **Bayesian and stochastic quality modeling**, and **meta-analytic synthesis of quality evidence**. This layer integrates value-based decision making under uncertainty with rigorous empirical aggregation beyond single SLR guidelines.

Key uncovered areas include:
- **Value-Based Software Quality** and utility functions that translate quality attributes into monetary value for tradeoff decisions.
- **Software Architecture-based Quality Prediction** using analytic models (e.g., CBAM extensions, ATAM quantitative variants).
- **Bayesian Statistical Methods** for software reliability growth, defect prediction, and quality inference with limited data.
- **Meta-analysis and Evidence-Based Aggregation** techniques tailored to software engineering experiments, including effect-size synthesis and heterogeneity assessment.
- **Technical Debt Quantification** models that link architectural degradation to future quality and maintenance cost.

These build directly on prior SEI, Boehm, Madachy, and Fenton foundations by adding formal economic utility, probabilistic inference, and statistical meta-research rigor.
