## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Architectural Decision Records (ADR) as a Core Curriculum Governance Mechanism and Their Integration into Cognitive Task Analysis for Curriculum Evolution**

A world-leading expert masters the systematic use of ADRs not merely as lightweight documentation but as a first-class, evolvable artifact for curriculum architecture governance, traceability of pedagogical trade-offs, and continuous refactoring—directly analogous to their role in software architecture. This includes formal ADR templates tailored for learning systems, decision-capture processes integrated with cognitive task analysis (CTA), and quantitative evaluation of decision fitness within 4+1 or decision-view models applied to curriculum.

Key next-layer knowledge includes:
- **ADR metamodels** and their application to curriculum patterns (template extensions from IEEE 42010 viewpoints).
- **Decision capture workshops** fused with CTA methods to elicit expert instructor trade-offs (e.g., cognitive load vs. authenticity).
- **Refactoring catalogs** for curriculum ADRs, including technical debt metrics adapted from software architecture literature.
- **Empirical evaluation** of ADR-driven curriculum evolution using longitudinal failure-rate data and cognitive load instrumentation.

This layer sits above the already-covered Seemann & Tartsch (2021), Lucke et al. (2022/2023), Krause-Levy et al. (2021), and basic MIT 6.170 ADR usage.
