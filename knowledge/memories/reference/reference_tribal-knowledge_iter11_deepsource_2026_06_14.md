## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Institutional Logics, Infrastructure Studies & Organizational Forgetting – Advanced Theory & Methods**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s tribal-knowledge galaxy next masters the micro-foundations of institutional complexity, the performativity of classificatory infrastructures, and the deliberate engineering of organizational forgetting within sociotechnical systems.

Key next-layer knowledge includes:

- **Institutional complexity and hybridity** at the field level, including how multiple logics are selectively coupled or compartmentalized inside organizations (Greenwood et al., 2011 already listed; next: Smets et al., 2015 on “Recombination”).
- **Performativity and infrastructural inversion** in classification systems and standards, extending Bowker’s datadiversity work into active infrastructure enactment (Lampland & Star, 2009; Busch, 2011).
- **Methods for tracing infrastructural becoming** over long durations using “following the actors” combined with archival trace ethnography (Anand, 2015; Jensen & Winthereik, 2013).
- **Organizational forgetting as active institutional work**: not mere decay but strategic erasure, mnemonic displacement, and “designed amnesia” in enterprise knowledge systems (de Vaujany et al. already listed; next: Anteby & Molnár, 2012 on “Collective Forgetting”).
- **Standards-as-institutions** (ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010 already listed; next depth: Timmermans & Epstein, 2010 on “standardization in action” and the STS of formal standards).

These layers move from descriptive institutional theory to interventionist, design-oriented understanding of how knowledge infrastructures are actively maintained, repaired, or intentionally forgotten inside complex software platforms.
