## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Post-Processor Kinematics, Compensation, and Safety-Critical Error Propagation in Multi-Axis Machining**

A world-leading post-processor expert masters the theoretical foundations of **machine-tool kinematic modeling**, homogeneous transformation matrices (HTM), and singular configurations in 5-axis toolpath conversion before numerical constants are applied.

Core topics include:
- Forward and inverse kinematics for serial and parallel kinematic machines using Denavit-Hartenberg parameters and screw theory.
- Rotary axis mapping, pivot distance compensation, and tool-center-point (TCP) vs. tool-tip programming.
- Trajectory blending, look-ahead buffering, and velocity/jerk limiting to prevent axis overshoot or servo following-error violations.
- Post-processor generation of safe machine-code structures compliant with ISO 6983 (G-code), ISO 14649 (STEP-NC), and ASME B5.50 toolholder standards.
- Kinematic error mapping, thermal error compensation, and volumetric error modeling per ASME B5.54 and ISO 230 series.
- Collision detection via swept-volume algorithms and real-time kinematic singularity avoidance.
- Cutter radius compensation (CRC) vector calculations and dynamic tool-length offset (TLO) handling under ISO 6983-1.

These layers draw from manufacturing-physics principles of rigid-body kinematics, control theory, and metrology to ensure the post-processor never generates motion that violates machine safety limits or induces chatter/vibration beyond stability lobes (theory only).
