## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER (Iteration 9 – SAFETY-CRITICAL WEDM Galaxy):**  
Multi-physics coupling of magneto-hydrodynamic plasma, wire electrode thermal softening, dielectric breakdown statistics, and subsurface microstructure evolution under high-frequency pulsed discharge. Focus on stochastic discharge probability models, cathode/anode energy partition under varying gap conditions, crystallographic texture and white-layer phase transformation kinetics in nickel-based superalloys and titanium alloys, plus standardized metrology of sub-surface damage using EBSD and synchrotron XRD.

**Key uncovered domains a world-leading expert masters:**

- Statistical discharge location and probability density functions derived from gap voltage/current stochastic analysis (Kunieda & Lauwers, 2009).  
- Energy partition ratios at anode/cathode as function of pulse on-time and dielectric conductivity (Xia et al., 1996; Zahiruddin & Kunieda, 2012).  
- Thermal-metallurgical modeling of white-layer formation, recast-layer cracking susceptibility, and heat-affected zone microstructure using Johnson-Mehl-Avrami-Kolmogorov kinetics linked to EDM plasma heat flux (Cabanettes et al., 2018).  
- EBSD-based quantification of plastic strain gradients and residual stress depth profiles induced by WEDM in aerospace alloys (NASA TP-2023-0018).  
- Dielectric strength recovery and deionization time constants under high-velocity flushing – linkage to pulse frequency limit and wire vibration eigenmodes (MIT OCW 2.854 Spring 2022 Lecture 17).  
- ISO 25178-6:2012 and ASME B46.1-2019 areal surface texture standards applied to WEDM-induced lay and peak-count parameters for fatigue-critical components.  
- Synchrotron high-energy XRD residual stress mapping and lattice strain tensor measurement in WEDM surfaces (NIST IR 8487, 2023).
