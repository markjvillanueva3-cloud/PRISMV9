## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer masters sensemaking under ambiguity, experience-factory capitalization of project knowledge, and intellectual-capital measurement to make tribal knowledge auditable and reusable in software organizations. Weick (1995) supplies theory on retrospective/prospective sensemaking processes that convert equivocal cues into actionable shared understanding for distributed engineering teams. Basili et al. (1994) institutionalize the Experience Factory as a concrete, iterative methodology (collect-analyze-package-reuse loop) that packages tacit lessons from software builds into organizational assets, extending beyond CoP or CMMI practices. Edvinsson & Malone (1997) add valuation models (human/structural/relational capital) with metrics such as the Skandia Navigator, enabling KM ROI tracking aligned with strategic capabilities.
