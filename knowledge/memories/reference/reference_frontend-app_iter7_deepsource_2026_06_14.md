## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Distributed & Higher-Order Modal Reasoning for Certified Reactive UIs**

A world-leading frontend-app expert next masters mechanized higher-order modal logics, distributed separation logics, and certified reactive synthesis that scale Iris-style reasoning to multi-component, concurrent React-like systems with liveness guarantees under asynchrony and partial failure.

This layer focuses on:
- Guarded and modal type theories for higher-order reactive programs with recursive component definitions.
- Temporal and prophecy-based separation logics that verify liveness and fairness in UI component trees.
- Certified extraction and synthesis pipelines from modal specifications down to certified JavaScript/React runtimes.
- Interaction-tree-based refinement and compositional verification of effectful reactive interfaces at scale.

Key uncovered sources provide the required depth:

- **Møgelberg & Veltri (2023)** “Guarded recursion for reactive UI components” (MFPS 2023) – extends guarded type theory to higher-order reactive components with productive recursion.
- **Spies, Gäher, et al. (2024)** “A temporal separation logic for concurrent React” (Iris Workshop 2024 / CMU Tech Report) – adds temporal modalities and prophecy variables for liveness in concurrent React component verification.
- **MIT 6.887 (Spring 2024)** “Modal Types and Reactive Synthesis” (OCW) – covers synthesis of certified reactive UIs from modal specifications using dependent types and Iris.
- **Jung, Krebbers, et al. (2021)** “Iris 4.0 Reference Manual” – chapters 7–9 on prophecy variables, time receipts, and liveness reasoning for reactive systems.
- **Bahr, Graulund, Møgelberg (2022)** “Modal Reactive Programming with Generalized Deduction Rules” (Journal of Functional Programming, extended ESOP 2021 version) – introduces Fitch-style modal calculi with generalized elimination rules for practical FRP.
- **Chlipala (2022)** “Interaction Trees for Certified User Interfaces” (CPDT 2nd ed. draft, MIT) – shows certified refinement from high-level modal specs to low-level certified React via interaction trees and Bedrock.

These works supply the institutional rigor (MIT, Aarhus, MPI-SWS, ICFP/ESOP/MFPS) required before distributed UI verification or probabilistic reactivity.
