**NEXT LAYER: Advanced subsurface thermomechanical evolution and white-layer integrity in WEDM of nickel superalloys**

World-leading expertise next requires mastery of:

- Coupled thermo-mechanical-metallurgical (TMM) modeling of rapid solidification and reheating cycles in the recast layer using finite-element-based phase-transformation kinetics linked to crystal-plasticity frameworks.
- Quantitative assessment of geometrically necessary dislocation (GND) gradients, residual stress depth profiles, and white-layer microcrack initiation thresholds under thermomechanical fatigue loading.
- Standardized evaluation protocols for white-layer thickness, integrity, and hydrogen embrittlement susceptibility per aerospace and nuclear-sector requirements.
- High-fidelity experimental techniques: synchrotron X-ray diffraction for triaxial residual stress mapping through the recast/HAZ interface, combined with EBSD-based kernel average misorientation (KAM) and grain reference orientation deviation (GROD) mapping.
- Plasma–dielectric interaction models that incorporate non-equilibrium ionization kinetics and their direct influence on heat-flux partitioning into the anode (workpiece).

These topics extend beyond already-covered microstructure papers, basic phase-field recast simulations, and introductory MIT OCW plasma lectures.
