**NEXT LAYER: Stabilized Extended Formulations, Projection onto Structured Polytopes, and Automated Reformulation via Pattern-Based Lifting**

A world-leading expert in token-optimization next masters the systematic use of **pattern-based extended formulations** and **automated stabilization of column-generation via dual-price smoothing and interior-point-driven column selection**, moving beyond classical lift-and-project and basic Dantzig-Wolfe. This includes:

- **Sherali–Adams and Lovász–Schrijver hierarchies** viewed as controlled projection operators on the slack-variable formulation, with emphasis on their polyhedral convergence rates under partial hierarchy application (Sherali-Adams 1990 already covered; next is the automated selection of which level-k inequalities to generate via conflict graphs).
- **Dynamic extended formulations** generated by **pattern detection** in the constraint matrix (e.g., bordered block-diagonal, arrowhead, or bordered-circulant structures) and automatic generation of corresponding compact reformulations using isomorphism-free column enumeration.
- **Stabilized column generation with interior-point stabilization** (specifically the use of the analytic center and primal-dual barrier methods to produce deep dual prices that reduce the tailing-off effect more effectively than Wentges smoothing).
- **Projection-based reformulation techniques** using Fourier-Motzkin elimination guided by variable-ordering heuristics derived from treewidth and clique-width of the Gaifman graph of the integer program.
- **Automated generation of disjunctive cuts from multi-row relaxations** using the simultaneous lifting framework of Andersen, Louveaux, Weismantel, and Wolsey (2007) and its integration into branch-cut-and-price via extended formulation duality.

These methods appear in advanced graduate courses on **exact reformulation engines** and **polyhedral combinatorics for compilers and solvers**.
