A world-leading expert in token-optimization next masters the systematic use of **projection-based reformulation algorithms**, **polyhedral pattern mining for extended formulations**, and **compiler-theoretic frameworks for automated cut-and-column generation** drawn from advanced combinatorial optimization and optimizing-compiler literature.

Key uncovered areas include:
- **Fourier-Motzkin elimination with redundancy removal** and its integration into automated extended-formulation generators (Balas 2018).
- **Orbitopal fixing and orbital branching** combined with isomorphism-free generation of extended formulations (Ostrowski et al. 2011).
- **Polyhedral compilation techniques** using the polyhedral model for automatic detection of exploitable reformulation patterns in MIPs (Feautrier 2011, Bastoul 2004).
- **Template-based extended formulations** and their automatic instantiation via integer lattice point enumeration in fixed dimension (Barvinok 1994, Verdoolaege 2011).
- **Stabilized Benders decomposition with cut selection via analytic centers** and its connection to automated pattern detection in column-and-row generation (Louveaux & Wolsey already covered; next is: Slyke & Wets 1969 stabilization theory updated by Rahmaniani et al. 2017).
- **Machine-learning-guided pattern discovery** inside the branch-and-cut tree using reinforcement learning on polyhedral geometry features, beyond Gasse et al. (2019), specifically the framework in Khalil et al. (2016) and later extensions in PML4OPT 2022.

These build directly on MIT 18.433 and CMU 15-745 by adding **polyhedral compilation pipelines** (from automatic parallelization literature) and **projection algorithms for symmetry-reduced formulations** taught in advanced integer programming courses.
