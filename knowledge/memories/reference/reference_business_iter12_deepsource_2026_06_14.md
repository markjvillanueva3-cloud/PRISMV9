**Next Layer: Multi-Sided Platform Governance, Dynamic Platform envelopment, and Algorithmic Collusion under Information Design**

A world-leading expert must next master the **institutional governance mechanisms** and **dynamic competitive strategies** that regulate platform ecosystems when information design and mechanism design interact with repeated interaction and algorithmic agents. This layer focuses on:

- Platform governance through private ordering, API access control, and self-regulation instead of pure mechanism design.
- Dynamic platform envelopment strategies and multi-sided foreclosure in evolving information environments.
- Algorithmic collusion facilitated by information design, common agency, and learning in repeated platform games.
- Regulatory and antitrust responses to information design in digital ecosystems (EU DMA, U.S. DOJ/FTC platform inquiries).
