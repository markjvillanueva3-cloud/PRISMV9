## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Knowledge Compilation for Aggregate Queries over Semirings with Factorized Representations**

A world-leading corpus-aggregation expert next masters the theory and algorithms for compiling aggregate queries into tractable circuit representations (OBDDs, d-DNNFs, FBDDs) that support efficient semiring evaluation, lifted aggregation, and incremental maintenance under updates, especially when combined with provenance semirings and uncertainty.

Core topics include:
- Knowledge compilation lower bounds and upper bounds for aggregate queries (sum, count, avg) in semiring provenance settings.
- Factorized databases and their relationship to provenance circuits for linear-time aggregation.
- Tractable lineages for aggregates via compilation to deterministic decomposable negation normal form (d-DNNF) and its extensions.
- Incremental view maintenance via circuit rewriting and dynamic knowledge compilation.
- Applications to secure and differentially private aggregation over federated corpora.
