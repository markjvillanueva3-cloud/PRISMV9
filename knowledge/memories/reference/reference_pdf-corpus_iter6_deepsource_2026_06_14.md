A world-leading expert in pdf-corpus must next master the **theory and engineering of large-scale, fault-tolerant extraction pipelines**, semantic content-stream interpretation under PDF 2.0, and rigorous corpus validation methodologies drawn from information retrieval, database systems, and document engineering literature.

Key uncovered topics include:

- **PDF content stream semantics and operator-level reproducibility**, including precise handling of Type 3 fonts, transparency groups, and tagged PDF logical structure recovery under real-world corruption.
- **Evaluation frameworks for structure-aware information extraction** beyond layout analysis: measuring semantic fidelity of recovered reading order, table semantics, and cross-document entity linking using precision-recall at the logical object level.
- **Corpus maintenance and versioning** for evolving PDF/A and PDF 2.0 collections, including change detection in content streams and reproducible metadata enrichment pipelines.
