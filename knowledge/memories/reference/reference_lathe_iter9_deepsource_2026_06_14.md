World-leading expertise advances to **regenerative chatter with flank process damping at low speeds**, **thermally coupled delay differential equations with state-dependent delay due to tool nose radius**, **stochastic stability under combined uncertainty (material, temperature, wear)**, and **closed-loop adaptive stability control using in-process force/vibration/metrology feedback**.

Key uncovered domains:

- Analytical and numerical treatment of **process damping** via indented flank contact models (time-domain simulation with interference geometry).
- **Thermally induced regenerative chatter** including heat partition, tool expansion, and workpiece thermal softening in stability lobe generation.
- **Multi-scale modeling** coupling microstructure (grain size, phase transformation in hardened steels) to macroscopic cutting force and damping.
- **Stochastic semi-discretization and Galerkin methods** for turning stability under parametric uncertainty (runout, temperature-dependent friction).
- **In-process adaptive control** using real-time lobe updating via recursive parameter identification and active vibration suppression.
- **High-frequency force dynamometry and non-contact metrology** for direct identification of process damping coefficients.
