## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: DRX–AS B Instability Coupling under Severe Plastic Deformation at Machining Strain Rates**

World-leading mastery next requires integration of **stored-energy-driven continuous dynamic recrystallization (CDRX) and discontinuous dynamic recrystallization (DDRX)** into linear perturbation stability analyses of adiabatic shear banding, using physics-based mobility functions calibrated to high-rate Kolsky-bar and Split-Hopkinson Pressure Bar (SHPB) data with in-situ high-speed infrared thermography.

**Key uncovered domains:**

- **Mecking & Kocks (1981)** evolution of dislocation density with concurrent recovery and recrystallization under large strain, high-rate deformation (one-parameter model extended to CDRX).
- **Rollett et al. (2004)** Monte-Carlo Potts model and vertex models for grain-boundary migration kinetics driven by stored-energy gradients in severe plastic deformation.
- **NIST IR 7917 (2013)** “High-Speed Machining Metrology for Verification of Thermo-Mechanical Models” – full-field DIC + IR uncertainty quantification protocols.
- **ASME Y14.5-2018** and **ISO 25178-2:2012** surface texture metrology standards applied to shear-band fracture surfaces for validation of DRX-induced damage.
- **Brown & Bammann (2012)** internal state variable (ISV) theory incorporating DRX volume fraction as an evolving scalar damage variable coupled to thermal softening ( viscoplastic self-consistent framework).
- **Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL) UCRL-TR-233043 (2007)** “Constitutive Models for Machining Simulations: Incorporating Dynamic Recrystallization” – hierarchical calibration using Taylor anvil, pressure-shear, and orthogonal cutting data.
- **Medvedev et al. (2018)** “Physics-based constitutive modelling of dynamic recrystallization in metallic materials under high strain rate deformation” – integration of Avrami-type kinetics with Molinari-type perturbation analysis (Int. J. Plast.).
- **MIT OCW 3.22 Mechanical Behavior of Materials (Spring 2022)** – modules on “Recrystallization under Extreme Conditions” and “Shear Localization in High-Rate Plasticity”.

These sources close the gap between phenomenological DRX softening and microstructure-aware stability criteria for safety-critical predictive machining simulations.
