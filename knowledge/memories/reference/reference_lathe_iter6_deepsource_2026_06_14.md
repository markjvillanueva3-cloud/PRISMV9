**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Nonlinear Thermo-Mechanical Stability, Process Damping at Low Speeds, and In-Process Metrology for Safety-Critical Turning**

World-leading expertise advances into state-dependent delay models with thermo-mechanical coupling, nonlinear process damping identification under variable chip thickness, and uncertainty propagation in stability lobe diagrams for hardened alloys. Key topics include:

- Chebyshev spectral collocation methods for periodic delay differential equations with nonlinear regenerative terms and temperature-dependent shear flow stress.
- Time-domain simulation of turning stability incorporating flank contact damping, ploughing forces, and velocity-dependent friction at low cutting speeds.
- In-process dynamic compliance identification using chatter test signals and operational modal analysis under actual thermomechanical loading.
- Propagation of cutting force coefficient uncertainty and modal parameter variation into probabilistic stability margins per NIST protocols.
