**Next Layer: Algorithmic, Model-Theoretic, and Practical Implementation Depth in Uniform Interpolation and Forgetting**

A world-leading expert next masters the precise algorithmic machinery, fixed-point characterizations, second-order quantifier elimination techniques, and implementation-level optimizations that move beyond theoretical decidability into scalable computation and verified tool chains.

Key uncovered topics include:

- Ackermann-style second-order quantifier elimination algorithms and their termination analysis for expressive DLs (ALC, ALCO, ALCI).
- Practical computation of uniform interpolants via resolution-based methods, hyperresolution, and ordered resolution with selection functions.
- Fixpoint-based characterizations of uniform interpolants and their use in computing strongest necessary and weakest sufficient conditions.
- Complexity-theoretic refinements: precise upper and lower bounds for uniform interpolation in fragments beyond ALC (including Horn and Horn-RegI).
- Implementation techniques: saturation-based forgetting engines, redundancy elimination, and modularization pipelines used in tools such as LETHE and MEX.
- Logical difference operators and their relationship to uniform interpolation for ontology versioning and diff computation.
- Conservative extension checking algorithms and their reduction to uniform interpolation.
