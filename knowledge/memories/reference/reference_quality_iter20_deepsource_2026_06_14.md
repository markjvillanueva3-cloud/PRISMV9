## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The subsequent tier in software quality expertise centers on empirical, operationalized measurement and process frameworks that translate abstract quality models (e.g., ISO 25010) and decision-theoretic foundations into repeatable organizational practice. Core elements include the Goal-Question-Metric (GQM) paradigm for deriving context-specific, goal-driven metrics with empirical validation; the standardized measurement process for defining, collecting, and analyzing quality data; Personal and Team Software Processes (PSP/TSP) for proactive defect prevention via defined individual/team disciplines; and assurance standards that embed verifiable quality requirements throughout the lifecycle. These layers add institutional depth via SEI methodologies, ISO measurement norms, and NASA-derived assurance practices, enabling quantitative quality prediction, technical debt tracking, and continuous empirical improvement in software systems.
