## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Mastery of Architectural Knowledge Graphs, Pedagogical Pattern Languages, and Curriculum Decision-Making under Uncertainty**

A world-leading expert next masters the integration of **architectural knowledge graphs** (AKG) with **4C/ID-based curriculum scaffolding** and formal **decision-making under uncertainty** models for evolving CS curricula. This layer moves beyond static ADRs and cognitive-load theory into dynamic, queryable knowledge repositories that support just-in-time pedagogical decisions, automated traceability of curriculum refactorings, and empirical evaluation of architectural choices in educational systems.

Key topics include:
- Formal representation of curriculum architecture as evolving knowledge graphs linked to learning objectives, instructional tasks, and assessment rubrics.
- Application of **pedagogical design patterns** (not just software patterns) within complex learning environments.
- Use of **utility-theory** and **Bayesian decision networks** for weighing architectural refactoring options under incomplete evidence.
- Longitudinal measurement of curriculum coherence using architectural metrics adapted from software architecture (e.g., coupling/fan-in/fan-out at the competency level).

**Concrete next sources:**

- Bass et al. (2021) already covered ATAM; next is explicit linkage of ATAM outputs into AKGs.
- Integration of **ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010** amendment processes with educational architecture documentation.
- Recent IEEE Transactions on Education work on **ontology-based curriculum modeling**.
