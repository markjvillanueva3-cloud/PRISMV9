**Next Layer: Mastery of Cognitive Task Analysis, Signature Pedagogies in SE, and Evidence-Based Curriculum Architecture for Computing**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s Academy galaxy next masters **cognitive task analysis (CTA)** for SE knowledge work, **signature pedagogies** specific to software engineering, and advanced **curriculum architecture theory** grounded in architectural thinking for learning systems. This layer moves beyond Bloom/Krathwohl, Shulman PCK, general CS curricula (CS2013/CS2023), and broad computing education research to the deliberate design of progressive, hierarchical knowledge structures that mirror software system design itself.

Key next sources include:

- **Cognitive Task Analysis** applied to expert software design processes (Clark et al., 2008; Crandall et al., 2006).
- **Signature pedagogies in computing** — the characteristic forms of teaching that organize professional formation in SE (Shulman extended by Gurung et al., 2009; Walker et al., 2010 on studio pedagogy in SE).
- **Curriculum as architecture / architectural thinking in education** — treating curriculum as a layered, modular, evolvable system with explicit interfaces, dependencies, and quality attributes (Seemann & Tartsch, 2021 already noted; extended in Lucke et al., 2022 on “curriculum engineering” and IEEE Transactions on Education work).
- **Learning progressions** refined through **knowledge integration** and **knowledge-in-use** frameworks from science/engineering education adapted to SE (National Research Council, 2012; Krajcik et al., 2014; extended to computing in ACM/IEEE curriculum design practice).
- **ABET EAC criteria** depth plus **continuous improvement loops** using direct and indirect assessment aligned to program educational objectives (ABET, 2022; Felder & Brent, 2016).

This layer equips experts to treat the Academy galaxy as a **software-intensive learning system** whose architecture must be explicitly designed, evaluated, and refactored using the same engineering rigor applied to PRISM itself.
