**Next Layer: Advanced Epistemic and Sociotechnical Knowledge Management in Software-Intensive Organizations**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s tribal-knowledge galaxy next masters the **sociotechnical infrastructure of knowledge ecologies**, moving beyond SECI, ba, and boundary objects into **infrastructural inversion**, **knowledge infrastructures**, and **epistemic cultures in engineered systems**. This layer emphasizes how classification systems, data standards, and coordination mechanisms become invisible yet constitutive of organizational memory and innovation velocity in large-scale software and systems engineering.

Key next-tier sources focus on:

- **Boundary infrastructures** and the politics of classification in information systems (Bowker & Star extensions).
- **Knowledge as sociomaterial practice** in distributed engineering environments.
- **Organizational memory as distributed cognition** in software architecture and lifecycle standards.
- **Infrastructural work** required to maintain epistemic coherence across heterogeneous engineering teams.

**Core uncovered areas include:**
- How knowledge infrastructures decay or “break” over time (infrastructure studies).
- The role of **classification schemas** and **standards** as active knowledge agents in systems engineering.
- **Transactive memory systems** and **collective mind** theories applied to software organizations.
- **Epistemic cultures** specific to engineering versus scientific domains.
