**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Curriculum Engineering & Learning Analytics (MIT-aligned CS/education depth)**

A world-leading expert in mit-curriculum next masters **curriculum sequencing as an optimization problem**, **knowledge-component modeling**, **prerequisite DAG inference**, and **adaptive learning systems grounded in cognitive science and Bayesian knowledge tracing**.

Core topics include:
- Formal modeling of curricula as directed graphs with prerequisite closure and topological sequencing (Heffernan & Heffernan, 2014).
- Cognitive Tutors and Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS) theory, including model-tracing and constraint-based modeling.
- Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT), Performance Factors Analysis (PFA), and Deep Knowledge Tracing (DKT) for predicting student mastery trajectories.
- Learning-analytics frameworks that treat curriculum as a data-driven policy optimization problem using Markov Decision Processes and reinforcement learning.
- Standards for reusable learning objects, competency frameworks, and SCORM/IMS-QTI/Learning Tools Interoperability (LTI) specifications.
