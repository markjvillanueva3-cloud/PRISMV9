## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) as a Core Curriculum Governance Mechanism**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s Academy galaxy masters the systematic use of **Architectural Decision Records** as both a pedagogical scaffolding tool and a living curriculum governance artifact. This layer extends beyond pattern languages and cognitive load theory into formal decision capture, traceability, and evolution of curriculum architecture—treating the curriculum itself as a long-lived, evolvable software system.

Key competencies include:
- Writing and maintaining ADRs for curriculum design decisions (context, forces, alternatives, chosen option, consequences, and status).
- Using ADR templates to manage technical debt in curricula, including deprecation and supersession of instructional approaches.
- Linking ADRs to learning outcomes, cognitive task analyses, and accreditation criteria for auditability.
- Applying lightweight ADR tooling and decision visualization in curriculum refactoring cycles.
- Integrating ADR practices into capstone-style curriculum studios where faculty treat curriculum evolution as an architectural practice.

This layer draws from software architecture practice applied to instructional systems engineering.
