## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer a world-leading expert masters centers on systematic ontology engineering methodologies that govern the full lifecycle of knowledge-graph construction, evaluation, and evolution for adaptive curriculum architectures. This includes competency-question-driven design processes, modular ontology networks, ontology matching/alignment for curriculum integration, and constraint languages that enforce representational integrity beyond OWL reasoning.

Key elements are METHONTOLOGY’s structured stages (specification, conceptualization, formalization, implementation, maintenance), the NeOn methodology’s networked ontology patterns for reusable learning modules, Gruninger-Fox competency-question grounding, and SHACL-based validation to ensure pedagogical consistency and machine-checkable learning paths in software architecture education.

These practices extend IEEE 42010 architecture descriptions and ACM CS2023 knowledge units into operational, evolvable systems while supporting formal verification of curriculum coherence.
