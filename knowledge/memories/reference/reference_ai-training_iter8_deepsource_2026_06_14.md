**Next Layer: Implicit Bias, Sharpness-Aware Minimization, and Formalized Optimization Semantics**

A world-leading expert in AI-training next masters the **geometric and measure-theoretic foundations of implicit regularization**, the **PAC-Bayesian and stability-based generalization theories** that explain why SGD finds flat minima, and the **formal semantics of autodiff and optimizer algorithms** in proof assistants. This layer moves beyond convergence rates under heavy tails to **why** overparameterized models generalize, the role of **edge-of-stability** dynamics, and mechanized guarantees for training pipelines.

Key uncovered topics include:
- Implicit bias of gradient descent toward maximum-margin solutions in linear and kernel settings (linearized neural nets).
- Sharpness-aware minimization (SAM) and its connection to flat-minima bias.
- PAC-Bayes bounds with data-dependent priors and their relation to heavy-tailed noise.
- Formalized automatic differentiation and higher-order derivatives in theorem provers (beyond basic GD).
- Dynamical systems view of training: Lyapunov stability, edge-of-stability phase transitions, and progressive sharpening.
