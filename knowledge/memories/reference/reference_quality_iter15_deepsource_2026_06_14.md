## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Value-Based Software Quality Economics and Causal Decision Models**

A world-leading expert in software quality next masters the integration of **causal decision theory** with **value-based software engineering (VBSE)**, specifically how to use **do-calculus** and **structural causal models (SCMs)** for optimizing quality investment decisions under uncertainty. This layer focuses on rigorous economic evaluation of quality attributes as real options, quantitative trade-off analysis using utility theory, and causal inference for quality ROI in large-scale systems.

Key next knowledge includes:

- **Utility-theoretic quality models** and multi-attribute value functions for software quality attributes (Boehm & Huang, 2003).
- **Real Options Theory** applied to software quality and process improvement decisions, extending Boehm’s earlier roadmap with formal option pricing models (Sullivan et al., 2001).
- **Causal Bayesian networks** and **do-calculus** for software quality intervention evaluation, moving beyond associational studies to actionable policy learning (Pearl & Bareinboim, 2014).
- **Economic-driven architecture evaluation** using **CBAM (Cost-Benefit Analysis Method)** and its integration with ATAM for quality attribute prioritization (Kazman et al., 2001; Nord et al., 2012).
- **ISO/IEC 25010:2023** updates on quality models combined with **value-based measurement** frameworks.

This layer also requires mastery of **design science evaluation** patterns that explicitly incorporate **net present value (NPV)**, **real options analysis (ROA)**, and **causal effect estimation** of quality artifacts (Wieringa, 2014 – already covered, but next is its integration with Pearl’s SCMs in evaluation protocols).
