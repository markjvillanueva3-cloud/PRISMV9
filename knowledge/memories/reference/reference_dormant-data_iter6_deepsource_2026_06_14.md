## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next-layer knowledge for dormant-data mastery focuses on formal verification of long-term data integrity, economic lifecycle modeling under uncertainty, and policy-driven archival governance drawn from distributed systems theory and digital curation standards.**

A world-leading expert next masters:

- **Probabilistic data auditing and proof-of-retrievability (PoR) systems** that provide cryptographic guarantees of intact dormant objects without full retrieval (Juels & Kaliski, 2007; Ateniese et al., 2007).
- **Formal temporal logic models for retention and deletion policies**, including process-algebraic verification of compliance over decades (Crampton & Huth already covered; extends to model-checking tools such as PRISM and TLA+ for policy automata).
- **Quantitative economic models of storage total cost of ownership (TCO) incorporating technology risk, media obsolescence, and real-options analysis** (Rosenthal et al., 2012; Lavoie, 2008).
- **OAIS functional entity extensions for “dormant” archival storage abstraction layers**, emphasizing information package transformation pipelines and representation information persistence (CCSDS 652.0-M-2, 2012).
- **Erasure-coded archival file system reliability analysis using Markov models and mean-time-to-data-loss (MTDL) under correlated failures**, building on but distinct from Miller et al. (2012) (Weatherspoon & Kubiatowicz, 2002; Baker et al. already covered; Plank et al., 2013).
- **MIT 6.033 and Stanford CS 355** treatment of secure, auditable long-term storage stacks and key management for archival keys.

These layers supply the mathematical and economic rigor required to design verifiable, cost-optimal dormant-data subsystems inside large-scale manufacturing intelligence platforms.
