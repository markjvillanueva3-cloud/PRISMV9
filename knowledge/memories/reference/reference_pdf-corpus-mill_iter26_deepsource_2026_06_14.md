The next layer emphasizes scalable digital library architectures and distributed pipelines for ingesting, indexing, and querying massive PDF corpora. This includes fault-tolerant abstractions for incremental milling of heterogeneous PDF collections (handling structure, layout, and embedded content per covered ISO standards), evaluation methodologies from large-scale test collections, and system design patterns that integrate parsing, metadata extraction, and retrieval models while preserving formal correctness guarantees from dependent types and proof assistants.
