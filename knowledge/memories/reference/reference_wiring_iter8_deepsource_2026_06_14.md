Key uncovered areas include:

- **Synchronous relational semantics** via clocked boolean relations and their categorical presentation as cartesian categories with synchronous feedback (Benveniste et al., 2010; Pouzet, 2006).
- **Polyhedral compilation for SDF and Lustre-like languages**, extending affine scheduling to handle multi-rate clocks and bounded buffers using integer linear programming over dependency polyhedra (Feautrier & Pouchet, 2011; Vasilache et al., 2016).

- **Categorical models of clocked circuits** using guarded recursion and ultrametric spaces lifted to symmetric monoidal categories with feedback (Milius & Litak, 2018; Bahr et al., 2022).
