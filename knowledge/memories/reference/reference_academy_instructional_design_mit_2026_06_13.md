## Instructional design (the pedagogy world-leading academies use)
- **Bloom's revised taxonomy** (Anderson-Krathwohl 2001): remember → understand → apply → analyze → evaluate →
  create. Map every lesson objective to a level; machining is heavily apply/analyze/create → hands-on + simulation.
- **Mastery learning** (Bloom 1984, "2-sigma problem"): require mastery of a unit before advancing; tutoring +
  formative feedback approach 1:1-tutor gains. PRISM Academy's skill-tree gating should enforce mastery.
- **Spaced repetition** (Ebbinghaus forgetting curve; SM-2 / Anki algorithm): schedule review at expanding
  intervals → durable retention of facts (feeds/speeds, codes, safety rules).
- **Mayer's multimedia principles** (coherence, signaling, redundancy, modality, segmenting): how to build
  video/animation lessons that don't overload. **Cognitive load theory** (Sweller): intrinsic vs extraneous vs
  germane load — worked examples + scaffolding for novices, fade to problem-solving.
- **Backward design** (Wiggins & McTighe, *Understanding by Design*): start from desired outcomes + assessment
  evidence, THEN design instruction. **Competency-based** progression (advance on demonstrated skill, not time).

## Assessment
- **Formative** (low-stakes, feedback-driven, drives mastery) vs **summative** (credential). **Item Response
  Theory** (item difficulty/discrimination) for adaptive quizzing. Auto-grade + rubrics.
