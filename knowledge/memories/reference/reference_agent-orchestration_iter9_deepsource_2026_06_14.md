**NEXT LAYER: Compositional Agent Orchestration via Session Types, Game Semantics, and Quantitative Verification**

A world-leading expert in agent-orchestration next masters **compositional mechanisms for multi-agent coordination** using higher-order session types, typed behavioral contracts, and assume-guarantee reasoning for orchestrated ensembles, combined with quantitative game-theoretic controller synthesis and probabilistic bisimulation metrics.

Key uncovered areas include:

- **Multiparty session types with dependent and graded refinements** for safe, enforceable orchestration protocols (Honda et al. 2016; Wadler 2012; Toninho & Yoshida 2018).
- **Game-semantic models of concurrency and agent interaction** at the system level, extending to typed strategies for open orchestration (Ghica & Tzevelekos already cited; Abramsky & McCusker 1999; Murawski & Tzevelekos 2016).
- **Assume-guarantee synthesis and contract-based compositional verification** for reactive multi-agent systems (Pnueli & Rosner already cited; Kupferman & Vardi 2001; Benveniste et al. 2018).
- **Quantitative verification and metrics for stochastic multi-agent orchestration**, including bisimulation distances and probabilistic contract refinement (Kwiatkowska already cited; Desharnais et al. 2004; Bacci et al. 2019).
- **Formal orchestration languages and calculus** grounded in the join calculus and ambient calculi with behavioral typing (Fournet & Gonthier 1996; Cardelli & Gordon 2000; Yoshida et al. 2013 – advanced extensions).

These build directly on already-covered synthesis, ATL, UPPAAL/PRISM model checking, and session-type foundations by adding **compositional scalability, metric-based refinement, and typed strategic reasoning** essential for trustworthy, large-scale agent orchestration platforms.
