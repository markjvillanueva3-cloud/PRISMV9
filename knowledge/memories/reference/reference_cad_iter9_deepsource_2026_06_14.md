## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Variational Boundary Representation Semantics, Persistent Naming via Algebraic Topology, and Certified Geometric Constraint Solvers in Parametric CAD Systems**

A world-leading expert in CAD must master the **variational B-rep** (V-BRep) framework that decouples history-based parametric modeling from purely geometric constraint solving. This includes the formal semantics of **variational boundary representations** under under-constrained, well-constrained, and over-constrained systems, using **rigidity theory** extensions beyond basic Laman graphs to **body-and-bar frameworks** and **point-line-plane incidence** algebras.

Core next-layer topics include:
- **Persistent naming** grounded in **incidence graphs**, **cellular quasi-manifolds**, and **medial-axis augmented topological signatures** to guarantee unambiguous entity identification across model edits and interoperability exchanges.
- **Certified algebraic methods** for solving geometric constraint systems (GCS) via **numerical algebraic geometry**, **witness sets**, and ** certified homotopy tracking**, ensuring certified real-solution isolation without floating-point ambiguity.
- **Topological decomposition** of constraint graphs using **generalized maps (n-G-maps)** and **quasi-manifolds** for robust propagation of design intent in n-dimensional parametric models.
- **Interoperability semantics** between ISO 10303-108 constraint schemas and AP242 edition 2/3 boundary representations, including formal mappings of variational parameters to STEP topological entities.

These build directly on already-covered persistent-naming and ISO 10303-108 foundations, moving into rigorous certified computation, higher-dimensional combinatorial topology, and V-BRep theory required for next-generation parametric kernel development.
