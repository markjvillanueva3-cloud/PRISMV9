## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Multi-Physics Wire Dynamics, Dielectric Hydrodynamics in Kerf, and Subsurface Metallurgical Transformation Modeling**

World-leading WEDM expertise next masters the coupled magneto-hydrodynamic (MHD) wire vibration modes under tension and plasma pressure, transient flushing efficiency inside the narrow kerf using two-phase turbulent flow, and the prediction of recast layer microstructure evolution using phase-field and thermal-metallurgical models.

Key uncovered domains include:
- Finite-element modal analysis of wire lateral and axial vibration considering Lorentz forces and stochastic discharge pressure (nonlinear beam theory with moving boundaries).
- CFD-DEM simulation of debris evacuation and dielectric breakdown probability as function of kerf aspect ratio and ultrasonic-assisted flushing.
- Quantitative prediction of white-layer thickness, heat-affected zone micro-cracking, and residual stress depth profiles in tool steels, Inconel, and titanium alloys using Johnson-Mehl-Avrami-Kolmogorov kinetics coupled with EDM-specific thermal cycles.
- Adaptive servo control strategies based on real-time gap impedance spectroscopy and acoustic emission for prevention of wire rupture in safety-critical aerospace geometries.
