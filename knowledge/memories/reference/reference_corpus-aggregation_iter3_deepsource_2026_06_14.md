**Next Layer: Advanced Theory and Systems for Scalable, Privacy-Aware, and Provably Consistent Corpus Aggregation**

A world-leading expert in corpus-aggregation next masters formal frameworks for **data integration under uncertainty at scale**, **lineage-aware polystore query optimization**, and **differentially private data fusion** drawn from graduate-level theory.

Key areas include:

- **Probabilistic data models and incomplete databases** (tuple-independent, block-independent, and BID databases) with query semantics under possible-worlds semantics (Suciu et al., 2011).
- **Declarative cleaning and entity resolution** using factor graphs and lifted inference, extending beyond HoloClean to scalable lifted belief propagation (Getoor & Machanavajjhala, 2012; Sen et al., 2010).
- **Provenance semirings** for polystore query planning, enabling algebraic combination of lineage across heterogeneous stores with different provenance models (Green et al., 2007; Glavic et al., 2014).
- **Differential privacy mechanisms** tailored to data integration, including composition theorems for multi-source fusion and private record linkage (Dwork & Roth, 2014 extended in Machanavajjhala et al., 2008; He et al., 2014).
- **Formal verification of data-integration consistency** using temporal logic and dependency constraints across autonomous corpora (Fagin et al., 2005; Mecca et al., 2011).

These build directly on the already-covered polystore, provenance, and privacy foundations with deeper theoretical machinery from database theory and statistical relational learning.
