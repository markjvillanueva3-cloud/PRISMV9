**Next Layer: Algebraic and Categorical Foundations for Typed Dataflow Wiring and Resource-Aware Synchronous Circuits**

A world-leading expert in PRISM’s wiring galaxy next masters the **categorical semantics of signal-flow graphs, string-diagram formalisms for linear dataflow, and graded modal type theories** that uniformly handle causality, latency, resource flow, and reversible computation in synchronous and hybrid wiring models. This layer supplies the algebraic infrastructure needed to reason equationally about wiring diagrams before translation to Lustre/Scade-style nodes or VHDL RTL.

Key uncovered topics include:
- **Symmetric monoidal categories and traced categories** as the native models of feedback and delay operators in synchronous languages (proven sound for causality analysis).
- **Bunched and graded modal types** that internalize notions of “number of tokens,” “clock ticks,” and “energy” directly in the type system of wiring programs.
- **Linear-logic and reversible-logic Curry-Howard correspondences** that give cut-elimination and proof-net representations for wiring nets with explicit resource accounting.
