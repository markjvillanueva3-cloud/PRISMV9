**NEXT LAYER: Safety-Critical Post-Processor Design for Parallel Kinematic Machines (PKM) and Five-Axis Systems**

**METHOD/THEORY:**  
Formal verification of post-processor algorithms using model-checking and temporal logic (CTL/LTL) to guarantee absence of axis-limit violations, singular configurations, and interpolation-induced contour errors under safety-critical constraints. Kinematic error propagation via screw theory and Lie-group methods for calibration of hybrid serial-parallel machine tools. Real-time trajectory generation with certified dynamic constraint enforcement (jerk, acceleration, velocity) using interval arithmetic and zonotope-based reachable-set computation. Safety-function integration per IEC 61508 and ISO 13849 into the post-processor kernel (Safe Torque Off, Safe Stop, Safe Direction, Safe Speed Monitoring mapped to NC blocks).

**STANDARDS DEPTH:**  
ISO 10218-1:2011 (Robots and robotic devices – Safety requirements for industrial robots – Part 1: Robots) and ISO 10218-2:2011 (Part 2: Robot systems and integration) applied to post-processor output validation. IEC 62061:2021 (Safety of machinery – Functional safety of safety-related control systems). ASME B5.57-2012 (Methods for Performance Evaluation of CNC Multi-Tasking Turning Centers) – safety-related axis coordination clauses.
