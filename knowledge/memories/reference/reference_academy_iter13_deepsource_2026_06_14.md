## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Mastery of Architectural Knowledge Management (AKM) and Curriculum-as-Architecture Evolution Frameworks**

A world-leading expert in PRISM Academy next masters **architectural knowledge management as a first-class pedagogical concern**, treating the Academy galaxy itself as a living, evolvable software architecture. This layer focuses on explicit representation, capture, and evolution of design decisions across curriculum modules, learning trajectories, and platform capabilities using formal AKM practices.

Key competencies include:
- Applying **architectural decision records (ADRs)** at scale within large curriculum repositories, including decision templates, decision graphs, and traceability to learning outcomes.
- Using **viewpoint-driven curriculum documentation** (inspired by ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010) to maintain multiple consistent views: pedagogical, cognitive-load, progression, and platform-implementation views.
- Mastering **curriculum refactoring patterns** as architectural refactoring, including detection of architectural erosion in learning pathways and systematic migration strategies.
- Integrating **just-in-time information** and **cognitive load management** directly into architectural decision making for complex skill acquisition (building on 4C/ID extensions).
- Employing **architecture evaluation methods** (ATAM, CBAM) adapted for educational program and platform design reviews.

This moves beyond introductory pattern application and basic ADR usage into systematic, tool-supported, organization-wide architectural knowledge management applied to curriculum engineering.
