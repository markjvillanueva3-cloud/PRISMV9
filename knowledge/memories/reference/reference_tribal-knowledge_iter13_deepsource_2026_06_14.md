## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Institutional Logics, Infrastructure, and Organizational Forgetting – Advanced Theory and Methods**

A world-leading expert next masters the **micro-foundations of institutional complexity** through practice-level mechanisms, coupled with rigorous methodological toolkits for studying infrastructural work and deliberate organizational forgetting.

Key next sources include:

- **Institutional logics as situated action**: how actors use logic multiplicity through “truces,” “selective coupling,” and “institutional bricolage” at the level of everyday practices (McPherson & Sauder, 2013; Pache & Santos, 2013).
- **Methodology of institutional ethnography and multi-site qualitative research** for tracing how standards become infrastructure (Bowker & Star, 2000 updated methods; Timmermans & Tavory, 2012 on abductive analysis).
- **Forgetting as active institutional work**: “managed ignorance,” “organizational amnesia,” and the strategic “remembering to forget” via rhetorical and material means (Anteby & Molnár already covered; next is Decker, 2016 on historical amnesia and Suddaby et al., 2016 on rhetorical history work).
- **Standards as living infrastructure**: evolution from boundary objects to “infrastructural inversion” and “classification systems as political artifacts” in high-stakes environments (Bowker, 2015; Edwards et al., 2013 on knowledge infrastructures).

These layers move beyond descriptive institutional complexity into **predictive models of logic recombination**, formal methods for infrastructural analysis, and deliberate forgetting strategies—essential for building robust “tribal-knowledge” systems that resist both ossification and drift.
