## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**Next Layer: Advanced Regenerative Chatter Theory, Nonlinear Dynamics, and Spindle-Bearing Thermo-Mechanical Interaction in Turning**

World-leading expertise advances to:

- **Time-domain simulation and nonlinear stability analysis** of turning processes including multiple regenerative effects, process damping, and variable time delay due to spindle speed variation.  
- **Nonlinear bearing stiffness and thermal preload evolution** under combined centrifugal, thermal, and cutting loads, including thermo-elastic spindle growth and its feedback on dynamic stiffness.  
- **Stability prediction incorporating measured spindle error motions** (radial, axial, tilt) under simultaneous thermal and dynamic loading.  
- **Probabilistic stability lobe diagrams** accounting for uncertainty in modal parameters, cutting process damping, and thermal boundary conditions.
