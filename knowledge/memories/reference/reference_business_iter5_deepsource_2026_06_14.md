## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Advanced Platform Pricing, Multi-Sided Governance, and Strategic Bundling Theory**

A world-leading expert next masters formal multi-sided pricing mechanisms under asymmetric information, cross-side network effects with elasticities, and dynamic bundling strategies that optimize platform governance and avoid envelopment. This includes Evans & Schmalensee’s (2016) “Matchmakers” framework for pricing in the presence of indirect network effects and participation constraints, together with Rochet–Tirole extensions on Ramsey pricing for platforms. 

Further depth covers Parker, Van Alstyne & Choudhury (2022) on “Platform Governance and Innovation Incentives,” examining how subsidy-side choice and access rules affect long-run ecosystem health. Institutional sources add NIST’s economic models for software bundling under uncertainty and BLS empirical work on observed platform price discrimination.

Key methodologies include:
- Two-sided Ramsey–Boiteux pricing
- Dynamic complementarity analysis
- Governance design via mechanism design under incomplete contracts

These build directly on the covered 2003–2011 pricing canon and MIT OCW innovation modules by adding testable, equilibrium-selection models used in antitrust and strategy research.
