**NEXT LAYER: Quality as Formal Economic Decision-Making Under Causal Uncertainty in Software Systems**

A world-leading expert next masters the rigorous integration of **statistical decision theory**, **Bayesian experimental design**, and **causal graphical models** into software quality investment decisions, moving beyond ISO 25010 product quality models and basic value-based SE roadmaps into explicit expected-value-of-information (EVOI) calculations, multi-attribute utility theory applied to architectural tactics, and transportability of quality evidence across development contexts.

This layer emphasizes normative frameworks for deciding which quality attributes to improve, when, and to what degree, using formal models of uncertainty, information value, and incentive alignment between stakeholders (principals vs. agents in software projects).

**Key uncovered areas include:**

- Bayesian decision analysis for software quality attribute prioritization (preposterior analysis)
- Real options theory applied to software quality refactoring and technical debt repayment
- Mechanism design and contract theory for quality incentives in outsourced or large-scale agile development
- Causal inference in software engineering experimentation (do-calculus for process improvement claims)
- Multi-attribute utility elicitation and normative quality models beyond ISO 25010
