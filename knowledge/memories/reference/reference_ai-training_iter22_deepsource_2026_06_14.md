## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer integrates empirical scaling laws with PAC-style generalization to predict optimal training compute/data/model tradeoffs, double-descent analysis for overparameterized regimes, and stochastic variational inference for scalable approximate Bayesian training. This advances beyond covered RG-PAC-Bayes, NTK, and mean-field limits by supplying concrete hyperparameter prediction methodology, phase-transition diagnostics in training dynamics, and extended risk-management processes for production AI pipelines.

Key elements include deriving power-law exponents, bounding generalization via margin-based or compression arguments under heavy-tailed noise (distinct from listed Alquier/Jiang works), and aligning training objectives with information-bottleneck principles for representation efficiency.
