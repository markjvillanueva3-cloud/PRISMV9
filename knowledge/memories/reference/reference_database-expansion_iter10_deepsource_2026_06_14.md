**NEXT LAYER: Quantitative Verification of Stochastic Hybrid Contracts for Database Elasticity under Learning-Augmented Control**

A world-leading expert in database-expansion next masters the integration of **stochastic hybrid systems (SHS)** with **assume-guarantee contract theories** for provably safe learning-augmented elasticity. This layer focuses on compositional verification of elastic scaling policies that combine continuous-time server provisioning, discrete reconfiguration events, and probabilistic machine-learning controllers, using formal contract algebras that guarantee stability and SLA adherence even when ML-based predictors are imperfect.

Key competencies include:
- Modeling database elasticity as piecewise-deterministic Markov processes (PDMPs) or stochastic hybrid automata with mode-dependent drift and reset maps.
- Applying contract-based design where elasticity contracts are formalized as pairs of assume-guarantee specifications over probabilistic temporal logic (PCTL, CSL, or STL).
- Using compositional reasoning to decompose large-scale elastic database clusters into verifiable sub-components (e.g., per-shard or per-tier contracts).
- Employing probabilistic model checking and controller synthesis techniques that account for both epistemic uncertainty (from learned models) and aleatoric uncertainty (from workload).
- Formalizing refinement and abstraction relations between stochastic hybrid models at different levels of fidelity for scalable verification.

This builds directly on prior Lyapunov, hybrid automata, and interface automata foundations by adding quantitative probabilistic contract composition and synthesis.
