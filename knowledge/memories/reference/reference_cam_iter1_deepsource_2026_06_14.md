## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Safety-Critical CAM Process Physics & Machining Dynamics (Iteration 1)**

A world-leading CAM expert next masters the fundamental theory of **process force prediction, regenerative chatter stability, and thermal-mechanical coupling** in milling and turning, grounded in mechanistic modeling rather than empirical constants.

Core topics include:
- Mechanistic force modeling using cutting pressure coefficients and edge forces (Altintas, 2012)
- Frequency-domain stability lobe diagram construction via zero-order approximation (ZOA) and semi-discretization methods
- Time-domain simulation of dynamic chip thickness including runout and helix effects
- Thermo-mechanical workpiece distortion and residual stress prediction in finish machining
- ISO 6986 and ASME B5.54 standards for machine tool performance and thermal error mapping
- NIST-reported best practices for uncertainty quantification in machining process models

This layer emphasizes analytical methods for predicting stability boundaries, force signatures, and part deflection without relying on proprietary constants, enabling physics-based feed scheduling, toolpath adaptation, and safe parameter selection in safety-critical aerospace and medical components.
