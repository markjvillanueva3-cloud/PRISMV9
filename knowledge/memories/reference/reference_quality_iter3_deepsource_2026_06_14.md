**NEXT LAYER: Quality in the Large – Architectural, Socio-Technical, and Empirical Quality Assurance at Scale**

A world-leading expert next masters the integration of **software quality attributes at the system-of-systems level**, focusing on **runtime quality trade-off analysis**, **socio-technical quality models**, and **large-scale empirical quality measurement programs**.

Key areas include:

- **Architecture-based quality evaluation and reasoning frameworks** that extend beyond ADD, emphasizing quantitative architectural risk and trade-off methods.
- **Technical debt quantification and management at enterprise scale**, including economic models of quality.
- **Continuous experimentation and A/B testing infrastructures** for quality in living systems (not just controlled experiments).
- **Socio-technical congruence and organizational metrics** linking team structure, communication, and delivered quality.
- **Modern quality metrics aggregation platforms** and **analytics dashboards** grounded in statistical process control adapted to software telemetry.
