**Next Layer: Formal Verification, Economic Game Theory, and Policy-Driven Retention in Dormant-Data Systems**

A world-leading expert in dormant-data next masters the intersection of **temporal logic model checking** for long-term retention policies, **mechanism design** for incentivizing honest long-term archival behavior, and **formal ontology-based provenance** for verifiable chain-of-custody over decades.

Key uncovered areas include:

- **TLA+ and temporal logic specifications** for verifying that archival policies remain invariant under Byzantine failures and organizational succession over century-scale time horizons (Lamport, 2002; Newcombe et al., 2015).
- **Mechanism design and algorithmic game theory** applied to storage contracts, particularly Vickrey-Clarke-Groves mechanisms and dynamic posted-price schemes that remain incentive-compatible as storage costs decay exponentially (Nisan et al., 2007; Sandholm, 2013).
- **Formal policy languages and compliance checking** using description logics and OWL-based retention ontologies to automatically verify adherence to regulatory retention schedules and destruction mandates across evolving legal regimes (Kahn & Wilensky, 2006; Dumortier, 2010).
- **Long-term key management architectures** using forward-secure hierarchical identity-based encryption and cryptographic key rotation with provable deletion guarantees (Boneh & Franklin, 2001; Bellare & Miner, 1999; Stanford CS 355, 2020).

These build directly on prior economic models and OAIS by providing machine-checkable guarantees that dormant-data policies remain both correct and incentive-aligned for 50–100+ year horizons.
