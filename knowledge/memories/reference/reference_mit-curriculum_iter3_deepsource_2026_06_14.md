A world-leading expert in mit-curriculum next masters formal probabilistic extensions of BKT, hierarchical skill modeling, automated discovery of knowledge components via statistical model selection, and policy optimization for adaptive sequencing under uncertainty.

Key areas include:
- Bayesian extensions using Dirichlet priors and individualized student parameters (Corbett & Anderson extensions, 2PL-IRT hybrids).
- Additive Factors Model (AFM) and conjunctive models for multi-skill credit assignment.
- Q-matrix validation and automated skill discovery using nonparametric Bayesian methods and matrix factorization.
- Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes (POMDPs) for optimal teaching policies.
- Curriculum sequencing via reinforcement learning and multi-armed bandit formulations grounded in cognitive theory.
- Integration with modern learning engineering pipelines using standards for competency frameworks and LTI Advantage services.
