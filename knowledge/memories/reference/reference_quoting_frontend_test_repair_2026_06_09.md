## What happened
Validating the quoting galaxy's "tested" clause, charlie ran the quoting test suites. **Backend = all green (1923 tests / 89 files).** Frontend had a nest of failures HIDDEN by a missing `ResizeObserver` polyfill in `web/src/__tests__/setup.ts` -- jsdom lacks ResizeObserver, Recharts' ResponsiveContainer references it at mount, so every chart-rendering quoting page threw `ReferenceError: ResizeObserver is not defined` BEFORE its real assertions ran. Fixed by an additive no-op polyfill (commit `62b114cb7b`, U-QP-WEB-TEST-RESIZEOBSERVER) -- `quote-specialization-pages.test.tsx` went 4/5 -> 5/5. That UNMASKED 10 pre-existing failures (the polyfill is additive, cannot regress a green test -- these were always red, just erroring on ResizeObserver).
