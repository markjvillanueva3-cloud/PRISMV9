## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
**NEXT LAYER: Multi-scale plasma-workpiece interaction and metastable phase transformation kinetics in recast layers of nickel superalloys under WEDM-induced non-equilibrium solidification.**

World-leading expertise advances to coupled magneto-hydrodynamic plasma column dynamics with rapid solidification theory, emphasizing dendrite arm spacing refinement, partitionless solidification, and GND accumulation at cell boundaries in single-crystal and directionally solidified nickel-base alloys. Focus shifts to quantitative prediction of recast-layer microcrack initiation via phase-field models that incorporate both thermal and solutal undercooling under extreme thermal gradients (>10^6 K/m) and cooling rates (10^5–10^7 K/s).

**Core topics to master next:**

- MHD plasma flow, dielectric flushing, and anode/cathode heat flux partitioning under high-pressure flushing (Klocke 2018 already covered; next: full two-temperature plasma + dielectric vaporization models).
- Non-equilibrium interface kinetics, solute trapping, and formation of γ′/γ″ metastable phases or amorphous regions in recast layers (extension of MIT 3.14 Lecture 22).
- Gradient crystal plasticity modeling of GND densities and back-stress evolution in recast layers under thermal contraction mismatch with substrate (extension of MIT 3.22 Lecture 18).
- Synchrotron Laue microdiffraction and high-energy XRD for spatially resolved triaxial residual stress and elastic strain tensors through recast + heat-affected zone.
- Fracture mechanics assessment of recast-layer microcracks as fatigue crack initiation sites under combined residual stress and external cyclic loading in turbine environments.

**METHOD/THEORY/standards depth:**

- Two-temperature MHD plasma models with dielectric dissociation and bubble dynamics.
- Kurz-Giovanola-Trivedi (KGT) model and phase-field solute trapping for rapid solidification of Ni-superalloys.
- GND-based strain-gradient crystal plasticity coupled to thermal-metallurgical fields.
- ISO 25178-2:2022 (Areal surface texture – Part 2: Terms, definitions and surface texture parameters) for advanced recast surface feature characterization.
- ASTM E915-2021 and ASTM E1426-14(2021) extended to depth-resolved synchrotron measurements per NIST TN 2201 methodology.
