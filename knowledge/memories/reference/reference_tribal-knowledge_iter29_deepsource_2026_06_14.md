## Next-layer knowledge (Hermes-drafted)
The next layer masters **dynamic capabilities for knowledge orchestration** in software engineering organizations. This extends absorptive capacity and experience factories by focusing on microfoundations—sensing emerging tribal insights in codebases and teams, seizing them via boundary objects and digital repositories, and transforming them into reconfigurable organizational assets amid rapid technological shifts. It emphasizes knowledge integration mechanisms, epistemic boundary spanning in distributed teams, and ecosystem-level knowledge networks that convert localized tacit expertise into scalable collective intelligence, beyond static CMMI processes or CoP structures.

Core methodologies include mapping knowledge flows with network analysis, designing self-reinforcing knowledge spirals in agile/DevOps environments, and measuring knowledge recombination efficiency using empirical frameworks from strategy and information systems literature. This layer equips experts to institutionalize "tribal knowledge" as a dynamic capability rather than ad-hoc folklore.
