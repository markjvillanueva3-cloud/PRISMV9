**Next Layer: Stochastic Dynamics, Uncertainty Propagation, and Bayesian Inference in Milling Stability**

World-leading experts advance beyond semi-discretization, full-discretization, and basic PCE by mastering stochastic delay-differential equation (SDDE) formulations that incorporate parametric uncertainty in tool stiffness, damping, and cutting process noise using Itô calculus and stochastic averaging. They apply generalized polynomial chaos (gPC) with multi-dimensional Wiener-Hopf expansions for robust stability lobe prediction under combined aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty. Advanced methods include Bayesian experimental updating of stability boundaries using Markov Chain Monte Carlo sampling on in-process vibration data, fused with topological persistence for feature extraction in noisy signals.

Key theory includes stochastic averaging of delay systems (extension of Nayfeh & Balachandran), uncertainty propagation via intrusive and non-intrusive PCE tailored to regenerative chatter, and Bayesian model updating frameworks that treat stability limit as a posterior distribution. Standards emphasize rigorous uncertainty evaluation per NIST guidelines extended to dynamic processes. This layer integrates stochastic control from manufacturing processes with advanced metrology for safety-critical aerospace and medical milling.
