**NEXT LAYER: MHD-Plasma-Solidification Coupling and Stochastic Subsurface Damage Prediction in Safety-Critical WEDM**

World-leading expertise advances to multi-physics simulation of plasma channel expansion under strong external magnetic fields, coupled with non-equilibrium solidification kinetics and stochastic discharge location modeling to predict crystallographic texture gradients, microcrack probability, and white-layer thickness variation in Ni-based superalloys and titanium alloys.

Key uncovered domains:
- Magnetohydrodynamic flow and Lorentz force distribution inside the discharge plasma column under transverse magnetic fields (MHD-EDM).
- Phase-field modeling of rapid solidification and dendrite arm spacing in the recast layer under extreme thermal gradients (>10^6 K/s).
- Stochastic process modeling of successive discharge probability density functions using Markov chains and dielectric breakdown recovery statistics.
- Quantitative EBSD-based metrics for geometrically necessary dislocation (GND) density gradients and kernel average misorientation (KAM) as subsurface damage indicators.
- Metrological traceability chains for areal surface texture and subsurface damage using hybrid SEM-EBSD-FIB tomography under ISO 25178 and ASME B46.1 frameworks.

These topics are treated in advanced manufacturing physics curricula and recent standards-track research.
