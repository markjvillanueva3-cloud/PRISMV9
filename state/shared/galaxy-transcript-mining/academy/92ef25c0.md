**DECISIONS**  
*Key architectural / scope choices and their rationale*

- **Slot‑binding wrapper (`/checkin-lima`)** – isolates the PRISM‑Academy work in a dedicated “lima” slot, preventing cross‑chat interference (R7 conflict‑fork).  
- **Free local video extraction** – replaced paid Whisper/Claude APIs with `openai-whisper`, Ollama vision models, and local PDF parsing to eliminate credit costs.  
- **Page‑by‑page PDF extractor** – pypdf + regex scoring gives deterministic tribal entries without external services (R12 fail‑loud for encrypted PDFs).  
- **Graph‑node bridges** – 47 nodes wired to consumer engines (`postprocessor`, `speedfeed`, `toolselection`, etc.) so catalog data is queryable by other PSN nodes.  
- **PWA + Cloudflare tunnel** – `cloudflared` exposes the local Vite dev server over HTTPS, enabling “Add to Home Screen” on iOS/Android (required for mobile training).  
- **AcademyHub drill‑down UI** – domain → sub‑category → auto‑sort courses by prereq DAG; improves discoverability and reduces cognitive load.
