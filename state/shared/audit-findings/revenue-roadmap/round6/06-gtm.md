# Round-6 Agent 6 — Go-To-Market / Acquisition Funnel Gap

**Verdict:** The v7.2 roadmap is **product-complete and funnel-blind** — 61 MS0 pages, 39+ MS1 billing units, 44 Master Post units, an entire training apparatus, and **zero acquisition units**. PRISM has the bait (free SFC, free Master Post — both designed correctly) but no hook, no line, no reel.

**Confirmed gaps:**
- **No marketing/landing site.** `mcp-server/web/src/pages/LandingPage.tsx` exists but is routed *inside the logged-in app* (`<Route path="home" element={<LandingPage/>}` under the auth shell). No public, unauthenticated, SEO-indexed site. App title = "PRISM Dashboard".
- **No signup flow.** `mcp-server/web/src/api/auth.ts` has `register({username,email,password})` POST, but no signup *page*, no email verification, no onboarding wizard. `LoginPage.tsx` exists; no `SignupPage.tsx`.
- **No trial mechanics built.** The pricing table *describes* a "14-day Pro, credit-card-required" trial; `U-SUB-05 TrialConversionEngine` is in MS1's deferred 39-unit block — and MS1 is pushed to "after SFC + Master Post have paying customers" → the trial is roadmapped *behind* the revenue it generates. Backwards.
- **No upgrade prompt built.** `UpgradePromptModal` is referenced with 5 designed triggers (4th calc/day, non-P material, Optimize click, Compare-3up, 6th recipe) — but only as text; no component, `FeatureTierEngine` (`U-SUB-03`) deferred, `state/shared/feature-tiers.json` **does not exist** despite being called "canonical."
- **No referral/affiliate system.** MS1 has `U-SUB-43 AffiliateFraudEngine` + a "thin shim" `U-SUB-18 ReferralEngine` — the *fraud heuristic* is specced before the *referral feature* exists. Backwards.
- **No SEO content, docs site, support channel, email onboarding, demo/sandbox.** Not mentioned anywhere. Round-5 a8: "LEARN trial→paid conversion KPI — not in any acceptance gate." Round-5 a7's projection assumed "~0.5% organic conversion, ~2% paid acquisition of 13,000 US job shops" — with no channel to deliver either.
- **Two free-tier hooks ARE designed (and they're the right ones):** SFC Free = 3 calc/day, P-steel only, watermarked CSV (v7.1 line ~438); Master Post Free = 5 posts/mo, watermarked output line `( Generated by PRISM Master Post — masterpost.prism.app )` (`U-REV-MP-37`, P0). Plus 3 scattered "free-tier acquisition funnel" notes in MS2: `U-INV-LATHE-05` cost-per-part calc ("viral free-tier funnel for fastener-industry quoting"), `U-INV-CROSS-03` tolerance-stack web tool ("surface as free-tier acquisition funnel"). Seed crystals — but no unit *connects* a free tool → signup → upgrade.
