## E. SAFETY GATES (non-negotiable — when may a result be "cleared for live run")

A result is `cleared for live run` **iff ALL** hold — and even then authorizes only a **supervised, dry-run-first, single-block, feed-hold-ready FIRST ARTICLE with a human at the E-stop**, never unattended production:
1. **Right config + resolved units** — intended `.mcfg` confirmed by UI read-back; controller RPost matches (VR-not-NGC for VMC-03/04); units declared+matching on NC and `.mcfg` (no inference). VMC-03/04 `unitsResolved:false` → double units check or stay un-cleared.
2. **Real, complete run** — editor fully rendered; sim reached **observed** terminal-complete (not a timer); report quiescent on re-read; simulated line/block coverage == full source program.
3. **Clean by the fail-CLOSED gate** — `evaluateSimulationReport` `pass && collisionCheckConfirmed`, `collisionCheckRan:true` earned by #2, zero collisions/limits/errors. **Empty/unconfirmed report is NEVER clean.**
4. **No error modal** during the run.
5. **Declared collision scope** — verdict carries `modeledBodies{machine,tool,holder,stock,fixture}`. Unmodeled holder/fixture/stock → downgrade to "kinematics + tool-collision clear ONLY; workholding UNVERIFIED — manual setup verification required".

**Irreducible floor:** `controllerVerified:false` is hardcoded — a sim-clean verdict is conformance + kinematics evidence, **explicitly NOT controller-verified and NOT a real-machine safety guarantee**.
