## Deconfliction

All 78 assigned unit IDs are globally unique — no two slots
share a unit (the allocator fail-louds and exits non-zero on any collision).

**Caveat (mustHumanVerify):** priority-queue filters units flagged shipped in
`MILESTONE_PROGRESS`. Its claim filter is best-effort only — a topic-string
token match, NOT a `slot-task-claim` lookup — so a unit actively claimed by a
peer may still appear here; run `node .claude/helpers/slot-task-claim.mjs check`
before claiming. A unit whose deliverable shipped but whose envelope was never
flipped (silent close-out debt) can also still appear — verify against
`/close-out-audit`. Most likely for the golf CLEANUP-MS0 queue, several of which look complete.

## How a slot consumes this

1. Operator runs the launch line for the slot (e.g. `/checkin-alpha /loop work my RGS queue`).
2. The slot reads its row in this spec, picks its rank-1 unit.
3. `/checkin` claims the unit (slot-task-claim), builds, per-file scrutiny, 3-of-3 Stop gate, commits.
4. The autonomous loop advances to rank-2, and so on down the queue.
5. On queue exhaustion, the slot pulls fresh from `node .claude/helpers/priority-queue.mjs --pick --slot <s>`.
