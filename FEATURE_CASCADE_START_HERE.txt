WHAT IS THIS?
=============
A complete design package for automatic feature discovery across PRISM sessions.

Problem: When Session N creates new engines/hooks, Session N+1 doesn't know
         they exist. 890+ hours wasted per 50-session roadmap on rediscovery.

Solution: Feature Cascade Protocol tracks what each session creates, publishes
          it automatically, and measures adoption. All metadata-driven (no new
          engines needed).
