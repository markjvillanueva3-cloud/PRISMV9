Divide minutes by 60
degree minutes to convert: 30° 42' 
divide minutes by 60: 42 ÷ 60 = 0.7

degree minutes and seconds to convert: 30° 41' 15"

divide decimal minutes by 60: 41.25 ÷ 60 = 0.6875
bring down degrees:  30.6875°
