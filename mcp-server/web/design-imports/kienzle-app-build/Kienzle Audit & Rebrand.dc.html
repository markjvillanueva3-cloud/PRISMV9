The Rebrand · 01 — The Name

Stop naming it
after light.

Prism is optical refraction — a pretty physics metaphor with nothing to do with cutting metal. And it's now legally Sandvik's.

The engine's real heart is the Kienzle specific-cutting-force law — the equation that turns chip geometry into the force behind every cut. Name the company what the machine already computes.

it's already in your codebase:
KienzleForceModelEngine.ts
src/physics/constants.ts → kc1.1 per ISO group
THE KIENZLE SPECIFIC CUTTING FORCE
Fc = kc1.1 · b · h(1−mc)
cutting force = specific force × chip width × chip thickness, corrected by the material's Kienzle exponent. Every speed & feed in the app descends from this.
