## CAD / CAM / Programming

- **CAD:** Mastercam, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Autodesk Inventor
- **CAM:** Mastercam (X-series + Mill 3D), hyperMILL, GibbsCAM, Fusion CAM
- **Post processors:** Custom-tuned per machine (Okuma OSP, Hurco WinMAX, Haas Pre-NGC, Mitsubishi FP/C30EA, Fanuc 31i)
- **Drawing intake:** PDF, STEP, IGES, DXF, native SolidWorks/Inventor/Fusion files. We routinely consume drawings exported from any major CAD platform our customers use.

## Process competencies

- **Cold-heading die manufacture** — punches, dies, KO pins, formers, trim dies
- **Lathe turning** — bar work to 3000 mm bed, 380 mm chuck (LB 3000EX Big-Bore for fastener-blank work)
- **Multi-axis turning + mill-turn** — Okuma Multus B250II handles complete-cycle parts with B-axis live tooling
- **5-axis milling** — Okuma M460V-5AX for swept compound geometry, undercut features, draft-walled die cavities
- **Wire EDM** — taper, conditional, multi-pass for hardened die plates and intricate die-cavity slugs
- **Sinker EDM** — graphite + copper electrode work, ribbed cavity finishing, sharp-corner detail
- **High-precision finishing** — Roku-Roku HC 658-II for mold-class surface finish (Ra < 0.2 μm achievable)

## Materials we routinely run

- Tool steels: A2, D2, M2, M4, S7, H13, 4140 PH, P-20, 420SS
- High-speed steels & cobalt grades (M42, T15)
- Carbides (PCD-tipped tooling, brazed inserts) — for select die-cavity work
- Stainless: 303, 304, 316, 17-4 PH, 15-5 PH
- Aluminum: 6061-T6, 7075-T651, MIC-6 cast plate
- Brass: 360, 385
- Copper / OFHC for EDM electrodes
- Heat-treated alloys for die-blocks (with our heat-treat partner network)
