## What's in this folder

| File | Purpose |

## Methodology

1. **Customer-base analysis** — JM's 34,786 indexed programs grouped by 301 distinct customer tags (after filtering noise like programmer-folder-names).
2. **Corporate-family research** — top 11 customer parents identified via web research (Steel Partners, Fontana Gruppo, ITW, MW Industries, etc).
3. **Lookalike vectors** — three independent paths:
   - **Same-parent expansion**: subsidiaries of current customers JM doesn't yet quote
   - **Same-end-market**: roofing-fastener / aerospace-fastener / automotive-fastener buyers analogous to current customers
   - **Geographic neighbors**: northern-IL and Wisconsin fastener-belt shops in JM's natural radius

4. **Prioritization** — Tier-A targets are PE-backed/changing-hands (procurement-window opportunity); Tier-B are established same-parent siblings; Tier-C are aspirational.

## How to use

1. Pick 5-10 prospects from `prospect-companies.md` Tier A or B for the first wave.
2. Find the right contact via LinkedIn (Director of Procurement / VP Operations / Director of Sourcing / VP Manufacturing) — search query suggestions are in the CSV.
3. Personalize the email template with the merge fields filled in.
4. Attach the capabilities one-pager (consider exporting as PDF first).
5. Track responses in a spreadsheet — recommend Tier-A at 2 emails 10 business days apart, then phone follow-up.

## Notes

- The list excludes JM's existing customers (the 100+ already in your CRM).
- The email template is intentionally generic-but-credible — it leans on JM's actual proven capability with similarly-shaped customers, not generic "we have CNC machines" language.
- "Why JM" angles cite specific parent-company facts (acquisitions, recent ownership changes) so prospects can tell you read the news, not a marketing list.
