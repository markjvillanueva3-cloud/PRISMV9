## Response handling — what to expect

**No reply (most common, ~70%):** wait 10 business days, send a one-line follow-up: *"Hi {FIRST_NAME} — circling back. Are RFQs going through someone else on your team?"*

**Reply asking for capability one-pager:** send the PDF version of `capabilities-overview.md`. Attach 2-3 photos of representative finished parts (if available). Offer a 20-min Teams/Zoom call.

**Reply asking for an RFQ:** ask for the drawing(s), expected annual volume, current vendor's tooling specs (if they'll share), and target lead-time. Promise written quote within 48 hours.

**Reply saying "currently committed to vendor":** ask to be the second source. *"Understood. We'd value being your secondary on this — would you accept a no-obligation quote on your next 2 RFQs to compare?"*

**Reply asking about certification:** be precise. ISO 9001 = "in progress" or "certified" — pick the truthful answer. Do NOT claim AS9100 unless certified.

## Volume-pacing recommendation

- **Wave 1:** 5 Tier-A targets, hand-picked, fully personalized variants. Send Tuesday 10 AM Central.
- **Wave 2:** 8-10 Tier-B sister-company targets. Send 5-7 days after Wave 1.
