# JM Die Company — Capabilities & Equipment

**Prepared:** 2026-04-21 · **Purpose:** Customer-facing work envelope + capability overview

JM Die is a cold-heading die & tooling shop serving **100+ fastener-industry customers** (including ITW, Alcoa, Optimas, SFS, Holo-Krome, Arconic, CAMCAR, EJOT, Fontana). Specialties: cold-heading dies, punches, electrode manufacturing (graphite & copper), wire EDM tooling, trilobe-pattern electrodes.
