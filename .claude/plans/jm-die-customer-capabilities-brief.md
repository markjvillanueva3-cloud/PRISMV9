# JM Die Company
## Capabilities & Equipment Brief

---

## Industries We Serve

**Fastener manufacturing** — cold heading, thread rolling, and rivet production. Tier-1 fastener OEMs, specialty forgers, and precision tooling customers across North America.

---

## Core Specialties

- **Cold-heading dies** — punches, inserts, and matched-set tooling
- **Electrode manufacturing** — graphite and copper, including trilobe and custom geometries
- **Wire EDM tooling** — progressive dies, stripper plates, punch-and-die sets, form tools
- **Sinker EDM** — die cavities, complex cores, fine-finish surfaces
- **Precision deep-hole work** — gun drilling for die inserts, ejector bores, and coolant passages
- **Centerless grinding** — high-volume cylindrical tolerance work on punches, pins, shafts
- **Complete CNC manufacturing** — from solid stock through inspection under one roof

### EDM — 7 Wire + 2 Sinker

- **7 wire EDM systems** — profile cutting, punch & die, taper cutting, skim passes, progressive-die work. Parallel-job capacity across the wire department keeps lead times tight on multi-part and multi-operation jobs.
- **2 sinker EDM systems** — die-sinking, electrode-burn operations, fine-finish surfaces. One system is dual-head for simultaneous electrode burns.

**Flagship wire EDM specs (Mitsubishi FA10S):** Submerged cutting, auto-threading, up to **30° taper**, **215 mm max workpiece height**, 80 mm U-V travel. Wires stocked: ⌀0.25 mm (MD+ Pro II), ⌀0.20 mm (MV1200S).

### Precision Grinding

- **Centerless grinding** — high-volume cylindrical tolerance work; ideal for punches, pins, and shaft-type components
- **Surface grinding** — flat grinding and surface finishing

### Deep-Hole Drilling

- **Gun drilling** — dedicated deep-hole capability for die inserts, ejector pin bores, coolant passages, and long aspect-ratio holes where conventional drilling can't hold tolerance

### Inspection

- **CMM** — dimensional verification and first-article inspection per customer specification
- **Optical comparator** — profile inspection and visual verification
