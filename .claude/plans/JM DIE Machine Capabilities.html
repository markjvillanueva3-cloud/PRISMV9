Precision Tooling for the Fastener Industry

Capabilities & Equipment Brief

Industries Served

Fastener manufacturing — cold heading, thread rolling, and rivet production. Tier-1 fastener OEMs, specialty forgers, and precision tooling customers across North America.

Core Specialties

  • Cold-heading dies — punches, inserts, and matched-set tooling
  • Electrode manufacturing — graphite and copper, including trilobe and custom geometries
  • Wire EDM tooling — progressive dies, stripper plates, punch-and-die sets, form tools
  • Sinker EDM — die cavities, complex cores, fine-finish surfaces
  • Hard turning and hard milling — finishing operations on hardened tool steels (50–62 HRC) in a single setup, replacing many traditional grinding operations
  • Precision deep-hole work — gun drilling for die inserts, ejector bores, coolant passages
  • Centerless grinding — high-volume cylindrical tolerance work on punches, pins, shafts
  • Complete CNC manufacturing — from solid stock through inspection under one roof

Electrical Discharge Machining — 7 Wire + 2 Sinker

  • 7 wire EDM systems — profile cutting, punch & die, taper cutting, skim passes, progressive-die work. Capable of wiring carbide (round extrusion), AR400 / AR500 abrasion-resistant plate, and hardened tool steels — tough-material capability many shops outsource. Parallel-job capacity across the wire department keeps lead times tight on multi-part and multi-operation jobs.
  • 2 sinker EDM systems — die-sinking, electrode-burn operations, fine-finish surfaces. One system is dual-head for simultaneous electrode burns.
Flagship wire EDM specs (Mitsubishi FA10S): Submerged cutting, auto-threading, up to 30° taper, 8.46" (215 mm) maximum workpiece height, 3.15" (80 mm) U-V travel. Wires stocked: ∅0.010" (0.25 mm, MD+ Pro II) and ∅0.008" (0.20 mm, MV1200S).

Precision Grinding

  • Centerless grinding — high-volume cylindrical tolerance work on punches, pins, and shaft-type components
  • Surface grinding — flat grinding and surface finishing

Deep-Hole Drilling

  • Gun drilling — dedicated deep-hole capability for die inserts, ejector pin bores, coolant passages, and long aspect-ratio holes where conventional drilling cannot hold tolerance

Inspection

  • Fleet of optical comparators — profile inspection, visual verification, and dimensional verification at multiple stations across the shop floor

Partner Network

  • Welding — full welding services (TIG, MIG, tool-and-die repair, build-up, and fabrication) provided through our trusted partner shop located next door. Single point of contact, single quote, single delivery. Your job moves between facilities without you moving paperwork.
